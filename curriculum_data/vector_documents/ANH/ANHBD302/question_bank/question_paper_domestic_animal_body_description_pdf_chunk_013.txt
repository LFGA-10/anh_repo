Section F: Creating (10 Questions)

(This section tests the ability to generate new ideas, products, or ways of viewing things.)

91. (Subjective) Design a simple, illustrated "cheat sheet" or key for a fellow student to quickly identify the main external body parts of a cow, as shown in Figure 14 of the document. Your design should creatively group and label the reference points (e.g., "Shoulder Points," "Pelvic Landmarks").

92. (Subjective) You are tasked with creating a public awareness poster for farmers about the importance of monitoring vital signs (temperature, pulse, respiration) in their livestock. Propose a catchy title, three key messages with simple visuals, and a short slogan for your poster.

93. (Subjective) Formulate a detailed checklist for a veterinary technician to use when performing a physical examination of a sheep's digestive health. The checklist should integrate knowledge of the digestive system's anatomy (mouth, teeth, rumen) and physiology (rumen movements, feces assessment).

94. (Subjective) Develop a mnemonic device or a short story to help students remember the names and order of the twelve cranial bones listed in the document (Frontal, Interparietal, Occipital, Parietal, Temporal, Ethmoid, Sphenoid).

95. (Subjective) Imagine you have to explain the concept of a synovial joint to a group of 10-year-old children on a farm visit. Create an analogy or a simple model using common farm items to illustrate the key parts: bones, cartilage, joint capsule, and synovial fluid.

96. (Subjective) Propose a research question that investigates the relationship between coat type (simple vs. composite) and thermoregulatory efficiency in cattle raised in different climates.
