78. (Subjective - Model Answer)
o Hyperthermia: An elevated body temperature due to
failed thermoregulation, where the body's heat load
exceeds its ability to cool down (e.g., heatstroke). The
body's set-point is normal. (2 marks)
o Fever: An elevated body temperature that is a regulated
response to a pyrogen (e.g., from an infection). The body's
thermostat is reset to a higher level. (2 marks)
