87. (Subjective - Model Answer)
o The blood-brain barrier is a significant challenge because
it is designed to be highly selective, blocking the passage
of most molecules from the blood into the brain to protect
it from harmful substances. (2 marks)
o This means that many potentially therapeutic drugs
cannot reach the brain tissue, making diseases like brain
tumors, infections (meningitis), and neurological
disorders very difficult to treat. Drug companies must
design molecules with specific properties (e.g., small size,
high lipid solubility) that can cross this barrier. (3 marks)
