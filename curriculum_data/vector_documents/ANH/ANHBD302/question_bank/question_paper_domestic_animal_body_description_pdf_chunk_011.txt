Section E: Evaluating (10 Questions)

(This section tests the ability to justify a stand or decision.)

81. (Subjective) A farmer has the option to buy a young calf or a 5-year-old cow. He wants an animal for long-term breeding. Using your knowledge of age estimation from horn rings and dentition, evaluate which animal would provide a more reliable age estimate and why. Justify your reasoning.

82. (Subjective) A student is preparing to dissect a specimen and is deciding between using basic dissecting tools or more specialized equipment. Critically evaluate the factors that should guide the selection of material, tools, and equipment for an anatomical dissection.

83. (Subjective) An animal is showing signs of severe dehydration. Would you expect its circulating blood volume to be high (hypervolemia) or low (hypovolemia)? Justify your answer by explaining how dehydration affects the fluid compartments of the body.

84. (Subjective) Two animals are suspected of having a fever. One is a chicken with a temperature of 41.5°C, and the other is a pig with a temperature of 40.0°C. Based on the normal temperature ranges provided, evaluate which animal is more likely to have a true fever and explain your reasoning.

85. (Subjective) A veterinarian must decide on a surgical approach for a dog with a deep muscle injury in the hind limb. Based on your knowledge of the main muscle groups (hamstring group, quadriceps, etc.), justify why a detailed knowledge of regional anatomy is more critical for the surgeon than just systemic anatomy.
