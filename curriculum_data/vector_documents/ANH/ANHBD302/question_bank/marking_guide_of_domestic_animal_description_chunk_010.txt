73. (Subjective - Model Answer)

o Systemic anatomy: Studies the body by organ systems
(e.g., all the bones of the skeletal system, all the arteries
of the circulatory system). (1 mark)
o Regional anatomy: Studies the body by areas or regions
(e.g., the thorax, the abdomen), focusing on the
structural relationships among all the systems (muscles,
nerves, blood vessels, etc.) in that region. (1 mark)
o Clinical application: A tumor in the thorax would affect
not just one system, but the lungs, heart, esophagus,
and major vessels within that confined space. A regional
approach is crucial for understanding the full impact and
planning surgery. (2 marks)
74. (Subjective - Model Answer)
o The hypothalamus, a part of the brain, monitors the
internal environment (e.g., blood hormone levels,
temperature). (1 mark)
o In response, it releases regulatory hormones that travel
to the pituitary gland. (1 mark)
o The pituitary gland, often called the "master gland," then
releases its own hormones that stimulate or inhibit other
endocrine glands (like the thyroid or adrenal glands).
This links neural signals to hormonal responses. (2
marks)
