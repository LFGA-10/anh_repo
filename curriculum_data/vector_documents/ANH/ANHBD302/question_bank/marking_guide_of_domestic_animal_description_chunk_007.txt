61. (Subjective - Model Answer)

o Monogastric: Single, simple stomach (e.g., pig). Digestive
strategy relies on enzymatic digestion in the stomach and
small intestine. (2 marks)
o Ruminant: Compound stomach with four chambers
(rumen, reticulum, omasum, abomasum). Digestive
strategy relies on microbial fermentation in the rumen to
break down cellulose, followed by enzymatic digestion in
the abomasum. (2 marks)
62. (Subjective - Model Answer)
o The mammalian system is tidal (air flows in and out of
the same passages), meaning oxygen-rich and oxygen-
poor air mix. (1 mark)
o The avian system is unidirectional. Air flows through the
lungs in one direction constantly, allowing for a more
efficient, continuous extraction of oxygen. (1 mark)
o Air sacs act as bellows, ensuring a constant flow of fresh
air through the lungs during both inhalation and
exhalation. This is a more efficient system to meet the
high metabolic demands of flight. (2 marks)
63. (Subjective - Model Answer)
o These are physiologically linked. A high body
temperature (e.g., from infection/fever) increases the
metabolic rate of cells. (1 mark)
o This increased metabolism requires more oxygen, leading
to an increase in respiratory rate (tachypnea). (1 mark)
o To deliver this oxygen and remove waste faster, the heart
must pump more often, leading to tachycardia. (1 mark)
o Scenario: An animal with a systemic bacterial infection
or after intense exercise. (1 mark)
