2. Restraint: Place the animal in a head gate or stanchion if

3. Approach: Stand to the side of the animal's head, never

4. Securing the Head: With one hand, firmly grasp the
animal's nose or place a hand through the halter. For
sheep/goats (as per Fig 74), you may need to sit the
animal on its rump and hold its head. For cattle (as per
Fig 75), you may need an assistant to hold the nose ring
or place a hand over the animal's eye to distract it while
you lift the lip.
5. Examination: Using your free hand, gently lift the
animal's upper lip or pull down the lower lip to expose
the incisor teeth. Do not put your fingers inside the
mouth.
6. Finish: Slowly release the animal's head and step back
calmly. (5 points for a clear, logical, and safety-focused
