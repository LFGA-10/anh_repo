o The calf would provide a more reliable age estimate. (2

o Reasoning: Age estimation in young animals using
dentition is very accurate as the eruption of permanent
incisors follows a predictable timeline (e.g., 2 teeth at 1
year, etc.). In a 5-year-old cow, while full permanent
dentition is present, the estimate becomes much less
precise. Horn rings can be misleading after a few years as
they become indistinguishable. The document notes that
after the 5th year, the three first rings are
indistinguishable, and by the 8th year, all rings are
indistinguishable. (3 marks)
82. (Subjective - Model Answer)
o Nature of the tissue: Dissecting soft tissue (muscles,
organs) requires fine scalpels, scissors, forceps, and
probes. Dissecting hard tissue (bone) may require saws
or stronger instruments. (2 marks)
o Goal of the dissection: A simple observational
dissection for teaching anatomy can use basic kit tools. A
more detailed research dissection may require specialized
micro-dissecting instruments. (1.5 marks)
o Species and organ size: The size of the animal and
specific organ dictates the size and type of tools needed
(e.g., large scalpel for a cow heart, fine forceps for a
nerve). (1.5 marks)
