How to take the body temperature of animals
Figures66, 67 and 68. Animal’s places where body temperature is taken.
If you suspect that the animal has a high temperature use your thermometer to check it.
Remember that a high temperature is one sign of ill health. When an animal has a high
temperature it has a fever.
B. Physiological factors affecting the body temperature
 Female, pregnant, young animals have a relatively higher temperature than male, non-
pregnant, old animal.
 In all healthy animals the temperature varies during the day.
 High atmospheric temperature increases the normal body temperature.
 Excitation, forced exercise increase the temperature.
 The normal body temperature increase after feeding.
