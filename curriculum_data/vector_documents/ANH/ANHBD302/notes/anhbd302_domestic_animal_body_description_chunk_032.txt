2. Structure of lower respiratory tract
Figure45. Structure of lower respiratory tract
Thelower respiratory stat with bronchi, ends with the alveoli, and includes all the air
passageways in between. Except for the two main bronchi that are formed by the bifurcation of
the trachea, all the structures of the lower portion of the respiratory tract are located within
the lungs.
Bronchial tree
It is the air passageways that lead from the bronchi to the alveoli. Its enter the lung and each
main bronchus divides into smaller bronchi, which divide into even smaller bronchi and finally
into tiny bronchioles. The bronchioles continue to subdivide down to the smallest air
passageway called alveolar ducts. The alveolar ducts end in group of alveoli called alveolar sacs.
