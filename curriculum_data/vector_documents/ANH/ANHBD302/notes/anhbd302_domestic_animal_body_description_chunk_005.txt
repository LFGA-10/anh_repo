The body of animal has the major divisions which are clearly distinguishable externally: the
head (caput), the neck (collum), the trunk (truncus), the tail (cauda), and the limbs (membra).
Each one of these sections is in turn divided into regions, which function as descriptive motifs
for topographic anatomy.
