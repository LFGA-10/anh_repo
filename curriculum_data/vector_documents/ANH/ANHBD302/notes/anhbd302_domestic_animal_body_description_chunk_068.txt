2. Elevated body temperature (Hyperthermia)
It is elevation of body temperature above the normal range. The following phenomena are met
with it:
Chill.
Uneven distribution of the external temperature of the body.
Loss of appetite & impaired digestion:
 The secretions of digestive juices are lessened.
 Peristalsis suppressed (constipation).
 Thirst increased
Mental depression.
Albuminuria
D. Thermoregulation in other domestic animals
The body temperature is influenced by many environmental factors for which poanimals must
cope with to be able maintain normal temperature. The methods of thermoregulation vary
among different animal species.
