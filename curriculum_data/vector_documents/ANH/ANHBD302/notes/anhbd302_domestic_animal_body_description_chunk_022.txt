LO.2.4. Describe circulatory apparatus
Introduction
The cardiovascular system consists of the heart and a system of vessels for distribution of the
blood to the tissues of the body and to the lungs for exchange of gases. Whether or not the
blood is oxygenated, vessels that carry blood away from the heart are called arteries, and
vessels that carry blood toward the heart are called veins. Circulation to the lungs (pulmonary
circulation) is functionally and anatomically separate from circulation to the restof the body
(systemic circulation).
 Topic1. Structure of heart
1. Heart
The heart is a cone-shaped hollow muscular structure. The base is directed dorsad or cra-
niodorsad and is attached to other thoracic structures by large arteries, veins, and the peri-
cardial sac.
