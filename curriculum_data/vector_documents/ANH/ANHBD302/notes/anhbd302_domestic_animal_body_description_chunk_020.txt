LO.2.3. Describe integumentary system
 Topic1. Characterization of the integumentary system for the domestic animals
The integumentary system is consisting of the skin, hair, nails and exocrine glands. The skin is
only a few millimeters thick yet is by far the largest organ in the body. Far from being just a
covering to make sure that the body's underlying tissues aren't exposed, the skin serves a
number of functions, ranging from helping the body eliminate waste to protecting the body
from physical trauma.
1. Definition of terminology:
Integument is an outer protective covering such as the feathers or skin of an animal, a rind or
shell.
2. Layers of the skin:
 Epidermis/cuticle: The epidermis is the outer layer of skin. The thickness of the
epidermis varies in different types of skin.
