layers of meninges, known as the dura mater, arachnoid mater and pia mater. These coverings
have two major functions:Provide a supportive framework for the cebral and cranial

Blood Brain Barrier
 There is a secure barrier between the blood and the CSF fluids that surround the brain
called the Blood Brain Barrier.
 This barrier is due to the tight fitting endothelial cells that prevent filtration of larger
molecules.
 The purpose of the Blood Brain Barrier is to protect the brain from chemicals, and
other harmful substances . In this process, Antibodies and medications also blocked
 Testing for those substances normally blocked - can indicate the level of disruption by
diseases: ie meningitis and multiple sclerosis.
