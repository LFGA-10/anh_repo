Pleura
The pleura are a thin transparent serous membrane which encloses the lungs. It also lines the
internal surface of the thoracic cavity.
 2 pleural membranes are present each forming a sac.
 Each sac comprises of a parietal part and a visceral part.
 Parietal pleura assume different names depending on the region as: costal pleura,
diaphragmatic pleura and mediastinal pleura.
 Visceral pleura/pulmonary pleuracover the surface of the lung.
Mediastinum
Mediastinum is the area between the right and left pleural sacs. It extends from the
thoracic inlet to the diaphragm.
It’s divided into:
 The cranial mediastinum: it is a space in front of heart that contains esophagus, blood
vessels, lymph nodes, vagus nerve, phrenic nerve, thymus ( in young).
 Middle mediastinum:(space occupied by heart) that contains the heart, origin of great
blood vessels, esophagus, tracheal bifurcation, vagus, phrenic nerves.
 The caudal mediastinum:(space caudal to the heart) that contains the abdominal aorta,
vena cava, esophagus, vagus and phrenic nerve.
