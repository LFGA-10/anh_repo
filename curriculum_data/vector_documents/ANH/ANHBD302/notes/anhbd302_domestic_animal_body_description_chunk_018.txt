3. Smooth muscle /white muscle or visceral
Smooth muscle is also called non striated involuntary muscle, or sometimes involuntary
muscle. It is found all over the body in places such as the eyes, the air passageways in the lungs,
the stomach and intestines, the urinary bladder, the blood vessels, and the reproductive tract.
It carries out most of the unconscious, internal movements that the body needs to maintain
itself in good working order.
