e) Sternum
The sternum also called the breastbone forms the floor of the thorax. It is made up of a series
of rod like bones called sternebra. Only the first and the last sternebra are named and used as
landmarks. The others are numbered from cranial to caudal. The first most cranial sternebra is
called the manubrium or manubrium sterni. The last most caudal sternebra is called the xiphoid
or xiphoid process. A piece of cartilage, known as the xiphoid cartilage , extends caudally from
the xiphoid process and is easily felt in most animals at the caudal end of the sternum.

f) The vertebral column
The vertebral column, also called the spine, protects the spinal cord, supports the head and
serves as an attachment site for muscles affecting body movements. The vertebral column
bones are grouped into the cervical (neck), thoracic (back), lumbar (loin), sacral (croup), and
caudal (tail) vertebrae.
