LO.2.2. Describe musculoskeletal system
 Topic1. Characterize the animal skeleton
A skeleton is the internal structure that protects and supports the soft organs, tissues and other
parts of a vertebrate organism, and is composed of bones and cartilages or, in certain animals,
cartilages alone.
1. Definition of terminologies:
 Skeleton is the system that provides support to an organism, internal and made up of
bones and cartilage in vertebrates, external in some other animals.
 Joint is any part of the body where two bones join, in most cases allowing that part of
the body to be bent or straightened.
2. Skeleton features
2. 1. Axial skeleton
Because the bones of the head and trunk are located along the axis of the body, they are
referred to as the axial skeleton.
a) The skull
The skull contains both cranial and facial bones.
