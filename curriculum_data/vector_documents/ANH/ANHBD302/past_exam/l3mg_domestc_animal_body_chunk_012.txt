Question 24 /5marks
What are the meaning and conclusions of secondary rumen contractions?
ANSWER
Secondary contraction: Also called the B-wave or forward-moving
contraction. Usually occurs after a primary contraction. /2marks
Conclusions
➢ Contraction of the ventral blind sac continuing up through the dorsal
blind sac using the dorsal coronary pillar./1.5mark
➢ Contraction proceeds across dorsal sac forcing gas Pocket to the
cardiac sphincter. /1.5mark
