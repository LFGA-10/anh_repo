Question 8 /3marks
There is a secure barrier between the blood and the CSF fluids that
surround the brain called the Blood Brain Barrier. Describe the process of
blood brain barrier.
Answer
Blood Brain Barrier
There is a secure barrier between the blood and the CSF fluids that
surround the brain called the Blood Brain Barrier.
❖ This barrier is due to the tight fitting endothelial cells that prevent
filtration of larger molecules.
❖ The purpose of the Blood Brain Barrier is to protect the brain from
chemicals, and other harmful substances . In this process, Antibodies
and medications also blocked
