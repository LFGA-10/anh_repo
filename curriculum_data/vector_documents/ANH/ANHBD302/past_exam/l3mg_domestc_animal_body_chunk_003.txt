Question 6 /2marks
What are the two (2) roles of pharynx for both digestive apparatus and
respiratory apparatus?
ANSWER
The pharynx
The pharynx is Common to both the respiratory system (nasopharynx) and
the digestive system (oropharynx)
Nasopharynx: Is the dorsal part of the pharynx which is located above the
soft palate. It communicates with the cavities of the middle ear through the
auditory / eustachian tubes. It has a respiratory function.
Oropharynx: Is the ventral part of the pharynx that is located between the
soft palate and the root of the tongue. It has a digestive function.
