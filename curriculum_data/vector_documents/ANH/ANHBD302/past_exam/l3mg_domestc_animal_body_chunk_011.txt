Question 21 /5 marks
The rhythm of the pulse is judged by observation of the interval occurring
between successive waves. Differentiate the two (2) types of pulse rhythm.
ANSWER
-Rhythmic or Regular: Individual pulse beats are separated by intervals of
equal duration. /2marks
-Arrhythmic or Dysrhythmia or Irregular: The interval is not regular.
