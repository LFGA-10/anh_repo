Question 12 /2marks
What are the meaning of the following terminologies for pulse and heart
beat?
1) Pulse rate
2) Heart rate
ANSWER
➢ Pulse rate:
In medicine, a pulse represents the tactile arterial palpation of the
cardiac cycle by trained fingertips. The pulse may be palpated in ant
place that allows an artery to be compressed near the surface of the
body, such as at the neck, wrist, at the groin, behind the knee, near
the ankle joint, and on foot.
Pulse rate is the number of heart beats per minute. Example: The
resting pulse rate for an average adult is between 60 and 80 beats.
➢ Heart rate is the speed of the heart beat measured by the number of
contractions(beats) of the heart per minute(bpm)
