Question 29 /4marks
The bovine horn serves to determine the age of cattle. What among the
following statements relate a true determination?
At 10 months or 12 months, the first ring appears.
At 6 months to 9 months, the first ring appears
At 20 months to 2 years, the second ring appears
At 20 months to 48 months, the second ring appears
ANSWER
At 10 or 12 months, the first ring appears.
At 20 months to 2 years, the second ring appears
