Questions 3 /3marks
Name the three (3) layers of the skin.
ANSWER
The 3 Layers of the skin are:
1)Epidermis/cuticle (1mark)
2)Dermis/cutis or derma (1mark)
3)Hypodermis or subcutaneous tissue (1mark)
More information.
1)Epidermis/cuticle: The epidermis is the outer layer of skin. The
thickness of the epidermis varies in different types of skin.
2)Dermis/cutis or derma
This is the second layer (underlying the epidermis). The dermis also
varies in thickness depending on the location of the skin.
3)Hypodermis or subcutaneous tissue
The subcutaneous tissue is a layer of fat and connective tissue that
houses larger blood vessels and nerves. This layer is important is the
regulation of temperature of the skin itself and the body. The size of this
layer varies throughout the body and from individual to another.
