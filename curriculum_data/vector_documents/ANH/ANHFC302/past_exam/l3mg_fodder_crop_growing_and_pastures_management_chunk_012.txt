17. Describe the land preparation operations. /5 marks
Reference
Curriculum 2019-L3, on 400page.
L.U:1 Cultivate fodder crops; L.O: 1.3 Prepare the land
Answer/
Seedbed preparation operations include activities such as:
Primary operation: Ploughing or digging (if hand tools are used)
Secondary operation: Harrowing to break large soil clods into smaller ones,
and sometimes rolling of the ground to firm the soil
The main reasons for preparing a seedbed are:
-To kill the weeds either by burying them or by desiccation through
exposures to the sun.
-To bury crop residues from previous season’s crop so as to make it easy to
plant.
. By burying the trash, the decomposition of animal and vegetable matter is
speeded up resulting in the addition of organic matter into the soil as well as
releasing of nutrients for use by the plants.
Seedbed preparation operations loosen up the soil thereby facilitating
rainfall infiltration into the soil as well as improving soil aeration.
