Advantages of sowing by broadcasting
1. It is faster than the row planting.
2. It can be easily used for obtaining a higher plant population than would
be the case with row planting especially in growing fodder crops.
3. It is ideal for small seeded crops.
4. It provides a good soil cover quickly.
Disadvantages
1. It is wasteful because it uses more seeds unnecessarily.
2. It is difficult to regulate the plant population.
3. It is difficult to use machine.

Answer/
Pasture productivity is done by Determining the location of the plots, that is
to say the squares of 4 to 25 m2 (Representative samples of the plant cover),
and protecting them by a fence if they are still grazed by cattle.
Making a regular mowing each time cattle could graze.
We weight the grass (fresh matter), and after we dry in oven at 105℃ to
determine the dry matter weight.
By summing annual cuts, we obtain the production level per year of a plot,
from which we calculate the productivity of the whole pasture (the
productivity is to say, DM/ha/year and production that is to say the
production of the whole pasture).
