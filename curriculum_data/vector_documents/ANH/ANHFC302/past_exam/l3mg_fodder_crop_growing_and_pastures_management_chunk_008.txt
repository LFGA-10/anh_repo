Sunlight
All plants require light from the sun for photosynthesis during their period.
Shortages of sufficient light lead to etiolated growth in plants, while too
much sunlight and heat in the early stage of plant growth are equally
harmful.

Relative humidity in the atmosphere is dependent on temperature. A high
humidity lowers the rate of evaporation and transpiration and,
consequently, water intake through the roots. Warm temperatures in a
humid environment conduce to the rapid multiplication of disease-causing
germs and, therefore, are not good for susceptible plants.
