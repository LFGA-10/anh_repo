Answer the requirements of a site for fodder crop cultivation:
The specific agronomic requirements for growing of forages differ from
species to species and may be site specific. The following are factors that
make particular place suitable for specific fodder crop: Altitude.
Some fodder crop better suited for low altitudes, while others prefer high
altitudes.
Temperature.
Temperature has a very strong impact on crop growth. Physiological plant
activities affected by temperature are: cell division, tissue growth,
photosynthesis, germination, flowering, ripening, etc. Very high
temperatures, speed up the rate of water loss through transpiration and
evaporation, thus leading to the wilting and eventual death of plants. Low
temperatures, on the other hand, reduce the rate of cell division and plant
growth, and the death of plants under a heavy frost.
Soil types
All fodder crops do not grow well in some types of soil. The availability of
fodder crop, the acidity or alkalinity depth, drainage, aeration, etc.., all
contribute to making the area suitable for specific fodder crops and
unsuitable for others.
