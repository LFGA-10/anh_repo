Reference
Curriculum 2019 L3, on 401 page
L.U: Cultivate fodder crops; L.O: 1.4. Select seeds
Answer. Seed selection criteria suitable for fodder crops:
✓ Nutritive value
✓ Growth rate
✓ Persistence
✓ Ease of establishment
✓ Ability to mix with other crops
✓ Cost of establishment and maintenance
04. What are the uses of focus tonningii as a fodder tree? /2 marks
Reference
Curriculum 2019-L3 on 407
L.U:2 Perform pastures layout and improvement, L.O.2.1. Identify
common pasture fodder trees and shrubs
Answer/ The uses of focus thoniingii:
F. thonningii plants are dry season animal fodder and other multipurpose
benefits.
It has been evaluated the local use, feed value, browse biomass production,
and effects on soil fertility.
In the pasture, it is used as shed for animals in summer, medicine, live
hedge for fencing and food for animals.
Edible Uses Fruit – cooked: A good jam can be made from the ripe fruits.
Leaves - cooked.
They are occasionally eaten as a vegetable.
