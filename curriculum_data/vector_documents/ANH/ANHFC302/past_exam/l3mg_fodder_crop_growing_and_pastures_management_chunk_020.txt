27. A farmer of Nyabihu District has a pasture of 5ha and he wants you to
help him to estimate the productivity of his pasture; supposed that all
the factors (types of grasses, soil richness, rainfall) are maintained.
What will be your attitudes/behavior in front of that situation in order
to come up with sustainable solution to the farmer? /4 marks

(Fodder Crops and Pasture Management) Page 17 of 18 – Marking Guides

Answer/
Pasture productivity is done by Determining the location of the plots, that is
to say the squares of 4 to 25 m 2 (Representative samples of the plant
cover), and protecting them by a fence if they are still grazed by cattle.
Making a regular mowing each time cattle could graze.
We weight the grass (fresh matter), and after we dry in oven at 105o C to
determine the dry matter weight.
By summing annual cuts, we obtain the production level per year of a plot,
from which we calculate the productivity of the whole pasture (the
productivity is to say, DM/ha/year and production that is to say the
production of the whole pasture).
