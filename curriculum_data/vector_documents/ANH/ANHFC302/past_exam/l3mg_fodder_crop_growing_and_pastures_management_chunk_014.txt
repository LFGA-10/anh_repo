20. Describe in brief the paddocking system. /5 marks
Reference
Curriculum 2019 L3 on 414 page
L.U:2 Perform pasture layout and improvement
L.O: 2.5. Fence and paddock pasture
(Fodder Crops and Pasture Management) Page 12 of 18 – Marking Guides

Answer/
Paddocking (Rotational grazing) involves fencing a pasture into several small
paddocks.
Paddocking has many advantages to the pastures grazing system:
• Paddocking enables rotational grazing
• It is possible to limit the use of pastures to specific periods and
particular animals.
• Livestock graze paddocks in series, moving to a new paddock when the
forage is ready for grazing. In general, put livestock into a paddock
when the forage is 25-30cm tall; remove livestock when the pasture is
grazed down to 8cm
• Rotational grazing ensures pasture re-growth and helps to maintain or
improve the condition of the pasture.
