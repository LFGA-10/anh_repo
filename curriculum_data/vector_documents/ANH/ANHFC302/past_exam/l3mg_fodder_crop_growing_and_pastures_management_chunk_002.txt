02. Show the major problems and limiting factors causing low fodder
crops production. /4 marks
Reference
Curriculum 2019 –L3-on 397 page
L.U :1Cultivate fodder crops; L.O: 1.1. Identify fodder crops
Answer:
The major problems and limiting factors causing low fodder crops
production are:
✓ small land area under fodder crops cultivation
✓ cultivation of low yielding fodder crops
✓ common use of traditional practices
✓ lack of research-based knowledge on fodder production and
management
✓ poor soil fertility and poor irrigation practices
✓ low rainfall and draught conditions
✓ loss from insects, diseases, and weeds
✓ limited use of chemical fertilizer
✓ Lack of quality and improved fodder seeds crop rotations and
overgrazing pastures.
