01. What is the importance of fodder crops? /4 marks
Reference
Curriculum 2019-L3on 397 page
L.U: Cultivate fodder crops; L.O: 1.1. Identify fodder crops
Answer:
Importance of fodder crops Pastures and fodder crops are the cheapest form
of animal feed available (in terms of quantity and quality).
✓ They provide a way of introducing farmers to the concept of improved
livestock nutrition They provide a large quantity of high quality and
palatable fodder within a short time.
✓ They are easy to grow; the husbandry requirements are similar to other
crops that are familiar to the farmer.
✓ Pastures and fodder, particularly the leguminous types, improve soil
fertility through their ability to fix nitrogen.
✓ If well managed, planted pastures help control soil erosion. Pasture seed
production and the sale of excess fodder can be a good source of income.
