The purpose of round-up in the ranching system is:
-To do an assessment of the animals
-To castrate the male
- For identification of the animals, usually by branding
- To vaccinate, spray, dip or otherwise treat against diseases such as
anthrax, foot and mouth disease, rinderpest etc.
- To take an accurate count of the stock.
- To separate breeding animals from steers and heifers.
- To wean calves
- To cull and market selected animals.

Answer/
A. Treatment of crop residues with urea
Urea treatment techniques
Treating crop residues with 4% urea solution at 45–50% moisture improves
the nutritive value by increasing the digestibility, palatability and crude
protein content.
The chopped material is soaked in urea solution mixed at the rate of 4 kg
urea (fertilizer grade) in 100 litres of water (4%). The urea-water solution is
sprinkled on batches of the chopped material as it is added to the pit.
The mixing can be done in the pit or on a plastic sheet on the ground before
packing the pit.
Urea treatment procedure
Weigh the straw to be treated, weigh an equal amount of water to that of the
straw add 40gm of urea per kg of straw and mix it thoroughly in water soak
the straw into the mixture for 6 hours drain the straw, and feed it to the
cattle
