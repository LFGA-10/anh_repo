24. Classify the advantages of rotational grazing. /5 marks

L.U: Manage fodder crops; L.O: 3.4. Apply pasture rotation and
ranching method
Answer/
The pasture area is divided into two or more sub pastures, also called
“paddocks,” with each sub pasture being grazed and “rested” in turn.
Rotational or periodic grazing is usually recommended for the following
advantages:
- Prevention of pasture overgrazing
- Prevention of loss of soil through erosion
- High level of pasture productivity
- Control of internal and external parasites
- Time that animals are on a paddock is referred to as the grazing period;
time off the paddock is the rest period.
