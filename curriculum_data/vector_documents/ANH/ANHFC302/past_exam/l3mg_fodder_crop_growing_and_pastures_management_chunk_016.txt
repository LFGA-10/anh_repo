Treatment of crop residues with molasses
Molasses treatment techniques
Molasses should not be used at more than about 20% of the diet. Because
ruminants given molasses-based diets grow more slowly than when the
grain is used as the major energy source, even when the protein and mineral
deficiencies of molasses are rectified.
Note:
Two syndromes can occur with molasses, both when there is very little other
feed for cattle to eat: molasses toxicity and molasses bloat.
