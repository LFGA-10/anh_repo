Answer/ The association of grasses with legumes has several
advantages:
 Legumes fix atmospheric nitrogen and reduce requirements in nitrogen
fertilizers
 Legumes facilitate grass growth in combination.
 Legumes increase protein concentration in the basic feed ration
 Legumes prolong the time of use (grazing days or carrying capacity) in dry
seasons,
 They facilitate the germination of grasses and annual plants,
 They can be used as “nursery” for seedlings
 Association is much more competitive with weeds than grasses or legumes
alone.
 Association has a better ground cover, which helps fighting against
erosion.
