L.U:3 Manage pasture grazing; L.O: 3.1. Organize the herd and flock
in the pasture
Answer/ The considerations when organizing herd/flock in the pasture:
Animal species: Animal species should be considered because varies in
size and utilize pasture differently.
Ages: As the age increases, the animal’s daily feed intake increase.
Animal Productivity: The type of production will influence the use of
pasture by animals.
Pasture carrying capacity: The number of animals the pasture will
support in a year should be considered when organizing a herd to avoid
pasture overgrazing.
Pasture fodder nutrients content: The nutritive value of pasture fodder
should be evaluated to meet daily animal nutritional requirement.
Animal nutritional requirements: This is crucial factor when organizing
herd in pasture because it help to know the number of animals to graze the
pasture.
