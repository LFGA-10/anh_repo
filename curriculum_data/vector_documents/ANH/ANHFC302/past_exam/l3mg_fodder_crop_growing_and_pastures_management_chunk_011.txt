16. Illuminate three (3) constraints to better utilization of crop residues

L.U:4 Improve the nutritive value of crops residues and straws; L.O:
4.3. Use crops residues and straws.
Answer/Constraints to better utilization of crop residues & remedies
• Low digestibility: Treat with alkali or urea to increase digestibility
• Low fermentable nitrogen: Supplement with urea or poultry manure
• Low bypass protein and low dietary fat: Supplement with bypass
protein-containing lipid, e.g. Oilseed meal and fish meal
• Low minerals: Add mineral nutrients especially Sulphur
• High fiber and low rate of passage Supplement with legume fodder or
young grass at 2 kg DM/100 kg LW.
