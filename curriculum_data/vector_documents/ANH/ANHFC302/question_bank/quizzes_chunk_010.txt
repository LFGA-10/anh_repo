Marking Guide (Total: 20 marks)
o a) Insufficiency of Maize Stover (4 marks):
▪ Explain that maize stover is a roughage with low
protein (often <6%), low digestibility, and low
energy. (2 marks)
▪ It can only meet maintenance requirements, not
production needs like milk yield. Supplementation
is necessary to provide a balanced diet. (2 marks)
o b) Urea Treatment Method (6 marks):
▪ Materials (2 marks): Chopped maize stover, urea
(fertilizer grade), water, a plastic sheet or pit,
weighing scale, watering can.
▪ Procedure (4 marks): (Step-by-step)
1. Weigh 100 kg of chopped stover.
2. Dissolve 4-5 kg of urea in 100 liters of water.
(1 mark)
3. Spread the stover on the plastic sheet and
sprinkle the urea solution evenly over it,
mixing thoroughly to achieve 45-50%
moisture. (1 mark)
4. Pack the treated stover tightly into the pit to
exclude air, and seal it with the plastic sheet,
weighing it down. (1 mark)
5. Leave it to treat for at least 3-4 weeks (longer
in cold weather) before opening and feeding. (1
mark)
