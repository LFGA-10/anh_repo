4. Question: A farmer has a large area of natural pasture that is
overrun with bushes and has poor-quality grass. As a livestock
extension officer, explain how you would advise the farmer to
improve this pasture using the techniques of bush
control and oversowing.
Marking Guide (Total: 8 marks)
o Bush Control (3 marks):
▪ Advise on mechanical methods like uprooting or
slashing with hoes/pangas, ideally before the
bushes seed. (2 marks)
▪ Explain that this exhausts the food reserves in the
roots, leading to their death. (1 mark)
o Oversowing (5 marks):
▪ Explain that oversowing is introducing improved
pasture species (like Desmodium or Rhodes grass)
into the existing natural pasture. (2 marks)
▪ Describe the process: First, overgraze the existing
pasture to reduce competition. (1 mark)
▪ Then, broadcast the improved grass or legume
seeds, mixed with fertilizer like TSP/SSP. (1 mark)
▪ Finally, remove livestock to allow the new species to
establish. (1 mark)
