Section 2: True or False Questions
Instructions: Indicate whether the following statements are True
(T) or False (F).
Level: Understanding
1. T / F Concentrates are characterized by high fiber content
and low digestibility.
(False - They have low fiber content and high digestibility.)
2. T / F Desmodium and Lucerne (Alfalfa) are examples of
leguminous fodder crops.
(True)
3. T / F Mechanical scarification and hot water treatment are
methods used to overcome seed dormancy.
(True)
4. T / F In broadcasting, it is easier to regulate plant population
and perform weeding compared to line planting.
(False - Line planting is easier for these operations.)
5. T / F Paddocking a pasture helps in controlling internal
parasites by allowing a rest period that breaks their life cycle.
(True)
6. T / F Creep grazing is a method where mature animals are
given access to the best pasture first, followed by their young.
(False - It allows young animals to access high-quality forage
while excluding mature animals.)
7. T / F Crop residues like maize stover are naturally high in
protein and are excellent as a sole diet for growing animals.
(False - They are low in protein and need supplementation.)
