1. Question: A farmer has a 2-hectare field and wants to
establish a pasture of Desmodium (a legume) for his dairy

cows. The seeds have arrived, but they are very hard. Describe
two methods the farmer can use to overcome seed dormancy
before planting, and explain the importance of inoculating the
Desmodium seeds.
Marking Guide (Total: 6 marks)
o Overcoming Dormancy (2 marks): Any two of the
following:
▪ Mechanical scarification (e.g., rubbing with
sandpaper). (1 mark)
▪ Hot water treatment (immersing in hot water and
allowing to cool). (1 mark)
▪ Acid treatment (using sulfuric acid). (1 mark)
o Inoculation Importance (4 marks):
▪ Desmodium is a legume that requires a specific
strain of Rhizobium bacteria to form nodules on its
roots. (2 marks)
▪ These bacteria fix atmospheric nitrogen into a form
the plant can use, improving soil fertility and
reducing the need for nitrogen fertilizer. (2 marks)
2. Question: You are advising a farmer who practices cut-and-
carry (zero-grazing) and wants to ensure his dairy cows have
enough feed during the dry season. Based on the module,
suggest two methods of forage conservation and briefly explain
the key principle behind each.
Marking Guide (Total: 6 marks)
o Method 1: Hay making (2 marks):
▪ Principle: Conserving forage by drying it to a low
moisture content (below 15-20%) to inhibit the
growth of mold and bacteria. (1 mark)
