o c) Urea-Molasses Treatment (6 marks):

▪ Procedure (4 marks): Similar to urea treatment,
but for 100 kg of stover, use 4-5 kg urea, 10 liters of
molasses, and 80-100 liters of water. Mix the urea
and molasses in the water thoroughly before
sprinkling. (2 marks) Follow the same steps for
packing and sealing. (2 marks)
▪ Additional Benefits of Molasses (2 marks):
▪ Improves palatability, making the stover more
acceptable to animals. (1 mark)
▪ Provides a readily available source of energy,
which helps the rumen microbes utilize the
nitrogen from the urea more effectively. (1
mark)
o d) Precautions/Urea Toxicity (4 marks):
▪ Warn that urea is toxic if consumed in large
amounts at once. (1 mark)
▪ Advise to mix the urea thoroughly in water; do not
feed dry urea. (1 mark)
▪ Introduce the treated stover gradually into the diet
to allow the rumen microbes to adapt. (1 mark)
▪ Ensure animals have access to plenty of clean
water. (1 mark)
