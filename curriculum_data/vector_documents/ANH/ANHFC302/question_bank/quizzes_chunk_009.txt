o Advice on Paddock Size (5 marks):
▪ Explain that paddock size is not fixed and depends
on several factors. (1 mark)
▪ The key factor is the daily herd forage requirement.
(1 mark)
▪ Formula: Paddock size = (Daily herd forage
requirement (kg) X Grazing period (days)) /
Available forage per hectare (kg). (2 marks)
▪ Advise that the farmer should estimate the available
forage in a representative area and calculate the
herd's total daily intake to determine the ideal size.
This can be adjusted with temporary fencing based
on seasonal growth. (1 mark)
