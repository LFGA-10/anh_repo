Section 2: Long Answer/Essay Questions (Evaluating and
Creating)

Instructions: Answer the following questions in detail,
demonstrating a comprehensive understanding of the topic.
Level: Evaluating
5. Question: A farmer with 50 heads of cattle and a 50-hectare
pasture wants to transition from continuous grazing to a
rotational grazing system. Critically evaluate the advantages of
this change. Using the formulas provided in the module,
calculate the number of paddocks needed if the desired rest
period is 30 days, the grazing period is 6 days, and the farmer
plans to have 2 animal groups. Then, advise the farmer on
how to determine the size of each paddock.
Marking Guide (Total: 15 marks)
o Evaluation of Advantages (5 marks):
▪ Prevents overgrazing and allows for pasture
regrowth, leading to higher and more sustained
productivity. (1 mark)
▪ Helps control internal parasites by breaking their
life cycle during the rest period. (1 mark)
▪ Prevents loss of soil through erosion by maintaining
better ground cover. (1 mark)
▪ Allows for more uniform grazing, reducing selective
grazing and waste. (1 mark)
▪ Enables better matching of forage supply to animal
demand. (1 mark)
o Calculation of Paddock Number (5 marks):
▪ Formula: Paddock number = (Rest period / Grazing
period) + Number of animal groups. (2 marks)
▪ Calculation: (30 days / 6 days) + 2 = 5 + 2 = 7
paddocks. (3 marks)
