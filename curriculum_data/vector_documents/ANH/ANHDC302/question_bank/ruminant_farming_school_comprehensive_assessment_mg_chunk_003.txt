1. Bos taurus cattle breeds are generally more resistant to tropical diseases and poor diet than Bos indicus breeds.
Answer: FALSE

2. In a "tail to tail" double-row cow shed layout, there is a wider central alley which is advantageous for cleaning and milking.
Answer: TRUE

3. Progeny testing is a selection method where an animal is evaluated based on the performance of its parents.
Answer: FALSE (It is evaluated on the performance of its offspring.)

4. The primary purpose of heterosis in cross-breeding is to ensure all offspring look identical to one parent.
Answer: FALSE (The purpose is for offspring to perform better than the average of the parents.)

5. The allantois is the inner fetal membrane that forms a sac immediately around the fetus.
Answer: FALSE (The amnion is the inner membrane.)

Qn5. Answer the following questions concisely. (35 Marks)

1. Application: A farmer wants to construct a tie-stall shelter for dairy cows. List THREE advantages and TWO disadvantages of this system. (5 Marks)
* Advantages: Animals are separated for individual care and feeding; easier to control animal behavior and monitor health; reduces harmful contamination between animals.
* Disadvantages: Animals are not free to move/exercise; requires more daily labour for tying and untying; lower quantity of manure collected compared to free stall.
