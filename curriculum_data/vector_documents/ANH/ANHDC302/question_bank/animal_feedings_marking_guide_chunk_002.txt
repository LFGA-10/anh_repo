Which of the following statements is true regarding the construction and characteristics of different types of silos?

A pit silo is constructed above ground with three concrete walls and a concrete base.

A clamp silo is the cheapest and easiest to construct, but it does not allow for good pressing.

A surface silo is made by digging a 2-meter-deep pit and filling it with grass, then stamping it down.

A clamp silo is made with three concrete walls, a concrete base, and is suitable for storing large quantities of fodder.

ANSWER

A. A pit silo is constructed above ground with three concrete walls and a concrete base.
Incorrect — A pit silo is actually constructed below the ground, not above.

B. A clamp silo is the cheapest and easiest to construct, but it does not allow for good pressing.
 Incorrect — While it is cheap and easy to make, it does allow for good pressing when properly managed.
