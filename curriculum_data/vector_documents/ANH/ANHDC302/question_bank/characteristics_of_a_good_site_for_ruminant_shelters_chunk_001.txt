Characteristics of a Good Site for Ruminant Shelters

Remembering

Q1: Which of the following is a characteristic of a good site for ruminant shelters?
a) Low-lying and flood-prone
b) Well-drained and elevated
c) Close to industrial areas
d) Far from water sources
Answer: b) Well-drained and elevated

Understanding

Q2: Why is it important for a ruminant shelter site to be well-drained?
a) To reduce construction costs
b) To prevent waterlogging and disease
c) To attract more animals
d) To improve aesthetic appeal
Answer: b) To prevent waterlogging and disease

Applying

Q3: If a farmer has two potential sites for a ruminant shelter—one near a river and another on a hill—which site should they choose and why?
a) Near the river, because it provides easy access to water
b) On the hill, because it is well-drained and less prone to flooding
c) Near the river, because it is closer to markets
d) On the hill, because it is cheaper to construct
Answer: b) On the hill, because it is well-drained and less prone to flooding

Analyzing

Q4: Compare the advantages and disadvantages of locating a ruminant shelter near a forest versus an open plain.
Answer:

Advantages of near a forest: Provides natural shade and windbreaks.

Disadvantages of near a forest: Risk of predators and limited space for expansion.

Advantages of an open plain: Ample space for expansion and better ventilation.

Disadvantages of an open plain: Lack of natural shade and exposure to strong winds.

Evaluating

Q5: Evaluate the suitability of a site that is flat, well-drained, but located near a noisy highway for a ruminant shelter.
Answer: The site is unsuitable because noise pollution can stress the animals, negatively affecting their health and productivity.
