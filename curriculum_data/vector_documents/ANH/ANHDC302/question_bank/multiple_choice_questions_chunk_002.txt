Here’s a set of multiple-choice questions with answers focused on Ruminant Farming – Cattle Breeds:

🐄 Multiple Choice Questions: Ruminant Farming (Cattle Breeds)

1. Which of the following is a characteristic of Bos taurus cattle? A) Prominent hump B) High resistance to tropical diseases C) High milk and meat yield D) Large dewlap and drooping ears ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Bos taurus breeds are known for high productivity but lower resistance to tropical conditions.

2. What is the origin of the Friesian/Holstein breed? A) South Africa B) North Holland C) Pakistan D) East Africa ✅ Answer: B Explanation: Friesian/Holstein cattle were originally bred in North Holland.

3. Which cattle breed is known for high butterfat content in its milk? A) Brahman B) Jersey C) Ankole D) Brown Swiss ✅ Answer: B Explanation: Jersey cattle produce milk with a high percentage of butterfat.

4. What distinguishes Bos indicus (Zebu) cattle from Bos taurus? A) Absence of horns B) Short legs and no hump C) Prominent hump and heat tolerance D) High milk yield in cold climates ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Bos indicus cattle have a prominent hump and are well adapted to hot climates.

5. Which breed is native to East Africa and known for its long horns? A) Sahiwal B) Jersey C) Ankole D) Alpine ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Ankole cattle are East African and famous for their long, elegant horns.

6. What is the primary use of the Sahiwal breed? A) Wool production B) Meat only C) Tropical dairy production D) Draft work ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Sahiwal is a tropical dairy breed with moderate milk yield.
