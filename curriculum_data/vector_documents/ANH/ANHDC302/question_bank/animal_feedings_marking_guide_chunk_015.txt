Jane owns a mixed farm where she grows maize and keeps 25 dairy cows.
Recently, she realized that feeding her cows with long, uncut hay was causing a lot of waste, and the animals were taking too long to finish their feed. She also noticed that land preparation for planting took too much time because her old tools were manual and slow.
To solve these problems, she decided to invest in modern farm machinery, including a tractor and a chaff cutter.

A. Identify two major challenges that Jane faced on her farm before purchasing the farm machinery.

B. Explain how a tractor could help Jane improve her crop production efficiency.

C. Describe how the chaff cutter would help solve the feeding problems she was experiencing with her dairy cows.

D. List any three maintenance practices Jane should carry out regularly to ensure both the tractor and the chaff cutter remain in good working condition.

ANSWER

A. Two major challenges Jane faced before purchasing farm machinery

Inefficient feeding of cows – Feeding long, uncut hay caused waste and increased feeding time for her dairy cows.

Slow land preparation – Using manual tools for planting maize was time-consuming and reduced overall crop production efficiency.
