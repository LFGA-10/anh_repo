B. How a tractor could improve crop production efficiency

A tractor can plow, harrow, and prepare the land faster than manual labor, saving time and labor costs.

It allows Jane to plant larger areas in less time, improving crop yield potential.

Tractors can also be used to transport inputs and harvested crops, reducing physical effort and speeding up farm operations.

C. How the chaff cutter would help with feeding problems

A chaff cutter chops long hay, grasses, and fodder into smaller, uniform pieces.

This reduces feed wastage because cows can consume chopped fodder more efficiently.

It saves feeding time and improves digestion, as cows can eat and digest chopped feed faster.

D. Three maintenance practices for tractor and chaff cutter

Regular cleaning – Remove dirt, debris, and crop residues after use to prevent rust and clogging.

Lubrication – Apply oil or grease to moving parts to reduce friction and wear.

Inspection and repair – Check for loose bolts, worn blades, or leaks; repair or replace damaged parts promptly.
