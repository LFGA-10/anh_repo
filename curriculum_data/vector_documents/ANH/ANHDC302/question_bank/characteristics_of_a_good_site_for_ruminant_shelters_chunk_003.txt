Creating

Q12: Propose a list of construction materials for a ruminant shelter in a hot and humid climate.
Answer:

Walls: Concrete blocks with ventilation gaps.

Roofing: Corrugated metal sheets with insulation.

Flooring: Concrete with a slight slope for drainage.

Doors and windows: Wooden frames with mesh for ventilation.

3. Identification of Ruminant Shelter Equipment

Remembering

Q13: Which of the following is a basic feeding equipment for ruminants?
a) Milking machine
b) Feed trough
c) Cooling fan
d) Breeding crate
Answer: b) Feed trough

Understanding

Q14: Why is proper lighting important in a ruminant shelter?
a) To improve the aesthetic appeal
b) To regulate the animals’ reproductive cycles
c) To reduce construction costs
d) To attract more animals
Answer: b) To regulate the animals’ reproductive cycles

Applying

Q15: A farmer wants to improve milk production in their dairy cows. Which equipment should they invest in?
a) Feed trough
b) Milking machine
c) Breeding crate
d) Cooling fan
Answer: b) Milking machine

Analyzing

Q16: Compare the benefits of using automated feeding equipment versus manual feeding in a large ruminant shelter.
Answer:

Automated feeding: Saves labor, ensures consistent feeding, but is expensive to install and maintain.

Manual feeding: Low-cost, flexible, but labor-intensive and less consistent.

Evaluating

Q17: Evaluate the use of cooling fans in a ruminant shelter located in a hot climate.
Answer: Cooling fans are beneficial as they reduce heat stress, improve animal comfort, and enhance productivity. However, they increase energy costs.
