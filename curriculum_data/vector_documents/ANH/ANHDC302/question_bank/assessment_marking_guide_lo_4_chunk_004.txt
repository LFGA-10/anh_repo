Bloom's Level 5 & 6: Evaluating & Creating

Open-Ended Questions

Explain the difference between pure breeding and cross-breeding, and give one advantage of each.
✅ Answer: Pure breeding mates animals of the same breed to maintain traits; advantage: uniformity. Cross-breeding mates different breeds; advantage: hybrid vigor (heterosis), improved performance.

Describe two methods of heat induction in ruminants and when each might be used.
✅ Answer:

Flushing: Providing extra nutrition before breeding to increase ovulation.

Oestrus synchronization: Using hormones to control cycle timing, useful for grouped calving.

A cow shows signs of metritis after calving. What are the likely causes, and what preventive measures could have been taken?
✅ Answer: Causes: retained placenta, unhygienic calving environment. Prevention: clean calving area, proper hygiene during assistance, ensure complete expulsion of placenta.
