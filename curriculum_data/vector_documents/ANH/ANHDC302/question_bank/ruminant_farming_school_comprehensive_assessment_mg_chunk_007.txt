**Model Answer:**

**Action Plan for Assisting a Normal Calving**

1.  **Observation & Confirmation:** Once the water bag appears and the cow begins active straining (Stage two), observe discreetly. Confirm a normal presentation: two front feet emerging first, with the hooves pointing downward, followed by the nose resting on the legs.

2.  **Preparation:**

    *   **Restrain the Cow:** Secure the cow in a clean, quiet, and well-bedded area.

    *   **Hygiene:** Thoroughly wash the cow's vulva and surrounding area with warm water and mild soap.

    *   **Personal Hygiene:** Wash your hands and arms thoroughly with soap and water. Use a disposable obstetrical sleeve if available.

3.  **Assisted Traction:**

    *   Only assist if progress has stalled despite the cow's strong contractions for about 20-30 minutes.

    *   Gently tie clean ropes above the fetlocks of the calf's front legs.

    *   **Pull only when the cow pushes.** Synchronize your effort with her contractions.

    *   Pull downwards at a slight angle (towards the cow's hocks), not straight back.

    *   The maximum force should be that of two strong adults. If the calf does not move with this force, stop and call a veterinarian, as it may be a dystocia.

4.  **Delivery & Immediate Calf Care:**

    *   Once the head and shoulders are delivered, the rest of the calf should pass easily.

    *   Immediately clear the amniotic sac from the calf's nose and mouth to allow it to breathe.

    *   Stimulate breathing by rubbing the calf's chest with a clean towel or piece of straw.
