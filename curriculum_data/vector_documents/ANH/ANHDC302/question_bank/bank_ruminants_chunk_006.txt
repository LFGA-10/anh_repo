2. What are the appropriate wall materials for ruminant shelters?

Wall materials should be chosen based on insulation properties and durability:

Wooden Boards or Timber: Commonly used but may require treatment to protect against pests and rot.

Metal Sheets or Panels: These are durable, easy to clean, and provide good protection from the elements.

Concrete Blocks or Bricks: Good for creating solid, durable walls that offer temperature regulation and protection.

Mesh or Wire Fencing: Used for ventilation and allowing airflow, especially in tropical climates.

Polycarbonate Sheets: These allow natural light to enter while offering good insulation properties.

3. What are the ideal floor materials for ruminant shelters?

Concrete: Durable, easy to clean, and can be sloped for drainage, though it can be hard on animals' hooves.

Rubber Mats: Comfortable for animals, reduces injury risk, and provides better footing.

Gravel or Sand: Easy to maintain and promotes drainage, but may require frequent replenishing.

Wood: Soft and natural, but can rot if exposed to moisture unless treated properly.

Earth or Soil: Natural but may require regular care to maintain cleanliness and drainage.
