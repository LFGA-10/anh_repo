Instructions: Indicate whether the following statements are True (T) or False (F).

In a pastoral system, animals are provided with supplementary feed by the farmer.

A key advantage of the tail-to-tail cow shed layout is the reduced danger of disease spread between animals.

The floor space requirement for an adult dairy cow in an open area is 3.5 m².

Answers:

F (In a pastoral system, the animal finds its own feed; no extra feed is provided.)

T

F (The requirement is 7.0 m² for the open area; 3.5 m² is for the covered area.)
