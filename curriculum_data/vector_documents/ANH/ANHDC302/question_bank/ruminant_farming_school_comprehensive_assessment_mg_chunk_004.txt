2. Analysis: Compare and contrast "Mass Selection" and "Progeny Testing" as methods for selecting breeding stock. (8 Marks)
* Mass Selection: Based purely on the external appearance (phenotype) of the individual. It is simple, does not require record keeping, and is effective for highly heritable traits like coat color. Animals are allowed to mate randomly.
* Progeny Testing: Based on the performance of an individual's offspring. It requires detailed record keeping and takes a long time (until the offspring mature). It is valuable for selecting for traits not expressed in one sex, like milk production in bulls.

3. Application: Describe THREE clear signs that would indicate a cow is approaching parturition. (6 Marks)
* Relaxation and sinking of the muscles on either side of the tail head.
* Swelling of the vulva and the presence of a mucus discharge.
* Enlargement of the mammary glands (udder) and may secrete a milky material.
* Restlessness and seeking seclusion.

4. Analysis: Explain why "flushing" (providing extra nutrition before mating) is a beneficial practice for ewes and does. What is its primary effect on reproduction? (8 Marks)
* Benefit: Flushing improves the body condition and nutritional status of the female just before and during the breeding season.
* Primary Effect: It increases the ovulation rate, meaning more eggs are released. This leads to a higher likelihood of multiple fertilizations, thereby increasing the lambing or kidding percentage (more twins or triplets).
