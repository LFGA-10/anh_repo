The Isolation Unit allows for the separation and treatment of sick animals within the flock without risking the health of the entire population.

The Crush is essential for safely restraining animals for routine health procedures (e.g., vaccination, deworming) and for treating sick animals, ensuring both operator safety and effective treatment.
While costly upfront, investing in these structures mitigates the much higher potential costs of a disease outbreak, which could lead to widespread illness, poor growth rates, deaths, and financial loss.
