Q18: Design a layout for a ruminant shelter that includes all necessary equipment for feeding, milking, cooling, and breeding.
Answer:

Feeding area: Feed troughs and automated feeders.

Milking area: Milking machines and storage tanks.

Cooling area: Fans and misting systems.

Breeding area: Breeding crates and artificial insemination tools.

Lighting: LED lights for proper illumination.
