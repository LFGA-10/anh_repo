1. Evaluation (10 Marks): A farmer has a herd of local Ankole cattle but wants to increase milk production. He is considering cross-breeding them with Friesian bulls through an "upgrading cross-breeding" program.
Evaluate the potential benefits and risks of this strategy for the farmer.

text

**Model Answer:**

**Benefits:**

*   **Increased Milk Yield:** The primary benefit is a significant increase in milk production in the crossbred offspring compared to the pure Ankole, as Friesians are high-yielding dairy breeds.

*   **Hybrid Vigor (Heterosis):** The first-generation cross (F1) may show improved overall health, growth rate, and fertility compared to both parent breeds.

*   **Retained Adaptability:** Through upgrading, the farmer can gradually introduce Friesian genetics while hopefully retaining some of the Ankole's valuable traits, such as resistance to local ticks and diseases and tolerance to heat, over several generations.
