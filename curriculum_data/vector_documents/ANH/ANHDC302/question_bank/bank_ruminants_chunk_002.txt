5. What are the common traits to look for when identifying a dairy cow breed?

Answer: Dairy cow breeds can be identified by their physical characteristics such as:

Body size and shape: Dairy cows are typically taller with a more angular body structure.

Milk yield: Breeds like Holstein produce the most milk, while Jerseys and Guernseys are known for high butterfat content.

Color patterns: Holsteins are black and white, while Jerseys are usually light brown with a white face.

6. How can you differentiate between different breeds of deer?

Answer: Deer species, such as white-tailed deer, mule deer, and red deer, can be identified based on antler shape, body size, and coat color. For example:

White-tailed deer: Has a white underside to its tail and a reddish-brown coat in summer.

Mule deer: Characterized by large, broad ears and a split tail.

Red deer: Larger body, with long antlers and reddish-brown coats in summer.

7. What are the key identifiers for camelids (like llamas and alpacas)?

Answer: Llamas and alpacas are both members of the camelid family, but they differ in size, ear shape, and fiber type:

Llamas: Larger, with longer, banana-shaped ears and a thicker neck. They are used as pack animals.

Alpacas: Smaller with shorter, more upright ears and produce finer wool that is highly valued.
