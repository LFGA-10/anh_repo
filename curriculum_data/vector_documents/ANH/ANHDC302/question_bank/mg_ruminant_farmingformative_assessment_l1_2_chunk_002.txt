Short Answer Questions (2)

Name two essential equipment types needed for feeding ruminants and briefly state the function of each.

Answer: Any two of the following:

Feeding Troughs: Keep animal food clean, within reach, and encourage the animal to eat the whole diet mix.

Chopper Machine: Mainly used for stalk forage (e.g., rice straw, maize stover) for ensiling.

Feed Grinder: Reduces the particle size of feed for better nutrient use, intake, and mixing.
