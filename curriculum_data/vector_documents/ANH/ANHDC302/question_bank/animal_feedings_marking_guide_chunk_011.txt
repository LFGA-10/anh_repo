Milking Machine & Milk Let-Down Questions

1. Primary function of the teat cup assembly:
 C. To attach to the teats and facilitate rhythmic suction of milk

2. How the pulsator contributes to milk let-down:
B. It creates alternating vacuum and air pressure to mimic the natural suckling action

3. Why the vacuum pump is essential:
 C. To generate the negative pressure needed to extract milk from the udder

4. Physiological response allowing milk letdown:
 B. Release of oxytocin, which contracts alveoli to release milk

5. Role of the milk claw/cluster:
C. To collect milk from all teat cups and channel it into the milk line
