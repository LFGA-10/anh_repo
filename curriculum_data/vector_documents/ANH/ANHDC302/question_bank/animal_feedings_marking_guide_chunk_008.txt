3. Protection from Pests

Consideration: Prevent access to rodents, birds, and insects. Use sealed containers or feed bins.

Explanation: Pests consume and contaminate feed, introduce pathogens, and increase losses through contamination and spoilage.

4. Storage Duration

Consideration: Use older feed first (first-in, first-out system) and avoid prolonged storage.

Explanation: Nutrient content (especially vitamins and fats) declines over time, and prolonged storage increases risk of spoilage and mold growth.

5. Cleanliness and Hygiene

Consideration: Keep storage areas clean, dry, and free from leftover residues or spilled feed.

Explanation: Residual feed can attract pests and promote microbial growth, contaminating fresh feed.

6. Proper Packaging

Consideration: Use airtight, moisture-proof containers for sensitive ingredients like oilseeds, meal, or concentrates.

Explanation: Protects feed from humidity, pests, and oxidation, preserving nutrient quality.

7. Avoid Contamination

Consideration: Store different feed ingredients separately, especially chemicals, medications, or toxic substances.

Explanation: Cross-contamination can be harmful to animals and reduce feed safety.
