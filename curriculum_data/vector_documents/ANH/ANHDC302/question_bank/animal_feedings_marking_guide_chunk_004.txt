You are applying both organic manure and inorganic manure to improve soil for growing Pennisetum. The recommended application rates are:

Organic manure: 10,000 kg per hectare

Inorganic manure: 100 kg per hectare

If you have a plot of 2 hectares, how much total manure do you need for both organic and inorganic applications?

20 tons

2020 kg

2100 kg

20.2 tons

ANSWER

Given:

Organic manure = 10,000 kg/ha

Inorganic manure = 100 kg/ha

Plot size = 2 hectares

Step 1: Calculate total organic manure

10,000Kg/ha*2ha= 20,000kg

Step 2: Calculate total inorganic manure

100kg/ha*2ha= 200 kg

Step 3: Total Manure

20,000kg+ 200kg = 20,200 kg

Convert into tons = 20,200kg = 20.2 tons

So the answer is D.
