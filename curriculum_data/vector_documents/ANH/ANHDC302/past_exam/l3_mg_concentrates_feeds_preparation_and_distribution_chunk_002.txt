Question 3. State out the importance of carbohydrates in the animal body.

/4marks

Answer:

Carbohydrates play the following roles in the animal body:

- They supply energy and heat which animals require for all metabolic processes in the body. /1mark

- They form, together with other indigestible matter, roughages that keep food moving along the digestive tract and prevent constipation. /1mark

- They can be converted and stored as fats and used as sources of energy

in the future. /1mark

- In ruminants, they provide a laxative effect, thereby preventing bloat/1mark
