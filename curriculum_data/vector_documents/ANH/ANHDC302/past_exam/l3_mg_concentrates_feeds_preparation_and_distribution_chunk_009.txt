Question 22. Relate the main roles of proteins in the animal body.   /5 marks

Answer:

Proteins play the following roles in the animal body:

- They are essential for growth in all living tissues and early maturity.

/1mark

- They are required for bodybuilding, the repair of old, worn out, or damaged cells and tissues.                                                            /1mark

- They are required in the formation of antibodies that help in fighting infection by foreign organisms like bacteria.                     /1mark

-They are constituents of some hormones like Progesterone.   /1mark

-They are required in the manufacture of enzymes that accelerate metabolic activities within the animal body.                        /1mark

-They are components of some animals’ products such as meat, milk

and eggs.     /1mark
