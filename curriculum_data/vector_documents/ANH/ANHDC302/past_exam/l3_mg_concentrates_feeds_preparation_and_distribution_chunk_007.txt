Answer:

In general mineral deficiency is characterized by:

- poor fertility: lack of heat signs and low conception rate.   	/1mark

- low milk production.                                                           	/1mark

- poorly developed bones in young animals (rickets).         	/1mark

- health disorders, for example, milk fever.                           	/1mark

- poor body condition, which may be accompanied by a change in coat color
