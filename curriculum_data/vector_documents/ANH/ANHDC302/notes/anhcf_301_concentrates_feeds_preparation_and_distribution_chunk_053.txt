L.O.5.3: Manage proteins and amino acids deficiencies and disorders
Amino Acids are the building blocks of the body. When protein is broken down by digestion the
result is 22 known amino acids. Eight are essential (cannot be manufactured by the body) the rest
are non-essential (can be manufactured by the body with proper nutrition). Sometimes the non-
essential should still be supplemented to ensure an optimal available quantity.
Besides building cells and repairing tissue, amino acids form antibodies to combat invading
bacteria and virus; they are part of the enzyme and hormonal system; they build nucleoproteins
(RNA and DNA); they carry oxygen throughout the body, and are part of all muscular activity.
