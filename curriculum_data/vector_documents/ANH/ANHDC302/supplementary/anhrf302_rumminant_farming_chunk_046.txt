 Content/Topic 2.Mating planning
A. Reproductive categories of females
 Immature: Is the young female cattle not fully developed
 Pubescent: Female cattle reaching sexual maturation.
 Mature pregnant: fully developed and physiologically holding an embryo(s) or fetus its own
uterus.
 Mature lactating: fully developed and physiologically developing (or not) an embryo(s) or
fetus its own uterus but solely in a phase of lactation period.
 Mature non-pregnant: fully developed and physiologically with no developing embryo(s) or
foetus its own uterus
 Resting female: those ones are waiting for a proper time of insemination. This condition can
depend on physiological condition, feeding and farmer request.
