When ovulation is about to occurs, a female animal goes on heat. This serves to alert the male animal of
the same species to mate with this female animal. Heat refers to the time when female farm animals are
ready for mating. If a female animal mates with a male animal during this period, fertilization may
occurs.
B. Mating methods
 Natural
Male animals have the natural ability to detect female animals on heat. Once detected, the male animal
mounts the female and deposits the sperms in the vagina. Natural mating takes places during the act of
copulation by the penis.
 Artificial
This is a mating by artificial means. Semen is collected from a male animal and introduced into vagina of
a female animal on heat using insemination equipment.
In cattle, semen is collected from a bull using artificial vagina (AV), screened to check whether it is safe,
diluted and stored in plastic straws in liquid nitrogen or solid carbon dioxide. The semen can stay live for
a period of 9 years. On the day of insemination, an inseminator gun (catheter tube) is used to introduce
the semen into the vagina.
