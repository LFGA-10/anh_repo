 Phenotypic characteristics:
 Small in size, which is a sheep with tail;
 Color of its coat varies from black to white and even to brown;
 Resistant to ecological condition of Rwanda
 Body weight varies between 30-35 kg
 Genotypic characteristics:
 It is a sheep raised exclusively for meat production and can accumulate up to 5 kg of fat
in the tail;
 This sheep is resistant and adapted to a variety of environments ranging from arid to sub-
humid areas and from the sea level up to 3,000 m altitude.
B. Exotic breeds
B.1. Mountain white
 Origin : French Pyrenees Mountain
 Phenotypic characteristics:
 This is big and heavy wool sheep, with a slightly miter head and long hanging ears;
 White coat color
 Hornless
