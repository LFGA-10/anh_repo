 Isolated from their companions

For these reasons, other confinement methods appropriate for the species should be sought. Tethering
of animals requires a high standard of animal husbandry and exceptional care, including regular
inspections.
Some species and individuals may not be suitable for tethering. Animals should never be tethered in
conditions where they are vulnerable to extreme weather.
These guidelines have been developed to assist people to tether animals correctly when circumstances
make it a necessary method of confining and protecting animals. They specify the requirements for
tethering dogs, sheep, goats, cattle, donkeys and horses.
