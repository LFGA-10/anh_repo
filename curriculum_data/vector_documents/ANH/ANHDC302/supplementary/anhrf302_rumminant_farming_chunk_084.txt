B.3. Finishing
This phase of beef production consists of full feeding of grain with limited amounts of roughage until
market weight and finish are reached. Older cattle may reach finish on pasture alone (or with only a few
pounds of grain/day) or after 60–90 days in the feedlot on high-grain rations to improve market grade
and to remove any yellow tinges from their body fat (due to stored carotene from pasture forage).
Weanling calves commonly are shipped directly to the feedlot and fed finishing rations for 100–150
days; yearlings require ~150 days, and older steers 100–125 days. Grain consumption of cattle on full-
