 Placenta delivering
A placenta is considered to be retained pathologically if an abnormally long time elapses between birth
of the young and delivery of the placenta. The placenta of the cow and ewe should be delivered within
24 hours. Sometimes the placenta and its membranes hang from the vulva.
 Treatment:
If the placenta does not come out:
 Restrain the animal;
 Wash your hands and arms with soap or disinfectant;
 Put your arm into vagina and grip the placenta;
 Pull very gently. If the placenta comes out easily pull it out. If it is difficult to pull it out leave it
there.
 Put some antibiotic through the cervix into the uterus
 If the animal has a fever or looks sick give an antibiotic by injection.
 Encourage new-born animals to suck the mother as soon as possible after they are born. This
helps make the uterus contract and squeeze the placenta out.
