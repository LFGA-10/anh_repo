B. Main parts of cowshed
 Floor
The most efficient floor is made of concrete in other to support the heavy weight of the cattle. The
concrete consists of a number of materials mixed together to make a very hard, rock-like substance
that can be used to make the floors and pillars of building.
The floor should be hard, durable and impervious to water and urine. It should be easy to clean,
slopping towards the drainage and should not be slippery.

 Walls
The walls may be made up of stones, wood, concrete reinforced steel, bamboo, mud or any material
available and suitable to the local environment. They ought to be very strong.
 Roof
The roof should be light, strong, and durable in addition to being weather proof. It should also be a
bad conductor of heat and free from tendency to condense moisture. For high rainfall areas, sloping
roofs are a must. Materials include galvanized iron sheets, wood, bamboo, papyrus and grass thatch.
