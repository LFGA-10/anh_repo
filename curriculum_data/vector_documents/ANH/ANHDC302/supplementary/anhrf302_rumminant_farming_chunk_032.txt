A. Characteristics of a good cowshed
A good cowshed should have the following characteristics:
 It should provide an internal environment that allows the animals to produce at their best, so
it should be neither too hot nor too cold.
 It should protect livestock against direct sun light cold winds and excessive heat.
 It should be well ventilated.
 It should protect livestock against predators, other vermin and thieves.
 It should be easy to clean and to disinfect.
 The structures within the house should be easy to adjust and/or remove so that a farmer can
quickly adapt the house for another use without paying a lot of money for renovation.
 The cowshed should be placed so that it is easy to reach and enter, and so that services such
as water and electricity can easily be provided. In addition, a livestock house should be near
feed stores, loading areas and other services.
