D.9. Observation of heat return
Observation for service returns tends to be concentrated around the normal oestrous cycle length of 18-
24 days. The absence of a return to oestrus at this time or around 42 days after service may indicate
that the cow is pregnant. However, this method has inaccuracies in that:
 it is dependent upon efficient and accurate heat detection;
 it assumes that non-pregnant cows return to heat at regular intervals;
 It assumes that cows with a cessation of oestrous cycles are pregnant. Up to 7% of pregnant
cows can show signs of heat, usually between the fourth and eighth month of pregnancy. These
cows (false negatives) could abort following repeat insemination or may be unnecessarily culled.
