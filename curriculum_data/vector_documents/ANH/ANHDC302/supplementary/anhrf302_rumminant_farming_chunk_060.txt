C. Post parturition care for dam
The dam may need some special attention soon after parturition such as:
C.1.Check delivering:
For all animals, the afterbirth must be expelled within 24 hours and the animal should not consume it.
By allowing the dam to exercise (walking around) or provision of warm water to the dam after calving,
expulsion of the afterbirth is accelerated If retained, the dam may die or become infertile due to rotting.
C.2.Check haemorrhage and tear:
Post-partum cares for the dam also consist of checking tears and haemorrhages caused by forced
traction of the foetus.
C.3.Check for twins
C.4.Dam cleaning:
Remove the fetal membrane from the calving area or pen floor; clean the pen and the rearing area of the dam to
reduce risk of infection by flies
C.5. Check for mastitis and differentiate with mammary oedema/congestion
C.6. Administration of stimulants
