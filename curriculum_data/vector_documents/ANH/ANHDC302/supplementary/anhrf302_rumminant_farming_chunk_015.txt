B.2. Boer
 Origin : South Africa. It is a crossbreed of European, Angora and Indian breeds to increase meat
size and productivity.
 Phenotypic characteristics:
 It is white on the body with a brown-red head and white line on the snout, with long ears
and hair;
 Ears are pendulous;
 Have prominent horns;
 It is a medium size goat: 50 to 70 kg for female and 80-100 kg for male.

 Genotypic characteristics :

 Valuable for meat, milk and skin but has been promoted for its meat potential.
 This goat is well adapted to tropical conditions but it hardly tolerates too moist air.
