A. Weaning process
A.1. Calf
Weaning is a period when a young animal stops depending on its mother. Calves are usually weaned
between 8-10 weeks of age. With good management and proper feeding they may be weaned between
3-8 weeks of age. In other situations, the two stay together for some time before the separation. In
preparation for that stage, the young one is slowly introduced to solid food as well as searching for food.
This enables the dam to prepare for the next gestation period.

From the second week onward a calf should be able to eat small amounts of good quality feed such as
young grass. This is necessary for the development of the rumen. Fresh, clean water should also be
available.
A.2. kid
Weaning methods for goat kids suggest that kids can be weaned earlier than the traditional weaning age
of 3 months. Kids can be successfully weaned at 9 kg of body weight, 8 weeks of age or at the time when
at least 30 g/day of solid feed are consumed. Weaning shock is reduced when any of the above criteria
are achieved. Restricted milk feeding encouraged solid feed consumption by goat kids
A.3. Lamb
Weaning should take place by 12 weeks from the start of lambing, sooner if the season has been poor.
Weaning at this time will help with the ewe's recovery and allow her to get back into shape before next
year's mating. It is essential that weaner is provided with adequate nutrition to meet their growing
needs. Feeding ewes while they have lambs at foot is a great way to 'imprint feed' lambs. The lambs will
learn to feed more quickly and will take to grain/pellets easily at weaning.
