Topic of the session:  Assisting Ruminant’s Parturition: Techniques and Management Practices

Range:

Parturition preparation

Normal parturition

Normal parturition assistance techniques

Post parturition cares for new born

Post parturition care for dam

Duration of the session: 120 mins

Objectives: At the end of this session the learner should be able to:

Explain the stages of parturition in ruminants and signs of normal versus difficult labor.

Identify appropriate facilities, equipment, and materials required for assisting parturition.

Demonstrate correct techniques for assisting ruminant parturition while ensuring animal welfare.

Apply knowledge of reproductive management to reduce risks and improve survival of both dam and offspring.
