15.

Outline the diagnostic process, treatment options, and prevention strategies for abscesses in livestock

ANSWERS

1. Diagnostic Process

Objective: Identify the cause, location, and severity of the abscess.

Steps:

History Taking:

Ask about onset, duration, trauma, vaccination, or infections.

Check for exposure to pathogens (e.g., Staphylococcus, Trueperella pyogenes, Fusobacterium).

Clinical Examination:

Look for localized swelling, redness, warmth, and fluctuation (fluid-filled).

Note if the animal has fever or shows systemic illness.

Palpation:

Assess size, firmness, and whether the swelling is soft (fluid) or hard (fibrous).

Aspiration / Sampling:

Use a sterile needle to aspirate pus for:

Bacterial culture and sensitivity to identify the causative organism and guide antibiotic therapy.

Laboratory Tests (optional):

CBC may show leukocytosis.

Imaging (ultrasound or X-ray) for deep-seated abscesses.

2. Treatment Options

Goal: Remove pus, treat infection, and promote healing.

Lancing and Drainage:

Make a small incision in the abscess wall under sterile conditions.

Drain pus completely.

Debridement:

Remove necrotic tissue if present.

Antibiotic Therapy:

Use systemic antibiotics based on culture sensitivity.
