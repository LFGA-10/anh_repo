Tissues Affected: Soft tissues – tongue, oral cavity, pharynx

Swollen, firm, and painful tongue (“wooden tongue”)

Difficulty eating or drinking

Drooling of saliva

Small nodules or abscesses in oral soft tissues

Lumpy Jaw

Causative Agent: Actinomyces bovis (Gram-positive bacterium)

Tissues Affected: Hard tissues – bones of the jaw (mandible or maxilla)

Clinical Presentation:

Hard, painless swelling of the jaw (“lumpy jaw”)

Slow-growing bony mass

May cause displacement of teeth

Occasionally fistula with pus in advanced stages

Onset & Progression: Chronic; slow progression over weeks to months

Pain: Usually painless in early stages

Diagnosis: Clinical signs, radiography for bone involvement, pus culture

Treatment: Sodium iodide therapy, surgical drainage if needed; antibiotics less effective

Explain how the tuberculin skin test is performed and interpreted in diagnosing Bovine Tuberculosis.

ANSWER

1. Purpose

To detect cell-mediated immune response in cattle exposed to Mycobacterium bovis, the causative agent of bTB.

2. Materials Needed

Purified Protein Derivative (PPD) tuberculin:

Avian PPD (control for non-specific reactions)

Bovine PPD (test for bTB)

Sterile syringe and needle

Calipers or ruler for measuring skin thickness

3. Procedure

Restrain the Animal:

Usually performed on the neck, just behind the ear.
