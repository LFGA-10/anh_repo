Among those  bacteria diseases which one characterized by the Rancid butter odor and crepitating swelling.

Blackleg

Anthrax

Brucellosis

Erysipelas

ANSWER

A. Blackleg

Explanation:
Blackleg is characterized by:

Rancid butter odor due to gas‐producing bacteria,

Crepitating swelling (a crackling sound when the swollen muscle is pressed) caused by gas accumulation in tissues,

Rapid onset and muscle necrosis.
