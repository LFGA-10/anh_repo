1.

Define infectious diseases

Infectious

Diagnosis

Abscess

Nodules

ANSWERS

a) Infectious:
Capable of causing disease by entering and multiplying in the body of a host.

b) Diagnosis:
The process of identifying a disease by examining its signs, symptoms, history, and results of laboratory tests.

c) Abscess:
A localized collection of pus within a tissue or organ, caused by bacterial infection and inflammation.

d) Nodules:
Small, firm abnormal growths or swellings in tissues or organs, usually caused by infection, inflammation, or disease.
