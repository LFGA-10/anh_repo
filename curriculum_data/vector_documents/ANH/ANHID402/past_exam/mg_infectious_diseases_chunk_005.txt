16.

Why are newborn calves especially prone to Omphalitis and Joint ill, and how can these diseases be prevented?

ANSWER

 Immature Immune System:

Newborn calves are born with an underdeveloped immune system and rely heavily on colostrum for antibodies.

If they don’t get enough good-quality colostrum, they are highly susceptible to infections.

  Umbilical Exposure:

The navel (umbilicus) is an easy entry point for bacteria from the environment.

Dirty calving areas, contaminated bedding, or poor hygiene increase the risk of Omphalitis (navel infection).

 Bacterial Spread to Joints:

Pathogens entering through the navel can spread via blood (septicemia) to joints, causing Joint Ill (Septic Arthritis).

 Environmental Factors:

Damp, dirty, or overcrowded housing increases exposure to bacteria such as E. coli, Staphylococcus, and Streptococcus.

17.

Compare and contrast Wooden Tongue and Lumpy Jaw in terms of causative agents, affected tissues, and clinical presentation.

ANSWER

Wooden Tongue

Causative Agent: Actinobacillus lignieresii (Gram-negative bacterium)

Tissues Affected: Soft tissues – tongue, oral cavity, pharynx

Clinical Presentation:

Swollen, firm, and painful tongue (“wooden tongue”)

Difficulty eating or drinking

Drooling of saliva
