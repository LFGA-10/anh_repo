Uterine prolapse
1. Definition
Uterine prolapse is the eversion (turning inside out) and protrusion
of the uterus through the vulva, usually occurring immediately after
parturition (calving, lambing, or farrowing)
2. Causes
❖ Prolonged or difficult labor (dystocia)
❖ Excessive straining after parturition
❖ Retained placenta
❖ Hypocalcemia (milk fever)
❖ Excessive traction on the fetus
❖ Poor uterine tone or relaxation of pelvic ligaments
3. Clinical Signs
❖ Large, red, fleshy mass hanging from the vulva
❖ Cotyledons visible (in cows and ewes)
❖ May contain fetal membranes
❖ Animal may be restless, straining, or in shock
❖ Bleeding, contamination, or trauma to the uterus
