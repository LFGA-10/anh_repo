IC 2.3: Assisting in orthopedic surgery
Topic 1: Sprain and Strain
1. Description
(a) Sprain
A sprain is an injury to a ligament (the fibrous tissue connecting
bones at a joint). It occurs when a joint is suddenly twisted,
overstretched, or subjected to abnormal movement, leading to
tearing or stretching of the ligament fibers. Common sites:
Hock, stifle, fetlock, carpal, and pastern joints in animals.
A sprain is an injury involving the stretching or tearing of a
ligament (tissue that connects bone to bone) or a joint capsule,
which help provide joint stability. A severely damaged ligament or
joint capsule can cause instability in a joint. Symptoms may
include pain, inflammation, and in some cases, the inability to
move a limb (arm, leg, foot). Sprains occur when a joint is forced
beyond its normal range of motion.
The most common cause of a leg sprain in dogs is trauma or injury.
