A partial dislocation is called a Subluxation.
Causes
❖ Trauma: Falls, blows, or twisting forces.
86

❖ Congenital deformities: Abnormal bone or ligament
formation (e.g., hip dysplasia).
❖ Pathological conditions: Joint disease, ligament rupture, or
muscle weakness.
❖ Sudden abnormal movement or forced extension/flexion.
Clinical Signs
• Pain and swelling at the joint.
• Loss of function or lameness.
• Deformity or abnormal joint contour.
• Crepitation (grating sound) on movement.
Some of the common symptoms of dislocation include the
following:
❖ Staggering or swaying
❖ Having problems or difficulty in standing up
❖ Decrease of physical activity or exercise
❖ Lameness of the dog’s legs
❖ Dog shows signs of discomfort whenever attempting to
stand up or lie down
❖ The dog has an abnormal looking gait
❖ The dog is reluctant to jump or run
❖ There may be instances where a clicking sound is heard as
the dog walks or stands up Shortening or abnormal
angulation of the limb.
Common Sites of Dislocation
• Hip joint (coxofemoral luxation) – common in dogs and
cattle.
• Shoulder joint (scapulohumeral luxation).
• Stifle joint (patellar luxation).
• Hock, fetlock, or carpal joints in livestock.
