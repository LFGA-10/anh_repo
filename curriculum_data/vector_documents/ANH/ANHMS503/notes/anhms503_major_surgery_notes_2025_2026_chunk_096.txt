EXTERNAL FIXATION OF FRACTURES
1. Definition
External fixation is a method of fracture treatment where the
broken bone is stabilized from outside the body using external
devices such as splints, casts, or external skeletal fixators
(ESFs).
These devices hold the bone fragments in correct alignment without
opening the fracture site.
