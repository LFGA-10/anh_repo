Structure of a Synovial Joint
❖ Articular surfaces: Smooth ends of bones covered by
articular cartilage.
❖ Joint capsule: Fibrous outer layer that encloses the joint
cavity.
❖ Synovial membrane: Lines the capsule and secretes synovial
fluid for lubrication.
❖ Ligaments: Tough fibrous bands that hold bones together and
maintain stability.
❖ Joint cavity: Contains synovial fluid to reduce friction during
movement.
2. Description of Dislocation
Dislocation (Luxation) is the complete displacement of the
articular surfaces of a joint from their normal anatomical position.
A partial dislocation is called a Subluxation.
Causes
❖ Trauma: Falls, blows, or twisting forces.
