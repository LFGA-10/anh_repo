Step 6: Irrigation and Haemostasis
✓ Irrigate the wound thoroughly with sterile saline or antiseptic
solution.
✓ Ensure complete haemostasis before closure.
Step 7: Wound Closure
✓ Close muscle and fascia layers with absorbable sutures (e.g.,
chromic catgut).
✓ Close skin with non-absorbable sutures (e.g., nylon or silk).
✓ Apply antiseptic dressing.
