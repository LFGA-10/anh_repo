6. Step-by-Step for Common Internal Fixation Methods
A. Intramedullary Pin Fixation
❖ Select a pin of proper diameter (≈60–70% of bone medullary
cavity).
❖ Insert the pin retrograde or normograde into the bone marrow
cavity.
❖ Align the bone fragments properly.
❖ Drive the pin across the fracture site into the opposite
segment.
❖ Cut off the protruding end of the pin and close the wound.
B. Bone Plate and Screw Fixation
❖ Select a plate that matches the bone’s contour.
❖ Drill holes through the plate into the bone using a bone drill.
❖ Insert screws through the holes and tighten them sequentially.
❖ Check stability of the construct.
❖ Irrigate the site and close the incision in layers.
