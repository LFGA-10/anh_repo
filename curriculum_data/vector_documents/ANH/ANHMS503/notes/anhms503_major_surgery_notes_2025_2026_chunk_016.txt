Topic 2. Team and Patient Preparation
18

This stage involves preparing the people who will be in the surgical
workplace.
Surgical Team:
•
o Attire: All personnel entering the OR must wear
appropriate surgical attire, including scrub suits, caps,
masks, and special footwear.
o "Scrubbing Up": Before entering the sterile field,
surgeons, surgical assistants, and scrub nurses perform
a surgical hand scrub to remove bacteria. They then don
sterile gowns and gloves.
o WHO Surgical Safety Checklist: The entire surgical
team performs a "time-out" just before the procedure
begins. This is a critical step in which all team members
verbally confirm:
▪ Patient identity.
▪ Surgical site.
▪ Procedure to be performed.
▪ Anticipated critical events, such as potential blood
loss or surgical difficulties.
Patient:
•
o Pre-operative Checks: The patient's identity, the
surgical site, and the procedure are confirmed multiple
times by different members of the healthcare team.
o Skin Preparation: The surgical site on the patient's body
is prepared to reduce the risk of infection. This involves
cleaning the area with an antiseptic solution, often in
concentric circles moving from the center of the incision
site outward.
o Draping: The patient is covered with sterile drapes, with
an opening (fenestration) that exposes only the surgical
site. This helps to maintain the sterile field.
