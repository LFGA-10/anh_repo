Use nylon sutures or sterile tape placed across the vulva lips with

3. Truss or Retaining Harness
A clean bandage, rope, or specialized harness tied around the body
and under the tail to keep the vagina inside.
d) Postoperative Care
❖ Apply antiseptic ointment around vulva.
❖ Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics for 3–5 days.
❖ Give mild sedatives or progesterone injections to reduce
straining and uterine activity.
❖ Keep the animal on soft bedding and avoid steep slopes.
❖ Provide a balanced, non-bulky diet (reduce ruminal
pressure).
❖ Monitor daily for recurrence or signs of infection.
e) Removal of Sutures
❖ Remove retaining sutures or harness just before parturition
to allow normal delivery.
❖ Observe closely after birth in case of postpartum uterine
prolapse.
5. Prognosis
Good, if treated early and tissue remains viable.
Guarded, if prolapse is recurrent, necrotic, or infected.
Animals with repeated prolapse should not be used for breeding
again (hereditary predisposition).
