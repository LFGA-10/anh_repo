Topic 3: Atresia management
Description of Atresia
Atresia is a congenital absence or closure of a normal body
opening or tubular organ.
It results from failure of a lumen (canal or passage) to develop
properly during embryonic growth.
Because of this, the normal passage of substances (such as feces,
urine, or secretions) is blocked or completely obstructed.
Mechanism (How It Occurs)
During embryonic development, certain organs (like the rectum,
anus, or intestines) form as hollow tubes.
If the recanalization process fails or the membrane separating
two parts does not rupture, the organ remains closed — leading to
atresia.
