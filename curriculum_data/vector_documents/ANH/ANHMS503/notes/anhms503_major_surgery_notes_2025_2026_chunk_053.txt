Clinical Signs
❖ Absence of normal body opening (e.g., no anus or vulva
opening)
❖ Abdominal distension due to accumulation of feces or gas
❖ Straining without defecation or urination
❖ Depression, anorexia, and eventual death if untreated
Clinical Importance
❖ Atresia is a surgical emergency because it causes complete
obstruction of normal body functions (e.g., defecation).
❖ Most forms require corrective surgery (creation of an opening
and joining of blind ends).
❖ Early detection and prompt intervention can save the animal
and restore normal function.
