4. Operative Procedure (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Skin Incision
✓ Make a linear incision over the fracture site, following the long
axis of the bone.
✓ The incision should be large enough to expose the entire
fracture line.
Step 2: Muscle Separation
✓ Carefully separate muscles and soft tissues along natural
planes using blunt dissection.
✓ Control any small bleeding vessels with haemostats or
ligatures.
✓ Avoid excessive damage to surrounding tissues.
Step 3: Exposure of Fracture Site
✓ Expose the broken bone ends clearly.
✓ Remove blood clots, debris, or necrotic tissue using sterile
gauze and saline irrigation.
Step 4: Reduction (Realignment)
✓ Bring the bone fragments into correct anatomical alignment.
✓ Check that the natural length, rotation, and axis of the bone
are restored.
✓ Maintain reduction manually or with clamps during fixation.
