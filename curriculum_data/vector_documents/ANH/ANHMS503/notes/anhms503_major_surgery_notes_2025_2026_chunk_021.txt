Technique of injection is important for safety reasons and for
patient comfort. Always aspirate before injecting.
Local anesthesia is generally very safe, but like any medication, it
can have side effects. These are usually mild and temporary.
Common Side Effects
❖ Soreness, bruising, or bleeding at the injection site.
❖ Tingling sensation as the anesthetic wears off.
❖ Temporary numbness or weakness in the affected area.
