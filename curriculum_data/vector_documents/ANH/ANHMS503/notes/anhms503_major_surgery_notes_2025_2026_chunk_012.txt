Topic 2: Asepsis of surgical instruments
Asepsis of surgical instruments is the foundation of preventing
surgical site infections (SSIs). It refers to the practices that ensure
surgical instruments are completely free of microorganisms
(bacteria, viruses, fungi, etc.) that can cause infection in a patient's
wound. Here's a breakdown of this vital process:
Methods of Instrument Asepsis:
There are two main stages to achieving aseptic surgical
instruments:
1. Cleaning: This involves removing visible dirt, debris, and
organic matter from the instruments.
Disinfection or Sterilization: This eliminates or destroys
•
