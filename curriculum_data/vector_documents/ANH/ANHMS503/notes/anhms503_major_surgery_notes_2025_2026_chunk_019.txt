Topic 2: Anesthesia
Anesthesia is a medical intervention that uses medications (called
anesthetics) to temporarily block the sensation of pain and
awareness during surgery, medical procedures, dentistry, and other
purposes. It allows medical professionals to perform procedures
without causing discomfort to the patient
Basic terms
✓ Sedation: decreased level of consciousness of various grades
and relief of anxiety
✓ Deep sedation: a drug induce depression of consciousness
during which cannot be easily awaken from sleep
✓ General anaesthesia: complete loss of all sensation either
with loss of consciousness
✓ Analgesia: suppression of pain
✓ Anoxiolysis: relief of anxiety
✓ Amnesia: loss memory, which can be anterograde (before
administration of a drug) or retrograde (after administration of
a drug)
✓ Analgesic sedation: a combination of analgesia and sedation.
The ideal anaesthetic agent:
• Non-flammable, non- explosive at room temperature
• Stable in light.
• Liquid and vaporizable at room temperature
• Stable at room temperature, with a long shelf life
• Stable with soda lime as well as plastics and metals
• Environmentally friendly: no ozone depletion
• Cheap and easy to manufacture
• Pleasant to inhale and non-irritant
• Induces bronchodilation
