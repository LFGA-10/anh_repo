2.The preparation of a surgical workplace
The preparation of a surgical workplace is a multi-faceted process
that involves several key stages to ensure a sterile, safe, and
efficient environment for both the patient and the surgical team. It
encompasses preparing the physical space, the equipment, and the
patient. This procedure is crucial for preventing infections and
ensuring the best possible surgical outcome.
