❖ Keep the suture for 5–7 days.
e) Postoperative Care
❖ Administer oxytocin (20–30 IU, IM) to stimulate uterine
contraction.
❖ Give broad-spectrum antibiotics for 5–7 days.
❖ Provide calcium borogluconate (IV or SC) if hypocalcemia is
suspected.
❖ Offer soft, easily digestible feed and monitor for straining or
recurrence.
❖ Remove the retention suture after 5–7 days if recovery is good.
5. Prognosis
❖ Good if treated early and uterus is not severely damaged or
contaminated.
❖ Guarded if there is severe trauma, infection, or tissue
necrosis.
❖ Poor if prolapse recurs or if there’s uterine rupture.
