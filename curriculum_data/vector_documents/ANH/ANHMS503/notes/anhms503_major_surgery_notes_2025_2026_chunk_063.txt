2. Anaesthesia
Pre-operative preparation
❖ Fast the animal for 12–24 hours (dogs/cats).
❖ Empty the bladder before surgery.
❖ Clip and disinfect the surgical site.
Anaesthetic protocol
68

❖ Sedation/Pre-medication:
o Acepromazine (0.05 mg/kg IM) or xylazine (0.5–1 mg/kg
IM)
o Atropine (0.04 mg/kg IM) to reduce salivation
❖ Induction:
Ketamine (10 mg/kg IV) + Diazepam (0.2 mg/kg IV), or
Thiopental sodium (10–15 mg/kg IV).
❖ Maintenance:
o Inhalation anaesthesia (halothane or isoflurane) if
available
o Intravenous maintenance with ketamine if inhalation
equipment is not available
❖ Local anaesthesia: In large animals, flank ovariectomy can be
done under local infiltration or paravertebral block.
❖ Analgesia:
Meloxicam or Carprofen post-surgery.
3. Incisions
Midline incision (most common):
Made on the ventral midline, from just caudal to the umbilicus to
the cranial brim of the pubis.
Flank incision (alternative):
✓ Especially used in large animals (cows, sheep, goats).
✓ Made midway between the last rib and the hip bone on the left
flank.
4. Operative Procedure
Step 1: Preparation
✓ Place the animal in dorsal recumbency (midline approach) or
lateral recumbency (flank approach).
✓ Scrub and drape the surgical field aseptically.
