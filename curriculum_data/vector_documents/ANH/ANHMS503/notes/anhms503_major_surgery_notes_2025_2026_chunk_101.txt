Control pain
❖ An anti-inflammatory medication such as phenylbutazone or
flunixin is often prescribed in an attempt to reduce

Control infection
❖ Depending on the length of time the horse was choked,
antibiotics are prescribed if there is a risk of aspiration
pneumonia.
Follow healing and recovery
❖ Feeding instructions, for herbivores, supply grass and
moistened pellets slowly over a 24-hour period and gradually
return to a normal diet by 7 – 21 days’ post-choke.
❖ Some horses may need permanent dietary changes if they
experience chronic esophageal obstruction.
❖ Monitor the temperature every day.
