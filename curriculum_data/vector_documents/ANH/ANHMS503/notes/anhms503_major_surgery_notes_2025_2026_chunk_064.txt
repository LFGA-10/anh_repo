4. Operative Procedure
Step 1: Preparation
✓ Place the animal in dorsal recumbency (midline approach) or
lateral recumbency (flank approach).
✓ Scrub and drape the surgical field aseptically.
69

Step 2: Incision
✓ Make a skin incision on the chosen site.
✓ Cut through subcutaneous tissue and linea alba (in midline
approach).
Step 3: Locate the Ovaries
✓ Retract the uterine horn to expose the ovary.
✓ The ovary is located caudal to the kidney and attached to the
uterine horn by the proper ligament of the ovary.
Step 4: Ligate the Ovarian Pedicle
✓ Clamp the ovarian pedicle using hemostats.
✓ Ligate the pedicle using absorbable suture (e.g., chromic
catgut or Vicryl 2-0).
✓ Cut the ovary distal to the ligature.
✓ Check for bleeding before releasing the pedicle.
Step 5: Repeat on the Opposite Side
Repeat the procedure for the second ovary.
Step 6: Close the Incision
✓ Close the peritoneum and muscle layer with simple
continuous sutures.
✓ Close subcutaneous tissue with simple continuous sutures.
✓ Close skin with simple interrupted or cruciate sutures.
5. Haemorrhage Control
Proper haemostasis is critical to prevent internal bleeding.
✓ Ligation of ovarian pedicle:
❖ Use absorbable suture material (catgut or Vicryl)
❖ Double ligation recommended (one encircling, one
transfixing).
✓ Avoid tearing of vessels: Handle ovarian pedicle gently.
