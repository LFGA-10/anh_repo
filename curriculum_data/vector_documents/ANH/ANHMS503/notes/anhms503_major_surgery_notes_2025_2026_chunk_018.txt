e-Operative Preparation (Operating Room and
Equipment)
This phase takes place before the patient is brought into the
operating room (OR). The goal is to create a sterile and functional
environment.
20

Cleaning and Disinfection: The OR is thoroughly cleaned and
•
disinfected according to strict hospital protocols. This includes
damp-dusting surfaces from top to bottom and from the center
of the room outward to reduce airborne microorganisms.
Equipment and Supply Check: All necessary equipment is
•
brought into the room, including the operating table,
anesthesia machine, surgical lights, and monitors. Each piece
of equipment is checked for proper function and calibrated as
needed.
Sterile Field Setup: The scrub nurse or surgical technologist
•
prepares the sterile instrument table. Sterile drapes are used
to create a large sterile field on which all surgical instruments
and supplies will be placed. Only sterile items may enter this
field.
Instrument Count: Before the surgery begins, the scrub
•
nurse and circulator nurse perform a count of all instruments,
sponges, and sharps (needles, scalpels). This count is
documented to ensure nothing is left inside the patient at the
end of the procedure
Apply handling and restraint techniques
Topic1: Methods of animal handling and restraint
• Psychological/Verbal: restrain the animal by using the voice
Physical: use of manual or mechanical means to limit some or
•
all of an animal’s normal movement
Chemical: This method utilizes drugs such as sedatives,
•
tranquilizers and anaesthesia to alter the patient’s mental
state, allowing the animal to stay immobilized and/or without
pain, and causing the patient to be cooperative during
procedures.
