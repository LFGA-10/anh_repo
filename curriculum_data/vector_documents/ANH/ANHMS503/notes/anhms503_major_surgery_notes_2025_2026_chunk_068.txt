Topic 8: Caesarean section
A cesarean section (“C-section”) is an incision through the
abdominal wall into the abdomen which is used to isolate the
uterus.
An incision is made through the uterus in order to deliver the calf.

Cesarean section is potentially indicated in cases of dystocia when
a calf cannot be delivered by fetal mutation and extraction.
C-Sections are generally performed on calves that are too large,
which is common with an immature heifer (first time mother) and
for embryo transfer calves.
