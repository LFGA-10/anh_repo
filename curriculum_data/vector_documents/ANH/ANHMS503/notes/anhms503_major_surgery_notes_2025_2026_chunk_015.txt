Typical preparation procedure for a surgical workplace:
Topic 1. Preparation of the Operating Room (OR)
This phase focuses on making the physical environment of the OR
ready for surgery.
17

Damp Dusting and Cleaning: The OR is thoroughly cleaned
•
from top to bottom. Cleaning starts with the highest
equipment, such as overhead lights, and works down to the
floor. This method helps to settle airborne microorganisms.
The cleaning process moves from the center of the room
outward to the perimeter.
Equipment Setup and Placement:
•
o Operating Table: The operating table is positioned in the
center of the room. It should be checked for functionality,
including the ability to tilt and rotate as needed for
various surgical positions. Safety straps are placed to
secure the patient and prevent injury.
o Anesthesia Machine: The anesthesia machine is placed
at the head of the operating table. It is thoroughly
checked to ensure all components are functioning
correctly, including oxygen supply, gas flow, and alarms.
o Surgical Instruments: A sterile table is set up with all
the necessary surgical instruments. The instruments are
arranged in a specific order to allow the surgical team to
access them efficiently.
o Monitors and Other Equipment: Patient monitors (ECG,
blood pressure, pulse oximeter, etc.), a ventilator, and
other specialized equipment (e.g., diathermy machine,
suction) are placed and checked to ensure they are
working properly.
Creating a Sterile Field: Once all the equipment is in place, a
•
sterile field is established. This is an area free of
microorganisms, created by using sterile drapes to cover the
patient and surrounding equipment. All objects that enter this
field must be sterile.
