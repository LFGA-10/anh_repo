Common Side Effects
❖ Soreness, bruising, or bleeding at the injection site.
❖ Tingling sensation as the anesthetic wears off.
❖ Temporary numbness or weakness in the affected area.
24

Less Common Side Effects
❖ Dizziness
❖ Headaches
❖ Blurred vision
❖ Twitching muscles or shivering
❖ Nausea or vomiting
Serious Side Effects
While rare, serious side effects can occur, especially if the
anesthetic is accidentally injected into a blood vessel. These can
include:
❖ Allergic reactions (rash, itching, difficulty breathing)
❖ Heart rhythm disturbances
❖ Seizures
❖ Low blood pressure
Toxicity and complications of local anaesthetics
✓ Muscle twitching
✓ Seizures
✓ Depression
✓ Unconsciousness
✓ Coma
✓ Respiratory arrest
Topic 3: General anesthesia
2. General anaesthesia: drug induced unconsciousness and
insensitivity (lacking feeling) to pain. Depending on the route of
administration, we can classify general anaesthesia as:
Inhalation: induced by inhalation of anesthetic gases
or volatile anaesthetic: nitrous oxide(N2O), xenon,
volatile anaesthetics (diethyl ether, isoflurane,
desflurane)
