3. Retention with Band or Harness
In small animals, a rectal retention bandage or tail tie may be

e) Amputation (If Tissue Is Necrotic)
56

❖ If the rectum is severely damaged, necrotic, or nonviable:
❖ Place a purse-string suture at the base to control bleeding.
❖ Excise (cut) the necrotic portion distal to the suture.
❖ Suture the mucosa to the skin margin using simple
interrupted absorbable sutures.
Apply antiseptic and antibiotic treatment post-op.
f) Postoperative Care
❖ Apply antiseptic ointment around the anus daily.
❖ Give broad-spectrum antibiotics for 3–5 days.
❖ Administer stool softeners or laxatives to prevent straining.
❖ Provide anti-inflammatory drugs if needed.
❖ Treat underlying causes (e.g., deworm, manage
diarrhea/constipation).
❖ Monitor for recurrence or infection.
5. Prognosis
Good, if treated early and underlying cause is corrected.
Guarded, if there is necrosis, infection, or recurrent prolapse.
