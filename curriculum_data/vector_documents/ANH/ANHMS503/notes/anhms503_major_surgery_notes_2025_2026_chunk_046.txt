Vaginal prolapse
1. Definition
Vaginal prolapse is a condition where part of the vaginal wall
protrudes through the vulva.
It usually occurs before parturition (late pregnancy) and is most
common in cows and ewes.
2. Causes
❖ Late pregnancy (pressure from the growing fetus)
❖ Overfeeding → fat around the pelvis
❖ Weakness or relaxation of vaginal and pelvic ligaments
❖ Estrogenic feeds (e.g., clover, mouldy feed)
❖ Excessive straining or coughing
❖ Steep slope or poor bedding (animal lies with hindquarters
downhill)
❖ Previous prolapse history (recurrence is common)
3. Clinical Signs
❖ Red, swollen vaginal mass protruding through vulva when
animal lies down
❖ May retract when standing (early cases)
❖ Mucus, dirt, or trauma on exposed tissue
❖ Animal shows restlessness, straining, frequent urination
❖ In advanced cases, prolapse may remain permanently
exposed and become necrotic or infected
