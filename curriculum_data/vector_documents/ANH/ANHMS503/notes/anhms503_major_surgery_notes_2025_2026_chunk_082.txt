❖ Mould the cast while still soft to fit anatomical contours.

C. Bandages
Definition: Bandages are soft coverings applied around an injured
joint for mild support and compression.
Types:
❖ Robert Jones bandage: Thick multilayered cotton and gauze
bandage for limbs.
❖ Modified Robert Jones bandage: Thinner version, used post-
operatively.
❖ Adhesive elastic bandages (Vet wrap): For mild support and
control of swelling.
Purpose:
❖ Reduce swelling and pain.
❖ Provide light immobilization.
❖ Protect the joint post-reduction or after cast removal.
