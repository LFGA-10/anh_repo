When reduction is decided upon it may be carried out in three
ways:

❖ Closed manipulation
❖ Mechanical traction with or without manipulation
❖ Open operation.
A. Closed Reduction
❖ Bone fragments are manipulated externally without surgery.
❖ Suitable for simple fractures with minimal displacement.
❖ Usually done under general or local anaesthesia.
Steps:
❖ Restrain the animal and administer anaesthesia.
❖ Gently manipulate and align the bone fragments into their
natural position.
❖ Confirm alignment by palpation or radiography.
❖ Apply immobilisation (splint, cast, or bandage).
B. Open Reduction
❖ Surgical exposure of the fracture site.
❖ Used for complex, displaced, or compound fractures.
❖ Bone fragments are aligned manually and stabilized with
internal fixation (pins, plates, wires, screws).
4. Immobilisation Techniques of Broken Bones
After reduction, immobilisation is essential to maintain alignment
during healing.
