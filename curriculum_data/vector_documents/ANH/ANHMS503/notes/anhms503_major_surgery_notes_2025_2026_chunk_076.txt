3. Immobilization (Compression or Bandage)
Immobilization supports the injured tissues and prevents further
damage.
a. Compression Bandage:
❖ Apply a firm but not tight bandage around the injured area
to reduce swelling and provide support.
❖ Use elastic or crepe bandage.
❖ Ensure normal circulation — toes should remain warm and
pink.
❖ Maintain for 2–3 days, changing daily if swelling or discharge
appears.
