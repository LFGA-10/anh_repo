IC 3.3: Care of patient after orthopedic surgery
Topic 1: Teat sprain
Control pain
✓ Use of analgesic drugs
Control infection
✓ Use of antibiotics
115

Follow healing and recovery
❖ Gradually reintroduce exercise to prevent stiffness.
❖ Avoid slippery surfaces and overexertion during recovery.
❖ Monitor for complications such as chronic lameness or
fibrous tissue formation.
Topic 2: Treat bone dislocation
Control pain
❖ Analgesics and anti-inflammatory drugs (e.g., Meloxicam,
Flunixin).
Control infection
❖ Antibiotics if open wounds or surgery involved.
Follow healing and recovery
❖ Physiotherapy after immobilization to restore joint mobility.
❖ Restrict activity until complete recovery.
❖ The patient should be well controlled in terms of blood
irrigation for the distal parts of the bandaged limbs.
❖ Supportive structures use to protect the joint may be removed
after minimal recovery depending on the age of the patient and
the severity of injury.
Topic 3: Treat bone fracture
Control pain
❖ Analgesics/Anti-inflammatories: To relieve pain and reduce
swelling.
❖ Immobilisation: Apply a light bandage or external splint for
support if needed.
