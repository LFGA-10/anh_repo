The procedure:
Enucleation is performed by an ophthalmologist (eye surgeon)
under general anesthesia.
Good anaesthesia, proper incisional and suturing procedures
dominate this phase.

Auriculopalpebral Nerve Block:
❖ Surgical manipulation of the eye is facilitated by
nerve blockade of the eyelids.
❖ Auriculopalpebral nerve block can be placed to
reduce upper eyelid movement prior to performing a
Peterson or retrobulbar block.
❖ The auriculopalpebral nerve can be palpated as it
crosses the zygomatic arch, roughly 5-6 centimeters
behind the supraorbital process.
