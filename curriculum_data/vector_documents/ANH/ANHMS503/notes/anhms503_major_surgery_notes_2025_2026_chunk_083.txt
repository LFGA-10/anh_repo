Topic.3. Treat fracture
Description of fractures
A fracture is a break or crack in the continuity of a bone, usually
caused by trauma (e.g., accidents, falls, or kicks), pathological
conditions (e.g., bone disease), or repeated stress. Fractures may
involve a single bone or multiple bones and can vary in severity
from small cracks to complete breaks.
