B. Casts
❖ Description: A hard encasement made of plaster of Paris or
synthetic material (fiberglass).
❖ Technique:
✓ After reduction, wrap the limb with soft padding.
✓ Apply wet plaster bandages evenly around the limb.
✓ Allow to dry and harden (15–30 min).
✓ Ensure circulation is not obstructed.
• Advantages: Provides firm, uniform support; good for long-
term Immobilisation.
