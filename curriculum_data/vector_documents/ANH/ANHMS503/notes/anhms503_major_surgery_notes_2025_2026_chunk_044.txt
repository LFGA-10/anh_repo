4. Treatment (Emergency Management)
Step-by-Step Procedure
a) Preparation
48

❖ Restrain the animal safely (standing position preferred; if
recumbent, place in sternal recumbency with hindquarters
elevated).
❖ Clean the perineal area and prolapsed uterus with warm,
antiseptic saline solution to remove dirt and debris.
❖ Remove fetal membranes gently if still attached.
❖ Apply epidural anaesthesia to reduce straining:
❖ Cow: 5–10 ml of 2% lignocaine between the first and second
coccygeal vertebrae.
❖ Ewe/Goat: 2–3 ml.
b) Control of Haemorrhage
❖ Apply cold saline compress to reduce swelling.
❖ Ligate any bleeding vessels if visible.
❖ In severe bleeding, apply pressure with clean cloth or gauze.
c) Reduction (Repositioning the Uterus)
❖ Raise the uterus on a clean sheet or tray to prevent further
contamination.
❖ If swollen, apply hypertonic solution (e.g., sugar or glycerine)
for 10–15 minutes to reduce oedema.
❖ Start pushing the uterus gently but firmly back through the
vulva using the palm of your hand and a closed fist.
❖ Begin at the cervical end (base) and gradually work inward,
ensuring all folds are unfolded and fully repositioned.
❖ Once the uterus is replaced, insert your arm through the vulva
to ensure both horns are fully everted and in normal position.
d) Retention of the Uterus
To prevent recurrence:
❖ Place a Bühner suture (or bootlace suture) across the vulva,
leaving space for urination.
❖ Alternatively, use infusion set tubing or shoelace method.
