IC.2.2: Assisting in abdominal surgery
Topic 1: Hernia and hernia repair
Treatment of the hernia A hernia is defined as the displacement of
an organ through a normal aperture (a true hernia) or pathological
aperture (false hernia or rupture). The mobile organs of the
abdomen are most commonly displaced through apertures in the
abdominal wall, diaphragm, inguinal canal or femoral canal.
