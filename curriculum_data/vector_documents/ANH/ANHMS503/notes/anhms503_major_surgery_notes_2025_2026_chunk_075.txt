2. Management of Inflammation
The goal is to reduce pain, swelling, and tissue damage during
the acute stage (first 24–72 hours).
a. Cold Therapy (first 24–48 hours):
❖ Apply cold compress or ice packs for 15–20 minutes several
times a day.
❖ This helps reduce inflammation, swelling, and pain.
b. Anti-inflammatory Drugs:
❖ Use NSAIDs such as:
❖ Flunixin meglumine (1.1 mg/kg IV or IM)
❖ Phenylbutazone (4–5 mg/kg)
