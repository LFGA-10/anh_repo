IC.2.1: apply head and neck surgery procedures
Topic 1: Management of an oesophageal choke
Complete oesophageal obstruction (choke) is considered to be an
emergency surgical condition in ruminants. Intra-luminal
oesophageal obstruction may occur due to vegetables, phytobezoars
, pieces of leather or rubber. Choke causes severe bloat which may
be life threatening if nottreated in time. The present module
describes the oesophageal obstruction and its surgical
management.
Description of oesophageal choke
Choke is an obstruction in the oesophagus (food pipe) and an
emergency in all species if it also obstructs the airway. In cattle the
object is usually stuck high up in the oesophagus near the throat,
or it can be in the entrance to the windpipe, which is rapidly fatal.
Objects causing choke include:
✓ plastic bags,
✓ potatoes and
✓ other whole vegetables, or
✓ large fruit like mangoes, apples and oranges.
