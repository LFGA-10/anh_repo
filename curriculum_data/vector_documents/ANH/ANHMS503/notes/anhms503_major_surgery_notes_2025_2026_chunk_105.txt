Topic 6: Perform Ovariectomy
Control pain
✓ Provide analgesics: e.g., Meloxicam (0.2 mg/kg IM) for 3
days.
Control infection
✓ Administer antibiotics: e.g., Amoxicillin or Penicillin for 5–7
days.
✓ Restrict activity: Prevent jumping or running for at least 10–
14 days.
Follow healing and recovery
✓ Monitor vital signs (temperature, pulse, respiration).
✓ Inspect the wound daily for swelling, discharge, or
dehiscence.
✓ Remove skin sutures after 10–14 days (if non-absorbable).
