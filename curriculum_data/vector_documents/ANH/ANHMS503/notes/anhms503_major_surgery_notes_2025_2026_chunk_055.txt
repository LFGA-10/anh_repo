4. Treatment (Surgical Management)
Objective: Create an artificial anal opening to allow fecal passage.
Step-by-Step Surgical Procedure
a) Preparation
❖ Restrain the animal gently but firmly (use an assistant if
needed).
❖ Clean and disinfect the perineal region with antiseptic
solution.
❖ Clip the hair around the area.
b) Anaesthesia
❖ Local anaesthesia: infiltration with 2% lignocaine (lidocaine)
around the perineal area.
❖ Sedation/general anaesthesia may be used in larger animals
(e.g., xylazine in calves).
c) Incision
❖ Identify the normal site of the anus (usually a small
depression is visible).
❖ Make a cruciate (cross-shaped) or circular incision through
the skin and underlying tissues.
❖ Carefully dissect deeper until the blind end of the rectum is
located.
❖ Take care not to damage surrounding structures such as the
urethra or vagina.
