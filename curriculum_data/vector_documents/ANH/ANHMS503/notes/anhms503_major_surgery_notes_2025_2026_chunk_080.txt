3. Restoration of Joint (Reduction)
87

Reduction means restoring the dislocated joint to its normal
anatomical position.
Types of Reduction
Closed Reduction (Manipulative Reduction):
❖ No surgical incision.
❖ Used in simple, recent dislocations.
❖ Performed under general or local anaesthesia to relax
muscles.
❖ The operator gently manipulates the bones into normal
alignment.
❖ Confirm by palpation or radiograph.
Open Reduction (Surgical Reduction):
Required when:
❖ Dislocation is old or complicated.
❖ Fractures are present.
❖ Ligaments or capsules are torn.
❖ Surgical exposure of the joint, removal of obstacles, and
repositioning of the bones.
❖ May include fixation (e.g., pins, screws, wire) to maintain
stability.
4. Immobilisation Techniques of Joint
After reduction, the joint must be immobilized to allow healing of
the capsule, ligaments, and surrounding tissues.
Purpose of Immobilization
❖ To maintain joint stability after reduction.
❖ To prevent re-dislocation.
❖ To reduce pain and inflammation.
❖ To promote tissue repair and recovery.
