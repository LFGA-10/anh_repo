5. Repairing the weakness: The weakened area in the

Topic 2: Manage prolapse
Description of Prolapse
Prolapse is a condition in which an organ or tissue falls, slips, or
protrudes from its normal anatomical position, usually through a
natural body opening.
It occurs when the supporting muscles, ligaments, or connective
tissues become weak, overstretched, or damaged, allowing the
organ to move outward.
In animals, prolapse most commonly affects structures of the
reproductive and digestive systems, such as:
❖ Vagina → vaginal prolapse
❖ Uterus → uterine prolapse
❖ Rectum → rectal prolapse
