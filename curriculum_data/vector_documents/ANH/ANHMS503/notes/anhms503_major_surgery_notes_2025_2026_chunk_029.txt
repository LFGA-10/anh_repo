✓ When taking a rectal temperature, the animal often shows
resistance, so it must be restrained and held firmly. Ensure
that the thermometer indicates the bottom of the scale before
you commence:
✓ Lubricate the bulb of the thermometer (ie with KY jelly or other
lubricant).
✓ Gently insert the thermometer into the rectum, slightly
rotating it as you go. Do not force entry.
✓ Retain in the rectum for a minimum of sixty seconds. For large
animals, ensure the thermometer stays in contact with the
wall of the rectum for a correct reading.
✓ Gently remove and wipe faeces from the end.
✓ Read immediately and record finding.
Pulse rate The pulse can be taken at any point on the body where a
major artery runs close to the body surface. Each pulsation
corresponds with a contraction of the heart. How to take the pulse
rate:
✓ With the aid of an assistant, restrain the patient.
✓ Ensure that the patient is relaxed and still.
✓ Using the tips of your fingers, apply them directly on the
artery.
✓ Count the rate of pumps over a minute.
✓ Record this information.
