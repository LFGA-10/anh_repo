5. Operative Procedure (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Incision and Exposure
❖ Make a longitudinal incision directly over the fracture site.
❖ Separate muscles and soft tissues gently using blunt
dissection.
❖ Expose the bone ends clearly, taking care not to damage blood
supply.
Step 2: Cleaning the Fracture Site
❖ Remove blood clots, debris, and necrotic tissue using sterile
gauze.
❖ Irrigate the site with sterile saline to maintain cleanliness.
Step 3: Reduction (Alignment)
❖ Bring bone fragments into correct anatomical alignment
(length, rotation, and axis).
❖ Hold them in place temporarily using bone clamps or
reduction forceps.
