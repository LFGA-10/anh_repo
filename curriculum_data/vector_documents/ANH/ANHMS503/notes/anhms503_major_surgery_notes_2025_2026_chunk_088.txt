OPERATIVE PROCEDURE FOR OPEN REDUCTION
95

1. Definition
Open reduction is a surgical method used to expose a fractured
bone, align the broken fragments anatomically, and stabilize them
using internal fixation devices such as pins, plates, wires, or
screws.
2. Objectives
❖ To restore the normal anatomical alignment of fractured
bones.
❖ To stabilize bone fragments to allow proper healing.
❖ To remove debris, blood clots, or dead tissue from the fracture
site.
❖ To prevent complications such as malunion, non-union, and
infection.
3. Preoperative Preparation
Patient preparation
✓ Fast the animal for 12–24 hours before surgery.
✓ Clip and shave the area around the fracture site widely.
✓ Clean and disinfect the area with antiseptic solution
(povidone-iodine or chlorhexidine).
✓ Apply sterile drapes to maintain asepsis.
Anaesthesia
✓ General anaesthesia is usually required (e.g., xylazine–
ketamine combination).
✓ Administer analgesics and antibiotics pre-operatively.
Positioning
✓ Position the animal according to the location of the fracture
(e.g., lateral recumbency for limb fractures).
