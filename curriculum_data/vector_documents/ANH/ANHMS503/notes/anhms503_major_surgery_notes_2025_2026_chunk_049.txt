Rectal prolapse
1. Definition
Rectal prolapse is the protrusion of the rectum through the
anus due to weakness, straining, or other underlying causes.
54

It may involve only the mucosa (partial prolapse) or the full rectal
wall (complete prolapse).
2. Causes
❖ Persistent straining due to:
❖ Diarrhea or constipation
❖ Urinary obstruction
❖ Difficult parturition
❖ Parasite infestation (especially in young animals)
❖ Rectal irritation (proctitis)
❖ Tail docking too short (in lambs)
❖ Coughing or heavy respiratory effort (in pigs)
❖ Overfeeding or poor nutrition leading to intestinal disorders
3. Clinical Signs
❖ Cylindrical, red, moist mass protruding from the anus
❖ Animal strains frequently
❖ Pain or discomfort
❖ Swelling, dryness, or necrosis of the exposed tissue (if
untreated)
❖ In severe or chronic cases, tissue becomes dark and may
slough off
