4. Treatment (Surgical & Supportive Management)
Step-by-Step Procedure
a) Preparation
Restrain the animal properly (standing preferred; if recumbent,
hindquarters elevated).
Clean the perineal region and prolapsed tissue with warm
antiseptic saline.
Remove debris and discharge gently.
Administer epidural anaesthesia to reduce straining:
Cow: 5–10 ml of 2% lignocaine
Ewe/Goat: 2–3 ml
b) Reduction (Repositioning the Vagina)
❖ Apply osmotic agent (e.g., sugar or glycerine) on prolapsed
tissue for 10–15 minutes to reduce swelling.
❖ Gently push the prolapsed vagina back into its normal
position using the palm and closed fist.
❖ Make sure the urinary bladder is not trapped inside before
final repositioning.
c) Retention Techniques (to prevent recurrence)
❖ After repositioning, retain the vagina using one of the
following methods:
❖ Bühner Suture (Bootlace technique)
❖ Most common and effective method.
❖ Pass a Bühner needle with sterile tape or tubing under the
skin around the vulva (like a purse string).
❖ Tie loosely to allow urination.
❖ Remove at calving/lambing time.
❖ . Button or Tape Suture (Horizontal mattress)
