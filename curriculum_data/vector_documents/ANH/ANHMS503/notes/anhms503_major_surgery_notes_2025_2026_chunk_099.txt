C. Bandages
❖ Description: Soft or elastic coverings that support and
immobilize the limb temporarily or along with splints/casts.
❖ Types:
✓ Robert Jones bandage – bulky, for temporary
immobilisation.
✓ Modified Robert Jones bandage – less bulky, for small
animals.
Procedure:
✓ Wrap multiple layers of cotton padding around the limb.
✓ Apply crepe bandage in overlapping turns from distal to
proximal end.
✓ Ensure even pressure and secure ends with adhesive tape.
Advantages:
✓ Reduces swelling and provides mild support.
✓ Useful for transport and temporary stabilization.
D. External Skeletal Fixators (Modern Technique)
Description:
A rigid frame made of pins or wires inserted through the bone
and connected externally by bars or clamps.
Components:
❖ Pins (transfixation pins): pass through the bone.
