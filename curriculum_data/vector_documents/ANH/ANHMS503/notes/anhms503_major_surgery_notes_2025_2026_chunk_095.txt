7. Wound Closure
❖ Irrigate the site thoroughly with sterile saline.
❖ Ensure haemostasis.
❖ Close the muscle and fascia with absorbable sutures (e.g.,
chromic catgut).
❖ Close the skin with non-absorbable sutures (e.g., nylon or
silk).
❖ Apply sterile dressing to the surgical site.
8. Postoperative Care
❖ Immobilisation: Apply a light bandage or external splint for
support if needed.
❖ Antibiotics: Administer for 5–7 days to prevent infection.
❖ Analgesics/Anti-inflammatories: To relieve pain and reduce
swelling.
❖ Restricted activity: Confine the animal to a small pen or cage
until healing progresses.
❖ Suture removal: After 10–14 days.
❖ Radiographic follow-up: To monitor healing and implant
position.
❖ Physiotherapy: Gradual movement exercises after 3–4 weeks to
prevent stiffness.
9. Advantages
• Provides rigid and stable fixation.
• Allows early limb use.
• Ensures accurate anatomical healing.
