5. Methods of External Fixation
A. Splints
❖ Description: Rigid supports placed along the limb to prevent
bone and joint movement.
105

❖ Materials: Wooden boards, bamboo sticks, PVC pipes, or
aluminum strips.
❖ Procedure:
✓ Clean and pad the limb with soft cotton to prevent
pressure sores.
✓ Apply splints along the limb, ensuring they cover joints
above and below the fracture.
✓ Secure splints with bandages or adhesive tape.
✓ Check regularly for tightness, swelling, or discomfort.
Advantages:
✓ Simple and inexpensive.
✓ Easy to apply and remove.
✓ Suitable for field or emergency use.
Disadvantages:
✓ Provides limited rigidity.
✓ May slip or loosen if not properly secured.
B. Casts
❖ Description: A cast is a hard shell applied around the limb to
provide complete immobilisation.
❖ Materials: Plaster of Paris bandages or synthetic materials
(e.g., fiberglass).
❖ Procedure:
✓ After reduction, wrap the limb with padding material.
✓ Dip plaster bandages in water and apply in
overlapping layers.
✓ Mould the cast to the natural shape of the limb.
✓ Allow to dry and harden (15–30 minutes).
✓ Check circulation and toe movement.
✓ Replace or adjust cast if swelling occurs.
