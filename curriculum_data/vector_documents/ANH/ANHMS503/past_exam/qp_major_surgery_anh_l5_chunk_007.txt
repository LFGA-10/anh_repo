f.	Comminuted fracture – the bone is shattered into small pieces. This type of complicated fracture tends to heal more slowly

g.	Avulsion fracture – muscles are anchored to bone with tendons, a type of connective tissue. Powerful muscle contractions can wrench the tendon free and pull out pieces of bone. This type of fracture is more common in the knee and shoulder joints

h.	Compression fracture – occurs when two bones are forced against each other. The bones of the spine, called vertebrae, can have this type of fracture. Older animals, particularly those with osteoporosis, are at higher risk.
