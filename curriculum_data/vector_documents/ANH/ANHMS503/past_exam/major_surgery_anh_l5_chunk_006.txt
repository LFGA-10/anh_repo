Q22. Explain the various rumen fixation techniques used during a rumenotomy to prevent contamination of the abdominal cavity. /10 marks

Answer: Techniques include:

Stay suture technique: Anchoring rumen walls to the skin incision.

Rumen skin fixation sutures: Fixing the rumen wall to the skin incision with continuous inverting sutures.

Weingarth ring technique: Using a ring to fix the rumen to the incision.

Rumen skin clamp fixation: Fixing the rumen with skin clamps.

Q23. Explain the methods of fracture immobilization /15 marks

Answer: there are four methods by which it may be effected:

by a plaster of Paris cast or other external splint

by continuous traction

by external fixation

by internal fixation

Q24. Classify fractures/15 marks

ANSWER:

a.	Closed (simple) fracture – the broken bone has not pierced the skin

b.	Open (compound) fracture – the broken bone juts out through the skin, or a wound leads to the fracture site. Infection and external bleeding are more likely

c.	Greenstick fracture – a small, slender crack in the bone. This can occur in young animals, because their bones are more flexible that an adult’s bones

d.	Hairline fracture – the most common form is a stress fracture, often occurring in the foot or lower leg as a result of repeated stress from activities such as jogging or running
