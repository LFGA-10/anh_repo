Q2O. What is the significance of "strangulation" in a hernia, and what are its potential consequences? /10 marks

Answer: Strangulation occurs when the blood supply to the herniated organs is impaired, leading to tissue necrosis. This can result in severe complications, including infarction.

Q21. Describe the procedure for a laparo-rumenotomy, including the preferred incision site and anesthesia techniques. /10 marks

Answer: A laparo-rumenotomy involves incising the rumen via the left abdominal wall. The preferred incision site is a left mid-flank vertical incision. Paravertebral anesthesia is commonly used, along with local anesthesia.
