The caudectomy is a common practice in the sheep rearing. This practice may be followed by various complications.

Elaborate a strategy aiming the prevention of these complications.

Answer (with marks allocations)

Different measures can be taken to prevent caudectomy complications:

Only dock if necessary;

Avoid docking lambs less than 24 hours old and older than a week of age;

Only dock healthy lambs;

Ensure lambs to be docked are protected against the Clostridial diseases;

Avoid docking in bad weather or in soiled, muddy surroundings or during the fly season;

Check afterwards, especially last thing at night, for signs of ill effects such as hemorrhage or excessive discomfort;

Ensure all operators are trained and competent.
