4. Pluviometer/Rain Gauge (Rainfall Measurement)
• Purpose: Measures the amount of rainfall in outdoor enclosures or during field studies.
• Use: Positioned outside to monitor rainfall levels and patterns, which can impact outdoor
animal habitats or research conditions.
• Importance: Monitoring rainfall helps in planning for enclosure management, drainage,
and understanding environmental changes affecting animal welfare.
5. Barometer (Air Pressure Measurement)
2

• Purpose: Measures atmospheric pressure, which can affect animal behavior and physical
conditions.
• Use: Installed in the animal facility to detect changes in air pressure that could influence
animal physiology or behavior.
• Importance: Sudden changes in air pressure can cause discomfort or stress in some
animals, particularly those sensitive to atmospheric conditions.
6. Digital Oxygen Analyzer (Oxygen Level Monitoring)
• Purpose: Measures the concentration of oxygen in the air.
• Use: Often used in controlled environments such as sealed chambers or rooms where
animals may be housed in lower-oxygen environments.
• Importance: Ensuring adequate oxygen levels is vital for preventing hypoxia and
ensuring proper respiratory function.
