5.2.3 Vaccines administration and possible adverse effects
1) Injections
✓ Appropriate vaccine administration is a critical component of a successful immunization
program.
✓ Ensure that the animal is restrained securely before injection. Use only sharp and straight
needles (discard dull, bent or burred needles).
Cattle vaccination techniques
2) Adverse Reactions
✓ Animals should be observed after vaccination for evidence of adverse reactions.
✓ Life-threatening anaphylactic responses can occur.
✓ Cattle should be observed for at least one hour post-vaccination.
✓ Itching, swelling and redness of the skin may indicate signs of a local adverse response.
✓ Adverse responses may not be limited to the injection area and may be visible around the nose,
anus and vulva.
5.2.4 Vaccination methods
Use needles of appropriate size, based on the age of the animal, weight of the animal, the route of
product administration, and the viscosity of the product.
✓ IM (intramuscular) – 18 or 16 g, 1 to 1 ½”
✓ SC (subcutaneous) – 18 or 16 g, ½ to 1”
