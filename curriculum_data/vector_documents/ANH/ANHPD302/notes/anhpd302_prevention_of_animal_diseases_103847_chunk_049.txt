4.4.4 Rationale of ectoparasites control
The rationale for the ectoparasite control is rather simple. It is more of putting the end to the life
cycle of the ectoparasites. It manly consists of either killing the parasites before they mature and
start to lay eggs or kill mature parasites capable of laying eggs. There is also a way of making the
environment non conducive for eggs hatching.
4.5 Perform chemoprevention against worms and protozoans
Both helminthiasis and protozoal diseases can be controlled using different approaches. In this
section chemoprevention is discussed.
4.5.1 Internal antiparasitic drugs
Internal antiparasitic drugs mainly consist of anthelminthic and antiprotozoal drugs. However,
some anti-ectoparasites can be administered by injections and thus, considered as medicines for
internal use though; they act on both internal and external parasites. The main example is
IVERMECTIN which is administered by subcutaneous route.
