3) Xylazine
Xylazineis commonly used to provide sedation and analgesia in ruminants as it is 10 to 20 times
more potent in these species than in others. The degree of sedation induced is dose-dependent but
also depends on the route of injection and the animal’s temperament.
Xylazine also induces some side effects:
✓ severe cardiovascular changes (bradycardia, AV blocks, decreased cardiac output)
✓ respiratory effects (hypoventilation, respiratory acidosis, hypoxemia and
✓ pulmonary oedema, especially in sheep)
✓ decreased GIT motility and bloating
✓ induction of uterine contractions
✓ sweating
