3. Intravenous
An intravenous injection requires skill to locate a vein, usually
the jugular vein in the neck, insert the needle, and ensure that
the needle remains in the vessel whilethe drug is given. These
injections should always be given by a veterinarian or
experienced animal health technician. Animals may react
quickly to drugs given in this fashion due to rapid absorption.
Very few drugs need to be given intravenously; however,
blood samples often need to be collected and the technique is
the same. Intravenous injections are usually done using an 18 or 20 gauge hypodermic needle.
