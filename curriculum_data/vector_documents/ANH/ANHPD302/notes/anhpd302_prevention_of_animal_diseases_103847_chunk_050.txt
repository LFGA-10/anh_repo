4.5.5 Control of protozoal infections
Being vector borne diseases, the control of protozoal infections consist of elimination of vectors
and intermediate hosts. But also, there are vaccines for some protozoal diseases.
4.5.6 Prophylactic plan
The prophylactic plan is determined by many factors such as animal species, diseases to prevent,
geographical area amongst others. Example of prophylactic plans in different animal species is
given on the appendix of this document.
4.5.7 Administration routes for chemoprevention drugs
- Per os, drenching: commonly used to administer some antihelmintic drugs. Drugs
admistered through this route are the ones with fewer complications as far as administration
is concerned. Therefore, farmers can easily administer drugs via this route with no
assistance of a trained veterinary or para-veterinary officer.
