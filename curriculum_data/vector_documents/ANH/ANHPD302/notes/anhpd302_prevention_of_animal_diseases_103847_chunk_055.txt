5.1.3 Brief description of common notifiable diseases
A notifiable disease is any disease that is required by law to be reported to government authorities.
The collation of information allows the authorities to monitor the disease, and provides early
warning of possible outbreaks. In the case of livestock diseases, there may also be the legal
requirement to destroy the infected livestock upon notification. Many governments have enacted
regulations for reporting of both human and animal (generally livestock) diseases. This usually
happens during pandemics.
