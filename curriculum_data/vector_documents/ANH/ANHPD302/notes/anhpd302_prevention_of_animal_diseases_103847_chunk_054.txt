✓ Toxico-infection: Toxico infection refers to the establishment of infection by the toxins
produced by vegetative organisms especially Clostridium spp., Staphylococcus etc. in the

✓ Contagious: capable of being transmitted by bodily contact with an infected animal/person or
object
✓ Epizootic: a disease or condition that occurs at about the same time in many individuals of the
same species in a geographic area.
✓ Panzootic: occurring among animals over a wide geographical area.
✓ Enzootic: An enzootic disease is constantly present in an animal population, but usually only
affects a small number of animals at any one time.
