4.4 Ectoparasites control methods
There are many methods of controlling ectoparasites but all of them can’t be discussed at this level.
In this part only the main ones are discussed.
4.4.1 External antiparasitics
Antiparasitic animal drugs are used to treat and control parasitic infections and infestations in
animals.
Example: Ivermectin
Ivermectin acts by causing paralysis in susceptible arthropods and nematodes. It is used in small
animals for treatmentof Sarcoptesscabeii,Otodectescynotis, andDemodexcanis; in cattle for
psoroptic mange, lice. It also acts on internal parasites.
4.4.2 Acaricides and insecticides
Insecticidesare any substance or a mixture of substances intended to prevent, destroy, repel, or
mitigate insects. Similarly,acaricidesare substances that can destroy mites. A chemical can exert
both insecticidalandacaricidal effects.
4.4.3 Techniques of applying insecticides and acaricides
The most popular method of controlling ticks on livestock is the application of acaricides directly
to the animal host. It is important that application techniques be thorough and that the acaricides
be highly effective against ticks without injuring the host.
