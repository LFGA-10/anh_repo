LEARNING UNIT 1: MONITOR PHYSICAL CONDITIONS OF ANIMAL HOUSING
1.0 Equipment to monitor animal house physical conditions
1. Thermometer (Temperature Monitoring)
• Purpose: Measures the temperature within the animal house to ensure it is within the
optimal range for the species being housed.
• Use: Placed in key areas of the environment to continuously or periodically monitor
temperatures. Digital or infrared thermometers can provide real-time data and alerts if
temperature exceeds preset limits.
• Importance: Maintaining the correct temperature is crucial for animal welfare,
preventing overheating or hypothermia.
2. Hygrometer (Humidity Monitoring)
• Purpose: Measures humidity levels in the air, which is essential for the health of animals.
• Use: Installed in areas where humidity control is vital, such as rooms housing reptiles,
amphibians, or small mammals that are sensitive to moisture levels.
• Importance: Excessive humidity can promote mold growth and respiratory problems,
while low humidity can lead to dehydration and skin issues.
