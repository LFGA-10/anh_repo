Q4. Enunciate the techniques used to prevent animal kicking. /4 marks
Answer/ 4 marks
Techniques to prevent animal kicking
✓ The most common method used to prevent kicking is lifting the tail.
✓ It is possible to tie the back legs together using a 'hobble' to prevent
kicking to an extent. However, it may be difficult to remove the hobbles
if the animal goes down for some reason.
✓ Tying a rope around the animal with the rope passing over the
transverse processes of the sacrum can be a very effective way of
discouraging kicking, and does not require an assistant so is useful for
lengthy procedures such as stripping out a mastitic quarter.

Q5. What is the purpose of restraining measures? /2 marks
Answer /2 marks
The method used should provide the least restraint required to allow the
specific procedure(s) to be performed properly, should minimize fear, pain,
stress and suffering for the animal, and should protect both the animal and
personnel from harm.
