Q16. Describe the behavior signs observed to the dogs (Dog-dog). /3 marks
Answer /3 marks
➢ Dog-dog. Play between dogs usually involves several behaviors that are
often seen in aggressive encounters, for example, nipping, biting and
growling. It is therefore important for the dogs to place these behaviors
in the context of play, rather than aggression.
