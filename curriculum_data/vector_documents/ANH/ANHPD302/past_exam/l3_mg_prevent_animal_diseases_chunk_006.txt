The negative importance of endoparasites
Importance of endoparasites
Endoparasites are those parasites that live within the body of the host.
There are four major groups of endoparasites— nematodes,
(PREVENT ANIMAL DISEASES) Page 6 of 24 – Marking Guides

acanthocephalans, Platyhelminthes (trematodes and cestodes), and
protozoans.
Infection with worms is common to occur. Contamination with a few
parasites is unavoidable, not to worry about and can even be useful in
building up resistance to those parasites. However, too many parasites
weaken a goat. The goat is more susceptible to diseases and can even die.
Some parasites also transmit diseases. Production and growth de- cline even
while no symptoms of disease show.
