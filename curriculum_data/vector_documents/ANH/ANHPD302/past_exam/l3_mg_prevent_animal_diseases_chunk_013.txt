Q20.Write a brief description of the following notifiable diseases (etiology/
transmission mode): /5 marks
• Anthrax

Answer /5 marks
A. Anthrax
Definition Anthrax also known as splenic fever, (Charbon bacteridien)
is an acute infectious disease chiefly affecting cattle, sheep, swine,
horses and other animal species including man caused by Bacillus
anthracis and characterized by septicemia, enlargement of the spleen
and hemorrhagic infiltration throughout the body. Probably this is the
most septicemic disease.
Transmission
Most often related to ingestion; herbivorous are often infected while
grazing or browsing, especially when eating rough irritant or spiky
vegetation; the vegetation has been hypothesized to cause wounds
within the gastrointestinal tract allowing entry of bacteria spores.
Indirect transmission by: Fomites – feed sacks; Bone meals; Biting
flies - blood sucking insects; Birds and wildlife spreading straw,
bedding and carcasses.
B. Brucellosis
B.1. Definition Brucellosis is a bacterial infection that spreads from
animals to people most often via unpasteurized milk, cheese and other
daily products. More rarely, the bactia that because brucellosis can

animals; the disease is caused by bacteria of genus brucella.
