Q14. Explain in brief the types of vaccines. /2 marks
Answer/2 marks
Types of vaccines and use
1.Live attenuated vaccines
Some vaccines contain live, attenuated microorganisms. Many of these are
active viruses that have been cultivated under conditions that disable their
virulent properties, or that use closely related but less dangerous organisms
to produce a broad immune response.
2.Killed vaccines
Inactivated or killed vaccines are made from microorganisms (viruses,
bacteria, other) that have been killed through physical or chemical
processes. These killed organisms cannot cause disease.
