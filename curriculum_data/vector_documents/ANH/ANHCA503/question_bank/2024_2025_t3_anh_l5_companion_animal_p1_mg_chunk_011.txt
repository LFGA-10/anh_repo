18. A family adopts a 1-year-old dog. The dog is showing (10marks)
signs of anxiety, including excessive barking, pacing,
and territorial marking inside the house.
a) What factors would you use to determine the
underlying cause of the dog's anxiety? / 5marks
Answer (with marks allocations)
History taking ( anamnesis)
Physical examination
Clinical examination
Laboratory exam
b) How does the dog interact with other animals or
people during or after training? /5marks
A dog’s interaction with other animals or people during or
after training depends on its temperament, socialization,
and training level.
Well-trained and socialized dogs tend to remain calm,
focused, and polite, while dogs with less training or poor
socialization may exhibit reactivity, fear, or overexcitement.
Addressing these behaviors through consistent training,
positive reinforcement, and controlled socialization can
improve the dog’s interactions and overall behavior.

19. Th e shelter has a new batch of rabbits. One staff (10marks)
member suggests cleaning their cages once a week to
save time. Another staff member insists on daily
cleaning. Who is correct, and why?
Answer: The staff member who insists on daily cleaning is
correct.
Explanation: Daily cleaning prevents the buildup of waste,
reduces the risk of disease, and minimizes stress for the
