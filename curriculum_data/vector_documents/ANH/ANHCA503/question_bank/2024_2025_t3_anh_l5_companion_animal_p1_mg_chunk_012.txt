20. A cat is diagnosed with diabetes mellitus and is (10marks)
showing symptoms like increased thirst, frequent
urination, and weight loss.
Question:
1) Determine the possible complications of untreated
diabetes in cats /5marks
2) suggest a treatment plan. /5marks
Answer (2 marks for each )
Untreated diabetes can lead to ketoacidosis, cataracts,
liver disease, and recurrent infections. The treatment plan
includes insulin therapy, a balanced diet, regular
monitoring of blood glucose levels, and follow-up visits to
adjust the treatment as needed.
