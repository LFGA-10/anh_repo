Question: Propose a training plan to prepare the camel for the
expedition.
Answer (3 marks for each)
ANH (Companion animal management) Page 15 of 16 – Marking Guides

1. Build trust with the camel through regular interaction
and positive reinforcement.
2. Gradually introduce the camel to carrying lightweight
loads, increasing the weight over time.
3. Train the camel to respond to basic commands like
"stop," "go," and "kneel."
4. Expose the camel to different terrains and environments
to simulate desert conditions.
5. Monitor the camel's health and behavior throughout the
training process to ensure it is physically and mentally
prepared for the expedition.
