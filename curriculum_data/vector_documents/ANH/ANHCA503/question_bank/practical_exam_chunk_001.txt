Integrated situation
In Musanze city there is a problem of straying dogs and cats especially Busogo sector. This is causing harm
like biting children, other domestic animals and spread of different diseases to the population. Actually 5
dogs and 3 cats of the stray animals are cached alive and withheld in special quarantine room located near
by the sector office. Today morning some of them they have start to show the signs of illness. As veterinary
technician, you are called by the executive secretary of Busogo sector to intervene in problem solving. She
asks you to help in training and treating these animal in order to be used again as guard dogs and
emotional support for cats.
You are asked to propose solutions by demonstration.
Assigned tasks:
 To make the breed identification of 1dog and 1 cat: 20min
 To help in construction of dog and cat’s houses: 1h
 To advise how to keep the companion animals: 20min
 To diagnose and treat their diseases: 40min
 Advise how to prevent companion animal diseases: 20min
All Materials, tool and equipment needed are available.
