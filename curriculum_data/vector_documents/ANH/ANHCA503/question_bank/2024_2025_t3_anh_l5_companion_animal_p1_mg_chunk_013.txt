22. A dog trainer is deciding between using clicker training (10marks)
and the Koehler method.
Question: Evaluate the advantage and disadvantage of
each method and recommend the best approach for
training a new puppy.
Answer (with marks allocations)
Clicker training is positive and effective for teaching new
behaviors but requires consistency and timing. The
Koehler method uses corrections and rewards but may be
too harsh for a puppy. For a new puppy, clicker training is
recommended because it builds trust and encourages
learning through positive experiences.
