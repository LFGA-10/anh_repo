18. A cat owner notices their cat has stopped using the (10marks)
litter box and is eliminating in other areas of the
house.
Question: Analyze the possible reasons for this behavior
and suggest solutions.
Answer ( one mark for each correct answer)
Possible reasons include stress, a dirty litter box, medical
issues, or a dislike of the litter type. Solutions include
cleaning the litter box regularly, providing multiple litter
boxes, using a different type of litter, and consulting a
veterinarian to rule out health problems. Addressing the
underlying cause will help resolve the issue.

19. A horse trainer is deciding between natural mating and (10marks)
artificial insemination for breeding.
Question: Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of
each method and recommend the best option for breeding
equines.
Answer (with marks allocations)
Natural mating allows for natural selection and bonding
but carries risks of injury and disease transmission.
Artificial insemination is safer, reduces disease risk, and
allows for genetic diversity but requires technical expertise
and equipment. For controlled breeding with minimal risk,
artificial insemination is the better option.
