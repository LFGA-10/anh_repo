22. Co mpare the long-term effects of vasectomy and (10marks)
castration in male companion animals in terms of
behavior, health, and population control.
Answer:
• Vasectomy:
o Behavior: Retains testosterone, so male
behaviors (e.g., marking, aggression) may
persist.
o Health: Lower risk of complications compared
to castration.
o Population Control: Effective in preventing
reproduction but does not reduce mating
behaviors.
• Castration:
o Behavior: Reduces testosterone, leading to
decreased aggression and marking.
o Health: May reduce the risk of certain cancers
but has a slightly higher risk of surgical
complications.
o Population Control: Highly effective in
preventing reproduction and reducing mating
behaviors.
Conclusion: Castration is more effective for population
control and behavior modification, while vasectomy is a
less invasive option that retains male behaviors.
