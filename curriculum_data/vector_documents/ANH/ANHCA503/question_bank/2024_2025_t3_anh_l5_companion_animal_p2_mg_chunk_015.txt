23. Yo u are tasked with developing a breeding plan for a pair (15marks)
of dogs. Create a comprehensive plan that includes
selection criteria for parent stock, mating techniques, and
methods for diagnosing pregnancy.
Answer:
Breeding Plan:
1. Selection of Parent Stock:
o Choose dogs with excellent phenotypic
characteristics (e.g., good health, temperament,
and conformation).
o Ensure genotypic characteristics are favorable
by reviewing pedigree and genetic testing for
hereditary diseases.
2. Mating Techniques:
o Use natural mating behavior if both dogs are
healthy and compatible.
o If natural mating is not possible,
consider artificial insemination to ensure
successful breeding.
3. Pregnancy Diagnosis:
o Perform abdominal palpation after 28 days to
detect pregnancy.
o Use echography for a more accurate diagnosis
and to monitor fetal development.
