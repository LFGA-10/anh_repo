A. Cleaning and Disinfection of the Sites
The first step in biosecurity is ensuring that the environment
where companion animals live or are housed is clean and
disinfected. This involves:
➢ Regular Cleaning
➢ Disinfection Protocols
➢ High-Touch Areas
➢ Ventilation
➢ Routine Monitoring
B. Cleaning and Disinfection of Materials, Tools, and
Equipment Used
In addition to cleaning the environment itself, it is vital to
maintain hygiene for all materials and equipment used in caring
for companion animals:
➢ Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
➢ Tools & Equipment Cleaning
➢ Disinfecting Procedures
➢ Storage Practices
➢ Regular Maintenance Checks
C. Disposing Wastes
Proper waste disposal is critical in biosecurity practices:
➢ Waste Segregation
➢ Biological Waste Disposal
➢ Regular Collection Schedule
➢ Sanitization Post-Disposal
