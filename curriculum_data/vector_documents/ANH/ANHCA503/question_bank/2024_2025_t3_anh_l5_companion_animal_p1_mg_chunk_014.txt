23. (15marks)
A veterinarian is designing a new dosage chart for different
medications based on the weight, age, and health status of
companion animals. For a specific medication that is dosed at
3 mg/kg for healthy adult cats, the veterinarian needs to
create a formula that adjusts the dose for cats of different
health statuses. For older cats (7 years and above), the dose
needs to be reduced by 20% due to decreased kidney function.
For young cats (less than 1 year old), the dose should be
increased by 15% to account for faster metabolism. If a 4 kg
healthy adult cat is prescribed this medication,
a. what would be the adjusted dose for an older cat
weighing 4 kg? / 7 marks
A) 9.6 mg
B) 12.0 mg
C) 15.0 mg
D) 13.8 mg
Answer:
A) 9.6 mg
Explanation: First, calculate the normal dose for a healthy
adult cat: 4 kg×3 mg/kg=12 mg
For an old cat, the dose needs to be reduced by 20% :
12mg-(12*0.2)=12mg- 2.4mg= 9.6 mg
