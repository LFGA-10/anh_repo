Answer:
• Feed Preparation: /5marks
o Create species-specific feeding plans based on age,
size, and health status.
o Use high-quality, commercially available pet food.
o For animals with special dietary needs, prepare
meals according to veterinary instructions.
o Create a food storage system that prevents
contamination.
• Feed Distribution: /5marks
o Implement a combination of fixed-amount and
time-restricted feeding for most animals.
o Use ad libitum feeding for small mammals that
require constant access to food.
o Forced feeding will only be utilized under strict
veterinary supervision.
• Water Provision: /5marks
o Provide fresh, clean water at all times using
gravity-fed water dispensers.
o Clean and refill water bowls multiple times daily.
o Provide water fountains for cats to encourage
hydration.
