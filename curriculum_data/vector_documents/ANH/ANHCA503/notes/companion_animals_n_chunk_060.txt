b) Fixed Amount Distribution
In fixed amount distribution, a specific quantity of feed is provided to the
animal at designated feeding times.
Advantages:
Allows for better control over the animal’s diet and caloric intake.
Helps manage weight effectively by preventing overeating.
Easier to monitor food consumption, which is crucial for managing health
conditions.
Disadvantages:
May not meet the needs of highly active animals or those with higher energy
requirements.
Can lead to anxiety or stress in animals that are accustomed to ad libitum
feeding if they feel restricted.
