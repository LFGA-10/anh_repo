Biological Waste Disposal: For biological waste (e.g., feces, urine-soaked
bedding), use designated containers that comply with local regulations
regarding hazardous waste management.
123

Regular Collection Schedule: Establish a regular schedule for waste
collection to minimize accumulation which can attract pests or harbor
pathogens.
Sanitization Post-Disposal: After disposing of wastes, sanitize the area where
waste was stored using appropriate disinfectants to eliminate any residual
pathogens.
Training Staff Members: Ensure all staff members involved in animal care are
trained on proper waste disposal procedures to uphold biosecurity standards
consistently.
By implementing these comprehensive biosecurity measures—cleaning and
disinfecting sites; maintaining hygiene for materials, tools, and equipment; and
ensuring proper waste disposal—owners can significantly reduce the risk of
disease transmission among companion animals.
