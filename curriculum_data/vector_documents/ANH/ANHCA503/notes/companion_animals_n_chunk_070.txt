C. Pregnancy Diagnosis Techniques
Pregnancy diagnosis in companion animals such as dogs, cats, horses,
donkeys, and camels is crucial for effective breeding management. Various
techniques are employed to determine whether an animal is pregnant, each
with its own advantages and limitations. Below are the primary methods used:
1. Recto Palpation
Recto palpation is a manual examination technique primarily used in larger
animals like horses and donkeys. This method involves inserting a gloved hand
into the rectum to feel the reproductive organs. The veterinarian can assess the
size and shape of the uterus and detect any changes indicative of pregnancy.
Advantages:
It allows for direct assessment of the reproductive tract.
