Companion animal breeds
Companion animals have different breeds; each breed has its performance on
which the choice may be based.
A. Dog breeds
19

a) Sheepdogs and cattle dogs:
➢ German shepherd dog
• Origin: Germany
• Size: Bread of large-sized or medium-sized working dog ;
• Weight: the weight standard is 30-40kg for males and 22-32kg for
female;
• Color of skin: The colors are black or black saddle with tan or gold to
light grey.
• Conformation: It is well balanced, with harmonious development of the
forequarter and hindquarter. The dog is longer than tall, deep-bodied,
and presents an outline of smooth curves rather than angles.
• Ear position: ears are standing straight up. But as many shepherd
owners learn, it can take a while for pups to acquire that signature
pointy-eared look.
• Color of eyes: The color of the eyes should blend with the color of the
coat. They should be as dark as possible.
➢ Belgian shepherd dog
• Origin: The origin of Belgian sheepdog (Chien de berger belge) is
Belgium;
• Size: The Belgian shepherd dog is a medium-to-large size dog.
• Shape: it is almond-shaped and the erect ears are triangular in shape.
• Weight: 29 - 34 kg; females 27 - 32 kg.
• Color of skin: it has a long black coat and an imposing appearance.
• Conformation. It is resembling with German shepherd breed save for
the hindlegs. They have domed forehead, a long, square-cut muzzle and
black noses with their ears pointed and fully erect.
