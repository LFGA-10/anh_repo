2. Abdominal Palpation
Abdominal palpation involves feeling the abdomen of the animal to detect signs
of pregnancy. This method is more commonly used in dogs and cats during
later stages of pregnancy when the uterus has enlarged significantly.
Advantages:
Non-invasive and can be performed without specialized equipment.
Useful for detecting larger litters as gestation progresses.
Limitations:
Less effective in early pregnancy when changes may not be palpable.
Requires experience to differentiate between normal abdominal contents and a
pregnant uterus.
3. Echography (Ultrasound)
Echography or ultrasound is a highly effective imaging technique that uses
sound waves to create images of internal structures. It is widely used across
various species, including dogs, cats, horses, donkeys, and camels.
Advantages:
Provides real-time images allowing for accurate detection of fetal heartbeats.
