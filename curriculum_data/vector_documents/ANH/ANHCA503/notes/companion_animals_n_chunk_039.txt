Origin
39

Dromedaries from Arabian Peninsula; Bactrians from Central Asia.
Size
Dromedaries: 1.8 - 2 m; Bactrians: 2 - 2.3 m.
Weight
Dromedaries: 400 - 600 kg; Bactrians: 600 - 1,000 kg.
Color of Skin
Dromedaries: Light tan/brown; Bactrians: Darker shades.
Conformation
Dromedaries have one hump; Bactrians have two humps.
Ear Position
Small ears positioned high on head for both breeds.
Color of Eyes
Large eyes with colors ranging from dark brown to lighter shades.

Housing of companion animals
Housing or caging systems should provide adequate space and accommodate
appropriate population densities, allow animal’s sufficient freedom of
movement. Preventive medicine areas for isolation of sick animals and
quarantine of newly arriving animals should be provided.
