Daily Cleaning: This involves removing waste, uneaten food, and any soiled
bedding from cages or living areas. For litter boxes, it is essential to scoop out
waste at least once a day.
Weekly Deep Cleaning: This includes washing cages or enclosures with pet-
safe disinfectants. It is important to remove all bedding and toys before
cleaning. Surfaces should be scrubbed thoroughly, rinsed well, and dried
completely before reintroducing bedding and toys.
Sanitization: In addition to regular cleaning, periodic sanitization using
appropriate products can help eliminate pathogens. Ensure that any cleaning
agents used are safe for pets.
Ventilation: Proper ventilation in living spaces helps reduce odors and
moisture buildup, which can contribute to mold growth and respiratory issues
in pets.
Monitoring for Pests: Regular inspections for signs of pests such as fleas or
ticks are necessary to maintain a healthy environment.
Bedding
Choosing the right bedding is vital for the comfort and health of companion
animals. Bedding materials should be:
Comfortable: Soft materials like fleece or cotton provide warmth and comfort
for pets.
Washable: Bedding should be machine washable to ensure cleanliness and
hygiene.
Non-toxic: Materials must be free from harmful chemicals that could pose
health risks to animals.
