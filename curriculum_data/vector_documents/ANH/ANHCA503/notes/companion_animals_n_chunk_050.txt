Roofing
The roofing phase protects companion animals from weather elements:
52

Truss Installation: Roof trusses should be installed according to design
specifications ensuring they are properly spaced and secured.
Sheathing Application: Install roof sheathing (plywood or oriented strand
board) over trusses providing a solid base for roofing materials.
Underlayment Installation: A water-resistant underlayment should be applied
over sheathing before installing shingles or other roofing materials to provide
an extra layer of protection against leaks.
Roof Covering Application: Depending on design choices (asphalt shingles,
metal roofing), install roofing materials ensuring proper overlap and sealing at
joints to prevent water ingress.
Ventilation Considerations: Ensure adequate ventilation systems are
integrated into the roof design to maintain air quality within the house for
companion animals.
Final Inspection: Conduct thorough inspections post-roof installation
checking for any potential leaks or structural weaknesses.
Finishing
The finishing stage focuses on making the interior suitable for companion
animals:
Interior Walls & Insulation: Install walls using non-toxic materials suitable
for pets; insulation should also be added between walls for temperature
control.
Flooring Selection: Choose flooring that is durable and easy to clean (e.g., tile
or sealed concrete) while being safe for animals (non-slip surfaces).
