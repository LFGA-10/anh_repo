B. Companion Animals House Construction Process Supervision
Constructing a house specifically designed for companion animals involves
several critical steps, each requiring careful supervision to ensure the structure
is safe, comfortable, and functional. Below is a detailed breakdown of the
construction process supervision focusing on four main areas: Levelling,
Elevation, Roofing, and Finishing.
Levelling
The first step in constructing a house for companion animals is levelling the
ground where the structure will be built. This process involves:
Site Assessment: Before any construction begins, it is essential to assess the
site for drainage patterns, soil type, and existing vegetation. This assessment
helps determine if additional grading or excavation is necessary.
Clearing the Site: Remove any debris, rocks, or vegetation that may interfere
with the foundation.
