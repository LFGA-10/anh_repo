g) Cage cards:
Each cat should have a cage card with all the information necessary to care for

Include the cat’s name, approximate age, sex and sterilization status, breed,
origin (surrender or stray), health information, and adoption fee. Note that an
individual record should be prepared for each animal. Records should include
a description of the animal, the date obtained, the source, the length of time
held, and any treatment provided together with its final disposition. Individual
animals should be identified in a consistent and recordable manner (e.g., tags,
cage cards, microchips, tattoos).
