➢ Eating habits
As herbivores, donkeys graze on grass and hay. Foods such as alfalfa and lush
spring grass can cause a donkey to get fat and develop a fat roll on its neck
which it will mostly likely have for the rest of its life.
D: Camels
Common Characteristics of Camels
Metabolism
Camels have a unique metabolism that allows them to survive in arid
environments. They can store fat in their humps, which serves as an energy
reserve when food is scarce. This fat can be metabolized into water and
energy, allowing camels to go for long periods without food or water. Their
metabolic rate can also adjust based on environmental conditions, enabling
them to conserve energy during extreme heat.
Temperature Regulation
Camels possess remarkable adaptations for temperature regulation. They
can tolerate body temperature fluctuations from 34°C to 41.7°C (93°F to
107°F) without sweating, which helps minimize water loss. During the
hottest parts of the day, camels can allow their body temperature to rise,
reducing the need for evaporative cooling through sweating.
Their thick fur insulates them against both heat and cold, providing
protection from extreme temperatures.
