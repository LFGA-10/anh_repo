3. Contextual Factors
Behavioral changes should also be assessed within context. Factors that might
influence behavior include:
Environmental Changes: New pets, moving homes, or alterations in routine can
affect an animal’s behavior.
Age-related Changes: Older animals may exhibit different behaviors due to age-
related health issues such as arthritis or cognitive dysfunction syndrome
(CDS).
Stressors: Loud noises, new family members, or other stress-inducing
situations can lead to temporary behavioral changes.
4. Monitoring Duration and Severity
It is crucial to monitor how long these behavioral changes persist and their
severity:
If a change lasts more than 24 hours without improvement, it warrants further
investigation.
The intensity of the change should also be considered; minor fluctuations may
not be concerning but significant shifts could indicate serious health issues.
