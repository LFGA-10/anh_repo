Digestive System
The digestive system of camels is highly specialized for processing low-
nutrient forage found in desert environments. They possess a three-
chambered stomach that allows for fermentation of plant materials by
microbes before digestion occurs in the intestines. This adaptation
maximizes nutrient absorption from tough plant fibers.
Urinary System
Camels have an efficient urinary system designed to conserve water
effectively. Their kidneys are capable of producing highly concentrated
urine, minimizing water loss even when dehydrated. Additionally, they can
reabsorb water from their urine before excretion.
Respiratory System
The respiratory system of camels is adapted for hot climates; they have large
nasal passages that help cool inhaled air before it reaches the lungs while
warming exhaled air before it leaves the body—this process conserves
moisture during respiration. Camels can also tolerate high levels of carbon
dioxide in their blood due to their ability to withstand dehydration.
