Providing Anti-stress
Stress management is critical for companion animals as stress can lead to
behavioral issues and health problems. To create a stress-free environment:
Safe Space: Provide a designated area where pets can retreat when they feel
overwhelmed. This could be a quiet room or a cozy corner with their favorite
bedding.
Enrichment Activities: Engage pets with toys, puzzles, and interactive
playtime to stimulate their minds and reduce boredom-related stress.
Routine Establishment: Animals thrive on routine; consistent feeding times,
walks, and play sessions help them feel secure.
Calming Products: Consider using calming sprays or diffusers containing
pheromones designed specifically for pets (e.g., Feliway for cats). These
products can help reduce anxiety levels in some animals.
Social Interaction: Spend quality time with your pet through gentle handling,
petting, or simply being present in their space; this fosters trust and reduces
anxiety.
