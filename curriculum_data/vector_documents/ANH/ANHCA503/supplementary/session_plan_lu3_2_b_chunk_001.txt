	Topic of the session: Training Equines and Camels: Application of Techniques for Effective Management

Range:

  Foundations of training

  Training techniques

  Equine training:

Basic handling (leading, tying, grooming).

Groundwork and obedience.

Behavioral correction and safety practices.

  Camel training:

Handling and restraint methods.

Training for riding, carrying loads, and obedience.

Managing temperament and stress.

  Application of training tools

  Animal welfare considerations

  Practical demonstrations

  Challenges and solutions.

Duration of the session: 120mins

Objectives: At the end of this session the learner should be able to:

Explain the principles of animal training and behavior modification.

Apply appropriate training techniques to equines and camels.

Demonstrate practical skills in handling, guiding, and reinforcing desired behaviors.

Assess the effectiveness of training methods in improving animal welfare and performance.

Relate training practices to safe management and human–animal interaction.
