Smart Collars and Activity Trackers
Smart collars and activity trackers are wearable devices designed to monitor
various aspects of a pet’s health and activity levels. These devices often track
metrics such as steps taken, distance traveled, sleep patterns, and even heart
rate.
100

Many smart collars connect to mobile applications that provide insights into
the pet’s daily activities and overall health trends over time. This data can help
owners make informed decisions regarding exercise needs and potential health
issues.
Smart Food and Water Bowls
Smart feeding systems have emerged as another innovative way to monitor
companion animal behavior. These bowls can dispense food at scheduled times
or in specific portions based on the pet’s dietary needs. Some models also
feature built-in scales that track food intake or water consumption levels. By
monitoring how much food or water a pet consumes, owners can gain insights
into their eating habits, which may indicate changes in health or stress levels.
Automatic Litter Box Systems for Cats
For cat owners, automatic litter box systems offer an efficient solution for
monitoring feline behavior while maintaining cleanliness in the home. These
systems automatically scoop waste after the cat uses the litter box, reducing
odor and mess for the owner. Some advanced models also include sensors that
track how often a cat uses the litter box, providing valuable information about
urinary habits or potential health issues such as urinary tract infections.
