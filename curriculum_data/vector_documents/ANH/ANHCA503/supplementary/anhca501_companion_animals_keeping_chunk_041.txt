H. Tennessee walking horse
Picture 29: Tennessee walking horse
Characteristics:
Origin: Tennessee, USA
Size: 146-174.5cm
Weight: 404-544kg
Colour of skin: the colour may be black, bay, chestnut, white.
Ear position: well-shaped and pointed ears
Colour of eyes: blue eyes
Conformation: neat head, clear and alert eyes and a tapered muzzle. The neck should be long and
graceful and the shoulders muscular and well sloping. The back should be short with good coupling at the
loins. The animal should be deep in the girth and well ribbed and the chest should be of good proportion
and width. The croup should be generally sloping and the hips well-muscled with muscular development
extending down toward the hocks. The legs should be flat and cordy.
