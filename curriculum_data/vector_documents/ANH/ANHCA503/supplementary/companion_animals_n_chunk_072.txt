4. Hormone Dosing
Hormone dosing involves measuring specific hormones associated with
pregnancy, such as progesterone levels. This method can indicate whether an
animal is pregnant based on hormonal changes that occur after conception.
Advantages:
Can provide definitive results regarding pregnancy status through blood tests.
Useful for monitoring hormone levels throughout gestation.
Limitations:
Requires laboratory facilities for testing.
Hormonal fluctuations can sometimes lead to false positives or negatives if not
interpreted correctly.
In summary, each pregnancy diagnosis technique has its unique applications
depending on the species involved and the stage of gestation being assessed.
The choice of method often depends on factors such as availability of
resources, expertise required, timing within the gestational period, and specific
needs related to breeding management
