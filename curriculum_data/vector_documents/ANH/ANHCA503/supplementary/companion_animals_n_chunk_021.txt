• color of skin: Dachshunds have a wide color variety.
Solid colors are: black, red (from strawberry blond to deep auburn),

• Conformation. A typical dachshund is long-bodied and muscular, with
short, stubby legs. Its front paws are unusually large and it has deep
chest that provides increased lung capacity for stamina when hunting
prey underground. Its snout is long with an increased nose area that
absorbs odors. The Dachshund's body is longer than it is tall.
It has an elongated head and a slight, convex skull that is arched with
protruding eyebrows. The muzzle is long. The jaw is robust and teeth
should meet in a scissors bite.
• Ear position: The mobile ears hang long on its cheeks.
• Color of eyes: are dark red or brown-black some time green eyes.
Long coated dachshunds have a silky coat and short featherings on legs
