B. Preparation of Companion Animal Feeds:
Feed Distribution Techniques
When preparing feeds for companion animals such as dogs, cats, horses,
donkeys, and camels, it is essential to consider various feed distribution
techniques. Each method has its advantages and disadvantages depending on
the animal’s needs, behavior, and health status. Below are detailed
descriptions of four primary feed distribution techniques.
a) Ad Libitum Distribution
Ad libitum feeding refers to providing animals with free access to food at all
times. This method allows animals to eat whenever they feel hungry without
restrictions.
Advantages:
It can be beneficial for animals that require constant energy intake or have
high metabolic rates.
