Step 1: forming  group

Trainer’s activity:

Gives  instructions concerning how to form home  and expert group by using jig saw

Asks learner to count from one to three

Forms four home  groups

Gives instruction to learner concerning to form three expert groups by seat in group according to the number counted (e.g one ,two ,three)

Asks the learner to go to his or her expert group

Learner’s activity:

Listen to the instructions

Counts one to three

Forms  four home groups

Forms expert group according to the number counted in home group

Goes to his/her expert group

Step 2:group discussion

Trainer’s activity

Gives instructions to the learner about how to discuss on the task in expert group

Gives to the learner activity in expert group

Asks learner to read and discuss on tasks given by trainer in expert group according to time set by trainer

Involves in expert group activity by ask learner if there is no problem or ask learner some questions and taking summary in his or her notebook
