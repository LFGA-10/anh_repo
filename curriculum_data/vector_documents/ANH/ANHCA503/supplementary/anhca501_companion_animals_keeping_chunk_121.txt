Application of newer human birth control agents, such as carbergoline and mifepristone, in pets is

birth control with minimal side effects.
A.3. The best method for preventing pregnancy of the bitch or queen
The best current nonpermanent method for preventing pregnancy in the bitch or queen is simply to
prevent breeding (copulation) by confining the individual indoors, away from intact (un-neutered) males.
