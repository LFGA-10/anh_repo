 Topic 5: Euthanasia of companion animals
Euthanasia is defined as an easy, painless death. In regard to animals, euthanasia is the act of killing an
animal in a human manner.
The primary objectives of animal euthanasia are:
 Relieving pain and suffering of the animal to be euthanized;
 Minimizing the pain ,anxiety, distress and the fear the animal experiences before consciousness
is lost;
 Inducing a painless and distress-free death.
