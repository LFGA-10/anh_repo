Frontline Plus for Cats
Frontline Plus is used for the control of fleas and ticks in cats, killing fleas within 24 hours of application
and ticks within 48 hours.
The medication is applied topically to the cat’s back at the shoulder blades, which then spreads by means
of your pet’s body oils.
