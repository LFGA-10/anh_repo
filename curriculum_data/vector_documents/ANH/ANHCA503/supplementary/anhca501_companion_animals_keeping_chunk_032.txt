D. Abyssinian cats
Picture 15: Abyssinian cats
Origin: Probably Egypt;
Size: medium-sized cat;
Weight: 3-4kg for male and 2-3kg for female;
Colour of coat: different colours from blue to lilac to red, the dense, silky fur of the Abyssinian was
originally silver or fawn in colour. The tail may be tipped with black.
Conformation: It has a long and muscular yet slender body and a relatively short tail.
Ear position: the ears are large, alert and moderately pointed, broad at the base and well cupped.
Color of eyes: Accepted eye colors are gold, green or hazel.
