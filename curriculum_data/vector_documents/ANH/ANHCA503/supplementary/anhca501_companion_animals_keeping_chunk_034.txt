G. Ragdoll cats
Picture 18: Ragdoll cats
Origin: Ragdoll is believed to have originated in California
Weight: Female: 3.6 to 6.8 kg, males are substantially larger, ranging from 5.4 to 9.1 kg or more.
Color: Ragdolls are born white and don't obtain their full colour until 2 years of age.
The main colors in the Ragdoll are seal, blue, chocolate, lilac, red and cream. Bicolor - white inverted "V"
in the facial mask, white legs, feet, ruff and stomach, point colored ears and tail. Colour point - facial
mask, ears, feet, tail and nose. A colour point has no white.
Conformation. They have large usually oval eyes, a long bushy tail and a long muscular body with
medium legs and large round paws and is large in size
Ear position: The ears are medium-size and feature rounded tips.
Colour of eyes: large blue eyes
