L O 3.2 – Feed companion animals
Feeding is an important aspect in the management of any farm animal. It requires knowledge of the
feeding behavior and nutrient requirements of animals for specific production functions.
The following elements should be considered:
 Feeders must allow easy access to food, and dirtying by urine and feces must be prevented,
 Food must be available in sufficient amounts to provide for normal growth, and maintenance of
normal body weight, reproduction, and lactation;
 Areas where food is prepared or stored must be kept clean, cool, dry, and free of vermin,
preferably off the floor on pallets, racks, or carts;
 Storage time should be minimized and the manufacturer's recommendations for proper storage
followed to preserve nutritional quality and prevent contamination.
 Animals must have access to fresh, potable, uncontaminated drinking water.
