A.10. Leptospirosis
Systemic antibiotic therapy by penicillin and its derivatives.
Vaccination with polyvalent inactivated vaccines;
Avoidance of exposure to free-ranging wildlife and domestic animals (rodents and rancoon).
A.11. Hepatitis
Once a dog has become infected with CAV-1 there is no treatment that will destroy the virus. The best
one can do is to support the dog with good nursing care, intravenous fluids and medicines to lighten
the workload on the liver. Antibiotics are also included in treatment to prevent additional infections.
