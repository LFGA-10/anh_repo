Credits: 8 Learning hours: 80
Sector: Agriculture and Food processing
Sub-sector: Animal health
Module Note Issue date: June, 2020
Purpose statement
This module is provided for the trainees of level five and describes the skills, knowledge and
attitudes required to perform companion animals keeping especially dogs, cats and equine
species.
Moreover, this module will enable the trainee to describe companion animal species and breeds;
house companion animals; keep companion animals and control specific diseases of companion
animals.
