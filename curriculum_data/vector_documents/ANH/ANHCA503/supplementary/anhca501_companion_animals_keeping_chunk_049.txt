A. Expected use
The type of horse purchased determines how easily it can perform the intended use:
 A quiet horse will work for a trail and pleasure horse as long as it is sound
 A relaxed, mannerly horse that has a prompt, flat-footed walk will be best for trail riding.
 Western horses tend to be lower headed, quiet, and most often heavier muscled being
purchased for show purposes and
 Hunters (horses) have longer, flatter strides and move forward with more impulsion and a higher
head carriage are being purchased for show purposes.

B. Soundness (physically capable of performing)
Horses must be sound enough to perform the expected activities. Horses that are lame in their
movement may have permanent problems that will limit the performance or make it vicious to use the
horse. Horses should also be sound in their breathing, vision, and reproductive capacity if for breeding.
C. Breed of horse
The breed will dictate to some extent the activities and performance abilities of the horse. These horses
lend themselves to the conformation and action to do well:
 Thoroughbreds are designed for high-speed sprints, so they are hunters
 Arabians for endurance racing,
 Quarter horses for agility and bursts of speed and they are also hunters
 Standardbreds for trotting and pacing.
D. Colour
The color of the horse has nothing to do with disposition, performance ability, or soundness. Color is,
however, a significant determining factor in many people's decision.
