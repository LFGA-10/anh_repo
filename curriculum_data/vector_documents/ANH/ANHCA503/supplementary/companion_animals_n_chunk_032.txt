Morgan horse
32

Characteristics:
• Origin: United states (Massachusetts );
• Size:145-157cm
• Weight: average weight 450kgs
• colour of skin: Red and black are the base calors; bay and broun are
modification of black
• Conformation: The head should be expressive with broad forehead; large
prominent eyes; with straight or slightly dished short face .The neck
should come out on top of an extremely well-angulated shoulder with
depth from top of withers to point of shoulder.
• Colour of eyes: blue eyes (it is not unusual to have a chestnut, bay,
black, or grey with one blue eye, often marbled, where the eye is only
partially blue.
Tennessee walking horse
Characteristics:
• Origin: Tennessee, USA
• Size:146-174.5cm
• Weight:404-544kg
• colour of skin: the colour may be black, bay, chestnut, white.
• Conformation: neat head, clear and alert eyes and a tapered muzzle.
The neck should be long and graceful andthe shoulders muscular and
well sloping. The back should be short with good coupling at the loins.
The animal should be deep in the girth and well ribbed and the chest
should be of good proportion and width. The croup should be generally
sloping and the hips well muscled with muscular development extending
down toward the hocks. The legs should be flat and cordy.
