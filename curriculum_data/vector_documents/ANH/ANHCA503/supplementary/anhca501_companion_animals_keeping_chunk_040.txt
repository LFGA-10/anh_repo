Picture 28: Morgan horse
Characteristics:
Origin: United State (Massachusetts);
Size: 145-157cm
Weight: average weight 450kgs
Colour of skin: Red and black are the base colour; bay and brown are modification of black
Colour of eyes: blue eyes (it is not unusual to have a chestnut, bay, black, or grey with one blue eye,
often marbled, where the eye is only partially blue.
Conformation: The head should be expressive with broad forehead; large prominent eyes; with straight
or slightly dished short face .The neck should come out on top of an extremely well-angulated shoulder
with depth from top of withers to point of shoulder.
