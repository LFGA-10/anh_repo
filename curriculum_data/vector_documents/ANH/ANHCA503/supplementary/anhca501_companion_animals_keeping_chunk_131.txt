L O 4.2 – Treat specific disease of companion animals
 Topic 1: Selection of appropriate treatment
A. Dogs and cats
A.1. Rabies
Vaccination
Restriction of dogs and cats movements and elimination of stray animals;
Dog registration;
Notification of suspected cases; Reduction of contacts rates between susceptible dogs by leash
laws.
A.2. Canine distemper
No specific treatment; immunization of pups
A.3. Diabete mellitus
The treatment is different for patients with uncomplicated diabetes and those with
ketoacidosis. Ketoacidotic diabetics are treated with intravenous fluids and rapid acting insulin. This
treatment is continued until the pet is no longer vomiting and is eating, then the treatment is the same
as for uncomplicated diabetes.
A.4. Gout
Allopurinal for gout, which reduces the amount of uric acid in the blood but includes side effects such
as cramping, vomiting and diarrhea.
For a natural, at home remedy, apple cider vinegar is sometimes recommended.
