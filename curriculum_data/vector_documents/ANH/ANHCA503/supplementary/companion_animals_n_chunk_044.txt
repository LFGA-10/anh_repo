3. **Bedding Quality**:

- The cleanliness of bedding plays a significant role in camel health. Dirty
bedding has been linked to increased disease prevalence among camels.
Maintaining clean bedding not only promotes good health but also enhances
comfort.
4. **Water and Feeding Facilities**:
- Access to clean water and adequate feeding space is critical for maintaining
good health and preventing competition among animals. Water points should
ideally be shaded to prevent overheating, and feeding spaces should allow each
camel sufficient room to eat without stress.
5. **Management Practices**:
- The experience level of caretakers influences the quality of care provided to
camels. Well-experienced caretakers are more likely to implement effective
management practices that enhance animal welfare.
6. **Social Structure Considerations**:
- Camels are herd animals that thrive in stable social groups. Overcrowding
can lead to increased aggression and stress; thus, careful consideration should
be given to the composition of herds based on age, sex, and social dynamics.
7. **Environmental Conditions**:
- The local climate must also be taken into account when designing camel
housing. Structures should provide protection from extreme weather conditions
while allowing for natural ventilation.
8. **Behavioral Enrichment**:
- Providing opportunities for natural behaviors through environmental
enrichment can improve the psychological well-being of camels housed in
captivity.
