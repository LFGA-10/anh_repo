c) Mixing Process
The mixing process is vital to ensure uniformity in the feed formulation:
Ingredient Preparation: All ingredients must be weighed accurately according
to the formulated recipe to ensure proper nutrient balance.
Dry Mixing: Dry ingredients should be mixed first using a horizontal mixer or
paddle mixer to achieve an even distribution of powders before adding wet
ingredients or fats.
Incorporation of Fats/Oils: If fats or oils are used in the formulation, they
should be added gradually while mixing to ensure they coat the dry ingredients
evenly without clumping.
Moisture Addition (if applicable): In some cases where moist feeds are
required (like pelleted feeds), water may be added at this stage carefully
controlled to avoid excess moisture which can lead to spoilage.
Pelleting/Extrusion (if applicable): For certain formulations intended for
specific animal types or conditions (like senior pets), pelleting or extrusion
processes may be employed which involve heating the mixture under pressure
followed by shaping it into pellets or kibble form.
