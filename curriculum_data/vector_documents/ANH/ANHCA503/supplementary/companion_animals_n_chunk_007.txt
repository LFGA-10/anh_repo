The senses
Cats have the same 5 senses as people do but to very different degrees.
❖ Sight
Cats have keen vision; they can see much more detail than dogs. Within retina
cats have type of cell called a cone gives cats excellent visual acuity and
binocular vision and also is responsible for color vision, it is uncertain whether
cats can see colors. This allows them to judge speed and distance very well, an
ability that helped them survive as hunters. Like dogs, cats also have a lot of
the retinal cells called rods, which are good at collecting dim light. In fact, cats
can see 6 times better in dim light than people, giving rise to the myth that cats
can see in the dark. Cats also have a reflective layer called the tapetum
lucidum, which magnifies incoming light and lends a characteristic blue or
greenish glint to their eyes at night. Cats have nictitating membrane also called
the third eyelid.
