5. Seeking Veterinary Advice
If significant behavioral changes are observed that last beyond a short period,
it is advisable to consult with a veterinarian. They can perform a thorough
examination and possibly recommend diagnostic tests to determine underlying
health conditions.
Conclusion

Regular observation of companion animals’ behaviors is vital for early detection
of potential health problems. By understanding what constitutes normal
behavior and being vigilant about any deviations from this norm—considering
environmental factors and monitoring duration—pet owners can play an
essential role in maintaining their pets’ overall health.
