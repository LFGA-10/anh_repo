Bactrian Camels: Heavier than their Dromedary counterparts, weighing
between 600 to 1,000 kg (1,320 to 2,200 lbs).
Color of Skin
Dromedary Camels: Their skin color ranges from light tan or beige to dark
brown.
Bactrian Camels: They typically have a darker coat that can vary from light
brown to dark brown or even black.
Conformation
Dromedary Camels: Characterized by a long neck and a single prominent
hump that stores fat.
Bactrian Camels: Have a stockier build with two humps that also serve as fat
storage; they possess a broader body compared to Dromedaries.
Ear Position
Both breeds have relatively small ears compared to their body size. The ears of
both Dromedary and Bactrian camels are positioned high on their heads but
can be slightly more rounded in Bactrians.
Color of Eyes
Both species typically have large eyes with thick eyelashes that protect against
sand and dust. The eye color can range from dark brown to lighter shades
depending on individual genetics.
