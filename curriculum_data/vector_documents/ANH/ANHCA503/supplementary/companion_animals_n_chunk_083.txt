Clicker training uses no physical compulsion or corrections and uses almost
entirely positive reinforcements. Some clicker trainers use mild corrections
such as a "non reward marker"; an Uhuh or whoops to let the dog know that
the behavior is not correct, or corrections such as a "Time out" where attention
is removed from the dog.
B. Cats training purposes
Cat training is the process of modifying a domestic cat's behavior for
entertainment or companionship purposes. There are various methods for
training cats which employ different balances between reward and
punishment.
House training
✓ Litter box training
Many cats and kittens will instinctively use a litter box without needing to be
taught, because of their instincts to eliminate in dirt or sand. Therefore, with a
new kitten, owners usually need to simply show the kitten where the litter box
is located, and how to get in and out. On occasion, some training is required to
help a new cat adjust to a litter box, or to help an older cat that suddenly stops
using the litter box.
✓ Inappropriate elimination
