Artificial Insemination
Artificial insemination (AI) is a reproductive technology used across various
companion animal species to enhance breeding efficiency and genetic diversity.
Process Overview: AI involves collecting sperm from a male animal and
introducing it into the reproductive tract of a female without natural
copulation. This method allows breeders to select desirable traits from different
genetic lines without requiring both animals to be present at the same location.
Sperm Collection: For dogs and cats, sperm is usually collected using an
artificial vagina or electro-ejaculation techniques under veterinary supervision.
For larger animals like horses and camels, collection methods may involve
specialized equipment designed for their anatomy.
Semen Evaluation: After collection, semen is evaluated for motility
(movement), morphology (shape), and concentration before being processed for
storage or immediate use.
Insemination Techniques:
For dogs and cats, AI can be performed transcervically or surgically depending
on the situation.
In horses, insemination can be done either fresh or frozen-thawed semen
directly into the uterus.
Donkeys follow similar procedures as horses due to anatomical similarities.
