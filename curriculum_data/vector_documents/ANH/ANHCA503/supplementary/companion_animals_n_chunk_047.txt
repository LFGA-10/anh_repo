Space Allowance
The size of the enclosure is crucial for camel welfare. A single camel requires a
minimum space of 5 x 10 meters, while a group consisting of one male and two
females should have at least 10 x 20 meters. This space allows for grazing,
exercise, and social interactions among camels, which are herd animals by
nature
