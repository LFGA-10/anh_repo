Prostatic hyperplasia
Etiology: Androgen in stimulation or altered androgen/estrogen ratio
Clinical signs: Clinical signs may be absent, or tenesmus, persistent or
intermittent hematuria and bleeding may occur.
Treatment and prevention: Medroxyprogesterone acetate may be useful.
Toxoplasmosis
Etiology: Toxoplasma gondii
Clinical signs: Usually subclinical illness in adults animals; In young
animals(puppies and kitten) the signs include fever, diarrhea, cough, dyspnea,
icterus, seizures and deaths.
Treatment and prevention: Sulfadiazine(15-25mg/kg) and
pyrimethamine(0.44mg/kg) act synegically; clindamycin is the treatment of
choice, administered PO every 12 hours for 3–4 weeks at 10–12.5 mg/kg in
dogs and 25–50 mg/kg in cats. As toxoplasmosis zoonosis; the hands of people
handling meat should be washed thoroughly with soap and water because
T.gondi in meat are killed by contact with soap and water.
