The average litter size is 3 to 5 kittens, but a young mother, or a first time mother can expect to have
around 2 to 3 kittens. At 3 to 4 years old a cat will be more likely to have around 4 to 5 kittens per litter,

smaller litters as the womb becomes a less hospitable place. Cats can have up to 3 litters per year.
 Reproduction in horses
The male parent of a horse, a stallion, is commonly known as the sire and the female parent, the mare, is
called the dam. Both are genetically important, as each parent provides half of the genetic makeup of the
ensuing offspring, called a foal.
C.1. Estrous cycle
The estrous cycle (also spelled oestrous) generally occurs during the spring and summer months,
although some mares may be sexually receptive into the late fall, and is controlled by the, the cycle first
triggered when the days begin to lengthen. The estrous cycle lasts about 19–22 days, with the average
being 21 days. As the days shorten, the mare returns to a period when it is not sexually receptive, known
as anestrus.
This cycle contains 2 phases:
 Estrus or follicular phase: 5–7 days in length, when the mare is sexually receptive to a stallion.
Estrogen is secreted by the follicle. Ovulation occurs in the final 24–48 hours of estrus.
 Diestrus or luteal phase: 14–15 days in length, the mare is not sexually receptive to the stallion.
The corpus luteum secretes progesterone.
