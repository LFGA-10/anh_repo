C.3. Foaling
Mares due to foal are usually separated from other horses, separation allows the mare to be monitored
more closely by humans for any problems that may occur while giving birth. Originally, this was due in
part to a need for protection from the harsh winter climate present when mares foal early in the year,
but even in moderate climates, foaling stalls are still common because they allow closer monitoring of
mares.
In the milder climates seen in much of the southern hemisphere, most mares foal outside, often in a
paddock built specifically for foaling, especially on the larger stud farms.
