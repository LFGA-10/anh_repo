3.1: SELECT SILO SITE
silo, in agriculture is airtight structure that encloses and protects silage
3.1.1. SILO SITE SELECTION CRITERIA
✓ Soil structure
✓ Land slope
✓ Methods of conservation
3.1.2. TYPES OF SILOS
• For a flat surface
✓ Surface silo: are the piles of hay that are placed on a flat soil and
which consist of packing forage on the ground and covering it with
plastic sheeting without any border, used for a small herd.
Advantages of surface silo
It is cheaper
It does not require any investment for the construction of
edges
It can be easily expanded
Disadvantages of surface silo
The silage quality is lower (packing is difficult to do)
✓ Pit silo: it is a cylindrical hole made in the ground. Walls can be
made of well packed or in bricks. Here, one (1) cubic meter of the
silo can have 650 to 700 kg settled silage.
Advantages of pit silo
Low cost only for digging
Good compaction
