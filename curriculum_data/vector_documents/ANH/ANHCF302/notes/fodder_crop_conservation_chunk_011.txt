young animals have particular needs in relation to food texture
and nutritional composition, since the digestive system works
differently at these stages.To improve digestive efficiency in
animal nutrition, specific ingredients should ideally be chosen
for formulations intended for adults and young, with the
perpetual aim of improving nutrient absorption and assuring
high palatability.
L U3: PERFORM SILAGE MAKING
Silage making is a method of preserving forage through wet method through
anaerobic lactic fermentation. The stopping of the forage degradation is got
through the combination of the lack of oxygen and a sufficient acidity, which
blocks the activity of various bacteria and fungi.
Depending on the various techniques, we get a fodder with a PH below 4 whose
the moisture content varies from 50% to 85%. Generally, the higher the dry
matter, the more difficult is to get the required anaerobiosis to start lactic
fermentation.
Hewever, we should ensure thet the grass to ensile is rich in sugar and relatively
low in nitrogen. It is thues difficult to achieve with some forages such as alfalfa,
low in sugar and high in soluble nitrogen.
