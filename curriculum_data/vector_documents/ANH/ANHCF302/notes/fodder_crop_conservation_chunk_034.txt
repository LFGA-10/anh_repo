Disposing of the bad quality silage
Causes of bad silage
• Poor compression or high residual oxygen content after preparation
• Too long cut
• Insufficient covering and closure of the silo
• Fodder crop composition
Feeding silage
Calculation of the quantities of silage and hays
Tips to calculate amount of silage and hays to feed animals
Silage is fed to dairy cows according to their body weight or 6-15kgs per cow per
day. Feeding should be done 3 hours before milking or fed after milking to avoid
the milk picking the silage smell. The feeding troughs should be washed after
feeding silage to avoid contamination and infection of the dairy cows.
Cows will voluntarily consume about 2 percent of their body weight or 24 pounds
per day.
1 pound=0.453 kg
• Make sure you remove all spoiled silage and do not feed it to your animals.
• Be very careful when removing silage from an earth pit; avoid feeding soil
residues to your animals.

• Feed any concentrates at the same time as when you are feeding the silage.
