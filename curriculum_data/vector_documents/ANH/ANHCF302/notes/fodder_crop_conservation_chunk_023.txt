3.7. Utilize silage additives
3.7.1. Objectives of silage additives
➢ Control the preservation process

➢ Fodder Crops quality improvement
➢ To improve nutrient composition of silage
➢ To reduce storage losses by promoting rapid fermentation
➢ To reduce fermentation losses by limiting extent of fermentation
➢ To increase aerobic stability
3.7.2. Calculation of silage additives amount
At least 40 litres of molasses can be added to 1.5 tonnes of silage. At the same
time, 20 ltres of the molasses have to be diluted by 40 litres of water to reduce
the sugar concentration. The silage can be removed after 45-60 days of
ensiling for feeding the animals.
3.8: Fill and compress silo
You will need to fill the silo and compact it as quickly as possible, and without
interruptions. Never leave the silo uncovered for more than a day.
3.8.1. Steps of silo filling
✓ Before you start filling, spread the plastic on the outer edge of the floor
and the walls and let it hang over the sides so you can cover the silo
later.
✓ Doposit the forage at the beginning of the silo and spread the forage in
a thin layer (max 15cm) throughout the silo.
✓ Compact the layer and repeat the process until the silo is filled.
3.9: Cover and seal silo
You will need to cover the silo immediately after completing the filling and
compacting. The cover needs to be made of airtight material to avoid
exposure to air as much as possible. A plastic sheet is the standard type
of cover used.
