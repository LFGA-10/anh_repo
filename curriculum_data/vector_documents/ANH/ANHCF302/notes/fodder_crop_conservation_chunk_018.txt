3.2.7. Advantages of silage
✓ It is wet forage that keeps its nutritive value or qualities.
✓ Silage can be easily made in wet season when the forages are more.
✓ It decreases the cost of feeds
✓ It lowers harvest losses
✓ It increases the forage quality
✓ Silage also can reduce labor needs through greater mechanization of
harvesting and feeding.
3.2.8. risk of silage
✓ Risk of botulism: it is related to the possible presence of dead animals in
plant bales or in the silo.
✓ Risk of intoxication: this is caused by fungal or bacteria toxins. It is a
risk for animals that consume the products of a poorly made silage. For
example, aflatoxins.
✓ Risk of listeria: in case of a poorly acidified silage which allows the
bacteria development including in dairy livestock farming, especially when
we produce cheese made from milk.
