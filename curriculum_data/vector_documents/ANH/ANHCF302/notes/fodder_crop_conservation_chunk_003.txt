1.2: SELECT FODDER HARVESTING AND CONSERVATION MATERIALS
1.2.1. SELECTION CRITERIA FOR FODDER HARVESTING MATERIALS
✓ Harvesting time: Leguminous fodder crops should be harvested at their
flower initiation stage or when crown buds start to grow, while grasses
should be harvested at their pre-flowering or flower initiation stage for hay.
High (or medium) quality silage is obtained from high (or medium) quality
grasses and fodders containing between 15-35 % dry matter. This is found
at the dough stage in maize, at flowering in sorghum, milk stage in oats
and at flowering stage in most of the grasses. Partial wilting of legumes is
necessary to reduce the water concentration to about 70%.
