NB: for making good silage, we must prevent the growth of mold and some
bacteria by making anaerobic and very acidic conditions (PH less than 4).
This can be done by creating favorable conditions for the development of lactic
bacteria, while preventing the development of butyric bacteria (PH greater than
5).
Calculating the amount of silage required
The amount of silage required and therefore the size of the silo, depends on the
following:
✓ Number and type of livestock to feed
✓ Length of the feeding period
✓ Percentage of silage in the full ration
✓ Water content of the silage
✓ Losses in percentage
✓ Density of the silage
3.2.4. Steps followed for silage making
❖ Cut or harvest the fodder at the right growth stage, when excess to feed
requirements and high in quality (flowering stage). We must harvest the
forage when the moisture content is between 60 and 72%.

matter (35 %). Dry matter is what remains after all of the water is
