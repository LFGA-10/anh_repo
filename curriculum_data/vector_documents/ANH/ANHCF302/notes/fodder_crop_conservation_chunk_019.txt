3.4: fodder crops wilting
3.4.1: definition of fodder wilting
What does wilting mean? Wilting is the process where forage is left in the field to
dry down usually in windrows to raise the dry matter level in the crop prior to
being chopped and ensiled.
3.4.1. Objectives of fodder Wilting
Is wilting grass silage really important? Wilting is an important step when
making grass silage as:
➢ It increases the dry matter (DM) percentage and
➢ reduces the quantity of effluent produced.
➢ It reduces transportation costs by removing surplus water
Factors that affect wilting rates
✓ Climatic factors such as: temperature, relative humidity, wind, rain, dew,
soil moisture, and solar radiation.
✓ Crop factors such as: character of the plant
✓ Husbandry factors such as: distribution of the forage in the field, times of
turning over
