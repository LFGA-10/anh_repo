4.10. Use of hay

All hay can be used by animals during the dry season, but if we do not have
much of it, and if that hay is inside of legumes, it is best to reserve it for calves
that we intend to wean and high producing dairy cows calving in dry season.
Valves ingesting hay drink a lot and very often, it is necessary to provide them
with clean water ad libitum.
Hay making process
✓ Mowing: act of cutting down grass with a hand implement or machine
✓ Tedding: lifts and separates hay in order to hasten dry down time
✓ Raking: gathering the dried fodder
✓ Baling: to tie up the raked fodder by using baling box and turning them
into bales
✓ loading
✓ hauling: this is truckage or trucking (transporting fodder by trucks)
✓ unloading
✓ storing
4.11. characteristics of good hay
✓ free from mold or dust
✓ young or less than a year
✓ bright color from dull green to golden yellow (slightly green in color)
✓ quality guaranty and clean
✓ pleasant aroma (sweet smell)
✓ dry but not be dump
✓ cold and heat could be indicative of fermentation
✓ soft and pliable to touch
4.12. characteristics of poor quality hay
✓ damp and mold with fermented smell
✓ seems excessively dusty
✓ excessive heat in the center of hay bale
