2.3: SELECT FODDER CROPS CONSERVATION METHODS
2.3.1. TYPES OF FODDER CONSERVATION METHODS
✓ Wet method: silage making
✓ Dry method: Hay making
✓ Conservation on foot/Fodder banks
2.3.2. FACTORS AFFECTING PALATABILITY OF CONSERVED
FODDER CROPS
✓ Conservation methods: palatability of conserved fodder can be
affected by the method used either hay or silage making.
✓ Type of fodder crops: some crops are more palatable than others.
✓ Composition of fodder crops: grasses are more palatable than
legumes because of sugar contained in them.
✓ Type of Used additives: some additives are more palatable than
others like molasses.
2.3.3. Factors affecting digestibility of conserved fodder crops
✓ Type of fodder crops: some fodder crops are more digestible
than others because of their chemical composition.
✓ Growth stage of fodder crops: Early cut fodder has higher
digestibility than late-cut. With maturity lignin contents of
plant/forage increases as digestibility is decreased.
✓ Composition of fodder crops: The chemical composition of feed
or animal foods is one the biggest factors in deciding digestibility.
