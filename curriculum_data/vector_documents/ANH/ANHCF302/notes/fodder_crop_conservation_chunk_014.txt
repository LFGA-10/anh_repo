3.2.2. Calculation of forage volume to ensile
The volume of a silo is calculated based on 4 main parameters:
✓ Nc: number of animals in each category of animal (dairy cows, heifer…)
✓ Rd: daily ration of each type of animal
✓ Df: number of feeding days in the year

✓ W: weight of silage per 1 m3, this depends on plant species, its dry matter
content, when putting in silo and heading up. Below low heights (1.5 to
2.5), that weight is around 600 kg for maize, 700 kg for prairie grasses and
legumes.
The volume of silo to plan is: V (m3) = Nc*Rd*Df/W
3.2.3. Calculations of the dimensions of a silo
✓ Clamp or semi underground silo: volume= length*width*height/2
✓ Surface silo: volume= length*width*height
