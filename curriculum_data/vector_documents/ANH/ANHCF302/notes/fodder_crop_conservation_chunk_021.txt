Advantages of chopping forages to ensile:
➢ Correct chop length minimizes air (oxygen) in the pile or bunker
➢ Correct chop length is important for fiber in the ration
➢ Forage chopping may minimize clostridium fermentation by
promoting greater packing density
➢ Closer substrate contact with the fermenting bacteria leads to
higher lactate yield and faster PH reduction.
➢ It helps in adequate compaction
