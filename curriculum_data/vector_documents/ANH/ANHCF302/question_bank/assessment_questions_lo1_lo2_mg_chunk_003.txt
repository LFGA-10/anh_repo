Level 3: Applying

A. Multiple Choice (Using knowledge in a new situation)

A farmer has a large quantity of maize stover with high fiber content. Which treatment would be most directly aimed at improving its digestibility?
a) Soaking it in water (Physical)
b) Spraying it with a urea solution (Chemical)
c) Storing it in a dry warehouse
d) Feeding it directly to young calves
Answer: b) Spraying it with a urea solution (Chemical)

For a farmer conserving alfalfa, which has a low native sugar content, what would be a logical and helpful practice?
a) Use only a homofermentative inoculant.
b) Add an acid-based additive to directly lower pH.
c) Add a supplement like molasses to provide fermentable sugars.
d) Ensile it at a very high dry matter content.
Answer: c) Add a supplement like molasses to provide fermentable sugars.

B. Short Answer

A farmer wants to make high-quality hay from a grass fodder. According to the document, at what growth stage should they ideally harvest it?
Answer: They should harvest it at the pre-flowering or flower initiation stage.

Describe the steps you would take to apply molasses to chopped forage when making silage.
Answer: I would dilute 1 litre of molasses with 3 litres of water. Then, I would sprinkle this mixture evenly over the layers of chopped forage, ideally using a garden sprayer for even distribution.
