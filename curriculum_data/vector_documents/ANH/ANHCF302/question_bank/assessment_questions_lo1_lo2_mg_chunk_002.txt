Level 2: Understanding

A. Multiple Choice (Interpretation and comprehension)

Why is partial wilting of legumes necessary before ensiling?
a) To increase the sugar content
b) To reduce the water concentration to about 70%
c) To make them more palatable for immediate consumption
d) To kill harmful fungi
Answer: b) To reduce the water concentration to about 70%

What is the underlying principle behind the chemical treatment of crop residues like straw?
a) To add significant nutritional value
b) To break or solubilize the chemical and physical bonds in cell walls
c) To change the color of the residue
d) To make the residue sterile
Answer: b) To break or solubilize the chemical and physical bonds in cell walls

If a farmer opens their silo and finds the silage is heating, which type of inoculant might they have benefited from using?
a) A homofermentative inoculant
b) A heterofermentative inoculant
c) An enzyme-based additive
d) A urea additive
Answer: b) A heterofermentative inoculant (as it improves aerobic stability and reduces yeast, which causes heating)

B. Short Answer

Explain one reason why inoculants might not work effectively in silage production.
Answer: One reason is a low sugar content in the crop, which is the main food source for lactic acid bacteria, limiting their ability to ferment and lower the pH effectively.

Briefly state two advantages of using silage additives.
Answer: Any two from: Improve the fermentation process, reduce dry matter losses, improve aerobic stability, increase nutritive value, improve hygienic quality.
