Qn4. State whether the following statements are TRUE or FALSE. Each question is worth 2 marks.

(10 Marks)

1. Treating crop residues with urea adds significant amounts of energy and vitamins to the feed.
Answer: FALSE (It primarily adds nitrogen and softens cell walls, adding no other nutrients.)

2. Grasses are generally more palatable than legumes because they contain more sugar.
Answer: TRUE

3. The "dry method" of fodder conservation is also known as silage making.
Answer: FALSE (The dry method is hay making.)

4. Non-ruminants digest roughages high in crude fibre better than ruminants.
Answer: FALSE (Ruminants digest them better due to pre-gastric fermentation.)

5. The optimal dry matter content for making high-quality silage is between 15-35%.
Answer: TRUE
