Assessment: Learning Unit 3 – Silage Making

Total Marks: 30
Time Allowed: 45 minutes

Section A: Remembering & Understanding (12 marks)

1. Multiple Choice – Choose more than two correct answers (4 marks)

Which of the following are types of silos mentioned in the document?
a) Pit silo
b) Tower silo
c) Clamp silo
d) Surface silo
e) Tube silo

✅ Correct answers: a, c, d, e

2. Fill in the Blanks (4 marks)

Complete the following sentences:

Silage making is a method of preserving forage through anaerobic lactic fermentation.

The ideal pH for good silage is below 4.

Wilting is done to increase the dry matter percentage and reduce effluent.

Chopping forages to 2–4 cm length aids compaction and fermentation.

✅ Answers: anaerobic lactic fermentation, below 4, dry matter, 2–4 cm

3. True or False (2 marks)

a) Alfalfa is an ideal forage for silage because it is high in sugar. False
b) A surface silo is suitable for large herds and provides excellent compaction. False

4. Multiple Choice – More than two correct (2 marks)

Which of the following are risks associated with poor silage making?
a) Botulism
b) Listeria
c) Aflatoxins
d) Excessive lactic acid
e) High ethanol production

✅ Correct answers: a, b, c, e
