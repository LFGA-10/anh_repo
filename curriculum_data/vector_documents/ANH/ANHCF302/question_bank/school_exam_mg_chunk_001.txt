Qn1. Choose ALL the correct answers for each question. Each question is worth 3 marks.

1. Which of the following are listed as leguminous fodder plants?
a) Pennisetum purpureum
b) Calliandra sp.
c) Medicago sativa
d) Setaria sphacelata
e) Leucaena leucocephala
Correct Answers: b, c, e

2. What are the primary uses of crop residues as mentioned in the document?
a) Human consumption
b) Bedding material for animals
c) Soil mulching
d) Manufacturing textiles
e) Bio-gas generation
Correct Answers: b, c, e

3. Which factors directly affect the digestibility of conserved fodder?
a) The color of the storage facility
b) The growth stage of the fodder crop at harvest
c) The type of fodder crop
d) The age of the animal consuming it
e) The brand of the harvesting tool
Correct Answers: b, c, d

4. What are the reasons why silage inoculants might fail?
a) Low sugar content of the crop
b) The color of the plastic sheeting
c) High dry matter content of the crop
d) Crop specificity of the bacterial strains
e) The time of day of harvesting
Correct Answers: a, c, d

5. Which of the following are considered physical treatments for crop residues?
a) Urea/Ammonia application
b) Grinding
c) Addition of enzymes
d) Pelleting
e) Soaking
Correct Answers: b, d, e
