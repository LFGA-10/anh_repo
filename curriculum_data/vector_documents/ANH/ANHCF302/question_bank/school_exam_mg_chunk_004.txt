1.  "The treatment of crop residues makes bad feed into good feed." Evaluate this statement based on the information in the documents. Do you agree or disagree? Justify your answer with at least two reasons. (10 Marks): 

text

**Model Answer:**

I disagree with this statement. According to the document, treatments do not transform bad feed into good feed. The primary principle of treatment is to break down the chemical and physical bonds in the plant's cell wall, which "softens" the fiber. This process improves the *rate* and *extent* of digestion and intake by the animal. However, it does not significantly enhance the fundamental nutritional profile of the feed, except for adding nitrogen in the case of urea treatment. A crop residue like wheat straw is inherently low in energy and protein; treatment makes the existing fiber more accessible but does not infuse it with high levels of new energy or protein. Therefore, it turns a *very poor* feed into a *moderately better* one, but not into a "good," high-quality feed.
