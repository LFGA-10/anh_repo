8. Open-ended – Scenario-based (4 marks)

A farmer’s silage smells like vinegar and has a dark green color. What likely went wrong during silage making, and how could it be prevented?

✅ Answer:

Problem: Acetic fermentation occurred due to poor compaction, excess oxygen, or high moisture.

Prevention: Ensure wilting to 30–35% DM, chop to 2–4 cm, compact thoroughly, and seal airtight to maintain anaerobic conditions.

9. Open-ended – Comparison (4 marks)

Compare the advantages and disadvantages of a pit silo and a tube silo.

✅ Answer:

Pit silo:
Advantages: Low cost, good compaction, good silage quality.
Disadvantages: Difficult to unload, risk of water seepage.

Tube silo:
Advantages: Airtight, portable, easy to manage portions, reduces spoilage.
Disadvantages: Higher initial cost, requires special bags, not suitable for very large quantities.
