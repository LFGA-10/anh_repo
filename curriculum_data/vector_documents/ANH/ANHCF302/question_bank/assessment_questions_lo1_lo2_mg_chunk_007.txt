Supplementation:

Necessity: Urea-treated stover is still primarily an energy source and lacks certain minerals, vitamins, and possibly bypass protein.

Suggestions: Provide a mineral lick containing salt, calcium, and phosphorus. Supplement with a concentrated source of protein (e.g., cottonseed cake) and energy (e.g., a small amount of grains) to meet the high nutritional demands of milk production. This ensures a balanced diet and maximizes milk yield.
