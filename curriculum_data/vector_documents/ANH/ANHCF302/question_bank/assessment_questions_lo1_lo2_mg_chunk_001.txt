Assessment Questions: Fodder Crop Conservation and Distribution

Level 1: Remembering

A. Multiple Choice (Recall of specific facts)

Which of the following is a leguminous fodder crop?
a) Pennisetum purpureum
b) Medicago sativa
c) Tripsacum laxum
d) Oats
Answer: b) Medicago sativa

What is the primary purpose of homofermentative lactic acid bacteria in silage making?
a) To improve aerobic stability
b) To produce acetic acid
c) To lower pH quickly and efficiently
d) To inhibit yeast growth
Answer: c) To lower pH quickly and efficiently

Which of these is a common problem associated with feeding too much molasses to livestock?
a) Increased milk production
b) Molasses toxicity
c) Improved digestion
d) Reduced bloat
Answer: b) Molasses toxicity
