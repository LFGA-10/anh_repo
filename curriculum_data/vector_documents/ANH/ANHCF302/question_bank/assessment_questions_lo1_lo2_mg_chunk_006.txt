Level 6: Creating

Essay Question
Propose a detailed plan for a dairy farmer to integrate the conservation of crop residues into their livestock feeding system. Your plan should:

Identify a specific crop residue available in the East Shoa Zone.

Select an appropriate treatment method and justify your choice.

Outline the steps for the treatment and conservation process.

Suggest how the treated residue should be supplemented to ensure a balanced diet for the dairy cows, explaining why this supplementation is necessary.

Sample Answer Guide:

Identification: The student should choose a residue like Maize Stover.

Treatment Method & Justification: Propose Urea Treatment. Justification: It is a low-cost chemical method that breaks down lignocellulose bonds, increases the crude protein content of the residue via the ammonia released, and improves palatability and digestibility.

Steps for Treatment:

Chop the maize stover to a manageable size.

Prepare a solution of urea in water (e.g., 4 kg urea in 100 litres of water for 100 kg of dry stover).

Sprinkle the solution evenly over the chopped stover while stacking it.

Compact the stack and cover it airtight with a polyethylene sheet for 2-3 weeks to allow for ammoniation.
