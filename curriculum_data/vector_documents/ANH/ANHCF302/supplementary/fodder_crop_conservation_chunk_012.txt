Hewever, we should ensure thet the grass to ensile is rich in sugar and relatively low in nitrogen. It is thues difficult to achieve with some forages such as alfalfa, low in sugar and high in soluble nitrogen.

In Rwanda, 2 plants are are often ensiled; penisetum and trypsacum and must be at the best stage, that is to say, at about 1 to 1.50 m high, not too old, between 2 and 3 months and as green as possible. It is interesting to mix them with desmodium (about 1/5 of desmodium).

3.1: SELECT SILO SITE

silo, in agriculture is airtight structure that encloses and protects silage

3.1.1. SILO SITE SELECTION CRITERIA

Soil structure

 Land slope

Methods of conservation

3.1.2. TYPES OF SILOS

For a flat surface

Surface silo: are the piles of hay that are placed on a flat soil and which consist of packing forage on the ground and covering it with plastic sheeting without any border, used for a small herd.

Advantages of surface silo

It is cheaper

It does not require any investment for the construction of edges

It can be easily expanded

		Disadvantages of surface silo

	The silage quality is lower (packing is difficult to do)

 Pit silo: it is a cylindrical hole made in the ground. Walls can be made of well packed or in bricks. Here, one (1) cubic meter of the silo can have 650 to 700 kg settled silage.
