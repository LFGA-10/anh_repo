Topic of the session:  Selection of Silo Site and Identification of Suitable Silo Types

Range:

Soil structure

Land slope

Methods of conservation

Types of silos

Duration of the session: 120mins

Objectives: At the end of this session the learner should be able to:

Explain the criteria for selecting a silo site

Identify different types of silos  and their characteristics.

Compare advantages and limitations of each silo type in relation to fodder crop, climate, and farm resources.

Demonstrate decisionmaking skills in choosing the most appropriate silo type for specific farm conditions.

Apply practical guidelines to ensure proper site preparation and silo construction for effective silage making.
