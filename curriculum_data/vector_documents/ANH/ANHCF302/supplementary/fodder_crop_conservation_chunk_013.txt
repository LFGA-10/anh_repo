 Pit silo: it is a cylindrical hole made in the ground. Walls can be made of well packed or in bricks. Here, one (1) cubic meter of the silo can have 650 to 700 kg settled silage.

Advantages of pit silo

Low cost only for digging

Good compaction

Good silage quality

                             Disadvantages of pit silo

Silo unloading is not easy

For a sloping or flat ground

Clamp silo: this silo consists of storing feed between two concrete walls used for a high forage quantity.

Advantages of clamp silo

Products to preserve is better

It is easy to unload silage

Disadvantages of clamp silo

It is more expensive

For only a sloping ground

Semi under-ground silo: it is a pit dug into the hillside.

Advantages of semi underground silo

It allows getting a good compaction

It is easy to remove silage

                           Disadvantages of semi underground silo

It is ideal for slopes only

It is expensive
