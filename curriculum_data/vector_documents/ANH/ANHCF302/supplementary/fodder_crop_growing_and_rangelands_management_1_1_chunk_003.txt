Introduction

Animal feeding is one of the most important pillars in raising livestock. The proper feeding of farm animals ensures their normal growth, development, and high productivity.

In Rwanda, with the recent advances in breeding technologies, many farmers have gained access to high-quality animal genotypes. To realize the full production potential of these genotypes, there is need for proper managerial practices, especially with regard to feeding. Farmers need to be knowledgeable on how to maximize productivity while minimizing costs of feeding. It is clear that establishment of pasture, conservation and treatment of crops residues is one of the sources of low cost feed.
