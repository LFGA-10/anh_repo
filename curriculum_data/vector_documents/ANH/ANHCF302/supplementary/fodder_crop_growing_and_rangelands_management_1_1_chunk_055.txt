Leaves paripinnate, long, narrow; leaflets in many pairs, rounded or oblong, usually asymmetric at the base, often glaucous; stipules minute or absent.

4.3.1.2 Ficus toniingy

Ficus toniingyis a very wonderful tree. Some people think of the Ficus toniingyas a sacred tree. Ordinary people used the tree as mainstay poles to the entrance and exit gates of their cattle enclosures.

Ficus toniingy is different from other trees. Many trees use birds and bees to carry pollen grains from one tree to another so that the trees can be fertilised and make new flowers, fruits or nuts. But some plants and trees such as Ficus toniingy have a remarkable ability to reproduce asexually. If a branch is cut and planted it grows into a very huge tree.

Ficus toniingytree has very many uses: many farmers and people with cattle use the Ficus toniingyas a shade-providing tree. The farmers plant many trees of it on the farm to shelter them against the scorching heat of the sun; the trunk of a huge tree, with its shady leaves, can provide shelter from the rain.

The leaves of Ficus toniingy is also used by many farmers to feed different domestic animals.
