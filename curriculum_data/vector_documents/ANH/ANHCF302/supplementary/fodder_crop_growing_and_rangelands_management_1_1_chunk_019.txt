1.3.3.2 Planting by vegetative parts

This is a method by which plants reproduce themselves from part that is not associated with the reproductive organs. Most of them have ample storage organs that serve as reserves when conditions are unfavorable. As soon as the conditions allow, the plants rejuvenate and start growing afresh.

Methods

Tuft splits: Tuft splits may be dug out by hand and taken to the establishment area and planted in furrows.

Stem cuttings: Ample soil moisture is necessary. Mature stems bearing three nodes are cut and planted in furrows or surface broadcast and covered with soil; two nodes buried and one left above ground.
