2.2.7 Qualities of good silage

The silage quality is valued by the examination of the products, but better, by the analysis.

1. Examination

color: a good silage keeps a yellowish green color; a dark green color is indicative ofthe presence of butyric acid

Smell: it should be fruity and acidic, but pleasant. A smell of vinegar is indicative ofacetic acid excess that reflects an inadequate compaction.

Analysis

Good silage has the following general characteristics:

Dry matter content close to or above 30%;

Acetic acid content of less than 2% of dry weight

pH below 4.5;

Total absence of butyric acid.

Feeding value above 0.65 FU/kg dry matter
