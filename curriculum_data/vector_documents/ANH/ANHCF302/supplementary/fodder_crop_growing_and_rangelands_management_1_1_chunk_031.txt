Dolichos analysed as a whole plant contains 16.8% crude protein with a dry matter yield of about 6 t/ha. The seed yield ranges from 1.1 to 3.4 t/ha with 20.25% crude protein content.

1.3.7.4 Stylosanthes guianensis

Stylo(Stylosanthes guianensis) performs well in wetter and warmer areas and on sandy soils.

Its ability to grow well on soils of low fertility is particularly valuable both to provide good-quality grazing on these soils and to improve the fertility of the soil. It is useful for weed control and It grows rather slowly in early establishment stages but persists better in grass–legume mixtures than does Desmodium.Stylosanthes

Establishment

Stylosanthes guianensis requires a firm, fine seedbed

The seed is treated in hot water for 3 to 5 minutes to break down had seed and with a fungicide before planting into seed beds

a dense seed bed of 10 m2 is planted at the rate 40kg/ha.

After 45 to 50 days, seedlings15- 20 high are transplanted in the field in a grid of 70cmx70cm (2seedlings/hill).

Apply 125-150kg superphosphate per hectare.

Mantenance

Weed as required.

When grown in a grass–legume mixture, graze to reduce the shading effect of grass during the early establishment stage.

Harvest or graze at 6 weeks at intervals of 1½ months, leaving a stubble height of 5 cm.

Stylosanthes guianensis has a crude protein content ranging from 12.1% to 18% and dry matter yields ranging from 4.1 to 6.7 t/ha per year.

1.3.7.5 Mucuna spp (Velvet bean)

Velvet bean is an annual climbing vine that grows 3-18 m in height. Without support it will spread over the ground.
