Semi-underground silo

 You horizontally dig a hole on a medium sloping site (around 30%). The usual dimensions are 2 meters high, 2 meters wide, for 6 meters long, but this depends strongly on the site. Ideally, the sides of the hole are isolated from ground by an impermeable material such as plastic or bricks, and the whole silage is covered with a waterproof and hermetic tarp.

Clamp silo

We must then construct the three concrete walls, and often a concrete screed. This silo is usually used to store large quantities of fodder. The most common dimensions are: 2.40m high; 3 meters wide; the length is variable but at least 6 meters. The volume is then about 20m3.

Surface silo

It is built by heaping up forage on a more or less horizontal site, and plastic tarp coverage. Dimensions are very variable. It is a commonly used type of silo and which can be adapted to Rwanda because it is cheaper compared with others but it presents a disadvantage: the pressing is not very good.

2.2.5 Bacterial activity of a silo

In silage, three types of fermentation can take place:

Acetic fermentation

The silo is initially colonized by aerobic acetic bacteria that early start and continue to grow ifthere is still oxygen. They convert soluble sugars in acetic and lactic acid, alcohol and carbondioxide. These bacteria rapidly disappear when the environment becomes too acidic (pH <4.5)and in the absence of oxygen.
