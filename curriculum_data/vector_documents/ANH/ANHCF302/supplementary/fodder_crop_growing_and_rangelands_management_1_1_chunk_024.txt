Management of  Pennisetum purpureum

Harvest napier when 1m high or at every 6-8 weeks to obtain optimal quality and quantity.

Maintain a stubble height of 5-10 cm from the ground level at each harvest to avoid weakening of root system which leads to low production in subsequent harvests.

Depending on the soil fertility and moisture, a cutting interval of 8 to 12 weeks is recommended.

To increase napier grass yields during the dry season one of the split applications of the recommended rate of nitrogen fertilizer should be done one or two months before end of rain season.

Other Uses of Pennisetumpurpureum

1. Napier when planted around maize field helps to control maize stalkborer infestation.

Plant the napier (Bana variety is the best) around the maize plot.

 In the first year plant napier at the onset of rains so that the napier starts before the maize. The moths will like to settle on the tall napier more than the maize.

 Plant at least three rows of napier all around the maize field.

2. Napier when planted along contour lines in pure stand or in a mixture of fodder legume trees controls soil erosion.
