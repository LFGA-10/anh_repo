5.2.2 Carrying capacity

The term “carrying capacity” is a measure of the number of animals that can be placed on a pasture reported on an average per day and throughout the year to achieve a targeted level of animal performance or economic production without causing deterioration of the pasture resource.

Knowing the carrying capacity avoids therefore any wastage and overgrazing resulting in underfeeding and fast flora degradation.

5.3 Manage grazing

The newly established pasture can be utilized within 2–8 months after sowing. This is only possible if proper grazing or cutting is ensured.

Rotational or periodic grazing is usually recommended. However, some species may not respond to rotational grazing and this situation has to be treated cautiously. As a precaution, stocking rate should be greatly reduced or grazing totally stopped when no more than 50% of the weight of herbage has been consumed.
