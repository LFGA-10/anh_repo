3.2.3.2 Urea treatment procedure

WEIGH THE STRAW TO BE TREATED

WEIGH AN EQUAL AMMOUNT OF WATER TO THAT OF THE STRAW

ADD 40gm OF UREA PER Kg OF STRAW AND MIX IT THOROUGHLY IN WATER

SOAK THE STRAW INTO THE MIXTURE FOR 6 HOURS

DRAIN THE STRAW AND FEED IT TO THE CATTLE

3.2.4 Treatment of crop residues with molasses

Most animals find molasses very palatable and it is often used to mask the taste of unpalatable ingredients such as urea and straws. It is viscous and helps to bind small particles such as mineral and vitamin sources into the mass of mixed food. Molasses is widely used as an animal food ingredient and has been used as an animal food ingredient and has been used successfully in diets for rabbits.

However, molasses should not be used at more than about 20% of the diet. Because ruminants given molasses-based diets grow more slowly than when grain is used as the major energy source, even when the protein and mineral deficiencies of molasses are rectified. This poorer performance is partly due to the high mineral content which can lead to diarrhea and partly to the predominance of acetic and butyric acids in the end products of rumen fermentation. Molasses is not suitable for pre-ruminant calves, kids or lambs because they do not have an endogenous sucrose.
