Risk of intoxication caused by fungal or bacterial toxins fungal: It is a risk for animals that consume the products of poorly made silage (e.g. aflatoxins).

Risk of listeria in case of poorly acidified silage which allows the bacteria development (including in dairy livestock farming, especially when we produce cheeses made from raw milk). There are also the risks of meningitis for young cattle fed with maize silage and subjected to an important stress (livestock farming conditions, abrupt change of feeding, change of an animal from a herd to another,...).

Risk of the production of ethanol which is toxic to ruminants, usually as a result of alcoholic fermentation due to the improper sealing of the silo or the plastic sheeting.

Risk of the excessive presence of butyric bacteria, following the incorporation of soil in the forage during harvesting operations.

Risk of pollution: The liquid produced by the silage of too wet plants is acidic, corrosive, odorous and pollutant (powerful eutrophicant).
