Establishment from cuttings

Use mature parts of Desmodium vines.

 Use freshly cut Desmodium vines.

 Bury two nodes leaving 1 or 2 nodes above the ground.

Space at 30 cm or 50 cm from row to row and 15- 30 cm from vine to vine.

Use same rates of fertilizer as for seed but place it in holes. Precaution: Make sure there are adequate rains when planting.

Management

Keep the stand free of weeds especially during the early stages before the crop covers the ground by use of jembe or herbicide 2,4-D Amine 72% at the rate of 2.5 litres per hectare.

Topdress with 2 - 4 bags of TSP or SSP fertilizer every year to maintain high yields of Desmodium (herbage and seed).

For herbage production, first cut should be 3-4 months from planting. Subsequent cuttings should be after 6 - 8 weeks interval.

Spray against pests, which are also a major enemy especially for Desmodium seed production.

Utilization

cut and feed in green form

cut and conserve in form of hay (whole or chopped)

3-6kg of green Desmodium herbage can be used to replace 1-2kg of dairy meal.

Yields

9-10 ton dry matter/ha per year of 18-20% Crude protein.

An acre produces 30-60kg of seed.

Other benefits of Desmodium

When intercropped with maize it will

Stop striga weeds growing

 Reduce maize stemborer damage

Provide nitrogen to the crop

 Control soil erosion

1.3.7.2 Lucerne (Medicago sativa)

Dairy farmers in the high-rainfall areas of East Africa prefer lucerne over any other forage legume, primarily because of its palatability, high nutritive and can sustain high milk production levels when fed to dairy cattle.
