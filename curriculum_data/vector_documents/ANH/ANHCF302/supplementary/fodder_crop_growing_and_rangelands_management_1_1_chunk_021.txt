1.3.5 Pest control

The word ‘pest’ is defined as anything that is destructive or a nuisance. As far as cultivated fodder crops are concerned, a pest is any organism that is destructive to fodder crops, either directly by damaging the plant, or indirectly through the introduction of disease-producing organisms such as virus.

Pest control is an extremely expensive business, but if pests are allowed to multiply, they can decimate a fodder crop.

1.3.5.1 Methods of pest control

The methods of pests control have been divided into three main categories:

Cultural and legislative (quarantine);

Chemical

Biological

However, the modern approach aims at using a combination of all methods wherever and whenever possible for maximum efficiency. This new approach is commonly termed’ integrated control’.

Cultural control

Some of the important cultural methods of pests control, include the following:

Early planting

Use of clean planting materials

Manuring

Field hygiene

Fodder crop rotation

Weed control

Growing resistant varieties

Quarantine and legislation

chemical control –pesticides
