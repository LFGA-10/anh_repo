Duration of the session: 120mins

Objectives: At the end of this session the learner should be able to:

Explain the importance of packaging in maintaining feed quality.

Identify appropriate packaging materials suitable for different types of concentrate feeds.

Demonstrate correct packaging techniques to minimize spoilage and losses.

 Design and apply labels that include essential information such as feed type, ingredients, date of preparation, expiry date, and storage instructions.

Evaluate packaging and labeling practices for compliance with feed safety standards and farmer usability.
