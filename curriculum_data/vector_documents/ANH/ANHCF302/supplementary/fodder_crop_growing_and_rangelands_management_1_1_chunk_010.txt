Temperature has a very strong impact on crop growth, because it affects many physiological plant activities such as cell division, tissue growth, photosynthesis, germination, flowering, ripening, etc.

The crop performance is affected by temperature variation in the both extremes (i.e high and low temperature) have a damaging effect on plants. Very high temperatures, for instance, speed up the rate of water loss through transpiration and evaporation, thus leading to the wilting and eventual death of plants. Low temperatures, on the other hand, reduce the rate of cell division and plant growth, and may even lead to cold burning and the death of plants under a heavy frost.

3.Soil

Soil is a very strong determining factor in the distribution of fodder crop because not all fodder crops grow well in the some types of soil. the availability of fodder crop, the acidity or alkalinity depth, drainage, aeration, etc.., all contribute to making the area suitable for specific fodder crops and unsuitable for others.
