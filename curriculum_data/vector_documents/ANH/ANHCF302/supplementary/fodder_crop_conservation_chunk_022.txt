3.8: Fill and compress silo

You will need to fill the silo and compact it as quickly as possible, and without interruptions. Never leave the silo uncovered for more than a day.

3.8.1. Steps of silo filling

Before you start filling, spread the plastic on the outer edge of the floor and the walls and let it hang over the sides so you can cover the silo later.

Doposit the forage at the beginning of the silo and spread the forage in a thin layer (max 15cm) throughout the silo.

Compact the layer and repeat the process until the silo is filled.

3.9: Cover and seal silo

You will need to cover the silo immediately after completing the filling and compacting. The cover needs to be made of airtight material to avoid exposure to air as much as possible. A plastic sheet is the standard type of cover used.

Cover the silo with additional piece of white plastic.
