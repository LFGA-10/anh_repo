Mowing: the plant should be cut at early flowering for legumes. Mowing can be done using sickle when areas are small, or using tractor mounted mowers when areas are very large.

Drying: it must be the most rapid possible and must be done in open fields in the sun. the grass must be turned 2-3 times a day. During rainy period, we must provide the maximum protection against water, by making small ricks that will be scattered once the rain ceases. Hay is dry after 2 days of good weather and contents about 78% of DM.

Storage: it must be stored under the shade. Hay can be preserved for long periods keeping intact its energy, proteins and minerals.

4.10. Use of hay

All hay can be used by animals during the dry season, but if we do not have much of it, and if that hay is inside of legumes, it is best to reserve it for calves that we intend to wean and high producing dairy cows calving in dry season. Valves ingesting hay drink a lot and very often, it is necessary to provide them with clean water ad libitum.

Hay making process

Mowing: act of cutting down grass with a hand implement or machine

Tedding: lifts and separates hay in order to hasten dry down time

Raking: gathering the dried fodder

Baling: to tie up the raked fodder by using baling box and turning them into bales

4.11. characteristics of good hay

free from mold or dust

young or less than a year

bright color from dull green to golden yellow (slightly green in color)

quality guaranty and clean

pleasant aroma (sweet smell)

dry but not be dump

cold and heat could be indicative of fermentation

soft and pliable to touch
