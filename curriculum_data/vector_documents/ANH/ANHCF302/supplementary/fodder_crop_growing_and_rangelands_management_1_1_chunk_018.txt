 Sowing method

Broadcasting

The seeds are spread by hand, taking care to ensure uniform distribution. The area should be divided into equal parts and the seeds divided into similar equal parts. The each portion of seed is broadcasted into one area. Wind is a factor to be considered especially with light seeds which may be blown over some distance. Mixing the seeds with materials such as sawdust, sand and fertilizers helps distribute the seed more evenly. All grasses and small seeded legumes are planted at a depth of 1.2-1.5 cm. Planted seed must be lightly covered with soil using a rake or leafless branch to ensure good seed and soil contact and for protection from birds and being washed away by rain. Broadcasting gives satisfactory stands when conditions are favorable for rapid seed germination and seedling growth.

 Sowing in line

This refers to planting crop seeds in rows. Enough space is provided between one plant and another along the line and also between one row and the next. It can be done either manually or by machine, depending on what the farmer deems appropriate within his means.
