Water supply should be adequate, reliable and clean. It may be obtained by construction of earth dams, tapping natural streams, sinking wells, or use of natural springs.

Mineral licks should be shifted from time to time as leaving them in one place leads to overgrazing and destruction of pasture in that particular area.

Breeding season and time of calving

On a ranch it is appropriate to have regulated breeding and calving times instead of letting the animals breed and calve at random. This enables a number of animals to mature simultaneously so that a farmer has a larger number of animals to market at the same time. In Rwanda unlike temperate regions, it is possible to have two breeding and calving seasons in a year. That is, April to June and October to December. These are times when there is plenty of pasture, and there is less menace from warble flies and incidence of diseases

Round up

This refers to the times of bringing in animals at least twice a year, for example, between April and June, and October and December, which could coincide with the breeding seasons and calving times.

The purpose of round up is:

To do assessment of the animals

To castrate the male

For identification of the animals, usually by branding

To vaccinate, spray, dip or otherwise treat against diseases such as anthrax, foot-and-mount,rinderpest etc.

To take an accurate count of the stock

To separate breeding animals from steers and heifers

To wean calves

To cull and market selected animals.
