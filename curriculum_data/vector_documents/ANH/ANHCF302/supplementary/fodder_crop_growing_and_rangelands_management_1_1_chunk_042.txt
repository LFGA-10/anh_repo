2.2.4 Building a silo

2.3.4. 1. Calculation of forage volume to ensile

The volume of a silo is calculated based on 4 main parameters:

Nc, number of animals in each category (dairy cows, heifer, ...)

Rd: daily ration of each type of animal.

Df: number of feeding days in the year

W: weight of silage per 1 m3> this depends on the plant species, its dry matter content,

When putting in silo and heaping up. Below low heights (1.5 to 2.5 m), that weight is around 600 kg for maize, 700kg for prairie grasses and legumes;

The volume of silo to plan is: V (m3) = Nc x Rd x Df / W

The building cost of a silo can be calculated from a cost of 1 m3 of silo

2.2.4.2 Calculation of the dimensions of a silo

Clamp or semi-underground silo: Volume = length * width * height /2

Surface silo: Volume = ~ length * width * height

Pit silo: Volume = Π * diameter2 * height /4

2.2.4.3 Construction of a silo

The main materials are: hoe to dig, machete to cut into small pieces (3 to 5 cm), wheelbarrow, tarp, brick and cement for the clump silo, ...

The pit silo

Digging a pit of 2 meters deep and 1,5 to 3,0 meters wide. Put a base of large stones in the bottom of the silo. Cut grass and fill the silo with it, stamping down the grass with your feet. The volume is then of 3-15 m3. To maximize the quality of silage, we should also isolate the sides with a material impermeable to air and water such as plastic or bricks.
