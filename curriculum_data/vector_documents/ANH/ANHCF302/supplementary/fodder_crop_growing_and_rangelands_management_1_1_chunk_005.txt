They have high moisture content except hay

Their feeding value is not known (variable)

They have low energy and protein content

They have a low palatability or acceptability.

Roughages are categorized by succulent (green pasture, Guatemala, potato vines, peelings of bananas, sweet potatoes, silage) and dry roughages.

Dry roughages have a low moisture content, high fiber content, low digestibility and nutritive value.

The palatability of roughages is improved by addition of molasses. Feeding ruminants on a high amount of roughages leads to high acetic acid concentration. This is important in the synthesis of milk butter fat content (BF)

1.1.2 Concentrates

These are commercially prepared feeds usually rich in food value and energy. Concentrates can be made from grains and grain by-product such as maize bran. They can also be made of a mixture of blood and bones. Other sources of ingredients are the oil-extracting factories where cotton and groundnut seed residues are obtained. Sugar refineries are also source of molasses which too, are used as ingredients.

Concentrates are characterized by:

 High food value both in energy and protein,

 Low moisture content

High digestibility,

Highly palatability,

Their feeding value is fairly constant (known),

Low fiber content,

Originate from both plants and animals.
