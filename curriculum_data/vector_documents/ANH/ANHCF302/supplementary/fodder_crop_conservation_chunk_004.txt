Material Cost: Materials for harvesting and conservation must be available at the low cost for preventing economical loss.

Availability: availability of materials and fodder crops must be considered.

Hygienic: the materials to be used during harvesting must be cleaned, not corrosive, no rust and free from other harmful substances.

1.2.2. SELECTION CRITERIA FOR FODDER CONSERVATION MATERIALS

Conservation methods: Materials can be selected according to the conservation methods like silage and hay because materials used during hay making are different from those needed during silage making.

Size: Fodder conservation materials can be selected according to the size or quantity of fodder to be conserved.

Cost: Materials to be used during fodder conservation must be cheap financially for avoiding economic loss.

Availability: Those materials must be available for easy transport and work

Efficiency: It is necessary for those materials to be efficiency and accurate for easy work and good production.

1.2.3. IDENTIFY SILAGE ADDITIVES

Silage microbial inoculants are selected lactic acid bacteria that are applied to dominate the naturally occurring fermentation processes of crops in the silo.

Silage additives can be used to remedy deficiencies such as lack of sufficient population of bacteria to support adequate fermentation, and low levels of fermentable carbohydrates. Most of the silage additives are applied as forages are chopped or during the loading phase.
