5.2 Pasture productivity

The productivity of a pasture is biomass formed during a specific time (usually one year), on a given area. The latter depends on several factors (type of grass, soil richness, rainfall... This production is always estimated in kg of DM / ha.

5.2.1 Estimation of pasture productivity

Pasture productivity is done by Determining the location of the plots, that is to say the squares of 4 to 25 m2 (Representative samples of the plant cover), and protecting them by a fence if they are still grazed by cattle. Making a regular mowing each time cattle could graze. We weight the grass (fresh matter), and after we dry in oven at 105o C to determine the dry matter weight.

By summing annual cuts, we obtain the production level per year of a plot, from which we calculate the productivity of the whole pasture (the productivity is to say, DM/ha/year and production that is to say the production of the whole pasture).
