Generally when the soil is depleted of nutrients by continual cropping or soil erosion basal applications of the macronutrients, especially nitrogen (100-150 kg/ha urea) and phosphorus (50 kg/ha triple superphosphate), are helpful for successful establishment on most soil types.

3.Liming

The soil has to be tested early to determine the need for lime. If lime is needed to neutralize soil acidity, it should be applied prior to the first tillage and, if possible, several months before planting. Lime should be incorporated into the soil whenever possible since lime reacts only with the soil that it contacts.

1.3.3 Planting technique

Planting is an important cultural practice and must be done correctly and in time. Most fodder crops do better if they are planted early in the growing season so as to make use of the rains. Early planting is especially critical in areas which have a very short and often unreliable rainy season.

There are two main ways or methods of planting fodder crops. First, planting from seed and second, vegetative planting. The farmer should choose the method that suits his purpose. The choice of the appropriate method should be based on the need to preserve the qualities or the genetic make up of the variety.
