1.Organic manure

Green manure, farm yard manure and compost are the most important organic materials incorporated in the soil to improve and maintain the soil fertility. In general, the benefits of incorporating organic manure can only be felt if the right quality and quantity of the manure is used, timely and on the right type of soil.Where available farm yard manure is a less expensive option for the smallholder, it can be applied at the rate of 5-10 tons/ha.

2.Inorganic fertilizers

Inorganic fertilizers are concentrated chemical compounds which are manufactured to supply specific requirements of plant nutrients. If applied, they enrich the soil with the particular element which is deficient. Most soil usually lack nitrogen, phosphorous and potassium.

Fodder crop will respond well to fertilizer application, if measures are also taken to have an adequate rainfall infiltration into the soil, while at the same time encouraging minimum leaching out of soluble plant nutrients like nitrates and sulphates. It is important to establish the exact nutrient that is lacking by the soil analysis, followed by the selection of the right type of fertilizer before treating it.

An improper fertilizer application, coupled with the wrong timing and amounts will adversely affect soil fertility and plant growth. Instead of improving or maintaining soil fertility, it may upset the soil pH, cause a micro-nutrient deficiency and lower optimum nutrient uptake by plant roots.
