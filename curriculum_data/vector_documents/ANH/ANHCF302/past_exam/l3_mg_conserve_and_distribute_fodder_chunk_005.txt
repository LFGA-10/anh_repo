Question 11: Penessetum and tripsacum are the most fodder crops used for silage making. At which best growth stage these fodder should be used to provide high nutritive value silage?											        (3 marks)

Marking scheme:

Pennisetum and Tripsacum should be used to provide high nutritive value silage when harvested:

at about 1 to 1.50 m high, (1mark)

between 2 and 3 months (1mark), not too old and as green as possible. (1mark)

Question 12: Harvested fodder crops to dry should comply with certain characteristics in order to make good quality hay. Explain by giving a clear example. 				        (3 marks)

Marking scheme:

All the plants may be made hay but it is clear that large-leaf plants will take longer to be made hay than fine-leaved plants.

Thus, it is inadvisable to make Pennisetum and Trypsacum.
