Question 18: Describe the steps of hay making technique. 	        (5 marks)

Marking scheme:

Mowing: the plant should be cut before bolting for grasses and at early flowering for legumes (1 mark)

Drying:

must be the most rapid possible and must be done in an open field in the sun (1 mark)

the grass must be returned 2-3 times a day (1 mark)

during the rainy time, we must provide the maximum protection against water (1 mark)

storage: hay should be stored under the shade (1 mark)

(reference: LU4: Perform hay making and storage, LO 4.1: Harverst fodder crop to dry., Curriculum level 3, Page 458, 2021)
