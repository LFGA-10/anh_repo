Question 25: The time/age to harvest fodder crop to dry plays a great influence on hay quality. Discuss the event of cutting period that influence the quality of hay. 			        (5 marks)

Marking scheme:

When the forage is cut at early stage, the quality must be poor (1mark) due to high water content (1mark)

At later stage, substances difficult to digest are developed (1mark) and the dry matter quality is reduced (1mark)

The best time to cut forage is just a bit before the ear emergence (1mark)
