Question 27: Discuss the ways/procedures of evaluating/appreciating the silage and hay quality to feed the livestock? 											      (10 marks)

Marking scheme:

The silage quality is valued by the examination of the products, but better, by the analysis. (1 mark)

Examination:

Color: good silage keeps yellowish-green color (1 mark); a dark green color is indicative of the presence of butyric acid (1 mark)

Smell: it should be fruity and acidic (1 mark). A smell of vinegar is indicative of acetic acid excess reflecting inadequate compaction (1 mark)

Analysis: good silage has the following general characteristics:

pH: 4.5		(1 mark)

Dm content: close to above 30%		(1 mark)
