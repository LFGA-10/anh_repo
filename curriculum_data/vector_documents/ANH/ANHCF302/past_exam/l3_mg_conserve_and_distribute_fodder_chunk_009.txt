Question 21: Describe the best practice of silo filling and compacting technique during silage making. 			        (5 marks)

Marking scheme:

Filling the Chopped fodder into the silos:

Filling the crop into the silo layer by layer. (1mark)

Compact the fodder all the time by continuous treading (stamping) (1mark) This helps to remove the air inside the silo. (1mark)

Try to fill your silo continuously in one day. (1mark)

(reference: LU3: Perform silage making, LO 3.8: Fill and compress silo., Curriculum level 3, Page 454-455, 2019

Question 22: Discuss the fodder crop drying methods. 	        (5 marks)

Marking scheme:

Fodder crop drying methods consist of:

Sun-drying: by exposing the fodder to the sun during a respected period. (2marks)

Oven drying: using a source of heat generated by the oven (1mark)

Chemical drying: (1mark) Drying agents: Potassium carbonate (1mark)

(reference: LU4: Perform hay making and storage, LO 4.2: Dry fodder crops., Curriculum level 3, Page 454-455, 2019
