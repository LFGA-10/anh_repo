8. What types of ruminant shelters are commonly used in production systems?

Answer: Ruminant shelters fall into different types, depending on the system:

Free or Cubicle Stalls Shelters: These are commonly used in intensive production systems, such as dairy farming, where cows are housed in individual cubicles, providing comfort and minimizing disease transmission. These are often equipped with bedding for comfort.

Tie Stalls Shelters: Cattle are tied to a fixed structure and are usually given limited space. These shelters are common in smaller, traditional, or semi-intensive systems. Tie stalls help manage individual animal health and feeding more closely.

9. How do you correct the design of a cowshed to improve productivity and welfare?

Answer: Correcting the design of a cowshed can help improve both productivity and the welfare of the animals. Key considerations include:

Space: Ensure cows have adequate space to move, lie down, and access feed and water.

Ventilation: Proper airflow is essential to prevent respiratory problems and maintain a comfortable temperature.

Comfort: Use proper bedding material (e.g., straw or sawdust) to keep the cows dry and comfortable.
