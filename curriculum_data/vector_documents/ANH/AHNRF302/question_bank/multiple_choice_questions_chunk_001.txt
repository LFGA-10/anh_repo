Multiple Choice Questions: Ruminant Farming

1. What is the main characteristic of ruminant animals? A) They have a single stomach B) They lay eggs C) They chew cud and have a four-compartment stomach D) They eat only meat ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Ruminants chew cud and have a stomach with four compartments—rumen, reticulum, omasum, and abomasum.

2. Which of the following is NOT a ruminant? A) Cow B) Goat C) Horse D) Sheep ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Horses are not ruminants; they have a cecum instead of a rumen.

3. What are the three feeding classifications of ruminants? A) Carnivores, Herbivores, Omnivores B) Grazers, Browsers, Intermediates C) Producers, Consumers, Decomposers D) Wild, Domestic, Hybrid ✅ Answer: B Explanation: Ruminants are classified as grazers, browsers, or intermediates based on their feeding habits.

4. Which compartment of the ruminant stomach is responsible for microbial fermentation? A) Abomasum B) Omasum C) Rumen D) Reticulum ✅ Answer: C Explanation: The rumen is the largest compartment and is responsible for microbial fermentation of fibrous feed.

5. Why are ruminants important in agriculture? A) They help pollinate crops B) They produce wool only C) They convert low-quality forage into high-value products like milk and meat D) They are used for transportation ✅ Answer: C Explanation: Ruminants efficiently convert fibrous plant material into valuable products like milk, meat, and hides.
