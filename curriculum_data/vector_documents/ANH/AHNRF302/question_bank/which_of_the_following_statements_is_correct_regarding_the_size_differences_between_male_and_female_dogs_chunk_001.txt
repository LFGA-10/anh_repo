Which of the following statements is correct regarding the size differences between male and female dogs? (2mark)
a) Males are usually smaller than females.
b) Males are usually larger than females.
c) There is no size difference between males and females.
d) Only giant breeds show size differences between males and females.

Answer : b) Males are usually larger than females.

2. How can a dog owner use a garden tool to keep the dog cool? (2 mark)
a) Spraying the dog with a garden hose
b) Using a garden rake to cool the dog down
c) Filling the garden with ice for the dog to walk on
d) Leaving the garden door open for airflow
Answer: a) Spraying the dog with a garden hose

3. To ensure proper drainage and prevent flooding, which of the following site features should you consider when selecting a location for a horse shelter?( 2mark)
a) Flat ground with no slope.
b) Elevated ground to avoid water accumulation.
c) A spot near a lake or river for easy water access.
d) A location with dense trees to block rain.
Answer: b) Elevated ground to avoid water accumulation.
