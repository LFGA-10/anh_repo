3. How does the socio-economic role of goats differ from that of cattle in various regions?

Answer:
Goats are important in many regions, particularly in developing countries, due to their low maintenance, ability to thrive in varied climates, and adaptability to rough terrains. Their socio-economic role includes:

Milk and Meat: Goats are primarily raised for their milk, which is easier to digest than cow’s milk, and their meat, which is lean and in high demand in various parts of the world.

Fiber and Hides: Certain goat breeds, like the Angora and Cashmere goats, are raised for their fibers, which are turned into high-quality wool products.

Manure: Goats provide manure that can be used for fertilizing crops, making them integral to sustainable farming systems.

Compared to cattle, goats require less space, feed, and water, making them more suited to smaller farms or nomadic pastoral systems.
