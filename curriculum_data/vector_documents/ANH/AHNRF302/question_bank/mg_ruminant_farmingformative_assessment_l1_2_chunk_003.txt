Explain why considering the humidity of the environment is important when selecting a site for a ruminant shelter.

Answer: Animals are generally better suited to a dry atmosphere.

Moisture in the environment is favorable for the development of disease-causing agents, meaning many diseases are favored by high humidity.

Open Question (1)

Discuss the role of ventilation in ruminant shelters, including the three ways it can be realized and an example of its use in cattle barns during both summer and winter.

Answer:

Role of Ventilation: Ventilation plays an important role in thermal regulation.

                          Ample air movement without a draft is essential. It works by bringing in

fresh air (oxygen) and removing stale air (excess moisture, ammonia, or carbon dioxide).

Ways to Realize Ventilation: Ventilation can be realized in three ways:

Using forced ventilation.

Natural ventilation.

The combination of both kinds of ventilation.

Use in Cattle Barns: The most commonly used system in cattle barns is natural ventilation.

Summer Use: During the summer, especially when the outside temperature is high and the outside air velocity is low, an additional forced ventilation system may be used. This is done to achieve more air movement in the animal zone to improve the heat dissipation of the animals.
