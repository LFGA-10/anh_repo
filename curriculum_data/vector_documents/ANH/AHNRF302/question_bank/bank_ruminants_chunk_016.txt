5. How does a Ranching system work in ruminant production?

Answer: Ranching is a form of non-traditional livestock production system where large herds of ruminants (e.g., cattle or sheep) are raised on extensive land. Unlike traditional or agro-pastoral systems, ranching often involves large, fenced properties, where animals graze in a controlled manner, with minimal human intervention. It is common in regions with large land areas and is focused on producing meat or other livestock products on a commercial scale.

6. What are the key features of a Rotational System in ruminant production?

Answer: A Rotational System involves rotating livestock through different pastures or grazing areas to prevent overgrazing and allow for pasture regeneration. The system has several benefits:

It helps maintain soil fertility by avoiding overuse of a single area.

It allows for better management of pasture resources.

It reduces the risk of disease spread, as livestock are regularly moved to new areas.

It can increase productivity by maintaining high-quality forage throughout the year.
