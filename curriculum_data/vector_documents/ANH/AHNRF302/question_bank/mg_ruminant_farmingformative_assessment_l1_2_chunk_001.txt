Multiple Choice Questions (3)

Which of the following is

not a characteristic of a good site for ruminant shelters?

a) Easy accessibility to the main road. b) Easy accessibility to feed and water supply. c) Near creeks that overflow in the rainy season. d) Land cleared of bushes and rocks.

Answer: c) Near creeks that overflow in the rainy season.

What is the primary characteristic that distinguishes

Bos indicus (zebus) cattle from Bos taurus cattle?

a) High yield of meat and milk. b) Absence of a hump. c) Presence of a prominent hump. d) Requires high management.

Answer: c) Presence of a prominent hump.

The main purpose of a

crush in a ruminant shelter is to:

a) Provide drinking water to animals. b) Hold an animal still to minimize the risk of injury during work. c) Grind feed for better nutrient intake. d) Keep cows, sheep, and goats cool to prevent heat stress.

Answer: b) Hold an animal still to minimize the risk of injury during work.

Multiple Choice Questions (2)

Which of the following is a selection criterion for the site of a ruminant shelter, related to the soil?

a) The ground should be as impermeable as possible. b) The ground should have a steep slope to facilitate cleaning. c) The soil must be sufficiently strong for proper manure collection. d) The site should be dominated by heavy noise.

Answer: c) The soil must be sufficiently strong for proper manure collection.

A ruminant shelter that fails to insulate or ventilate properly often results in the accumulation of

what on the walls and ceiling in cool weather?

a) Ammonia. b) Carbon dioxide. c) Moisture. d) Sunlight.

Answer: c) Moisture.
