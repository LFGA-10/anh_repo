Section A: Remembering & Understanding (10 marks)

1. Multiple Choice – Choose correct answers (4 marks)

Which of the following are true regarding the reproductive cycle of a cow?
a) The estrous cycle averages 21 days.
b) Ovulation occurs about 12 hours after the end of standing heat.
c) The corpus luteum regresses due to high progesterone levels.
d) Progesterone inhibits LH release during diestrus.
e) Estrus is initiated by a surge in estrogen.

✅ Correct answers: a, b, d, e

2. Fill in the Blanks (4 marks)

Complete the following sentences:

The process of mating animals of different breeds is called cross-breeding.

Fecundity refers to the ability to produce offspring frequently and in large numbers.

The placenta is the life-support system for the developing fetus.

Dystocia refers to difficult birth requiring assistance.

✅ Answers: cross-breeding, Fecundity, placenta, Dystocia
