Fill-in-the-Gap Questions and Answers

Ruminants are characterized by their ______ stomachs and ______ behavior. → Answer: four; cud-chewing

The cud is a food bolus that has been ______. → Answer: regurgitated

Species such as cows, goats, and deer are examples of ______ animals. → Answer: ruminant

Grazers like cattle and buffalo consume mostly ______ quality grasses. → Answer: lower

Browsers such as moose and mule deer eat highly nutritious ______ and ______. → Answer: twigs; shrubs

Intermediates like goats have nutritional requirements midway between ______ and ______. → Answer: grazers; browsers

The four compartments of a ruminant stomach are the rumen, reticulum, ______, and ______. → Answer: omasum; abomasum

Horses are not ruminants but have a ______ that performs a similar function. → Answer: cecum

Bos taurus cattle are characterized by the absence of a ______. → Answer: hump

Bos taurus breeds generally have ______ resistance to tropical diseases and parasites. → Answer: less

Bos taurus cattle require ______ management and produce high yields of ______ and ______. → Answer: high; meat; milk

Friesian/Holstein cattle were originally bred in ______. → Answer: North Holland
