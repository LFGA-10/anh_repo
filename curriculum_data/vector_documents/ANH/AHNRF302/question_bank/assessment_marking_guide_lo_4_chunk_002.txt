7. Open-ended – Short Answer (4 marks)

Explain the difference between eutocia and dystocia, and give one cause of dystocia in heifers.

✅ Answer:

Eutocia: Normal birth without complications or human intervention.

Dystocia: Difficult birth requiring assistance.

Cause in heifers: Excessive fetal size relative to the birth canal, often due to mating with a larger bull.

8. Open-ended – Scenario-based (3 marks)

A cow has not expelled the placenta within 24 hours after calving. What steps would you take to manage this situation?

✅ Answer:

Restrain the cow.

Wash hands/arms and wear gloves.

Gently attempt manual removal; if difficult, stop pulling.

Administer intra-uterine antibiotics.

Encourage calf to suckle to stimulate oxytocin release.

Monitor for fever or systemic infection.
