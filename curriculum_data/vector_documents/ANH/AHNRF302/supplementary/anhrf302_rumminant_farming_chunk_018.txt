B.2. Mérinos
 Origin: Spain but was developed in Australia.
 Phenotypic characteristics:
 Merino is medium sized sheep, hornless;
 Its wool is white all over its body;
 The body weight of adult female is about 70 kg and ram from 120 to 140 kg for meaty
bread. For dairy breeds, ewe weighs 40-60 kg.
Figure 13 : Mérinos sheep
 Genotypic characteristics :
 This is a hardy wool sheep yielding 6 kg in ewes and 8 kg in rams;
 It is a good diseases resistant;
 It is slow maturing with a light narrow body;
 It will survive in the harsher, drier areas;
 Little tolerant to high air moisture content
