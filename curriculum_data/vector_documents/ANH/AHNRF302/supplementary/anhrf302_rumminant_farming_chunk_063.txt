 Uterine prolapse reduction
E.1. Definition
Uterine prolapse is a common spontaneous condition in cattle, sheep, goats and other animals occurring
immediately after parturition where the uterus comes out through the vulva. Among the cause of
uterine prolapse include:
 A large calf in anterior presentation that may have been hip locked for some time. When there
was too much effort to make it deliver, an incidence is getting higher.
 In older cows, in a case of hypocalcaemia because of less concentration of uterine muscle.
