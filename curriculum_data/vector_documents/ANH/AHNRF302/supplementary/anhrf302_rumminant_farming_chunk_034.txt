 Content/Topic 2.Tips to design small ruminants shelter
A. Characteristics of good small ruminant shelters
 Housing for small ruminants should provide protection from winter weather.
 Small ruminant barns or sheds are often open to the south
 The barns must be dry, draft free and well ventilated
 The windows of the sheds should provide an adequate light
 Small ruminants shelter should provide access to pens for cleaning
 Doors should be wide so that animals do not crowd each other as they go through.
B. Main parts of small ruminant shelters
 Floor: the ground floors may be made of concrete, murram or compacted earth. The floor
should be regularly cleaned to avoid accumulation of parasitic eggs and larvae in small
ruminant house.
 Walls: imitating a room to another and provide fencing structure to the ruminant against
divagation and escaping motion.
 Roof: protection from rain fall sunshine
C. Dimensions and spaces requirements for floor:
The barn type shed is wide open, the peripheral wall having a height of 1.20 m. Its surface is
12mx6m for 60 adult sheep or goats.
