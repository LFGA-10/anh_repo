A.5. Feeding dry cow
The nutrients needs of dry cows are generally not as high as during lactation. Over feeding will result in
cows getting too fat during the dry period. Limit the amount of corn silage fed because of its high energy
content. The following conditions must be respected.
 Maintain a total dry matter intake during this period at about 2%of body weight
 Roughage intake at about 1% of body weight
 The amount of grain depends on the quality and type of forage fed
 Supplementation with Vitamin A, D, minerals such Ca and P reduce problems with retained
placenta and milk fever.
