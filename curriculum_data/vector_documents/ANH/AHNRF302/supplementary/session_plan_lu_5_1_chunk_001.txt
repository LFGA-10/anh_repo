Duration of the session: 120 mins

Objectives: At the end of this session the learner should be able to:

Identify the nutritional requirements of cattle at different stages (growth, lactation, maintenance).

Calculate appropriate feed rations for cattle using concentrates, forages, and supplements.

Demonstrate correct feeding practices to ensure optimal health and productivity of ruminants.

Recognize the relationship between proper feeding and successful reproduction management.

Apply practical skills in distributing cattle feed efficiently and safely.
