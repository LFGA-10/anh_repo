 Content/Topic 3: Mating preparation
A. Heat induction
 Flushing
It is feeding practice carried out in female animals. Towards the oestrus period, female animals are fed
on extra nutritious feeds before they are served, to ensure that during ovulation, many eggs are
released. As a result, when fertilization takes place, many offspring are produced.
 Male presence:
The male presence in a herd of flock is very important to heat stimulation but feeding and managerial
cost is added when this system is optionally chosen.
 Oestrus synchronization
A management technique that makes use of hormones to control or reschedule the estrous cycle. This is
useful to group females for parturition (calving interval), shorten breeding and reduce estrus detection.
B. Heat detection
 Signs of heat include:
 Standing to be mounted (standing heat)
 Mounting other animals
 Restlessness and nervousness
 Clear mucous vaginal discharge
 Reduction of feed intake
 Licking and smelling other animals
 Frequent urination
C. Heat observation timing
The farmers must visit their farms and observe any morphological or physiological change at the vulva,
secretion with any strange behavior. This visit should be done every morning and evening for every day
in all mature herd and flock.
