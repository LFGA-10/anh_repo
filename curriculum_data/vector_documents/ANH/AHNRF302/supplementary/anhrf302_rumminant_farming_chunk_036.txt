 Drying Off Dairy Animals
Due to the amount of milk they produce, the drying-off process is often more complicated for dairy
animals (cattle, goats and some sheep). Preparation for dry off should begin at least two weeks prior to
the dry-off date with a significant change in the animal’s diet. Slowly reducing the energy content of the
diet and feeding primarily a high-fiber diet will reduce the nutrients available for the animal to make
milk; this is often all that is needed to reduce milk production to a level that makes dry off safe and
simple.
 Fecundity
Fecundity of animal is the ability to produce offspring’s frequently and in large number. Other authors
define fecundity as an aptitude/ performance of females’ animals individually or in herd of producing a
fertilized semen or ovum able to be fertilized.
