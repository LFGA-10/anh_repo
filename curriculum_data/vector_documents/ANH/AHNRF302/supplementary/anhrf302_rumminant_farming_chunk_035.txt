 Content/Topic 1.Control zootechnical parameters
A. Growth
Ruminant growth is the increasing in size depending on age and feed, depending from a multiplication
of living cells of the body.
A.1. The cattle growth stages are:
 Calf: young (male or female) cow from birth to 6 months.
 Heifer: young female cow over 6 months until her first calving.
 Young bull: young male cow from 18 to 24 months.
 Cow: adult female of 2 years and more.
 Bull; adult male and breeder.
A.2. Goats growth stages are:
 Kid: refers to the goat from birth until the age of 6 months.
 Goat or doe: is the female adult goat, in breeding age (from 7 months).
 Buck (or Billy goat): is the male adult goat, in breeding age (from 7 months)
A.3. Sheep growth stages:
 Lamb (male or female) gimmer lamb (female): it is the male and the female of sheep aged 0 to
4 months.
 Hog (male) / gimmer lamb (female): it is the male and the female of sheep after weaning
between 5-9 months.
 Ewe: is the female adult in breeding age.
