LO 3.2 – Identify ruminant shelter
 Content/Topic 1: Free/Cubicles and Tie stalls shelters
A. Free/cubicles stalls shelter
 Concepts
The cows are not tied up, they can walk around freely. In this type of housing there is usually a loafing
area and a lying area, with a feeding area separated from the lying area. The habitat is cut up in 3 parts:
a feeding area covered, a rest area covered and an exercise area not covered.
 Purpose
The cows are forced to walk frequently and this help in getting a good manure.
 Advantage:
 The manure is spread over a large floor (high quantity of the manure) because there is
accumulation of coarse straw on the rest area and disposal of manure once or twice a year.
 Each animals have access to feed and rest area
 The animals are free in room
 Disadvantages:
 Contamination of udder caused by accumulation of manure spread over a large floor
 Application of high quantity of straw for mulching (litter).
 Demand a large area.
