Construction Materials for Shelters

Objectives: At the end of this session the learner should be able to:

Apply selection criteria for choosing shelter sites.

Explain environmental and animal welfare considerations in site selection.

 Identify suitable construction materials for ruminant shelters.

Differentiate between types of wall and floor materials used in shelter construction.
