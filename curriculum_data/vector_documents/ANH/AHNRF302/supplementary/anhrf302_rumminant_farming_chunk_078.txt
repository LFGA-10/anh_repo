For spring/summer breeding programs, this could be meadow regrowth or brome grass pasture. Their
supplemental feeding regime can be equated to the program for bred-yearling heifers. These bulls are
growing rapidly, in addition to replacing the condition they lost during the breeding pasture. Extra care
and feed of yearling bulls after the breeding season should increase their longevity. These bulls should
also have a Breeding Soundness Evaluation well before the start of the breeding season.
