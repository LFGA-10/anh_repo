A.2. Feeding heifers
Good quality forage is the basis for feeding heifers during this period. Some grain may be need for
proper growth. A grain of 0.72-0.8kg per day is desirable. Heifers on mature or heavy grazed pasture will
need additional feed. Be sure the ration meets the mineral and vitamin needs of the heifer.
A.3. Feeding pregnant cow
The cow in pregnant period must fed properly because feeding during the last 2 months of gestation is
especially important and the condition of the heifer at calving will strongly influence the amount of milk
produced during the first lactation. The heifer should be growing more rapidly during the last 2 or 3
months of gestation.
Feeding grains on 6 weeks before calving at the rate of 1% of body weight. The balanced diet is
important. Limitation of the salt in the diet during the last mounts of pregnant must be considered
because the excess o salt can cause udder edema.
