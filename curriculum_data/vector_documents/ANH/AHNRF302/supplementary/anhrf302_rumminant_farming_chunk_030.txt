A. Permanent free stall shelters for small ruminants
Small ruminants need to be provided with a reasonable shelter to protect them from rain and other
factors. Goats in particular are less tolerant to rain and will more readily seek shelter in wet weather.
 Free/Cubicles stall shelters
 Concepts
The small ruminant are not tied up, they can walk around freely. In this type of housing there is usually a
loafing area and a lying area, with a feeding area separated from the lying area. The habitat is cut up in 3
parts: a feeding area covered, a rest area covered and an exercise area not covered.
 Purpose
The small ruminant are forced to walk frequently and this help in getting a good manure.
 Advantage:
The manure is spread over a large floor (high quantity of the manure) because there is
accumulation of coarse straw on the rest area and disposal of manure once or twice a year
Each animals have access to feed and rest area
The animals are free in room
 Disadvantages:
Contamination of udder caused by accumulation of manure spread over a large floor
Application of high quantity of straw for mulching (litter).
Demand a large area.
