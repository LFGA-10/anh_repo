 Content/Topic 6: Small ruminants’ Fattening shelter
 Concepts
It is a barn designed for rest, feeding, and for some care.
 Purpose
To allow the small ruminants a required place for qualitative feeding with a rapid growth and a finisher
feeding before slaughter.
 Advantages:
 The animals are well kept with lower or no loss of energy.
 The barn avoid harmful contamination for animal
 Disadvantages:
 The animal are not free in movement,
 Skeleton is not strong enough due to little physical exercise.
