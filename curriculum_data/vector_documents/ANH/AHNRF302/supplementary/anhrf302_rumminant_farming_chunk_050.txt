C.3. Infertility
Causes of infertility
 Poor feeding affecting physiological function;
 Immature and infertile spermatozoa;
 Infections in the reproductive systems or reproductive diseases;
 Poor insemination techniques;
 Free martin: when twin calves of different sexes are born, the male is fertile while the female is
infertile. Its reproductive system is deformed and it cannot conceive. This is a free martin.
 Abnormal embryo that fails to implant (dead foetus )
 Deposition of fats which block the fallopian tubes
D.Gestation
The gestation period is the time during which the animal is pregnant. During pregnancy the fetus
develops in the uterus. The fetus is surrounded by a watery fluid enclosed in membranes. Blood vessels
in the umbilical cord supply nutrients and oxygen and carry off waste products.
D.1. Development of fetal membranes (chorio-amniotic membranes)
Glossary, the placenta consists of layers of soft membranes that form two fluid-filled sacs (amnion and
allantois) around the developing fetus.
D.2.Amnion (inner layer):
This is the layer immediately around the fetus. It forms a sac around the fetus called amniotic sac.
D.3. Allantois: is another layer surrounding the amniotic sac which forms the fluid-filled called allantoic
sac.
