B. Post parturition cares for new born
B.1. Breathing stimulation
As soon as a baby animal is born, make sure that it is breathing.
 If the baby is having difficulty breathing, hang it upside down. Rub the chest to make any fluid in
the lungs come out of the nose;
 Lift the new-born animal up by the back legs for a minute or so to let mucus and fluid drain out
from the lungs. You can hold the back legs of a small animal and swing it round to make mucus
come out of the nose;
 You can also c lean mucous away from the mouth and nostril by hand.
 Pull the new-born animal to where the mother can reach it easily to lick it dry and put the new-
born so that it sits up.
 Put a piece of dry grass up the baby animal’s nose. This makes it cough then it starts breathing.
 Take the weight to determine the birth weight
