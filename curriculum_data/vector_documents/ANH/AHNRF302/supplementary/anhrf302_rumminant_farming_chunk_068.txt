 Cleaning and Sanitation

residues of milk, disinfection kills most harmful bacteria. Clean the containers as follows: Rinse with cold

B. Milk collection techniques
There are two methods used to remove milk from the udder of the cow and these are hand and
machine milking
B.1.Hand milking
The milker squats and ties the hind legs with milker’s rope, washes the udder with warm water and dries
it up with a clean towel. He then applies a salve on the teats to avoid cracking.
The thumb finger is placed along the teat and the index finger closes the top of the teat. Then pressure
is exerted on the teat by squeezing the teat rhythmically. Milk trapped in the teat cistern is drawn
downwards into the container. Avoid pulling the teats during milking as this may lead to milk hold-up.
B.2. Machine Milking
A milking machine is a common equipment on large dairy farms where hand milking may be
impossible .a milking machine is composed of the vacuum supply unit, the pulsator, the vacuum
controller, the teat cup, the teat cup linear and milk receiving unit.
