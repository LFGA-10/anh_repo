C. Brahman

Origin: Is an exotic breed developed in USA using the following breed: Guzerat, Nellone and Nir.

Phenotypic characteristics:

The coat color ranges between light grey, red and black. Bulls have darker shoulders, thighs and neck regions. Mature bulls weigh between 720-900 kg while the cows are 440-630 kg.

They have a prominent hump, dewlap and drooping ears.

Figure 6 : Brahman bull (male and female)

Genotypic characteristics:

They have the fast growth rate which makes them good beef cattle;

They are tolerant to excess heat and diseases

LO 1.2 – Identify goat breeds

 Content/Topic 1: Main characteristics of goat breed

There are many breeds of goats recognized over the world but, we are detailing the most important goat breads farmed in Africa especially in Rwanda and east Africa.

A. Local breed

Origin: The local breed is originated from East African.

Phenotypic characteristics:

It is an animal of small size with well-proportioned body whose females are thin and males more stocks.

Its hair is short and fine.

The body weight of males is 25 kg while the female is 22 kg.

The coat color varies from black to white but may be brown mottled.

The horns are small and light while ears are short, pointed and lie horizontally.

Figure 7 : Local breed goat

Genotypic characteristics

It is a meat breed only
