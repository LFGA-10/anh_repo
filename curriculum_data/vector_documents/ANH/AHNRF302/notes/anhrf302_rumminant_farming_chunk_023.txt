LO 3.2 – Identify ruminant shelter

 Content/Topic 1: Free/Cubicles and Tie stalls shelters

A. Free/cubicles stalls shelter

Concepts

The cows are not tied up, they can walk around freely. In this type of housing there is usually a loafing area and a lying area, with a feeding area separated from the lying area. The habitat is cut up in 3 parts: a feeding area covered, a rest area covered and an exercise area not covered.  Purpose

The cows are forced to walk frequently and this help in getting a good manure.

Advantage:

The manure is spread over a large floor (high quantity of the manure) because there is accumulation of coarse straw on the rest area and disposal of manure once or twice a year.

Each animals have access to feed and rest area

The animals are free in room  Disadvantages:

Contamination of udder caused by accumulation of manure spread over a large floor  Application of high quantity of straw for mulching (litter).

Demand a large area.

B. Tie stalls shelters

Concepts

It is a barn in which each animal (cattle) is attached and has a definite place, designed for rest, feeding, milking and for some care.

Purpose

To have the cows in roofed shelter with individual stall.

The cow is tied to prevent the motion.
