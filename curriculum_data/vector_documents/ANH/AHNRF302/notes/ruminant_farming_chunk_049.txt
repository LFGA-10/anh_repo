B. Mating planning and purpose
To plan any mating condition in a ruminant farm is based on
multiple factors mainly:
• Feed availability
• Market
• Farm managerial capacity
• Content/Topic 3: Mating preparation
A. Heat induction
is the process of artificially bringing a female animal into
estrus (heat)
• Flushing
It is feeding practice carried out in female animals. Towards the
oestrus period, female animals are fed on extra nutritious feeds
before they are served, to ensure that during ovulation, many eggs
are released. As a result, when fertilization takes place, many
offspring are produced.
• Male presence:
The male presence in a herd of flock is very important to heat
stimulation but feeding and managerial cost is added when this
system is optionally chosen.
