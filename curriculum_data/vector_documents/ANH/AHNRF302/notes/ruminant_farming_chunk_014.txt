• Nature of the soil

The soil type will determine the type of foundations that are
necessary, and may also provide materials for walls. The land
should be cleared of bushes, rocks and any other obstructions.
Avoid damp areas or near creeks over flowing in rainy reason. The
slope of land and drainage should be considered so as to avoid
flooded; conversely, it is difficult to construct a building on land
that slope too much. The ground should be as permeable as
possible to avoid mud and puddles of matter near the buildings. It
must be sufficiently strong to ensure proper collection of urine and
manure to facilitate cleaning and proper disinfection. It must be
resistant to insulation. It should not be slippery to man and
animals. It is essential to give the soil a slight slope (1to2 cm/m2).
• Temperature
In tropical regions, the problem is to protect animals against
temperature and excessive sunlight. The room temperature
depends on the outside temperature of nature, the wall thickness
of the material (the insulating wall).The orientation allows
buildings protection against too heavy sun stroke, or against the
rain and winds
• Wind direction and air supply
