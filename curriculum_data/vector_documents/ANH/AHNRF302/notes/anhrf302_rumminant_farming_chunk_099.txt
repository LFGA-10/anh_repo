Content/Topic 3. Management of vitamins deficiency A. Multi vitamins injection

Figure 46 : Multi vitamins

Multivite is indicated for the prevention and treatment of vitamin deficiencies in animals particularly during periods of illness, convalescence and general unthriftiness.

Contra Indications: None

Application Rate

Cattle Application: For intramuscular or subcutaneous injection  Dose rates/directions for use:

Cattle: 20-30 ml, Calves: 5-10 ml

The injection may be repeated at intervals of 10-14 days.

Sheep & Goats Application: For intramuscular or subcutaneous injection.

 Dose rates/directions for use: Sheep: 5-10 ml Lambs: 2-5mlGoats: 5-10 ml
