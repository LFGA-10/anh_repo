Design cow shed
A. Characteristics of a
goodcowshed
A good cowshed should have the following characteristics:
• It should provide an internal environment that allows the
animals to produce at their best, so it should be neither too
hot nor too cold.
• It should protect livestock against direct sun light cold
winds and excessive heat.
• It should be well ventilated.
• It should protect livestock against predators, other vermin
and thieves.
• It should be easy to clean and to disinfect.
• The structures within the house should be easy to adjust
and/or remove so that a farmer can quickly adapt the
house for another use without paying a lot of money for
renovation.

and enter, and so that services such as water and
