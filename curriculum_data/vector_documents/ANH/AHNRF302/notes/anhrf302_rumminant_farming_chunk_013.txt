B.1. Galla goat

Origin : East Africa

Phenotypic characteristics :

Thin and small goat, completely white, relatively small head with concave profile;

Ears with medium length, little erect and slender;

Long neck;

Long limbs;

Body weight varies between 35-55 kg for buck and between 30 to 45 kg for goat;

Horns are relatively short and turned outwards.

Figure 8 : Galla goat

Genotypic characteristics

It has the good aptitude for fattening, thus it meat breed;

This breed is adapted to high temperatures in hot regions.

B.2. Boer

Origin : South Africa. It is a crossbreed of European, Angora and Indian breeds to increase meat size and productivity.

Phenotypic characteristics:

It is white on the body with a brown-red head and white line on the snout, with long ears and hair;

Ears are pendulous;

Have prominent horns;

It is a medium size goat: 50 to 70 kg for female and 80-100 kg for male.

Figure 9 : Boer goat

Genotypic characteristics :

Very prolific breeds’ kidding rate of 1.5, 50% twins and 7% triplets.

Mean daily milk is 1.2- 1.8 kg ; with average milk yield over 160 kg / lactation period of 120 days

Butter fat (BF) Content is about 5.6%

Valuable for meat, milk and skin but has been promoted for its meat potential.

This goat is well adapted to tropical conditions but it hardly tolerates too moist air. B.3. Alpine
