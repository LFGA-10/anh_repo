Weaning calves

Weaning is a period when a young animal stops depending on its mother. Calves are usually weaned between 8-10 weeks of age. With good management and proper feeding they may be weaned between 3-8 weeks of age. In other situations, the two stay together for some time before the separation. In preparation for that stage, the young one is slowly introduced to solid food as well as searching for food. This enables the dam to prepare for the next gestation period.

From the second week onward a calf should be able to eat small amounts of good quality feed such as young grass. This is necessary for the development of the rumen. Fresh, clean water should also be available.

A.2. Feeding heifers

Good quality forage is the basis for feeding heifers during this period. Some grain may be need for proper growth. A grain of 0.72-0.8kg per day is desirable. Heifers on mature or heavy grazed pasture will need additional feed. Be sure the ration meets the mineral and vitamin needs of the heifer.

A.3. Feeding pregnant cow

The cow in pregnant period must fed properly because feeding during the last 2 months of gestation is especially important and the condition of the heifer at calving will strongly influence the amount of milk produced during the first lactation. The heifer should be growing more rapidly during the last 2 or 3 months of gestation.
