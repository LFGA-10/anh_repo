C. Brown Suiss

Origin: A native of Switzerland and it is the oldest of the pure dairy cattle

Phenotypic characteristics: The skin color is pure brown varying from light to dark pigmentation. The nose and tail are black. They have strong legs. The head is short and large. The female may weight 600-700 kg body weight while a male weight from 900-1000 kg body weight.

Figure 3 : Brown Suiss cows

Genotypic characteristics:

It is a dairy and meaty breed.

The milk production can reach 5000 kg / lactation of 300 days the milk of this breed has high protein rate.

It has resistance to the heat to the cold and food shortage

It can well survive on high altitude of more than 4000 meters

Content/Topic 2: Main characteristics of Boss indicus (zebus)

The characteristics of Boss indicus are:

A prominent hump;

A large surface area per unit of body weight, due to a well-developed dewlap, hump and large ears; this enables them to lose heat quickly;

Superior adaptation to high temperatures and intense solar radiation;

Late maturity and rather low fertility;

Ability to live on poor quality fodder;

Greater stress-tolerance to shortage of water;

Resistance to tropical diseases and parasites;

Long face;

Long or short horns;

Long legs and developed naval flap.

Uganda in the district of Tori; Kigezi and Ankole
