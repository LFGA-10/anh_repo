B. Tie stalls shelters
• Concepts
It is a barn in which each animal (cattle) is attached and has a
definite place, designed for rest, feeding, milking and for some
care.
• Purpose
✓ To have the cows in roofed shelter with individual stall.
✓ The cow is tied to prevent the motion.
• Advantages
✓ The animals are separated or in groups: adult males and
females, heifers, calves individually.
✓ The animal is tied to give place to control his behavior and to
give to each according to his needs.
✓ The barn avoids harmful contamination for animal
Disadvantages
✓ The animal is not free in stall
