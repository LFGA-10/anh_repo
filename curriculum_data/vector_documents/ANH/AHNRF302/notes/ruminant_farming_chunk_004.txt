B. Jersey
• Origin: Jersey breed is originated on the Islands in
the English Channel
• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ A small fine-skinned hardy animal.
✓ Its color is various shades of fawn and white; normally
has black ring around the eyes.
✓ Bulls can be blackish in color.
✓ The average weight of adult is 350-450 kg.
• Genotypic characteristics
Early maturing and with excellent dairy characteristics. Although
the milk yield of breed is not high, (4000l / lactation) the breed is
popular all over the world. There are several reasons for this
popularity: a high percentage of butterfat in the milk compared to
other breeds.
