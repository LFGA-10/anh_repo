A new-born animal can only use antibodies from colostrum within six hours after it is born. After the animal is about three weeks old it starts to make own antibodies. It is very important for a new-born animal to get enough colostrum to drink from mother in the first few hours of its life.

In addition, make sure the baby animal sucks its mother’s teat as soon as possible. When the mother feels a baby sucking, her brain releases a hormone called oxytocin into her blood that makes milk flow from udder. It also makes the uterus contract and pushes the placenta out.

C. Post parturition care for dam

The dam may need some special attention soon after parturition such as:  C.1.Check delivering:

For all animals, the afterbirth must be expelled within 24 hours and the animal should not consume it. By allowing the dam to exercise (walking around) or provision of warm water to the dam after calving, expulsion of the afterbirth is accelerated If retained, the dam may die or become infertile due to rotting. C.2.Check haemorrhage and tear:

Post-partum cares for the dam also consist of checking tears and haemorrhages caused by forced traction of the foetus.
