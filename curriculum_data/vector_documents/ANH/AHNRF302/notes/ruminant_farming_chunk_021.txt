Those pastures must be naturally rich to satisfy animal feed
requirements. In addition, veterinary monitoring is difficult
because animals are released, and it is often limited to prophylaxis
(vaccines). For those two reasons, it is usually local breeds that are
raised in that way because they are resistant to local epidemics
and require less feed than high-yielding exotic breeds.
Nevertheless, theft risks are very high since they are not
continually monitored by the farmer.
