B. Main parts of small ruminant shelters
✓ Floor: the ground floors may be made of concrete, murram
or compacted earth. The floor should be regularly
cleaned to avoid accumulation of parasitic eggs and larvae
in small ruminant house.
✓ Walls: imitating a room to another and provide fencing
structure to the ruminant against divagation and escaping
motion.
✓ Roof: protection from rain fall, sunshine
