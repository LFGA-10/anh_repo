Pure breed and cross-breed animals.
It is important to understand the differences between pure
breeding and cross breeding.

A. Pure breeding: is the mating of organisms that belong to the
same breed (animals) or variety (plants). This means that any of
the offspring of pure-bred parent pair is extremely likely to show
the same traits as any other offspring of the same parent pair.
B. Cross-breeding
It is the mating of plants or animals that do not belong to the same
breed or variety.
B.1.Heterosis effect
Crossbreds often perform at a higher level than the average
performance of the purebreds that make up the crossbred. The
offspring produced perform better than parents.
Example for cross-breeding would be mating a Friesian bull
with a Sahiwal cow.
B.2. Cross breeding purpose
Introduction of good genes formerly missing in the herd. This may
result in the development of a new breed. The offspring of cross-
breeding usually perform better than either of the parents. This is
known as hybrid vigour or heterosis. Diluting effect of bad treats
found in a particular breed, for example, genetic diseases.
