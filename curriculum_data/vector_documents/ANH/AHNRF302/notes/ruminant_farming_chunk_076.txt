B.2. Machine Milking
A milking machine is a common equipment on large dairy farms
where hand milking may be impossible .a milking machine is
composed of the vacuum supply unit, the pulsator, the vacuum
controller, the teat cup, the teat cup linear and milk receiving unit.
Vacuum supply creates vacuum within the entire system and this
is the principle on which the machine works. When the teats cups
are put on the teats, the vacuum pump creates a vacuum inside
the rubber teat cup liners as well as between the rubber liners and
the metal in shells. As a result, the vacuum created holds the teat
cup in place, the teat canal opens up and milk follows out.
In order to avoid injury to the animal, the pulsator ratio should be
set according to the manufacturer’s instructions. The pulsation
ratio is the time the inhalation is in milking phase compared to the
resting phase. The vacuum controller is a valve in the vacuum
supply unit, which performs the following during milking.
Machine milking has the advantages of clean milk production,
saving labour and it is quick. However, costs of instalment are
high, faulty machines can injury the animals, there are higher
chances of mastitis spread through teat cup and power failure can
affect milking.
