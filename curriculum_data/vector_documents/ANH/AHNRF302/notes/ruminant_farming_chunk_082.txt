• Feeding milk replacers
After 3 days the calf may be feed whole milk or milk replacer. The
quantity of 2.3 kg is fed to small breed calves and large breed
calves are fed about 3.2 kg. Feed this amount for 3 weeks on an
early weaning program and for 4 to 5 weeks on a liberal milk
program.
