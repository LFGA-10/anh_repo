• Oestrus synchronization

A management technique that makes use of hormones to control
or reschedule the estrous cycle. This is useful to group females for
parturition (calving interval), shorten breeding and reduce estrus
detection.
B. Heat detection
 Signs of heat include:
✓ Standing to be mounted (standing heat)
✓ Mounting other animals
✓ Restlessness and nervousness
✓ Clear mucous vaginal discharge
✓ Reduction of feed intake
✓ Licking and smelling other animals
✓ Frequent urination
C. Heat observation timing
The farmers must visit their farms and observe any morphological
or physiological change at the vulva, secretion with any strange
behavior. This visit should be done every morning and evening for
every day in all mature herd and flock.
