Some species and individuals may not be suitable for tethering.
Animals should never be tethered in conditions where they are
vulnerable to extreme weather.
These guidelines have been developed to assist people to tether
animals correctly when circumstances make it a necessary method
of confining and protecting animals. They specify the requirements
for tethering dogs, sheep, goats, cattle, donkeys and horses.
