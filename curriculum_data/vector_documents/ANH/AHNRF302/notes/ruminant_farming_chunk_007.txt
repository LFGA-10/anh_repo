• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ The Ankole cattle are animals of an average to superior
stature; with a straight back; a rounded hump which is
nevertheless hardly noticeable in the female.

✓ The horns are long and fine but there are varieties without
horns or with short horns and others with very developed
horns (Inyambo cattles).
✓ Uniform colored coats are most frequent mostly being dark-
red brown or dark red and sometimes red pie.
✓ Bulls weigh between 330-500 kg while the cows are 200-410
kg.
• Genotypic characteristics: Ankole breed produces good
quality milk up to 1100 liters per lactation (180 days) and the
beef is of relatively good quality. It greatly tolerates heats,
parasites and diseases (tick borne diseases), thus can adapt to
a wide range of climates
