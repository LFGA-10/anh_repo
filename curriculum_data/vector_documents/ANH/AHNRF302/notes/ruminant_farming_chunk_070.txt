 Post-partum metritis treatment
 Metritis
A clear or blood-stained red/brown discharge often comes from the
vagina after a healthy animal has given birth. It does not smell
foul. But a white /yellow discharge or a bad smell is a sign of
infection in the uterus. This is called metritis and animal
sometimes get it soon after they have given birth.
Infection gets into the uterus through the vulva and vagina,
especially when an animal gives birth in dirty place. Animals often
get metritis when the placenta does not come out.
