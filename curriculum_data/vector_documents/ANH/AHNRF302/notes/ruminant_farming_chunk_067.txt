Haemorrhage and tear management
C.1. Haemorrhage
Cows giving birth in the delivery process because of improper or
Midwifery uterus and ruptured blood vessel damage resulting in
massive blood loss occurs. Although the incidence of clinically is
not high, but the cure rate is low because of its high mortality and
to losses caused by cattle households. The main clinical cows’
postpartum haemorrhage occurred mostly primiparous beef cows
households seek immediate benefits as early as with the
phenomenon common to calving when the body is not fully
reached maturity, the narrow birth canal, causing obstructed
labor, postpartum haemorrhage occurred. Clinical investigation
found that improper selection of bulls cold finishing relatively high
incidence of dystocia. Cow prevention measures to control
postpartum haemorrhage a good beginning of cows with age and
weight. Cows with early age of not less than 16 months, and
important to the adult body weight of 70% (or 350 kilograms) or
more before breeding. Rational feeding and management,
strengthening exercise into the dry period, should be appropriately
reduced energy diet, increased protein feed and minerals, it is
necessary to ensure normal fetal growth and development.
C.2. Tears
Depending on the severity of the tear, perineal stitching may be
required to promote normal tissue healing and function.
