Goats:
✓ Conformity to breed type (either beef or dairy)
✓ Developed udder with two well-spaced teats
✓ Fast growth rate
✓ High fecundity (regular kidding rate)
✓ Free from inheritable diseases
✓ Normal physical appearance (not deformed)
✓ Should still be young enough
✓ Resistant to common parasites and diseases
✓ Adaptation to environmental conditions should be good.
