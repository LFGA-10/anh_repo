A. Ankole zebu
• Origin: Originate from East Africa as a crossbreed between
breeds from Egypt, Ethiopia and Asia. The spread of Ankole
cattle covers an area between longitudes 27o and 32o East; and
latitudes 3o and 5o North corresponding to the following
regions:
• Uganda in the district of Tori; Kigezi and Ankole
• Tanzania in the District of Bokula ‘ kibondo, kasulu and ofipa
• Republic of Burundi
• Republic of Rwanda
• D.R.C: the territoiries of Bunia ; District of Bukavu(kivu)
territories of Rutshuru; Uvira and Ouvenga.
• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ The Ankole cattle are animals of an average to superior
stature; with a straight back; a rounded hump which is
nevertheless hardly noticeable in the female.
