A. Permanent free stall shelters for small ruminants
Small ruminants need to be provided with a reasonable shelter to
protect them from rain and other factors. Goats in particular are
less tolerant to rain and will more readily seek shelter in wet
weather.
Free/Cubicles stall shelters
✓ Concepts
The small ruminant is not tied up; they can walk around freely. In
this type of housing there is usually a loafing area and a lying
area, with a feeding area separated from the lying area. The habitat
is cut up in 3 parts: a feeding area covered, a rest area covered and
an exercise area not covered.
