Bull pens: where natural service has been adopted as a method of breeding, and more than one bull is being kept on the farm, you should separate them in individual boxes with strong walls.

Calving box: two to three weeks to calving, transfer pregnant heifers and cows to the calving boxes. Each animal is kept in an independent box, fed, watered and protected from inclement weather and predatory birds. The animals are watched to ensure smooth delivery.

Milking parlor: adjacent to the parlor is a milk recording room where milk is recorded, chilled and stored ready for dispatch. A feeds/concentrate store is attached to the milking parlor.

Draught animal shed: this is for draught animals if used at the farm for transporting fodder and other items.

Crush and weighing shed: for restraining animals during operations, treatment and weighing.

Isolation unit: this should be isolated from the rest of the farm buildings. It is where sick animals are kept for treatment. They are fed and watered separately from the healthy ones. Attendants at this unit do not work with the rest of the animals.

Quarantine unit: where newly purchased animals are first kept and observed for 2-3 weeks for any signs of disease. It is also a separate unit and a distance away from the rest of the farm buildings.
