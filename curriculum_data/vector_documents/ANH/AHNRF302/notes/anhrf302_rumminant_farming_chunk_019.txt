LO 2.2 – Identify ruminant shelter equipment

 Content /Topic1: Tips on basic ruminant shelter equipment and its functions

A. Feeding equipment

Feeding troughs: Keep passage clean and clear, keep animal food clean and within reach, rust and corrosion resistant and encourages animal to eat the whole diet mix.

Chopper machine: The chopper is mainly used for stalk forage, such as rice straw, wheat straw, maize stover and maize for ensiling.

Feed grinder: Grinding or particle-size reduction of feed for better use in term of nutrient, intake and mixing.

Feed mixer: is to blend feed ingredients – no matter the particle size, density or water content – into a uniform, consistent mixture

B. Watering equipment

Water troughs: A watering trough (or artificial watering point) is a human-made or natural receptacle intended to provide drinking water to animals, livestock on farms or ranches or wild animals Water tank: a water storage facility.

C. Milking equipment

Milking machine: is designed to harvest milk efficiently and maintain healthy teats

Milking can: a container used to transport milk from farms
