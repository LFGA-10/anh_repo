Metaestrus: The end of sexual receptivity marks the beginning of
metaestrus, the postovulatory phase dominated by corpus luteum

function. During this period, serum estrogens decrease and
progesterone increases.
Diestrus and Anoestrus: Polyestrous animals have a short period
of inactivity before the proestrus phase of the next cycle. This is
diestrus. Animals with long periods between cycles or Polyestrous
animals that stop cycling (e.g., due to change in season) enter a
long period of inactivity termed anoestrus.
 Maturity and precocity
Maturity
Corresponds to the period an animal reach its maximal
development. This means that they can reach the production or
breeding stage quickly. This will contribute to an increase in the
number of animals on the farm.
Precocity:

or physical traits. In this case sexual activity an animal can have
precocious puberty.  Prolificacy
