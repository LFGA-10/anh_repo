A. Traditional system
A.1. Pastoral system
Is to raise completely outdoors, free, in pasture or rangeland,
where the animal finds its own feed. No extra feed is provided to
them. It implies a low density of animals per area unit, and so it
requires having very large areas of unexploited natural pastures.
In terms of equipment, it requires only a very small investment,
since no construction is required.
