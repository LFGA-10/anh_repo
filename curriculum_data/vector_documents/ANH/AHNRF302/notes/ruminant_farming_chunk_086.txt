A.4. Feeding milking cows
In order to improve the quantity and quality of milk production
and satisfy the requirements of a dairy cattle, a balanced diet that
contains the following elements is required:
• Roughages: a good forage from 60-80 % of the dry matter that
contains grasses and leguminous are used for dairy cattle
rations are hay, silage and pasture should come from
roughages.
• Concentrates: The ration must be supplemented with
concentrates such as grains (corn, wheat, barley…), proteins
(soy bean meal, sun flour meal, cottonseed meal…).
• By-products and other processed (alfalfa meal, cottonseed,
molasses, potatoes, wheat brain…)
• Minerals (calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, potassium,
sodium, zinc…),
• Vitamins (vitamin A and B are important but other vitamins
may be needed in dairy rations.) then lactating dairy cow
require more water.
