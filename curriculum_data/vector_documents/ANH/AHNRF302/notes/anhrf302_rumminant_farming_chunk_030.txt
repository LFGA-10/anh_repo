Walls

The walls may be made up of stones, wood, concrete reinforced steel, bamboo, mud or any material available and suitable to the local environment. They ought to be very strong.

Roof

The roof should be light, strong, and durable in addition to being weather proof. It should also be a bad conductor of heat and free from tendency to condense moisture. For high rainfall areas, sloping roofs are a must. Materials include galvanized iron sheets, wood, bamboo, papyrus and grass thatch.  C.  Dimensions and spaces requirements:
