• Content/Topic 2: Main characteristics of Boss indicus (zebus)
The characteristics of Boss indicus are:
✓ A prominent hump;
✓ A large surface area per unit of body weight, due to a well-
developed dewlap, hump and large ears; this enables them to
lose heat quickly;
✓ Superior adaptation to high temperatures and intense solar
radiation;
✓ Late maturity and rather low fertility;
✓ Ability to live on poor quality fodder;
