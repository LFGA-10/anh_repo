B.3. Alpine
• Origin: Alps in Europe (Swiss breed).
• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ Hair coat varies from short to long;

✓ Color is dark brown to black color with light stripes on
either side of the face which are often very distinct;
✓ Many have long horns;
✓ Limbs are solids, dry joints and well conformed;
✓ The udder is large, well set forward as backwards,
retracting well after milking;
✓ Teats, separate from udder, are forward facing and
substantially parallel;
✓ The chest is deep;
✓ Basin is wide and gentle slope;
✓ Mature body weight is 50-70 kg in female and 80-100 kg
in males;  Average height range to 75-80 cm.
• Genotypic characteristics:
✓ They are imported for its milk production;
✓ Average daily milk yield approximately 0.9-1.3 kg with
average of 274 kg over 209 days of lactation period
✓ Butter fat content is 3.6%.
✓ Relatively hard and has adapted fairly well to tropical
conditions but does not tolerate too moist air;
✓ It suits both in housing and in pasture or mountain life
