B.2. Boer
• Origin : South Africa. It is a crossbreed of European, Angora
and Indian breeds to increase meat size and productivity.
• Phenotypic characteristics:

✓ It is white on the body with a brown-red head and white
line on the snout, with long ears and hair;
✓ Ears are pendulous;
✓ Have prominent horns;
✓ It is a medium size goat: 50 to 70 kg for female and 80-
100 kg for male.
• Genotypic characteristics :
✓ Very prolific breeds’ kidding rate of 1.5, 50% twins and 7%
triplets.
✓ Mean daily milk is 1.2- 1.8 kg ; with average milk yield over
160 kg / lactation period of 120 days
✓ Butter fat (BF) Content is about 5.6%
✓ Valuable for meat, milk and skin but has been promoted for
its meat potential.
✓ This goat is well adapted to tropical conditions but it hardly
tolerates too moist air.
