Most ewes will lose weight during lactation. Weight loss during lactation affects protein requirements. The more weight ewes lose, the higher their protein requirement will be.

Growing kids feeding

The lighter breeds should reach 90 pounds in a similar timeframe the average for the whole plant. The kid grazing on improved pasture, browsing in woodlots or consuming alfalfa hay is able to select plant parts which have a higher nutritional value than laboratory analyses of the forage samples might show.. If a doe is weaned at 8 weeks, weighing 20 lbs and is to kid at 12 months at 110 lbs, then she must gain 90 lbs in 10 months or approximately 1/3 lb daily. Therefore, the nutritional program must aim for a growth rate of approximately 150 gm daily with consideration for both the nutritional requirements of the growing doe and the growing fetus over the 5-month gestation period. If does are freshening at 15 to 20 months, then the proper size is more easily attained with less grain feeding. Fresh water and exercise are also important in the post weaning period.

Farming system

F.1. Grazing (Extensive system)

With regard to the time aspect, sheep must have at least 8 hours a day for grazing. The animals should be given water to drink during the midday rest. Free grazing involves the intensive use natural resources: the sheep are kept under permanent surveillance by man, who controls both the time and the place used for  grazing, but the areas available for grazing are usually far in excess of the animals’ strict requirements.
