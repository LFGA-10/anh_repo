Machine milking has the advantages of clean milk production, saving labour and it is quick. However, costs of instalment are high, faulty machines can injury the animals, there are higher chances of mastitis spread through teat cup and power failure can affect milking.

C. Udder health care

Animal should be allowed to assemble within the milking area in good time .This allows them to settle down.

The hind legs are tied with help of milker’s rope and the udder washed with warm water mixed with disinfectant. The udder is then wiped dry with a clean disposal towel. The hind quarter is brushed or groomed to remove loose hair or dust.

Learning Outcome 4.6: manage weaning

 Topic 1.Ruminant weaning

A. Weaning process

A.1. Calf

Weaning is a period when a young animal stops depending on its mother. Calves are usually weaned between 8-10 weeks of age. With good management and proper feeding they may be weaned between 3-8 weeks of age. In other situations, the two stay together for some time before the separation. In preparation for that stage, the young one is slowly introduced to solid food as well as searching for food.

This enables the dam to prepare for the next gestation period.
