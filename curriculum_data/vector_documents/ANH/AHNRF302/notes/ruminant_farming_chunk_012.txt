LO 1.3 – Identify sheep breeds
There are many distinct breeds of sheep over the world.

This section will includes basic information concerning the most
common breeds of sheep we are likely to find in Rwanda Countryside
along with inclusion of some of our rare breeds.
Content/Topic 1: Main characteristics of sheep breeds
A. Local breed
• Origin: are obtained from the different breeds raised in central
Africa.
• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ Small in size, which is a sheep with tail;
✓ Color of its coat varies from black to white and even to brown;
✓ Resistant to ecological condition of Rwanda
✓ Body weight varies between 30-35 kg
• Genotypic characteristics:
✓ It is a sheep raised exclusively for meat production and can
accumulate up to 5 kg of fat in the tail;
✓ This sheep is resistant and adapted to a variety of
environments ranging from arid to subhumid areas and from
the sea level up to 3,000 m altitude.
