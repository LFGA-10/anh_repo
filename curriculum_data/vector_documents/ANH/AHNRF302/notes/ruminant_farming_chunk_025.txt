• Content/Topic 2: long, short stalls
A. Long stall
Is the stall which the distance between the edge of the manger
(animal side) and of the slurry trench is greater than the length of
the animal? It requires a lot of litter and large manpower. The
dimensions of long stalls are: 1.10 m wide and 2 to 2.50 m long.
B. Short stalls
Is the stall which the distance between the edge of manger (animal
side) and the slurry trench is equal to the distance between before
leg and hind legs of the animal, plus 10 cm. it allows a reduced
use of clean straw and is less expensive to labour, but the yield in
manure is lower? There is also possibility of clean with water. The
dimensions of short stalls are: 1.10 m wide and 1.60 m in length.
Note: The stall must have a slope of 1 to 3% and should be
perpendicular to the trench slurry. The stalls can be separated by
dividers fixed at 1 m height, tubular steel, without bumps
(roughness surface0, while the width of the stall is 1.50 m
especially for animals with large horns.
