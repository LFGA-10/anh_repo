B. Exotic breeds
B.1. Mountain white
• Origin : French Pyrenees Mountain
• Phenotypic characteristics:

✓ This is big and heavy wool sheep, with a slightly miter
head and long hanging ears;
✓ White coat color
✓ Hornless
• Genotypic characteristics:
✓ This sheep is wool producer with 4.5 to 5.5 kg annual
production for ewe and 6.5 to 7.5 kg for ram;
✓ Resistant to diseases and climatic conditions optimal Tº
of 5 to 18ºc) but low tolerant to high humidity air content.
B.2. Mérinos
• Origin: Spain but was developed in Australia.
• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ Merino is medium sized sheep, hornless;
✓ Its wool is white all over its body;
✓ The body weight of adult female is about 70 kg and ram
from 120 to 140 kg for meaty bread. For dairy breeds, ewe
weighs 40-60 kg.
• Genotypic characteristics :

✓ This is a hardy wool sheep yielding 6 kg in ewes and 8 kg
in rams;
✓ It is a gooddiseases resistant;
✓ It is slow maturing with a light narrow body;
✓ It will survive in the harsher, drier areas;
✓ Little tolerant to high air moisture content
