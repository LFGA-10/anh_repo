 Metritis

A clear or blood-stained red/brown discharge often comes from the vagina after a healthy animal has given birth. It does not smell foul. But a white /yellow discharge or a bad smell is a sign of infection in the uterus. This is called metritis and animal sometimes get it soon after they have given birth.

Infection gets into the uterus through the vulva and vagina, especially when an animal gives birth in dirty place. Animals often get metritis when the placenta does not come out.

The treatment of metritis is done immediately when the animal shows the above sign(white /yellow discharge or a bad smell) and fever. Give a general anti-biotherapy by injection or put antibiotic tables into the uterus by hand but this is not the best way to treat metritis. Even severe infection can be treated.

Prevention of metritis

Make sure the place where an animal gives birth in clean and dry. Do not always use the same place without cleaning it up.

Wash your hands and arms carefully with soap or disinfectant before putting them into an animal to help with a birth. You can cause infection if your hands and arms are not clean.

Animals often get metritis after a difficult birth because the vagina or cervix has been damaged.
