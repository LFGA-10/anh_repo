Drying Off Dairy Animals

Due to the amount of milk they produce, the drying-off process is often more complicated for dairy animals (cattle, goats and some sheep). Preparation for dry off should begin at least two weeks prior to the dry-off date with a significant change in the animal’s diet. Slowly reducing the energy content of the diet and feeding primarily a high-fiber diet will reduce the nutrients available for the animal to make milk; this is often all that is needed to reduce milk production to a level that makes dry off safe and simple.
