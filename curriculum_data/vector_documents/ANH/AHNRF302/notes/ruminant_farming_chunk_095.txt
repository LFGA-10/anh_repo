B.5. Farming system
There are two basic types of production: intensive (zero grazing)
and extensive. Intensive production is high input (capital,
management, facilities) and requires high output for sustainability.
For dairy goats and sheep, this type of production is characterized
by minimal grazing/ pasture use, with most nutrient needs
provided by harvested forages and concentrates.
Extensive production is a lower-input production requiring lower
output for sustainability. Naturally occurring feedstuff is used and
supplemented as needed with concentrates and harvested forages.
An extensive goat or sheep dairy will have few buildings for the
animals, allowing them to graze and forage when not being milked.
