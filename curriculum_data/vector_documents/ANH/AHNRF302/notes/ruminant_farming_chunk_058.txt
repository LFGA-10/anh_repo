D.8. Abortion
It is premature expulsion from the uterus of the products of
conception, either the embryo or a nonviable fetus.
Mortality: It represents the number of new born dead from
calving/ farrowing date to weaning date.
They are divided into 3 categories:
• Death before giving birth (abortion)
• Death during giving birth (still birth)
• Death during weaning period
D.9. Observation of heat return
Observation for service returns tends to be concentrated around
the normal estrous cycle length of 18 days. The absence of a
return to estrus at this time or around 42 days after service may
indicate that the cow is pregnant. However, this method has
inaccuracies in that:
• it is dependent upon efficient and accurate heat detection;
• it assumes that non-pregnant cows return to heat at regular
intervals;
• It assumes that cows with a cessation of oestrous cycles are
pregnant. Up to 7% of pregnant cows can show signs of heat,
usually between the fourth and eighth month of pregnancy.
These cows (false negatives) could abort following repeat
insemination or may be unnecessarily culled.
