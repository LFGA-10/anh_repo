B. Post parturition cares for new born
B.1. Breathing stimulation
As soon as a baby animal is born, make sure that it is breathing.
• If the baby is having difficulty breathing, hang it upside down.
Rub the chest to make any fluid in the lungs come out of the
nose;
• Lift the new-born animal up by the back legs for a minute or
so to let mucus and fluid drain out from the lungs. You can
hold the back legs of a small animal and swing it round to
make mucus come out of the nose;
• You can also c lean mucous away from the mouth and nostril
by hand.
• Pull the new-born animal to where the mother can reach it
easily to lick it dry and put the newborn so that it sits up.
• Put a piece of dry grass up the baby animal’s nose. This makes
it cough then it starts breathing.
• Take the weight to determine the birth weight
B.2. Umbilical cord cutting and disinfection
The umbilical cord usually breaks on its own and is not a problem.
If a long cord is still attached to the new-born animal tie a sterile
string round it and cut it to about 3 cm long.
