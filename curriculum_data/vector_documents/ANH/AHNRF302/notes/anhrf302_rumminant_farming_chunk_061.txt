E.2. Restraining technique for uterine prolapse reduction

Management

Be protected from further trauma and contamination by wrapping a wet towel or sheet around it. Sacro-coccygeal epidural injection for controlling the straining (2 % Lidocaine:5ml

In standing position:

Raise the prolapse uterine to the level of vulva by assistant’s hands or something which can support it like a board or sheet.

If possible, an anterior body is positioned lower than posterior body to make it return easily.

Removal of placenta

Treatment the wound if there are (Suturing the site of rupture, Haemorrhage)

E.4. Uterine prolapse complications

The prolapse possibly happens again when the replacement was not completed.  To be replaced it exactly, better to use a material like a big beer bottle (1 L) for pushing the uterus in the vagina. Of course, don’t remain the bottle inside after pushing.
