Deposition of fats which block the fallopian tubes

D.Gestation

The gestation period is the time during which the animal is pregnant. During pregnancy the fetus develops in the uterus. The fetus is surrounded by a watery fluid enclosed in membranes. Blood vessels in the umbilical cord supply nutrients and oxygen and carry off waste products.

D.1. Development of fetal membranes (chorio-amniotic membranes)

Glossary, the placenta consists of layers of soft membranes that form two fluid-filled sacs (amnion and allantois) around the developing fetus. D.2.Amnion (inner layer):

This is the layer immediately around the fetus. It forms a sac around the fetus called amniotic sac.

D.3. Allantois: is another layer surrounding the amniotic sac which forms the fluid-filled called allantoic sac.

D.4. Chorion (outer layer): the outside of the allantoic sac is covered by the Chorion, which attaches to the uterine lining. The Chorion is linked to the fetus by the umbilical cord. The umbilical card is link between the fetus and the nutrient and waste exchange structures of the placenta.

D.5.Umbilical cord

As its name implies, it is a cord like structure that contains blood vessels (the umbilical arteries and umbilical vein) and a drainage tube from the fetus urinary bladder.
