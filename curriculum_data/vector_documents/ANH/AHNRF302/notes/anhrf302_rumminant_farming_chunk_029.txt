 Content/Topic 2.Tips to Design cow shed A. Characteristics of a good cowshed

A good cowshed should have the following characteristics:

It should provide an internal environment that allows the animals to produce at their best, so it should be neither too hot nor too cold.

It should protect livestock against direct sun light cold winds and excessive heat.

It should be well ventilated.

It should protect livestock against predators, other vermin and thieves.

It should be easy to clean and to disinfect.

The structures within the house should be easy to adjust and/or remove so that a farmer can quickly adapt the house for another use without paying a lot of money for renovation.

The cowshed should be placed so that it is easy to reach and enter, and so that services such as water and electricity can easily be provided. In addition, a livestock house should be near feed stores, loading areas and other services.

The floor should be made of rough concrete for easy cleaning. It should have a slight slope leading down to a drain so that no water is retained in the building B. Main parts of cowshed

Floor

The most efficient floor is made of concrete in other to support the heavy weight of the cattle. The concrete consists of a number of materials mixed together to make a very hard, rock-like substance that can be used to make the floors and pillars of building.

The floor should be hard, durable and impervious to water and urine. It should be easy to clean, slopping towards the drainage and should not be slippery.

Walls

The walls may be made up of stones, wood, concrete reinforced steel, bamboo, mud or any material available and suitable to the local environment. They ought to be very strong.
