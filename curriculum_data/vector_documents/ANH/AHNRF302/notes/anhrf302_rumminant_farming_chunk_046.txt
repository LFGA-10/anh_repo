B. Mating methods

Natural

Male animals have the natural ability to detect female animals on heat. Once detected, the male animal mounts the female and deposits the sperms in the vagina. Natural mating takes places during the act of copulation by the penis.

Artificial

This is a mating by artificial means. Semen is collected from a male animal and introduced into vagina of a female animal on heat using insemination equipment.

In cattle, semen is collected from a bull using artificial vagina (AV), screened to check whether it is safe, diluted and stored in plastic straws in liquid nitrogen or solid carbon dioxide. The semen can stay live for a period of 9 years. On the day of insemination, an inseminator gun (catheter tube) is used to introduce the semen into the vagina. C. Post mating follow-up

C.1.  Post mating process

Once mating is carried out successfully, and hopefully ovulation has taken place, the two gametes (sperm and ovum) combine to form one single cell of the new individual, called the zygote. This fusion of gametes is referred to as fertilization. Shortly after ovulation, the corpus luteum forms on the ovary. It releases the hormone progesterone. This hormone causes four things to happen:

The fertilized egg (embryo) is implanted in the uterus.

Others eggs are stopped from forming.

The pregnant condition is maintained.

The mammary glands begin to develop.

The mammary glands produce the milk to feed the young when they are borne.
