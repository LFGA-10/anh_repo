B.2 .Cross breeding purpose

Introduction of good genes formerly missing in the herd. This may result in the development of a new breed. The offspring of cross-breeding usually perform better than either of the parents. This is known as hybrid vigour or heterosis.  Diluting effect of bad treats found in a particular breed, for example, genetic diseases.

B.3.Type of cross breeding

Upgrading or absorption cross-breeding

Is a form of cross-breeding in which cross-bred females are crossed back to a male of a superior pure breed for several generations. For example, in the first generation a Friesian bull with desirable characteristics such as high milk yielding and Ankole cow with good resistance to tick borne diseases.

Subsequent generations of cows are also mated with Friesian bulls or with the original Friesian bull.  They will acquire more and more Friesian characteristics as time goes on, while maintaining the basic Ankole ones.
