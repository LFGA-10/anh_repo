These guidelines have been developed to assist people to tether animals correctly when circumstances make it a necessary method of confining and protecting animals. They specify the requirements for tethering dogs, sheep, goats, cattle, donkeys and horses.

LO 5.3 – Manage nutritionals deficiencies in ruminants

 Content/Topic 1: Management of carbohydrates, Fats, lipids and protein deficiency

A. Carbohydrate  deficiency

Deficiency of carbohydrate (energy) is the most common nutrient deficiency which limits the performance of grazing animals. Feed may be inadequate due to overgrazing, drought, poor quality or digestibility or expense. Sometime forage may contain an excess of water, limiting energy intake.
