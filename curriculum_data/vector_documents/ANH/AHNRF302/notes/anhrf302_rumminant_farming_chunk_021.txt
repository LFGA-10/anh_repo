LO 3.1 – Classify production systems

 Content/Topic 1: Classification of ruminant production systems A. Traditional system

A.1. Pastoral system

Is to raise completely outdoors, free, in pasture or rangeland, where the animal finds its own feed. No extra feed is provided to them. It implies a low density of animals per area unit, and so it requires having very large areas of unexploited natural pastures. In terms of equipment, it requires only a very small investment, since no construction is required.

Those pastures must be naturally rich to satisfy animal feed requirements. In addition, veterinary monitoring is difficult because animals are released, and it is often limited to prophylaxis (vaccines). For those two reasons, it is usually local breeds that are raised in that way because they are resistant to local epidemics and require less feed than high-yielding exotic breeds. Nevertheless, theft risks are very high since they are not continually monitored by the farmer.
