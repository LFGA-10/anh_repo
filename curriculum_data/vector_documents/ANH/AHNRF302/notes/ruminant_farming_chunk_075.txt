B. Milk collection techniques
There are two methods used to remove milk from the udder of the
cow and these are hand and machine milking
B.1.Hand milking
The milker squats and ties the hind legs with milker’s rope,
washes the udder with warm water and dries it up with a clean
towel. He then applies a salve on the teats to avoid cracking.
The thumb finger is placed along the teat and the index finger
closes the top of the teat. Then pressure is exerted on the teat by
