Pure breed and cross-breed animals.

It is important to understand the differences between pure breeding and cross breeding.

A.Pure breeding: is the mating of organisms that belong to the same breed (animals) or variety (plants). This means that any of the offspring of pure-bred parent pair is extremely likely to show the same traits as any other offspring of the same parent pair.

B.Cross-breeding

It is the mating of plants or animals that do not belong to the same breed or variety.

B.1.Heterosis effect

Crossbreds often perform at a higher level than the average performance of the purebreds that make up the crossbred. The offspring produced perform better than parents.

Example for cross-breeding would be mating a Friesian bull with a Sahiwal cow.
