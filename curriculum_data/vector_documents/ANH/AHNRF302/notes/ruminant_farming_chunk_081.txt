A. 1. Feeding calves
 Colostrum feeding
A new born calf must be fed on colostrum’s in the first three days.
It plays the following roles:
✓ It contains antibodies from the dam which provide immunity
up to a time when the young animals make its own. Antibody
can only be absorbed in this period
✓ It is highly nutritious and liquid form which the young one can
easily consume.
✓ It is a laxative which helps the alimentary canal to get rid of
the first food thus avoiding constipation because the animal’s
diet at that stage lacks roughage.
✓ It is also highly digestible.
 Artificial milk replacers
The high-quality milk replacers are made from dairy products
rather than plant products. Milk replacers that have the following
protein sources are preferred:
✓ Skim milk powder
✓ Buttermilk powder
✓ Dried whole whey
✓ Delactosed whey
✓ Casein
✓ Milk albumin
