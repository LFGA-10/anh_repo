✓ It is an animal of small size with well-proportioned body whose

✓ The coat color varies from black to white but may be brown
mottled.
✓ The horns are small and light while ears are short, pointed and
lie horizontally.

✓ It is a meat breed only

✓ Thin and small goat, completely white, relatively small head

✓ Long limbs;
