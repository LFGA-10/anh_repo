C. Udder health care
Animal should be allowed to assemble within the milking area in
good time .This allows them to settle down.
The hind legs are tied with help of milker’s rope and the udder
washed with warm water mixed with disinfectant. The udder is
then wiped dry with a clean disposal towel. The hind quarter is
brushed or groomed to remove loose hair or dust.
Learning Outcome 4.6: manage weaning
 Topic 1.Ruminant weaning
A. Weaning process
A.1. Calf
Weaning is a period when a young animal stop depending on its
mother. Calves are usually weaned between 8-10 weeks of age.
With good management and proper feeding, they may be weaned
between 3-8 weeks of age. In other situations, the two stay
together for some time before the separation. In preparation for
that stage, the young one is slowly introduced to solid food as well
as searching for food.
This enables the dam to prepare for the next gestation period.
From the second week onward, a calf should be able to eat small
amounts of good quality feed such as young grass. This is
necessary for the development of the rumen. Fresh, clean water
should also be available.
