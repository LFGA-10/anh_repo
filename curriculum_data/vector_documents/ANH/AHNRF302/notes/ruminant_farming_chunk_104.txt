Water
Figure 44 : water supplies
Small ruminants should have ad libitum access to clean, fresh
water at all times. A mature animal will consume between ¾ to 1
½ gallons of water per day. Water requirements and intake
increase greatly during late gestation and during lactation. Water
requirements increase substantially when environmental
temperatures rise above 70oF and decline with very cold
environmental temperatures. An animal’s nutrient requirements
will increase if it has to consume cold water during cold weather.
