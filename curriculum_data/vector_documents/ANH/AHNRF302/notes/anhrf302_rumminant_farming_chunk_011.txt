Phenotypic characteristics:

The Ankole cattle are animals of an average to superior stature; with a straight back; a rounded hump which is nevertheless hardly noticeable in the female.

The horns are long and fine but there are varieties without horns or with short horns and others with very developed horns (Inyambo cattles).

Uniform colored coats are most frequent mostly being dark-red brown or dark red and sometimes red pie.

Bulls weigh between 330-500 kg while the cows are 200-410 kg.

Figure 4 : Ankole cow and bull

Genotypic characteristics: Ankole breed produces good quality milk up to 1100 liters per lactation (180 days) and the beef is of relatively good quality. It greatly tolerates heats, parasites and diseases (tick borne diseases), thus can adapt to a wide range of climates

B. Sahiwal zebu

Origin: The Sahiwal is originated from PAKISTAN.

Phenotypic characteristics:

The animal is heavily built short-legged lethargic in appearance with reddish or light brownish- red color; sometimes bearing white markings;

The skin is loose and pliable;

The hair very fine and glossy;

The forehead is large, horns directed towards the back, with medium format;

Its fetlock is very large;

The udder is generally large and pendulous.
