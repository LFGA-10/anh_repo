• Genotypic characteristics
This is a very good tropical dairy breed. Although it cannot compete
with the exotic breeds from the temperature climates as far as milk
yield is concerned. A good cow may give 3000-3500 kg per lactation.
C. Brahman
• Origin: Is an exotic breed developed in USA using the following
breed: Guzerat, Nellone and Nir.
• Phenotypic characteristics:
✓ The coat color ranges between light grey, red and black.
Bulls have darker shoulders, thighs and neck regions.
Mature bulls weigh between 720-900 kg while the cows are
440-630 kg.
✓ They have a prominent hump, dewlap and drooping ears.
• Genotypic characteristics:
✓ They have the fast growth rate which makes them good beef
cattle;
✓ They are tolerant to excess heat and diseases
