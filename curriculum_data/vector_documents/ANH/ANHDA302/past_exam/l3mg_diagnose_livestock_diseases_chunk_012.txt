Question 22: During clinical examination of male genital organs, visual
inspection and palpation are employed to examine the testes,
prepuce and the penis. What are the conditions or diseases you
may find during this checking?
(5 marks)
Answer:
• Orchitis,
• cryptorchidism,
• scrotum hernia,
• obstruction of urethra by calculi,
• phymosis,
• paraphymosis,
• inflammation of the prepuce, testes and penis.
(1 mark for each correct element, retain 5)
