Question16: The air bubbles removal is commonly done after drugs withdraw
from the bottle for injection. Describe its procedures. (5marks)
Answer:
- Point the syringe upwards and tap the barrel with your finger to make
air bubbles move upwards into the syringe tip. 2.5 marks
- Slowly and carefully push the plunger to eject the air bubbles from the
syringe before injecting the product. 2.5 marks
