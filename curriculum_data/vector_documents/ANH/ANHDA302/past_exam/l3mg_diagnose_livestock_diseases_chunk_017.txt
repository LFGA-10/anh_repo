Question 30: Before performing any injection, animal care must take into
consideration a good predilection sites or spot. Help the farmers
of the following animals to choose the best spot for Intravenous
injection: Pig, Cattle, Sheep and Goat, Poultry.
(10 marks)
Answer:
The best spot for Intravenous injection
- Pig: Ear vein (2marks)
- Cattle: Jugular vein. (2marks)
- Sheep: Jugular vein. (2marks)
- Goat: Jugular vein. (2marks)
- Poultry: Wing vein (2marks)
