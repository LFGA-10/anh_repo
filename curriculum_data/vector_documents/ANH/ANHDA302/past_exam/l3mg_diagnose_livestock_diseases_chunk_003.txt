Question 5: Define the following terms applied to the livestock diseases.
(5 marks)
a. Pathogen
b. Pathogenesis
c. Pathogenicity
d. Pathology
e. Cosmopolite
Answer:
✓ Pathogen: may also be referred to as an infectious agent, or simply a
germ. It is any organism that can produce disease. 1 mark
✓ Pathogenesis: is the biological mechanism that leads to a diseased
state. 1 mark

✓ Pathogenicity: Refers to the ability of an organism to cause disease (ie,
harm the host) 1 mark
✓ Pathology: is the study of the causes and effects of disease or injury.
1 mark
✓ Cosmopolite: Growing or occurring in many parts of the world; widely
distributed 1 mark
