Question1: Outline the five (5) types of personal protective equipment.
(5 marks)
Answer:
• Respiratory Protection (Masks) protect from inhalation of infectious
aerosols (e.g., Mycobacterium tuberculosis)
PPE types for respiratory protection Particulate Respirators Half- or full
elastomeric respirators Powered air purifying respirators (PAPR)
• Eye protection (Goggles) protect eyes 1 mark
• Face shields protect face, mouth, nose, and eyes 1 mark
• Ear protection Using PPE, and wearing it properly, is vital to avoid
unnecessary injury in the workplace 1 mark
• Hand protection (Gloves) protect hands when handling dirty items and
potentially touching body fluids. 1 mark
• Leg protection boot and Gowns are worn over your clothes and
uniforms. Germs can even live on them and be transferred other places.
Gowns can keep you clean when you are exposed to body fluids and
dirty items. 1 mark
