PART 5: ESSAY QUESTIONS (3 × 10 Marks = 30 Marks)
49. (Evaluating) Disease outbreak response (10 marks)
Distribution of marks:
• Initial assessment (biosecurity, observation): 2 marks
• Anamnesis and history taking: 2 marks
• Clinical examination: 2 marks
• Sample collection and handling: 2 marks
• Diagnosis and reporting: 1 mark
• Justification of approach: 1 mark
Expected answer structure:
Step 1: Initial Assessment and Biosecurity
• Isolate affected animals immediately
• Implement farm biosecurity measures (footbaths, protective clothing)
• Observe from distance before handling
Step 2: Anamnesis
• Collect full history from farmer
• Number affected, onset, progression
• Recent introductions, movements
• Vaccination status, previous outbreaks
Step 3: Clinical Examination
• General examination (posture, behavior, vital signs)
• Systematic examination of body systems
• Identify specific clinical signs
