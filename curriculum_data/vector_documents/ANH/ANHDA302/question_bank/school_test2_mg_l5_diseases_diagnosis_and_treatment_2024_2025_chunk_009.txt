Virology is a study of virus. Answer to the following questions related to the viruses:

List any 3 criteria on which base the classification of the viruses?

Differentiate a Monopartite genome from a Multipartite genome in terms of their nucleic acid?

Highlight any 5 (two) steps of lytic replication cycle for the viruses.

Answer:

The 3 criteria are the MORPHOLOGY, CHEMICAL COMPOSITION and MODE OF REPLICATION.

In a Monopartite genome the entire genome occupies one nucleic acid molecule while in Multipartite it occupies several nucleic acid segments.

The lytic cycle includes 5 steps: (each carries 2 marks)

Attachment of virus to susceptible host cell

Entry of virus nucleic acid into host cell

Replication of viral nucleic acid

Assembly of new viruses

Release of new viruses from a lysed host cell.

Briefly describe the following information find on the drug notice:

Expiration date

B. Side effects

Withdrawal time

Trade name

Antidote.

Answers:

Expiration date: It is the date on after which a product expires and should not be used.

 side effects: abnormal changes in the body following drug administration.

 Withdrawal time: the period of time from when a drug is administered to when the drug concentration falls below the tolerance.

 trade name: name provided by the company and can’t be reused by another company for the commercial purposes.

 antidote: a medicine taken or given to counteract a particular poison.
