Movement control:
No animals on or off premises
•
Stop all farm operations (milking, shearing)
•

Secure all exits
•
People:
Essential personnel only
•
Full PPE for all handlers
•
Footbaths at all entry/exit points
•
Shower in/shower out if possible
•
Vehicles:
No vehicle movement
•
Disinfect any that must leave
•
Carcasses:
Leave in place until instructed
•
Cover to prevent scavengers
•
No removal or disposal
•
Equipment:
Dedicated equipment for affected area
•
Disinfect all used equipment
•
No sharing with other farms
•
Environment:
Contain runoff
•
Control vermin
•

Secure feed stores
