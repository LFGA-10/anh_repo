Key reasons for different vaccination approaches:
1. Age of susceptibility: MD affects young birds; ND and IB
affect all ages
2. Virus characteristics: MD is cell-associated; ND and IB are
shed in secretions
3. Immune response: MD requires cell-mediated immunity;

4. Epidemiology: MD is ubiquitous; ND has eradication zones;
