The pre-necropsy evaluation refers to the preparation of materials tools and equipment. Answer to the questions below:

Highlight any 4 (four) materials used as EPE for necropsy?

Explain the purpose of using EPE during necropsy activities?

Answer:

The following are the materials used as EPE during Necropsy: (Consider only 4)

Ventilation Systems

Biohazard Waste Containers

Disinfectant Sprays and Solutions

Splash Shields or Barriers

Floor Drains with Biohazard Precautions

Negative Pressure Rooms.

The purpose of using EPE during Necropsy: to safeguard the surroundings and ensure that the necropsy process does not contaminate the workspace or expose the environment to biohazards.

(10marks)

ELISA test relies on the principle of antigen-antibody binding. Briefly explain each of the following procedures involved in this test.

Coating

Binding

Detection

Signal Generation

Quantification

Answers:

	Coating: A plate is coated with either viral antigens (to detect antibodies) or specific antibodies (to detect antigens).

	Binding: The sample (e.g., blood, serum) is added. If the target (antigen or antibody) is present, it binds to the plate.

	Detection: A secondary antibody linked to an enzyme is added, which binds to the target.

	Signal Generation: A substrate is added that reacts with the enzyme to produce a color change.

	Quantification: The intensity of the color is measured to determine the amount of target present.
