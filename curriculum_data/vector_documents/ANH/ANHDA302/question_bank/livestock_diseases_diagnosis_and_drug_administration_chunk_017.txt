ANATOMICAL/BEHAVIORAL REASONS FOR DIFFERENCES:
1. Size and strength: Cattle require mechanical aids; poultry
need gentle hand restraint
2. Defense mechanisms: Cattle kick sideways; pigs bite; poultry
flap and peck
3. Social structure: Herd animals (cattle) respond to group
movement; pigs individualistic
4. Stress response: Flight vs fight vs freeze varies by species
5. Physical vulnerabilities: Poultry bones fragile; pig snout
sensitive; cattle tail sensitive
6. Sensory priorities: Vision important in cattle; hearing/smell
in pigs
