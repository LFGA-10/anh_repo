Expected answer:
ANTHRAX PREVENTION PROGRAM
1. Vaccination Protocol
Vaccinate all grazing animals annually (cattle, sheep, goats)
•
Timing: 2-4 weeks before the season when outbreaks are
•
expected (usually before rains)
Dose: 1 mL subcutaneously (as per vaccine type)
•
Do NOT vaccinate animals showing signs of illness
•
Deworm before vaccination for better immune response
•
2. Carcass Disposal (Critical!)
DO NOT open the carcass - anthrax spores sporulate on
•
exposure to oxygen
Burn the carcass completely where it lies, or
•
Deep bury (at least 2 meters) with quicklime (calcium oxide)
•
Burn or deep bury contaminated materials, bedding, and soil
•
Disinfect the area with 5% formalin or 10% bleach
•
3. Biosecurity Measures
Quarantine affected area immediately
•
Restrict animal and human movement
•

Report to veterinary authorities (notifiable disease)
•
Vaccinate in-contact animals
•
Personal protective equipment (PPE) for handlers
•
Monitor human contacts for signs (zoonotic risk)
•
