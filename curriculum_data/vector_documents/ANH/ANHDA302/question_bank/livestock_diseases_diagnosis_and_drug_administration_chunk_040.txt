PROTOCOL FOR RECOGNIZING AND REPORTING NOTIFIABLE
DISEASE OUTBREAKS

1. CLINICAL SIGNS TO WATCH FOR (RED FLAGS)
General indicators:
Sudden death in multiple animals
•
Rapid spread through herd/flock
•
Severe depression and fever
•
Unusual nervous signs
•
Vesicles/blisters on mouth/feet
•
Hemorrhagic syndromes
•
Respiratory distress with high morbidity
•

2. INITIAL ACTIONS UPON SUSPICION
DO:
Isolate affected animals immediately
•
Restrict movement of animals, people, vehicles
•
Post warning signs at farm entrance
•
Record all observations (photos if possible)
•
Contact veterinarian immediately
•
DO NOT:
Move animals on or off the farm
•
Allow visitors
•
Remove carcasses or products
•
Administer treatment (may mask signs)
•
Open carcasses (risk of sporulation)
•
