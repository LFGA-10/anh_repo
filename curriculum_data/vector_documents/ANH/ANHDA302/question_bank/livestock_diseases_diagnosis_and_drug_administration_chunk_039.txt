Deworm with broad-spectrum anthelmintic

Check for external parasites after 2 weeks

6. ACCLIMATIZATION
Gradual introduction to new feed over 14 days
•
Monitor feed intake and adaptation
•
Provide stress-reducing management
•
Observe social behavior before mixing

Dedicated PPE for quarantine workers
•
Work quarantine animals last in daily routine
•
Disinfect boots and equipment between groups
•
No shared water sources with main herd
•
Waste and manure managed separately

No signs of illness for 30 days

Negative test results
•

Vaccinations complete
•
Parasite control complete

Gradual introduction to main herd (nose-to-nose contact first)

30 days allows incubation of most diseases

Testing identifies carrier animals

Vaccination ensures immunity before exposure

Acclimatization reduces stress-induced disease

Biosecurity protects existing herd
