VACCINE STORAGE AND HANDLING PROTOCOL
1. Cold chain maintenance: 2-8°C at all times; never freeze
2. Transport: Insulated cool boxes with ice packs
3. On-farm storage: Dedicated vaccine refrigerator with
thermometer
4. Reconstitution: Use only provided diluent; use within 1 hour

5. Equipment: Sterile needles; change needles regularly
6. Records: Batch numbers, expiry dates, animals vaccinated
7. Adverse reactions: Observe animals for 1 hour post-
vaccination
Important considerations:
Deworm before vaccination for better immune response
•
Do not vaccinate sick animals
•
Stress-free handling during vaccination
•
Pregnant animals: use killed vaccines only
•
