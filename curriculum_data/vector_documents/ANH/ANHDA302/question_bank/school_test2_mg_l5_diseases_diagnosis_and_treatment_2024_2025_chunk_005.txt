Analyze how the presence of a capsule contributes to the pathogenicity of bacteria?

Answer (with marks allocations)

The capsule is a key virulence factor that enhances bacterial pathogenicity by:

Evading phagocytosis: The capsule prevents immune cells (e.g., macrophages and neutrophils) from recognizing and engulfing the bacteria.

Protecting against desiccation: It helps bacteria survive in harsh environments.

Inhibiting complement activation: The capsule can block the binding of complement proteins, reducing opsonization and lysis.
