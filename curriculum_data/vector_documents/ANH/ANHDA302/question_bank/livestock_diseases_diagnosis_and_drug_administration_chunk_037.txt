RATIONALE FOR ECTOPARASITE CONTROL PROGRAMS
Biological rationale: Break the parasite life cycle by:
1. Killing adult parasites before egg-laying
2. Killing immature stages
3. Creating environments unfavorable for egg hatching
Strategic timing:
Treat before peak parasite seasons

1. Chemical control: Strategic acaricide application
2. Biological control: Natural predators, resistant breeds
3. Environmental management: Pasture rotation, hygiene
4. Quarantine: Prevent introduction of resistant ticks
5. Monitoring: Regular inspection, resistance testing
