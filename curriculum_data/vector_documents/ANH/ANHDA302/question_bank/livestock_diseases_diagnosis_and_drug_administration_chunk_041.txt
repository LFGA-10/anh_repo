3. INFORMATION TO COLLECT
When contacting authorities, have ready:
Farm details:

Owner name and contact
•
Property address and GPS coordinates
•
Farm identification number
•
Animal details:
Species affected
•
Number of animals on farm (total)
•
Number sick
•
Number dead
•
Age groups affected
•
Vaccination history
•
Clinical details:
Date first noticed
•
Progression over time
•
Specific signs observed
•
Any post-mortem findings (if seen)
•
Photos/videos if available
•
Epidemiological information:
Recent animal introductions
•
Visitor history
•
Neighboring farms with similar signs
•

Recent livestock markets attended
•
Wildlife contact
•
4. REPORTING PROCEDURE
Immediate actions:
1. Contact farm veterinarian
2. If notifiable disease suspected, veterinarian reports to:
o District Veterinary Officer
o National veterinary authorities
o Chief Veterinary Officer
Information flow:
Farm → Private veterinarian → District Vet Office → National
•
Authority → OIE (if confirmed)
Legal requirement:
Failure to report is an offense
•
Report even if uncertain - better to rule out
•
Confidentiality maintained
•
5. BIOSECURITY MEASURES WHILE WAITING
Movement control:
No animals on or off premises
•
Stop all farm operations (milking, shearing)
