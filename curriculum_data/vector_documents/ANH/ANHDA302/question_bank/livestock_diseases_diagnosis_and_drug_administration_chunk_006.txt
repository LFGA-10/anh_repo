PART 5: ESSAY QUESTIONS (30 Marks)
Answer ANY THREE questions. Each question carries 10 marks.

49. (Evaluating) You are called to a farm where several cattle are
showing signs of a contagious disease. Evaluate the steps you
would take from initial assessment through to diagnosis, including
sample collection and biosecurity measures. Justify your approach.
50. (Creating) Design a comprehensive waste disposal protocol for
a veterinary clinic that handles both biodegradable and non-
biodegradable waste. Include classification, disposal methods, and
safety considerations.
51. (Analyzing) Analyze the behavioral signs of stress in at least
four different livestock species. Compare how these signs differ and
explain why understanding these differences is important for
animal welfare.
52. (Evaluating) A veterinarian needs to administer medication to
an aggressive bull. Evaluate the different restraint options available
(physical and chemical), considering animal welfare, handler safety,
and procedure effectiveness. Recommend the most appropriate
approach and justify your choice.
53. (Creating) Develop a sample collection and handling protocol
for a suspected outbreak of a zoonotic disease on a mixed farm with
cattle, sheep, and poultry. Include types of samples needed,
collection sites, labeling requirements, and transportation
procedures.
