❖ Animal and/or herd history
History taking or anamnesis is the process of obtaining information on the animal patient about
its illness, onset of illness, feeding practice etc. through careful questioning of the owner.
History taking is important for the following reasons:
To get complete information about the illness of the animal patient, as the animal patient cannot
talk as in the case of human patient.
Owner is the first person to notice the illness and he will have noted the clinical symptoms,
which will give us some important clues for the diagnosis.
To rule out the diseases those are species specific and age specific. For example, FMD in
bovine not in horses, milk fever in lactating cows not in calf.
