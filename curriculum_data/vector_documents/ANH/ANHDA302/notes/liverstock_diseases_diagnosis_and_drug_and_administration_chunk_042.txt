Topic 3: Drug administration techniques
❖ Per os: drugs are administered through the mouth
❖ Parenteral: drugs are administered by injection
✓ Intramuscular: Intramuscular injections are the most common injection
method and require the needle to be inserted into a muscle. Intramuscular
injections are commonly given in the triangular area of the neck, in front of
the shoulder.
✓ Subcutaneous: Subcutaneous injections are given under the skin using a
short needle, 1 - 2.5 cm long
✓ Intravenous: An intravenous injection requires skill to locate a vein, usually
the jugular vein in the neck, insert the needle, and ensure that the needle
remains in the vessel while the drug is given.
✓ Intraperitoneal: this route is required when the drugs are recommended to
administer into the peritoneal cavity
✓ Intrammamary: this route is required when the drugs are recommended to
administer into the peritoneal cavity
✓ Epidural: this route is required when the drugs are recommended to
administer through epidural during birthing like anesthesia
