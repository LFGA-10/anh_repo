Why the animal owner should know the weight of an animal
livestock owners often need to know the weight of their animals in order to:
➢ determine proper feed rations,
➢ to administer the proper dosage of a medication,
➢ or to track how individual animals grow and use their feed.
Approved and properly calibrated livestock scales are the most accurate and consistent
method for determining body weight.
However, there are several methods that can be used to determine an animal’s weight when
access to scales is not possible:
✓ visual observation or guessing,
✓ weigh tapes, and
✓ weight estimation formulas.
Visual observation is usually very inaccurate and not recommended for use when determining
rations, medication dosages, or when an accurate weight is important.

Weigh tapes are specially marked tapes used to measure the heart girth and convert that
measurement to a fairly accurate estimate of the animal’s body weight. Weigh tapes are easy
to use and can be utilized effectively for monitoring purposes when used in a consistent
manner by the same person. They can be purchased for cattle,
sheep, goats, hogs and horses through various livestock supply
companies.
With weight estimation formulas, a common tape measure is used to determine the heart girth
and body length measurement.
These measurements are then used to calculate the animal’s weight. The weight estimation
formulas described below are usually more accurate than a weigh tape and almost always more
accurate than visual observation in determining an animal’s weight.
