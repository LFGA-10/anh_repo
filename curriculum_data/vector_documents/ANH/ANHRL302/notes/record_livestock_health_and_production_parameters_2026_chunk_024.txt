Relative humidity = the relationship between the moisture content of the air and the maximum

5. Temperature
Air temperature is measured with thermometers. Common thermometers consist of a glass rod
with a very thin tube in it. The tube contains a liquid that is supplied from a reservoir, or "bulb,"
at the base of the thermometer. Sometimes the liquid is mercury, and sometimes it is red-colored
alcohol.
6. Rain fall
The standard instrument for the measurement of rainfall is the 203mm (8 inch) rain gauge. This is
essentially a circular funnel with a diameter of 203mm which collects the rain into a graduated
and calibrated cylinder. The measuring cylinder can record up to 25mm of precipitation
