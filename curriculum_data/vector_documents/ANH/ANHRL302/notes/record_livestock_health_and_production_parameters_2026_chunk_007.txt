Human error in recording tattoo numbers is one drawback of tattooing livestock ears, and tattoos
may fade or become difficult to read over time. Some animals and circumstances may benefit
more from the use of alternative forms of identification, such as microchipping or ear tags.
Weighing the benefits and drawbacks of each identification technique is essential before making
a final choice.
