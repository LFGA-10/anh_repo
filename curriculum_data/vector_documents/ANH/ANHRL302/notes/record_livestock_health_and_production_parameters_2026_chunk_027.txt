Milk record is the process of making measurement of milk yield and composition of milk
produced by individual animal and recording them.
The importance of milk recording help to know
• The quality of feed
• To make selection of higher productive calves
• Progeny testing of bulls
• Removing (culling) of unproductive animals
Components of milk
The milk is a product secreted by mammary gland of a female in lactation period it is composed
mainly by:
• Water
• Fat
• Lactose
• Casein
• Minerals
• Vitamin
• Whey protein
Factors influencing (affecting) milk yield
• Species: the milk produced varies according to the species
• Breed: the milk produced varies according to the breed eg: local breed and exotic breed
• Individually: large animal produces more milk eg cow normally secret 8-10% of its body
weight
• Stage of lactation: the peak of milk secretion is obtained at 2 months and decrease
slowly
