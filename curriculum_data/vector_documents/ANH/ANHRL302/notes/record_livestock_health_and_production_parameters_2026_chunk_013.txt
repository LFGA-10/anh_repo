If you suspect that the animal has a high temperature use your thermometer to check it. Remember

Physiological factors affecting the body temperature
13 | P a g e

Female, pregnant, young animals have a relatively higher temperature than male,
nonpregnant, old animal.
In all healthy animals the temperature varies during the day.
High atmospheric temperature increases the normal body temperature.
Excitation, forced exercise increase the temperature.
The normal body temperature increase after feeding.

minute.
Tachypnea or fast breathing is abnormally rapid breathing
Bradypnea is abnormally slow breathing rate
