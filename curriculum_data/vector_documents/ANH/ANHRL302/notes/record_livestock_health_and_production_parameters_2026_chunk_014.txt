MEASURE RESPIRATION RATE
a) Physical method
There are two (2) methods such as:
Paper method
Abdominal method (flank method)
Paper method
Respiration rate can be measured by placing a paper in front off the nostril the paper will move
toward the nostril during inspiration and pushed away during expiration.
Flank method/Abdominal method
Observation of right flank movement it rises during inspiration and falls during expiration
b) Auscultation method auscultation note bronchial sound and alveolar sound the
respiration is recorded by placing a stethoscope over the chest.
FACTORS AFFECTING RESPIRATION RATE
Factors influencing respiration rate are:
Environmental temperature
Age
Sex
Exercise
Nutrition (feeding)
Body condition (obesity)
