1.3. IDENTIFY LIVESTOCK HEALTH PARAMETERS TO RECORD
Introduction
One cannot become a clinician if he/she is not able to assess physiological parameters of an
animal. Those health parameters are assessed as vital signs.
Vital signs are group of four to six most important medical signs that indicate the status of the
body’s vital functions.
These measurements are taken to help assess the general physical health of an animal, give
information to possible diseases shows the progress toward recovery.
There are several parameters but in this manual a few of them are discussed.
Body weight
Body length
Body height
Thoracic circumference
Body temperature
Pulse and Heart Beat
Blood Pressure
Respiration
Rumen movements
