Methods of livestock identification
1. Ear notching is a simple and handy method to identify animals individually from a
distance. There are variations in systems, but each identifies the animal by an individual
number expressed in the relative positions of a combination of notches in the ear rim.
2. Tattooing: Tattooing is a marking technique in which a permanent mark is placed in an
unobtrusive area such as on the ear of an animal.
3. Ear tagging: is a process of Placing the tag in the animal by piercing a hole in the ear
with a secure backing is most common.
4. Livestock branding is a technique for marking livestock so as to identify the owner
5. Electronic microchip application: is a form of identification that involves the
implanting of an electronic chip, with a miniature radio transponder and antenna, under
the skin of an animal.
