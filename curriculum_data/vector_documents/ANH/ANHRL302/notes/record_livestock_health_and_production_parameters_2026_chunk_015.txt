PULSE RATE AND HEART RATE MEASUREMENT
Definition of terminologies
Pulse rate:
In medicine, a pulse represents the tactile arterial palpation of the cardiac cycle by trained
fingertips. The pulse may be palpated in ant place that allows an artery to be compressed
near the surface of the body, such as at the neck, wrist, at the groin, behind the knee, near
the ankle joint, and on foot.
Pulse rate is the number of heart beats per minute. Example: The resting pulse rate for an
average adult is between 60 and 80 beats.
Heart rate is the speed of the heart beat measured by the number of contractions(beats)
of the heart per minute(bpm)
Bradycardia is a low heart rate, defined as below 60 bpm at rest.
Tachycardia is a high heart rate, defined as above 100 bpm at rest.
