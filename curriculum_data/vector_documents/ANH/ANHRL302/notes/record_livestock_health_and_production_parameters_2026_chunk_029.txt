L.O 4.4Recoding of livestock body weight
Body weight: is defined as the mass or quantity of heaviness of an animal it is expressed in
kilograms (kg)
The importance of measuring body weight is:
 Weight is very effective in assessing reproduction efficiency and growth performance
 Determine the health status of animal when measured in relation to its age
 Used for calculate correct dose of drug
 Used for calculating amount of feed for animal
 Establish value of live animal
 Help to determine the weaning time
