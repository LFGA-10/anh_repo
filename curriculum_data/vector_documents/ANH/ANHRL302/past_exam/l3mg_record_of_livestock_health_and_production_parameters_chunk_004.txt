Question11.What are the benefits (importance) of milk recording in dairy
farming ? 5 marks
ANSWER
(Record Animal Health And Production Parameters) Page 4 of 14 – Marking Guides

Milk record is the process of making measurement of milk yield and
composition of milk produced by individual animal and recording them.
The importance of milk recording help to know
• The quality of feed 2marks
• To make selection of higher productive calves 1mark
• Progeny testing of bulls 1mark
• Removing (culling) of unproductive animals 1mark
