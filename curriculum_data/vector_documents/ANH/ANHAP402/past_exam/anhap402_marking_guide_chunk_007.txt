20. Discuss clearly about the 5 main cells of the bone with a precision on their roles during the process of bone tissue management? /10 Marks

Osteoblasts: bone forming cells synthesize and secrete un-mineralized ground substance and are found in areas of high metabolism within the bone.

Osteocytes: mature bone cells made from osteoblasts that have made bone tissue around themselves. They maintain healthy bone tissue by secreting enzymes and controlling the bone mineral content; they also control the calcium release from the bone tissue to the blood.

Osteogenic cells: respond to traumas, such as fractures, by giving rise to bone-forming cells and bone destroying cells.

Osteoclasts: bone absorbing cell – large cells that break down bone tissue – important to growth, healing, remodeling.

Bone lining cells: made from osteoblasts along the surface of most bones in an adult. Bone-lining cells are thought to regulate the movement of calcium and phosphate into and out of the bone.

21. Phagocytes are the cells involved as defensive cells of the organism. Explain briefly the role of the cells listed below: /10 Marks

Mast cells: dilate blood vessels and induce inflammation. They also recruit Macrophages and Neutrophils.

Natural killers: they kill tumor cells and virus infected cells.

Dendritic cells: Presents antigen on its surface thereby triggering an adaptive defense.

Monocyte: They differentiate into Macrophages and Dendritic cells in response to inflammation.

Eosinophils: releases toxins that kills bacteria and parasites but also cause tissue damage.
