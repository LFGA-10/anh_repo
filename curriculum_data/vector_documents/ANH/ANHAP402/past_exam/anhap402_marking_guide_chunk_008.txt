a. Briefly describe the biological digestion emphasizing on its main steps? /9 Marks

The first: the microbes carry out the fermentative breakdown of compounds that the animal cannot digest notably cellulose and some of the other structural carbohydrates of plant cell walls such as hemicelluloses.

The second: major function carried out by the microbes is synthetic. During this step is synthetized the Vit. B and essential amino acids.

The third: The microbes permit waste nitrogen from animal metabolism to be recycled into new animal protein rather than being excreted. In ruminants, however, the urea can diffuse from the blood into the rumen, where certain of the microbes break down the urea to make ammonia (NH3).
