Based on the roles of an antibody during the process of immune system, briefly describe the process of Opsonization and prevention of Microbial adhesion performed by the antibody? /4 Marks

Opsonization: Antibodies "tag" pathogens by binding to their surface, marking them for destruction.

Prevention of Microbial adhesion: By coating the surface of pathogens, antibodies prevent them from adhering to host cells, which is necessary for infection to take hold.
