19. Phagocytosis is a vital process for the animal organism. Answer appropriately to the following questions:

Define this process properly as used in physiology? /2 Marks

	It is the process by which cells ingest other cells, cell fragments, bacteria and other small particles. So, it is the process by which certain living cells called phagocytes ingest or engulf other cells or particles.

Explain briefly their functions prior to the animal body safety? /2 Marks

	Maintenance: removal of tissue debris and dead cells, important in nonimmunological and immunological clearance (apoptosis), Osteoblast/Osteoclast (bone remodeling), epithelial cells phagocytose rod outer segments shed from photoreceptors.

	Host defense: destruction of invading pathogens, deficiencies in phagocytosis lead to recurrent, life threatening infection; starts Antigen Presentation.

Discuss clearly about the effects of Osmosis plant and animal cell with a precision on the Outcome. /6 Marks

	In an isotonic solution: concentration of solute outside cell = concentration inside cell → no change in size.

	In a hypertonic solution: concentration of solute outside cell > concentration inside cell → cell

	Shrinks.

	In a hypotonic solution: concentration of solute outside cell < concentration inside cell → cell swells.
