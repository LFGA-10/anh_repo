2.2.4 CESTODOSES

The adults Taeniidae are found in domestic carnivores and man. The scolex has an armed rostellum with a concentric double row of hooks. The gravid segments are longer than they are wide.The intermediate stage (larva) is a Cysticercus, Strobulocercus, Coenurus or Hydatid cyst. And occur only in mammals. The main important genera of this family “Taeniidae” are Taenia and Echninococcus.
