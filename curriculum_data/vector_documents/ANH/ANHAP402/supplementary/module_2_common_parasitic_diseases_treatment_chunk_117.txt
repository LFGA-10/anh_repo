Toxoplasmosis

Toxoplasma gondi

The direct transmission will be cat to cat ingesting mature bradyzoites. Indirect cycle is the the cat is infected by ingesting toxoplama infected animals

In dog:

.fever

.anorexia

.diarrhoea

.pneumonia

.neurvous signs

In cats:

.rarely found

.enteritis

.encephalitis

.pneumonia

In ruminant:

.fever, dyspnea, nervous signs,

It is based on serology and the demonstration of the organism in the tissue. More recently the ELISA test has been developed

No complete satisfactory treatments but the combinttion of antimalarial drugs (pyrimethamine and sulphadiazine) are effective against the tachyzoite.

Control is by proper cleaning of litter of cat and disposal of feces. Raw meat should not be given to cat, vaccination of sheep
