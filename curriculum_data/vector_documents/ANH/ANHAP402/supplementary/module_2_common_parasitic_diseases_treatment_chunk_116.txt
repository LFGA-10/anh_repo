Cowdria ruminantium

Larvae of Abryomma tick become infected when feeding on infected cattle. Transmission to the vertebrate host by gut contents or through the saliva of infected tick while feeding

It is characterized by:

.High fever (to 420C in peracute form)

.Nervous signs

.Hydropercardium

.Hydrothorax

.Oedema of  lungs

.Oedema of brain

.Death

 The disease is developed in: Peracute , acute or chronic forms

In general it is clini-cally difficult, but it is best done at post mortem inspection (Looking on lesions).
