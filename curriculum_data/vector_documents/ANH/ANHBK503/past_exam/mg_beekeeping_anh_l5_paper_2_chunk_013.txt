Toads: In the humid tropics toads are notorious bee-eaters. In this case always place the
hives at least 50 cm above the ground.
Ants, termites: In many areas ants are a great threat to bees, and care must be taken to
protect the bees from them. One solution is to suspend the hives with wires. A second
solution is to use hive stands which are protected from ants and termites by grease. Fix a
collar of zinc or aluminium to each leg of the stand and grease the underside of these
collars once a year with old crankcase oil.
