06 ( 5 marks)
Honey bee workers perform a series of movements for communication. List and define
them.

ANSWER
Movement of workers (Dance language)
Honey bee workers perform a series of movements described as following:
 Waggle dance: to teach other workers the location of food sources more than 150m
from the hive. Scout bee fly from the colony in search of pollen and nectar. If
successful in finding good supplies of food, the scout return to the hive and dances
on the honey comb: the honey bee first walks straight ahead, vigorously shaking its
abdomen and producing a buzzing sound with the beat of its wings.
 The round dance: honey bees also use two variations of waggle dance to direct
others to food sources closer to home. The round dance, a series of narrow circular
movements alerts colony members to the presence of food within 50m of the hive.
 The sickle dance, a crescent-shape pattern of moves alerts works to food supplies
within 50-150m from the hive.
