Marking scheme:
1) Keep the apiary clean: cut grass short and trim branches that reach the hives to
prevent pests like ants from crawling into hives and disturbing the bees.
2) Grease wires holding hives to keep off crawling pests - particularly ants.
3) Do not make noise around the apiary, particularly machinery, as it can agitate bees.
4) Supplement the bee’s source of nectar by planting certain plants around the apiary
i.e. Bananas, Sunflower, Mango trees, Citrus, Coffee, Eucalyptus, Paw paws, Passion
fruits, Croton, Acacia, Bottle brush and Calliandra etc. Trees will also give shade to
the bees and provide a screen between them and people and animals nearby.
5) Remove old combs from the hive. This helps to prevent wax moth damage.
6) Do not spill honey near the apiary. It will attract pests and cause the bees to fight
with their neighbours (bees from other nearby hives).
7) Inspect hives regularly to monitor the progress of the bees and ensure no pests are
attacking them (see later for the list of pests)
8) When the bees are making honey, inspect the hives once a week by opening them
up. At other times inspect hives every month. Simply looking at the hives (without
opening them) can be done almost daily.
