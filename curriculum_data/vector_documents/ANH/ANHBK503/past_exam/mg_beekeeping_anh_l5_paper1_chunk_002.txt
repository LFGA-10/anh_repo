drone, worker, selectively fertilized, not fertilize.
- ………… are typically haploid, having only one set of chromosomes.
They are produced by the queen if she chooses …………. an egg.
- …………. produced from an egg that the queen has ………………. from
stored sperm.
- ………….can be recognized by her long abdomen, which extends far beyond
the tip of her wings in the resting position.
- There is only one ………… in each bee colony.
Marking scheme:
- Drone are typically haploid, having only one set of chromosomes. They are
produced by the queen if she chooses not to fertilize an egg.
- Worker produced from an egg that the queen has selectively fertilized from
stored sperm.
- Queen can be recognized by her long abdomen, which extends far beyond the
tip of her wings in the resting position.
- There is only one queen in each bee colony.
