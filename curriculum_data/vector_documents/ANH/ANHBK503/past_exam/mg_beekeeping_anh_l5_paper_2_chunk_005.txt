13 Workers are female bees and have two sets of chromosomes. They are produced from an (5marks)
egg that the queen has selectively fertilized from stored sperm. Workers typically develop
in 21 days, State 5 duties of workers
Marking scheme:
1. Cleaning the cells and removing debris and dead bees from the bottom of the hive;
2. Feeding the queen, the drone and the larvae which have hatched out of the eggs;
3. Guarding the flight entrance of the colony;
4. Maintaining the temperature of the brood combs at 350C
5. Producing the wax, building combs and sealing the honey and brood cells
6. Converting the nectar into ripe honey
