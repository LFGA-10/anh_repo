4. Question: Explain the difference in function and development
between the three castes in a honey bee colony: the queen,
the workers, and the drones.
Marking Guide (Total: 9 marks)

o Queen (3 marks):
▪ Function: Only fertile female; lays eggs (up to
2000/day); produces pheromones to regulate the
colony. (1 mark)
▪ Development: Develops from a fertilized egg fed
exclusively royal jelly in a special queen cell. (2
marks)
o Workers (3 marks):
▪ Function: Sterile females; perform all colony tasks:
cleaning, feeding larvae, guarding, foraging,
producing wax. (1 mark)
▪ Development: Develops from a fertilized egg in a
worker cell, fed royal jelly only for first few days,
then pollen/honey. (2 marks)
o Drones (3 marks):
▪ Function: Males; only purpose is to mate with a
virgin queen. They have no sting. (1 mark)
▪ Development: Develops from an unfertilized egg in a
larger drone cell. (2 marks)
