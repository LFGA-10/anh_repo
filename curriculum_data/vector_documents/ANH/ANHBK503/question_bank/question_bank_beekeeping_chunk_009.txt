Section 2: Long Answer/Essay Questions (Evaluating and
Creating)
Level: Evaluating

5. Question: A beekeeper has two colonies: Colony A is very
strong, with a high population, but the beekeeper notices
swarm cells on the bottoms of the frames. Colony B is weak,
queenless, and has a spotty brood pattern with a foul smell.
Critically evaluate the situation for each colony. For Colony A,
propose a management strategy to prevent swarming. For
Colony B, based on the symptoms described, diagnose the
likely problem and outline the steps you would take to manage
it, including both treatment and biosecurity measures.
Marking Guide (Total: 15 marks)
o Evaluation of Colony A (Swarming) (4 marks):
▪ The presence of swarm cells indicates the colony is
preparing to swarm due to overcrowding and/or a
strong queen. (2 marks)
▪ If not managed, the colony will likely lose its queen
and a large portion of its workforce, reducing honey
production. (2 marks)
o Prevention Strategy for Colony A (4 marks):
▪ Colony Splitting: Divide the strong colony into two
smaller ones. This reduces overcrowding and
creates a new colony. (2 marks)
▪ Provide more space: Add another honey super to
give the bees more room. (1 mark)
