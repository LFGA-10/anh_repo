Marking Guide (Total: 9 marks)
o Definition (2 marks):

▪ Swarming: Natural reproduction of a colony where it
divides. (1 mark)
▪ Absconding: Entire colony abandons the hive. (1
mark)
o Primary Cause (3 marks):
▪ Swarming: Caused by overcrowding, abundant
resources, and preparation for reproduction. Queen
cells are built. (2 marks)
▪ Absconding: Caused by stress factors like food
shortage, disturbance (pests, beekeeper), bad hive
conditions, or disease. No queen cells are built for
replacement. (1 mark)
o Outcome for Original Hive (4 marks):
▪ Swarming: The old queen and a large group of
workers leave, but a new queen (or queen cells) is
left behind. The original colony continues. (2 marks)
▪ Absconding: The entire colony leaves, abandoning
the brood nest. The original hive is left empty and
the colony does not continue there. (2 marks)
