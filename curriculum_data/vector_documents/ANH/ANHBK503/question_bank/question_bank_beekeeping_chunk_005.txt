Section 1: Short Answer Questions (Applying and Analyzing)
Instructions: Answer the following questions concisely but
completely.
Level: Applying

1. Question: A farmer wants to start a small apiary but is
unsure where to place the hives. List and explain four key
environmental factors they should consider when selecting an
apiary site.
Marking Guide (Total: 8 marks)
o 1 mark for each correctly identified factor and 1 mark for
a brief explanation (up to 4 factors, total 8 marks).
Acceptable answers include:
▪ Source of water: Bees need fresh water for cooling
the hive and raising brood. (2 marks)
▪ Sunlight: Hives should be placed in an open area
with plenty of sunshine (facing east) to encourage
early foraging and reduce irritability. (2 marks)
▪ Wind protection: The site should have natural
protection (hills, trees) from strong winds. (2 marks)
▪ Availability of nectar/pollen: The area must have
melliferous plants within foraging distance (approx.
1 mile). (2 marks)
▪ Distance from people/animals: Hives should be
away from constant noise, traffic, and disturbance.
(2 marks)
