2. T / F Worker bees are female and have a barbed sting, which
often results in their death after stinging.
(True)

3. T / F A queen bee can live for 4-5 years, but she is most
productive in her second year.
(True)
4. T / F Absconding is a form of colony reproduction where the
colony splits into two, leaving behind a new queen.
(False - Absconding is when the entire colony leaves,
abandoning the brood nest, due to stress or lack of resources.)
5. T / F When harvesting honey, it is best to take both capped
and uncapped combs to maximize the amount collected.
(False - Only capped honey should be harvested as uncapped
honey has high water content and will ferment.)
6. T / F American Foulbrood (AFB) can be effectively treated with
antibiotics without destroying the colony.
(False - AFB is incurable, and infected colonies and equipment
must be burned.)
7. T / F The ideal placement for a beehive entrance is facing
south to avoid the midday sun.
(False - It is best to face the hive east to catch the morning sun;
never south.)
8. T / F Drones are male bees that develop from unfertilized eggs
and have a primary function of mating with a new queen.
(True)
