Level: Creating
6. Question: You are a master beekeeper training a new group of
farmers. Design a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for

a honey harvest. Your SOP must be clear, step-by-step, and
safe for both the bees and the beekeeper. It should include the
following sections:
a) Pre-harvest preparation (including criteria for selecting
frames).
b) Step-by-step procedure for removing frames from the hive.
c) Step-by-step procedure for extracting and storing honey.
d) Post-harvest care for the bee colony.
Marking Guide (Total: 20 marks)
o a) Pre-harvest Preparation (4 marks):
▪ Criteria: Only remove frames with capped honey.
Uncapped honey has too much water and will
ferment. (2 marks)
▪ Do not take frames containing brood. (1 mark)
▪ Gather all necessary equipment: smoker, hive tool,
bee brush, protective clothing, clean buckets,
uncapping knife, honey extractor. (1 mark)
o b) Step-by-Step: Removing Frames from the Hive (6
marks):
▪ Step 1: Suit up in protective clothing. Light the
smoker and gently puff cool smoke into the hive
entrance. Wait a minute for the bees to calm down.
(1 mark)
