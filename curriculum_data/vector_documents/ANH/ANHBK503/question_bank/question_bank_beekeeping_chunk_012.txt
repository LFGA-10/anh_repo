Removing Frames from the Hive (6
marks):
▪ Step 1: Suit up in protective clothing. Light the
smoker and gently puff cool smoke into the hive
entrance. Wait a minute for the bees to calm down.
(1 mark)

▪ Step 2: Carefully remove the outer and inner covers
using the hive tool. (1 mark)
▪ Step 3: Gently remove frames from the honey super
one by one. (1 mark)
▪ Step 4: Use the bee brush to gently brush any
remaining bees off the frame back into the hive. (1
mark)
▪ Step 5: Place the harvested frame in a covered
bucket or container to prevent robbing. (1 mark)
▪ Step 6: After taking the desired frames, carefully
replace any untouched frames, close the hive, and
secure the covers. (1 mark)
o c) Step-by-Step: Extracting and Storing Honey (6
marks):
▪ Step 1: In a clean, bee-proof room, use an
uncapping knife or fork to remove the wax cappings
from the honeycomb. (1 mark)
▪ Step 2: Place the uncapped frames into a honey
extractor and spin them. The centrifugal force will
fling the honey out. (2 marks)
▪ Step 3: Drain the honey from the extractor through
a fine strainer or cheesecloth into a food-grade
bucket to remove wax and debris. (1 mark)
