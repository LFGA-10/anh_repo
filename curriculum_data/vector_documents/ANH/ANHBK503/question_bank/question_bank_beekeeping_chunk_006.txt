2. Question: During a hive inspection in early spring, a
beekeeper notices a spotty brood pattern with larvae that have
turned from pearly white to yellow and brown. There is a sour

smell, and many cells are open. The colony appears weak.
Based on these symptoms, which disease is the most likely
cause? Describe the recommended treatment steps for this
condition.
Marking Guide (Total: 8 marks)
o Disease Identification (2 marks): European Foulbrood
(EFB) .
o Treatment Steps (6 marks):
▪ Remove the severely affected frames and melt them
down. Replace them with frames from healthy,
unaffected colonies. (2 marks)
▪ Confine the queen to a queen cage within the hive
for 5-10 days. This gives the worker bees a chance
to clean out all the diseased larvae. (2 marks)
▪ After releasing the queen, treat the colony with an
antibiotic (e.g., tetracycline) mixed with a 1:1 sugar
solution to help clear the infection. (2 marks)
