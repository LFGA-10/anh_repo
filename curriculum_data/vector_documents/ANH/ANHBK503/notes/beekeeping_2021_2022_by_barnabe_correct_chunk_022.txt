1.2.3. Foundation sheet
This consists of a sheet of beeswax (2-3 mm thick) into which a cell pattern has
been pressed. The bees build up the cell walls on this pattern. The use of
foundation sheets promotes the construction of a regular comb provided, they
are made of pure beeswax and the right cell size is pressed.
1.2.4. Colours of beehives
Painting a beehive is simple if you remember this basic principle: “Paint the
outside and leave the inside where the bees live unpainted”.
Paint helps hive to last longer and protect wooden hive parts from the rain,
snow, sun and other weather elements.
When choosing a topcoat color, white is the classic color to pick for a hive,
but almost any color will work.
