Centrifuging the honey
Requirements: Centrifugal honey extractor; uncapping knives or forks; one or
two basins 15 cm deep made of Aluminium, Tin, galvanized iron or plastic, in
which a few uncapped frames can stand; an uncapping tray; a honey strainer
or nylon stocking; cheese cloth and a vessel.
Procedure of extraction of honey from hives with frames by using a
centrifugal honey extractor are:
i. Frames containing honey are removed and place the frames one by one on a
support on top of a recipient covered with a sieve,
ii. Honeycombs are disoperculated with the aid of an appropriate folk and
honey fall on the sieve,
iii. Frames with disoperculated honeycombs are put in an extractor of frame
model and centrifugal force projects honey at the wall of the extractor and
residues especially wax are removed,
iv. After extraction honey is placed in a decanting recipient for ascension of
light remains and remains a heavy sedimentary,
v. Maturation must be covered and days should not exceed 4 to 8 days
because honey is hygroscopic product and thus can absorb water, which
can lead to its fermentation.
5.3. Harvest ancillary products of beehives
5.3.1. Beeswax
Beeswax melts at temperatures between 63 and 65 °C. Overheated or burnt
wax is worthless. Wax that has been melted in iron, copper or zinc pots loses
