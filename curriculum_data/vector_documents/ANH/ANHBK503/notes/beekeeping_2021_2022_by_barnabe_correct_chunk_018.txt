1.2.1. Fixed comb hives (traditional hives)
Fixed comb hive is man-made cavity that can be bark (hard cover of trees)
cylinder, basket straw, wooden box, metal can etc.
The bees directly attach their comb at the upper surface of fixed comb hive.
There is a space left by bees between combs. In this type of hive, the comb
cannot be replaced. During honey harvesting the beekeeper cut the comb and
this destroys colony.

Advantages of fixed comb hives:
✓ They are cheap and
✓ Easy to make.
✓ They are seldom troubled by ants and raccoons etc. as the hives are
usually hung up in a tree, or covered with a layer of mud.
✓ The colony is not constantly disturbed by a beekeeper.
✓ Swarm can be obtained by using this hive
✓ This hive is quickly populated due to its small size.
Disadvantages:
✓ The colony cannot be inspected.
✓ When cutting out the combs, the combs with brood cannot be put back,
and are thus lost.
✓ It is difficult to prevent swarming.
✓ It is difficult to replace a queen.
✓ It produces little honey
✓ Susceptibility to many diseases
