1.3.2. Characteristics of beehives
❖ Colors of Beehives
Paint the outside and leave the inside where the bees live unpainted. Paint
helps protect wooden hive parts from the rain, snow, sun and other weather
elements.
When choosing a topcoat color, white is the classic color to pick for a hive,
but almost any color will work. It is best to avoid black or another very dark
color because these colors may cause the hive to get too hot in the summer
sun, depending on the location.
❖ Parts and measurement of beehives
✓ Bottom board: It can be made either by taking a piece of wood 550
mm long, 406 mm broad and 22 mm thick, or by joining 2 wooden
boards together, nailing in position.
✓ A brood chamber: It is a rectangular box without top and bottom
and inside is made of 22 mm thick wood. In the bottom board you
can leave a ventilation hole of ± 15 × 30 cm, which is covered with
fine wire mesh. The brood chamber holds 10 frames, which are
kept separated at the right distance by means of side bars, nails or
staples.

chamber. A queen excluder serves to keep the queen out of certain
part of the hive. The queen excluder is placed between the honey
area and the brood area.
