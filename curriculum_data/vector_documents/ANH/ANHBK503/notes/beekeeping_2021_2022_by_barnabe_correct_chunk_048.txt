Some melliferous plants found in Rwanda
For their feeding, the bees exclusively depend on plant. From those plants, the
bees harvest the pollen and nectar for honey production.
The plants which give the pollen are called pollen plants and those which
give nectar are called melliferous plants.
Nectar is sweat liquid that the bees collect from flowers while pollen is a fine
powder, produced by the male part of a flower, which is carried by insects or
the wind and causes the female part of the same type of flower to produce
seeds.
