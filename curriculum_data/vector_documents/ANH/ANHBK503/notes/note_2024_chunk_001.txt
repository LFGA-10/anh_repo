BEEKEEPING
GENERAL INTRODUCTION
What Is Beekeeping?
Beekeeping is the art of managing bees in order to obtain honey, beeswax and
other bee products for both food and income (and sometimes medicine).
Beekeeping requires little space and compliments other farm activities.
Beekeeping does not need good soil.
What is bee?
A bee is a flying insect closely related to wasps and ants. But in context of
Beekeeping, honeybee also known as Apis mellifera is a social insect known
for its important role in pollination and honey production. Bees are members of
the order Hymenoptera and the superfamily Apoidea.
Importance of Beekeeping
• Bees do not compete with livestock for food.
• Bees help the pollination of flowers, plants and crops.
• Bees help to increase the quantity and quality of flowering crops (coffee,
papaw, banana, avocado, macadamia, mangoes, etc.…).
• Bees fly 3 km from the hive so you benefit from other people’s flowers.
Bees produce honey, beeswax and propolis (used in medicines) and other
products such as royal jelly, pollen, bee venom and bee brood. Honey, beeswax
and propolis are the three products currently exploited in Rwanda.
