Procedure of introducing the queen to an already established colony
When the hive is in its final position,
✓ gently smoke it through the entrance,
✓ open it and
✓ gently smoke the top and
✓ introduce the cage.
✓ kill the queen of the colony to receive the young queen and
✓ destroy all the queen cells that may be present.
✓ If the colony has been queenless for some days, make sure that a queen has
not hatched and destroy all the queen cells.
