(1). The head: It comprises two antennae which play role of sensor organs
(they allow the bees to hear, to feed and touching).
The antennae facilitate bees to be directed, recognize the perfume and nature
of flowers as well as other substrates. They also help the bees to recognize the
odor of their hive.
The head also has the eyes (compound eye and simple eyes), mouth and small
horn to suck nectar.
(2). The thorax: It comprises three pairs of legs and two pairs of wings. The
legs are used to move and collect pollen and wings are used to fly and aerate
the hive.
(3). The abdomen: is the largest part of the bee.
It is divided into 7 segments whose the last includes the stinger connected to a
poison gland.
The venom of bees can cause danger if it is introduced in great quantity into
the body.
The abdomen is also considered as crossroad of respiratory apparatus,
circulatory and digestive system.
