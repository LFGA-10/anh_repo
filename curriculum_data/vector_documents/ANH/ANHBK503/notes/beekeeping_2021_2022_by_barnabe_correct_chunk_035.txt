Movement of workers (Dance language)
Honey bee workers perform a series of movements described as following:
 Waggle dance: To teach other workers the location of food sources more
than 150m from the hive. Scout bee fly from the colony in search of
pollen and nectar. If successful in finding good supplies of food, the
scout bee returns to the hive and dances on the honey comb.Thehoney
bee first walks straight ahead, vigorously shaking its abdomen and
producing a buzzing sound with the beat of its wings.
 The round dance: Honey bees also use two variations of waggle dance to
direct others to food sources closer to home. The round dance, a series of
narrow circular movements alerts colony members to the presence of
food within 50m of the hive.
 The sickle dance: A crescent-shape pattern of moves alerts works to
food supplies within 50-150m from the hive.
