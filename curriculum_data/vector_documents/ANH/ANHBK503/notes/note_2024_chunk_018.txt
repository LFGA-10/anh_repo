2.2.3 Scouting
Scouting is a process where scout bees go out into the world and search for
things the colony needs (pollen, nectar, water and places to live). Once they
discover what they’re searching for, they go back to the colony and report the
location and quality of what they found.
2.2.4 Swarming Phenomenon
Swarming is a honey bee natural mode of reproduction for honey bee colony
when conditions are right for the colony to divide and establish a new hive.
Swarming typically occurs in the spring or early summer when the colony's
population has grown large, and resources are abundant. A swarm usually
consists of the old queen (sometimes a new one) and 50 to 60% of the worker
bees in the swarming colony.
