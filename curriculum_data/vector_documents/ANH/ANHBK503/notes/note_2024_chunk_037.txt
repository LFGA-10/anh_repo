✓ Replacing Removed Frames: After harvesting the frames containing
honeycomb, beekeepers carefully return any frames that still contain
honey or brood (developing bee larvae) to the hive. This ensures that the
bees have enough food and resources to sustain themselves and the
colony.
✓ Covering the Hive: Once all necessary frames have been removed or
replaced, beekeepers close the hive by replacing the inner and outer
covers. This helps to protect the hive from the elements and maintain the
hive's internal temperature and humidity.
✓ Collection of Honeycombs: The harvested frames containing honeycomb
are transported to a designated processing area, where the honey will be
extracted from the comb. Beekeepers may use specialized equipment
such as honey extractors to facilitate this process.
