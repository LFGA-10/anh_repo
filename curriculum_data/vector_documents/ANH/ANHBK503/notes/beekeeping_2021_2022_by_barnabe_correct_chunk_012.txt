0.4. Methods of Beekeeping
There exist three method of beekeeping management:
✓ The gathering(hunting or wild honey harvesting)
✓ Traditional beekeeping and
✓ Modern beekeeping
(i). Gathering /hunting/Wild honey harvesting
It is a method which consist in seeking honey called wild honey at the place
that the bees colonized (holes, rocks, houses).
(ii). Traditional beekeeping / Fixed comb hives
A fixed comb hive is a hive in which the combs cannot be removed or
manipulated for management or harvesting without permanently damaging the
comb.
The traditional beekeeping method consists of hanging or placing empty hive in
tree in anticipation that a swarm of bees come to dwell in.
