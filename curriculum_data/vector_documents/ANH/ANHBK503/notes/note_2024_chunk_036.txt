2.5.4 Harvesting bee products techniques
Smoking, open covers of the hives, removal of frames, Brushing the bees
from the frames, replace removed frames, cover the hive, collection of
honey combs, Extraction of the honey.
✓ Smoking the Hive: Before opening the hive, beekeepers often use a
smoker to calm the bees and reduce the likelihood of defensive behavior.
The smoker emits cool smoke, which triggers a response in the bees'
alarm pheromones, encouraging them to gorge on honey and prepare to
flee in case of fire. This calms the bees and makes them less likely to
sting.
✓ Opening the Hive: Beekeepers carefully remove the outer and inner
covers of the hive to access the frames containing honeycomb. These
covers are usually secured with hive tools and may be stuck together
with propolis, a resinous substance produced by the bees.
✓ Removing Frames: Once the hive is open, beekeepers carefully lift out
individual frames from the hive body. These frames contain the
honeycomb, which is filled with honey and capped with beeswax.
Beekeepers inspect each frame to ensure that the honey is mature and
ready for harvest.
✓ Brushing the Bees: Before removing the frames from the hive, beekeepers
may gently brush or shake the bees off the frames using a bee brush or a
gentle puff of air. This helps to minimize the number of bees that are
inadvertently harvested along with the honeycomb.
