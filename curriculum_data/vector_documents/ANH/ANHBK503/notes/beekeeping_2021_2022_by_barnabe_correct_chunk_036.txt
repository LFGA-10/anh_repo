3.1.3. Queen
Each colony has only one queen, except during and a varying period following
swarming preparations or supersedure.
Feature characteristics of Queen in Beecolony
A queen is easily distinguished from other members of the colony by the
following characteristics:
✓ Queen is the longest bee of the three types of bees in the hives.
✓ Queen is the only mother in the hive.
✓ Queen has wings that are shorter than its abdomen
✓ Queen has sparking golden hairs like those of drones.
✓ Queen is mentally less developed than the worker bees.
✓ Queen lays an average of 2000 eggs per day depending on how old she is
and other factors like availability of food in locality.
✓ Queen mates with 7 to 8 drones that die soon after process.
✓ Queen is fed by worker bees on loyal jelly.
✓ The queen controls the activities in the hive by secreting some substances
(pheromones) that the lick from her body and pass on.
