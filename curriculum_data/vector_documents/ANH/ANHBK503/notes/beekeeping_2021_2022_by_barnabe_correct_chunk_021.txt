Langstroth hive is composed by many parts such as:
✓ The stand: any four legged stand 15-25 cm high may do. Its upper
dimensions should be such as to support the bottom board property.
✓ Bottom board: (wooden stand on which the hive rest or stay)
It can be made either by taking a piece of wood 550 mm long, 406 mm broad
and 22 mm thick, or by joining 2 wooden boards together, nailing in position
✓ A brood chamber or Hive body (large wooden box that hold 10 frames of
comb. This space is for bees to rear brood and store honey for their own
use).
It is a rectangular box without top and bottom and inside is made of 22 mm
thick wood. With a fixed bottom board and flight board; or a loose bottom
board on top of which is placed a bottomless brood chamber. In the bottom
board you can leave a ventilation hole of ± 15 × 30 cm, which is covered with
fine wire mesh.
✓ Frame and foundation (wooden frames that hold sheets of beewaxes
foundation)
✓ A queen excluder: (not absolutely necessary, plastic or metal sheet placed
horizontally between brood chamber and honey super to keep the queen in
brood)
There are two kinds, the perforated grid (a perforated zinc or plastic sheet)
and the bar or wire grid.
✓ Honey Super: (box with frame of honey comb which store surplus honey =
honey to harvest)
✓ Top ventilation screen used for ventilation during transport with 2- 3 mm
mesh wire screen.
