The honey extractor is a machine with centrifugal force designed to remove
honey from the combs with greater speed than by draining under gravity.
It is composed of cylindrical tank in which a cage of metal cloth solid turns
around its axis.
The combs are placed in wire mesh cages in the middle of a drum which is
usually of tinned metal sheeting.
Figure 8: Honey extractors
A rotating movement, at the rate of a kilometer in three minutes, brings
about a centrifugal force on the comb. The wax is retained by the wire mesh,
but the honey goes through the mesh and drains against the sheet metal wall
of the tank at the bottom of which it flows out through a special tap
