LEARNING UNIT 1: IDENTIFY BEEKEEPING TOOLS AND EQUIPMENTS
1.1. Identify tools and equipment
1. Smoker: A bee smoker is essential for working bees.
Roles of smoker
✓ The smoker is used to give smoke to the bees for easy handling;
✓ The smoker is for warning the bees, calming them, directing them, in a
word, for speaking to them;
✓ The smoke warns the bees that something is about to happen.
