In domestic bees one can distinguish the 4 species which are:
Apiscerena, Apisflorae,ApisdorsataandApismellifera or Apismellifica
A) Apiscerena:It covers the southern east of Asia. It seems like
Apismellifera. Some assumptions have said that those species are able to
cross each other but it is not possible. The Apiscerena is not productive.
B) Apisflorae:Alsocalled “little honey bee” because it is smallest among
Apis species. This species is found in southern –east of Asia and
Philipine. It cannot go over 500m of altitude; it makes one wax of 30 cm
of wide and 25 cm of length on free air.
On the above part of wax, it stores the honey which achieves rarely 1kg.
Under part of wax is reservoir for pollen and larvae.
