Apiary standards
Apiary standards refer to guidelines, best practices, and regulations
established to ensure the responsible and sustainable management of beehives
and honeybee colonies. While apiary standards vary by region, some of them
are: Registration and Licensing, Hive Placement and Density, Disease
Management, Queen Management, Beekeeper education, Record Keeping,
Beekeeping Equipment, Environmental Impact, Community Relations,
Emergency Response.
When to inspect the apiary:
➢ Inspect hives regularly to monitor the progress of the bees and ensure no
pests are attacking them.
➢ Inspect the hives once a week by opening them up in times of
production. At other times inspect hives every month. Simply looking at
the hives (without opening them) can be done almost daily.
➢ Observing the bees for a few minutes can tell you a lot. Are the bees
collecting pollen? (This is the colored substance on the legs of workers). If
they are, it indicates that the bees are feeding the young. It shows that
the bees are healthy and have a laying Queen.

without slopes for the apiary.

❖ Use a leveling tool, such as a level or transit, to ensure the
