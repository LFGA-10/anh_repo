1.2.3. Selection of construction materials
The beehives serve as the home of bees, they protect the bees against the bad
weather and facilitate in beekeeping. The beehives are made up by various
materials (Wooden, Bricks and Mortar, and Metal) but wood is recommended
because it allows ventilation.
1.2.4 Orientation and apiary standards
The bee's greatest enemy is the midday sun. It melts the wax and the honey,
destroys combs and drowns bees. Anyway, it prevents bees from going out as it
forces them to ventilate the hive. It is thus absolutely necessary to shade the
hives from the sun with trees. The ideal placement of beehives with regard to
sunlight depends on various factors, and there is no one-size-fits-all answer as
different climates and conditions may warrant different considerations. It is
best to face the hives east. The sun rouses the foragers earlier. If this
