3.4.4. Requeening
Many queens live five years and some up to nine years, but vitality decreases
with age.
Most beekeepers prefer to replace the queen before she begins to fail.
Requeening is relatively inexpensive, especially when you consider the
production loss and generally weakened colony due to a failing queen.

3.5. some important practices used in beekeeping
1.Decanting of traditional hive into a modern one
This method is done by transfer bees from a traditional hive into inhabited
modern hive.
Decanting is done during flowering period. This is because in this period there
is a lot of nectar (food for the bees). The traditional hives take about 2-3 days in
a whole hive near the newly populated hive to accustom the bees of the new
environment.
Procedure
During this operation, we proceed as follow:
✓ Give a little smoke to calm them,
✓ Remove the honey (cells of brood) of the traditional hive and brush them in
the modern hive,
✓ Shake down the traditional hive into a modern one to populate it.
The comb with brood will be transferred and fixed to the modern hive and later
they will be removed after 25 days to the be sure that all brood has emerged
the cell.
When transferring the colony from old movable comb hive to the new one, we
simply move the frames of old queen to new one and then transfer the queen
which will be followed by the workers.
This is done after taking little honey and spread it on the new hive.
