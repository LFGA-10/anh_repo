3.4.2. Hiving a Swarm
You can increase your numbers of hives by collecting swarms.
Swarms settle in all kinds of places, so there is no single procedure for
capturing them and putting them in a hive.
After a swarm leaves a hive it might settle on a nearby tree, fence post, side of a
building, or other similar object.
Swarms near the ground are relatively easy to capture. Put cloth sheet on the
ground by the bees and place a hive or box on it.
