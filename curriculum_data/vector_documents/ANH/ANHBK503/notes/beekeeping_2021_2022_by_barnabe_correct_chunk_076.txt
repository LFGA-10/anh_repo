4.3.1.3. Wax moth
(a) Description:
This pest occurs in weak colonies which cannot occupy all the frames, and in
supplies of unused frames with comb.
There are two kinds of wax moth, the larger and the smaller wax moth, 2 and 1
cm long respectively and both grey.
They lay their eggs in cracks and grooves. The eggs hatch into caterpillars
which move to the frames.
(b) Symptoms
✓ Grey caterpillars 0.5 to 2.5 cm long can be seen scrawling over the top bars
when you remove the inner lid.
✓ Combs are affected
