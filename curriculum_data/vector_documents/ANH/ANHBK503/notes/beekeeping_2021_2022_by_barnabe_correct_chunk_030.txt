B) Hive placing:
 If you don’t want to hang the hives, you can place them on stands. It is
best to use this method for the mud hive as it is not strong enough to
hang.
 Remember: the stand should be sturdy and high enough for the hive to
be at waist height.
 Put the legs of the stand in cans of used engine oil to prevent pests
getting into the hive.
 Hives on stands are more prone to attack by the honey badger (pests and
diseases).

The technique of hanging bee hives frequently used by some apiarists when
installing beehives are:
✓ Hang hives using strong greased galvanized wires to protect the bees from
pests
✓ Hang hives in or under well shaded trees
✓ Suspend hives from wires so that predators such as the honey badger can
not push them over.
✓ To hang hives in order to allow base harvesting.
✓ To use trees or solids poles to hang the hives.
✓ To hang hives at waist height above the ground level.
✓ To hang hives or solid poles to hang the hives at waist height above the
ground level to facilitate hives visits and honey harvesting.
