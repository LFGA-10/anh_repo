2.3.1 Identification of Equipment and Tools
➢ Protective clothing
1. Overall
A wide variety of overalls (bee suits) is available to beekeepers in a wide price
range. The most expensive bee suits are not always the best or easiest to use.
Overalls are useful to avoid getting propolis on your clothing and greatly reduce
stings if maintained properly and laundered regularly. Coveralls or shirtveils
(long-sleeved shirts) made especially for beekeepers with attached, removable
veils are popular.
White or tan clothing is most suitable when dealing with worker bees. Other
color are acceptable, but bees react unfavourably to dark color, fuzzy materials,
and clothing made from animal fiber.
2. Veil
It is worn over the face for protection against stings. It should be made of black
light material such as nylon netting. They are two types : The fabric net veil
and the metal one.
The fabric net veil has the advantage that it does not take up any room and it
can be put in the pocket. But it has the disadvantage of making the operator's
head feel hotter and impairs his vision.
