4.3.4.7. Animals
Toadswillwillingly eat bees that they find below the hive. These are lost bees,
since they do not have the strength to fly again. In any event, the services that
toads render besides are compensation for this rare bit of gourmandizing.
Miceare harmful in the hives. They eat the wax and the honey; they destroy
the combs to make their large, often very comfortable, nests. It is easy to
prevent mice from entering the hives by reducing the metal entrances (mouse
guards) in autumn and winter.
