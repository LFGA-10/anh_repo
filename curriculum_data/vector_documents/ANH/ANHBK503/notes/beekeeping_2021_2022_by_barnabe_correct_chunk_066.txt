4.1.3.3. Supersedure
Supersedure is queen replacement without colony division. If the old queen
begins to fail (reduction of producing pheromone), workers will construct queen
cells to rear a replacement queen. These supersedure cells are usually located
on the face areas of the comb. The old queen does not leave the colony in
supersedure. The new queen mate, returns to the colony, and begins to lay.
