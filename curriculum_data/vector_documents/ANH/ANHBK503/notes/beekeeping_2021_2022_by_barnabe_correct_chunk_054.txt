2. Re-enforcement of a week colony
When the population of bees is weak before harvesting honey, we can join two
hives which are weak in production and increase productivity. This operation is
done in the evening hours.
To install the new colony of mixed bees,
✓ first take the weak hive, hold it with one hand, and
✓ reverse it to the strong colony by putting a paper with holes between the two
hives.
This will make a mixer of the two colonies onto one hive. The strong queen will
kill the weaker queen and bees have hived in one and remain with one queen.

3. Transhumance of the hive
It may happen to take a hive from one place to another place.
Transhumance can be done by giving bees more melliferous flora or by putting
the hive in the shelter or by changing of the owner. For not losing bees, the
operating is done at the evening when they are coming back or entering.
We close the flying hole with a piece of paper, by grass or by cow dung. It is not
good to damage the frames by sudden movements; we transport the hive
respecting its direction.
To accustom bees, the fly holes is opened only 2 cm at the first day and
completely at the second.
