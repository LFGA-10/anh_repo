4.1.3.4. Absconding
Abscondingis the abandonment of a nest site by a whole colony. It is usually
due to excessive disturbance of the colony by predators or beekeepers, to
diminishing resources in an area, bad organization of beekeeper for example
when using unsuitable hive, attacked by rain…an excessive temperature or
sun may also be the cause of absconding.
4.1.3.5. Orphaned hive/colony
When the queen die by natural or accident case, the hive becomes orphan. The
accident may caused by beekeeper during visiting. The signs of this situation
are:
✓ The bees become anxious and aggressive,
✓ Presence of royal cell for replacing the queen
✓ Absence of egg and if death occurred in some days before, the larva are
not present.
When the queen died during mating period (outside) the worker cannot
construct the royal cell due to absence of brood and the colony become
irreversible orphan and disappear completely.
