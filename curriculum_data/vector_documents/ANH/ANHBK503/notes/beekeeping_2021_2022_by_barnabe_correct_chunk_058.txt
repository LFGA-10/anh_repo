3.6. Annual work calendar of an Apiary
1. Work during the honey season
At this period, there are an intense activity of the workers and a strong laying
of the queen, which causes swarming.
These periods are:
February-march; June-July and September-October
Works to be carried out during this period:
✓ Collect honey
✓ Addition of the rises
✓ Capture of the swarms and settlement of the hives
✓ Fight against swarming
✓ Install queen excluder
2. Work during the weak season
Atthe time of weak season, activities of the hives are slowed down. The queen
lays less, the bees decrease and are much attacked by the predators.
This period corresponds to: April, January, May, November, and December.

3.7. Visit of the hive
It is good to visit the hive once in 15 days; good hours are morning
(6h00:10h00) and evening at 4h00 pm. This is because bees are less aggressive
and calm with this time.
The followings are precautions to consider before visiting the beehive
✓ Protect yourself by using veil to avoid stings,
✓ Get a smoker that provides smoke to calm bees
✓ Avoid noise and sudden movement which disturb bees
