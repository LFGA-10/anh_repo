• Laying of the queen
The queen only occupies the laying, 4-5 days after fecundation the laying may
begin. The eggs have the white color and measuring 2mm. It is covered by
coating substance which fixes it on the cell. One egg weighs 0.132 mg.
Recall that average weight of queen is 200mg. The laying is not done by
random, but follow established order, the queen begins by central cell and
continues by doing centric circle.
The laying of the queened pends upon many factors such as:
✓ Season,
✓ Age of queen,
✓ Number of worker bees in colony,
✓ Volume of shelter.
The fertilized eggs give workers and non-fertilized eggs give drones. For this
reason, when the queen is old and spermatheca is expired, the hive becomes
drones. This may also occur when the queen dies and there are no fertilized
eggs in brood.

• Development of honey bee
Development of honey bee goes through four main stages: eggs, larva, pupa
and young adult. The development duration varies according to the class of
bee(worker, queen and drone).
