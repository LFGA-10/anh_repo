The smoker helps in making bees consume honey instead of flying and
stinging.
The smoker consists of a metal fire pot and grate with bellows attached. It
consists of a tin can provided with a spout for directing smoke from the
smouldering material inside it with the help of bellows.
Figure 1: A smoker with bellows
To produce large quantities of cool, thick smoke, coals must be above the grate
and unburned materials must be above the coals.
Suitable smoker fuels include corn cobs, wood shavings, pine needles,
cardboard, punk wood, bark, sumac bobs, cotton rags, dry leaves, and bailer
twine.
An alternative liquid smoke is available that you mix with water and spray onto
the bees with a mister-type applicator.
A pipe, cigar or cigarette can often replace a smoker.
