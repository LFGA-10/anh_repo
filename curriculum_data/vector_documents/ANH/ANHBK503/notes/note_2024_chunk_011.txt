2.1 Select starter colonies
2.1.1 Description of starter colony
Colony is collective organism made up of single bee family, consist of one
queen, drones and thousands of workers honeybees.
Composition, Characteristics and Function of Colony members
Workers
Workers are female bees and have two sets of chromosomes. They are produced
from an egg that the queen has selectively fertilized from stored sperm.
Workers typically develop in 21 days. A typical colony may contain as many as
60,000 worker bees. Workers exhibit a wider range of behaviours than either
queens or drones.
Depending on the needs of the colony, the young workers perform the
following duties:
✓ Cleaning the cells and removing debris and dead bees from the bottom of
the hive;
✓ Feeding the queen, the drone and the larvae which have hatched out of
the eggs;
✓ Guarding the flight entrance of the colony;
✓ Maintaining the temperature of the brood combs at 350C
✓ Producing the wax, building combs and sealing the honey and brood
cells
✓ Converting the nectar into ripe honey
