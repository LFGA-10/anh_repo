ii. Top bar
Top bars are wood bar wholes with mirrors natural comb spacing. The bees
attach their comb to these bars.
iii. Cover
A cover protects a hive from the weather, it sets above the top bar
Advantages
✓ Easy to build
✓ Facilitate comb rotation
✓ Cheap and adoptable to local building materials
✓ Produce the highest quality of comb
✓ Less colony disturbance
Disadvantages
✓ Not compatible with standards equipments,
✓ Heavy to move
✓ Cannot disassembled to reduce weight
✓ Produce less honey
✓ Not suitable for large-scale beekeeping
✓ Take longer to work
