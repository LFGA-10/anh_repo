✓ Honey robbing events
The main honey robbing events that should be identified by the beekeeper
during apiary visits are:
✓ High number of bees flying around the apiary and actively scouting around
the outside of near by colonies.
✓ Bees fighting at the entrance and or flying to gain enter into the colony
through areas where two supers meet and or brood.
✓ Colonies in an apiary may have a heithtened (to intensify or elevate)
defensive.
✓ There often are more dead bees at colony entrance or deed bees lie on the
landing board or on the ground infront of the hive.
