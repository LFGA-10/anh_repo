(ii) Beekeeping Constraints
Generally, all these constraints affect the production of honey and other
products of beehives.
✓ Lack of sufficiency modern hives;
✓ Climate change;
✓ Use of insecticides;
✓ A knowledge less of beekeepers about beekeeping techniques;
✓ Insufficiency of harvesting and transport equipments;
✓ Insufficiency of experts in honey processing;
✓ The long travel to the beekeeping center;
✓ The use of the local smokers;
✓ An unorganized deforestation;
✓ We don’t know the quantities of honey produced in Rwanda;
✓ Lack of skills and technology;
✓ Application by oldest man resulting in low quantity produced for
consumption only;
✓ Non acceptability of people for this art.
(iii) Suggested solutions at above constraints
1. Creation of some association or cooperatives
2. Encourage people to the application of this art
3. Make rules to beekeepers to develop their activities
4. Change the mind of beekeepers to produce not only for consumption but
also for marketing
5. To encourage beekeepers to get loan in order to produce
6. Make modern hives and other equipment’s
