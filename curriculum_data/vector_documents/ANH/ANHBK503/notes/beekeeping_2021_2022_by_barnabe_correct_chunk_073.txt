The circumstances under which artificial nutrition in beekeeping is
needed are:
✓ We can retain a swarm in a hive and help to honey comb,
✓ The dry season that takes long time and is very warm and thus we can
avoid the mortality of bees by scarcity
✓ We can strength a young swarm or a very weak hive
✓ We can find that there are bees in a hive when season indicates as very
weak (as many flowers have been collected)
Procedure:
✓ Mix 1 kg of sugar with 1.5 litres of water
✓ Put every day 1/10 of this mixture in a hallow out plate or an open recipient
✓ Put inside the blade or stores to avoid bee drowning
✓ The recipient containing the syrup will be put on the empty under the
frames
✓ We can also put in the recipient of honey or of crashed honey comb or
banana juice. It is good to put water near the hive if there is no flowing
water or river nearby.
