supersedure, or swarming.
✓ For emergency: When an old queen is accidentally killed, lost, or removed,
the worker bees select younger worker larvae to produce emergency queens.
These queens are raised in worker cells modified to hang vertically on the
comb surface.
✓ For supersedure: When an older queen begins to fail (decreased production
of queen substance), the colony prepares to raise a new queen. Queens
produced as a result of supersedure are usually better than emergency
queens since they receive larger quantities of food (royal jelly) during devel-
opment.
✓ For swarm: When the hive population becomes too big according to the size
of shelter, the colony is divided into two parts.
How to find the Queen?
In the hive with a queen excluder we have a mechanical means quickly to find
the queen without endangering her, and without the beekeeper being
particularly experienced.
