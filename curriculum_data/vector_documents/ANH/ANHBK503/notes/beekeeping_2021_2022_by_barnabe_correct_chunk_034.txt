Works performed by workers in beecolony
Depending on the needs of the colony, the young workers perform the
following duties:
✓ Cleaning the cells and removing debris and dead bees from the bottom of
the hive;
✓ Feeding the queen, the drone and the larvae which have hatched out of the
eggs;
✓ Guarding the flight entrance of the colony;
✓ Maintaining the temperature of the brood combs at 350C
✓ Producing the wax, building combs and sealing the honey and brood cells
✓ Converting the nectar into ripe honey
