2. Elevating: Lift hives off the ground for various benefits, including
ventilation and flood prevention.
Process:
❖ Use hive stands, pallets, or similar structures to elevate the hives
❖ Elevating hives helps with hive ventilation, reduces the risk of
flooding, and makes hive inspections easier. Ensure the elevation
is stable and levelled.

3. Roofing: Provide shelter and protection for beehives from weather
elements
Process:
❖ Choose a durable roofing material, such as metal or weather-
resistant wood
❖ Construct roofs that provide adequate coverage for the hive
without interfering with bee traffic
❖ Ensure proper ventilation to prevent overheating during hot
weather.
1.3. Install beekeeping equipment’s
1.3.1. Identification of apiary equipment’s.
❖ Beehives Description
Beehives are hallowing containers that can be closed and are purpose made to
house bees. The hive used by a beekeeper must be appropriate to his way of
beekeeping.
➢ Traditional beehives or fixed comb hives
These can be made from a hollow log, a wooden box, an earthen pot
or a metal container. The bees fill all the available space with combs
from the top downwards. The honey can be removed only removing
one wall of the hive and breaking or cutting out the honey combs.
