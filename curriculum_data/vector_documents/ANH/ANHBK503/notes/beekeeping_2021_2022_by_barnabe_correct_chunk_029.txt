2.4. Equip an apiary
2.4.1. Placing the hives
A) Hanging hives: Do not hang the mud/stick hive as it is not strong enough
• Use two strong and heavy posts, each about 2.5 to 3 meters long.
• Dig two holes about 3/4 of a meters deep and 2 meters apart.
• Pack soil and stones around the posts. Make sure the posts are very
firm.
• Think ahead to when the hive will be heavy with honey - if they are not
firm they will fall over later.
• Now that the holes are dug and the posts are in position, use wires to
hang the hive between the two posts.
