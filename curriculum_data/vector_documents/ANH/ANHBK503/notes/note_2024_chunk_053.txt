3. Black Queen Cell Virus
BQCV Is caused by virus in the Cripavirus genus, BQCV causes mortality in
queen bee pupae, with dead queen bee larvae turning yellow and then brown-
black. The diseases are most common in spring and early summer. It is
believed that infection with BQCV may be transmitted by Nosema apis, a
microsporidian parasite of honey bees that invades the gut of adult honey bees.
At latter stages of infection, the dead queen bee may change to brown-black.
The walls of the queen cell bee also become darker, brown-black color. If
Nosema diseases is present within a queen bee breeding operation, it is always
useful to look for signs of BQCV on regular basis. Is through to be transmitted
by nurse bees when they feed larvae infected brood food. And may remain
