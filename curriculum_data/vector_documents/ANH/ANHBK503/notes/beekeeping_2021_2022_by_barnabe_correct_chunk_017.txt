1.2. Select beehives
Beehives are hallow containers that can be closed and are purpose made to
house bees.
Under natural conditions bee colonies will choose their own nesting site, which
can be any hallow tree, a rock cavity or a discarded container.
In general, the following three types of beehives are most commonly used:
✓ Fixed comb hives
✓ Removable comb hive with top bars (Kenyan top bar beehive)
✓ Removable comb hives with frames.
