2.2.3. Protecting the hives
(i) Sun: The hives must always be protected from the hot sun.
There are three ways of doing this:
1) Place the hives in or under a tree which offers sufficient shade.
2) Place an additional roof made of corrugated iron (straw or leaves) above
each one of the hives (if they are widely spaced).
3) Put the hives close together and build one extra roof (zinc sheets, straw,
reeds, and leaves) to cover all of them.
(ii) Wind: Place the hives with their flight entrance out of the prevailing
wind direction. If the hives are in an open place and there is a continuous
strong wind from one direction, it is a good idea to plant a hedge or construct a
windscreen (grass, straw, leaves).
