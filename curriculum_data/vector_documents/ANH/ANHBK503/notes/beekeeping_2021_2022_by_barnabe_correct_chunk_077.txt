4.3.2.Bacterial diseases
4.3.2.1.European foul brood
(a)Description (causes)
The disease is caused by bacteria which attack the larvae at an early stage.
When there are no larvae the disease cannot persist.
(b) Symptoms:
✓ The larvae die before the cells are capped (pupation).
✓ The diseased larvae turn from white to a dirty yellow and then to brown.
✓ There is a sour smell in the hive.
✓ There are many open cells in the combs with capped brood.
✓ The workers drag out the dead larvae.
(c)Treatment:
(i). mechanical treatment
✓ Remove the severely affected frames and melt them down. Provide frames
from unaffected colonies.
