➢ Nectar
Nectar, a liquid high in sucrose, is produced in plant glands known as
nectaries. It is an important energy resource for honey bees
➢ Propolis
Propolis or bee glue is created from resins, balsams, and tree saps. Those
species of honey bees that nest in tree cavities use propolis to seal cracks in
the hive. Dwarf honey bees use propolis to defend against ants by coating the
branch from which their nest is suspended to create a sticky moat. Propolis is
consumed by humans as a health supplement in various ways and also used
in some cosmetics.
➢ Wax
Worker bees of a certain age will secrete beeswax from a series of glands on
their abdomens. They use the wax to form the walls and caps of the comb. Bee
wax can be used in making candle, cosmetic and skincare products,
woodworking and furniture polishes, food preservation, leatherworking, sealing
and lubricating.
