4.3.2.2. American foul brood
(a) Description(causes)
The disease is caused by spore forming bacteria and is very contagious. The
bacteria infect the larva and the disease is transferred through infected frames
and hives, but also through honey.

(b) Symptoms:
✓ The bees die in the larval or pupal stage (capped brood).
✓ The cell capping of the affected larvae are indented.
✓ The brood nest is irregular.
✓ In the affected cells you will find a sticky mass, which forms threads when
you remove it from the cell with a matchstick.
✓ There is a smell of glue (glutton) in the hive.
✓ Affected larvae are dark brown to black.
(c) Treatment:
✓ All the bees and frames of an infected colony must be burnt in a hole in the
ground;
✓ Scorch the chambers, bottom board and lids clean, and then wash them
with water and washing soda;
✓ Prevent spreading of the disease by not feeding any honey, and by not
exchanging combs, frames or hives.
