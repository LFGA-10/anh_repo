The round dance
Honey bees also use two variations of waggle dance to direct others to food
sources closer to home. The round dance, a series of narrow circular
movements alerts colony members to the presence of food within 50m of the
hive.
The sickle dance
A crescent-shape pattern of moves alerts works to food supplies within 50-
150m from the hive.

2.2.2 Defense
Honeybees build a collective defense against intruders:
✓ Stinging: When a honeybee perceives a threat, it may sting to defend
itself or the hive. The stinger contains venom, which can cause pain and
allergic reactions in humans and other animals.
✓ Alarm Pheromones: When a honeybee stings, it releases alarm
pheromones that signal danger to other bees in the vicinity. These
pheromones can trigger an aggressive response from other bees,
prompting them to join in defense of the hive.
✓ Guard bees
✓ Mass defense
✓ Winged Guards: Some bees within the hive, known as "guard bees,"
patrol the area around the hive, scanning for potential threats. If they
detect a threat, they may engage in aerial combat with the intruder to
drive them away.
