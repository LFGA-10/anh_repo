2. Brush (whisk broom)
Is often employed to brush off bees from honey combs before it is taken away
for extraction. This helps to brush the bees off the honey so that you can easily
see if it is worth harvesting.
3. Queen cage/Box
A small container to hold a queen bee, the cage protect the queen from an
almost certain onslaught and gives her a safe place to hide, mostly in new
queenless colony inorder to determine whether the new queen will be accepted
or rejected.
Figure : Queen cage/Box
4. Queen Excluder
A queen excluder serves to keep the queen out of certain part of the hive. The
queen excluder is placed between the honey area and the brood area.
