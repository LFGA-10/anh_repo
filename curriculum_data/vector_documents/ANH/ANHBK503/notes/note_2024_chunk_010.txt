❖ Hanging hives:
✓ Do not hang the mud/stick hive as it is not strong enough
✓ Dig two holes about 3/4 of meters deep and 2 meters apart.
✓ Pack soil and stones around the posts. Make sure the posts are
very firm.
✓ Think ahead to when the hive will be heavy with honey - if they are
not firm, they will fall over later.
✓ Now that the holes are dug and the posts are in position, use wires
to hang the hive between the two posts.
