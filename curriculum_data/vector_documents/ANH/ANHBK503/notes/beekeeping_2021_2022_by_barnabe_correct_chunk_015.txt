All beekeepers ought to possess at least two veils, one for themselves and one
for a helper.
Most beekeepers, especially beginners, wear a veil for all beekeeping work.
By means of the veil they will feel safer and more confident, so they will work
faster and with greater skill.
3. Gloves: They may be made of heavy leather or cloth and are useful for
beginners to develop confidence but handling of frames becomes cumbersome,
therefore, a good beekeeper will never prefer to use gloves.

3. Hive tool: It is a piece of flattened iron with hammered down edges.
Roles of hive tool:
Hive tool is used for:
✓ Prying the frames in the hive
✓ For scraping bee glue
✓ A hive tool helps you to open the hive cover and the top bars without over
shaking the hive,
✓ It splits across the propolised top bars which a beekeeper should have used
much energy.
✓ A hive tool also works as a knife because it has a sharp edge
