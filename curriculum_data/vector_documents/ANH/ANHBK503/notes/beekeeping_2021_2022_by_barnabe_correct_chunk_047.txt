C) Apisdorsata: Also name “stone bee, great Indian bee”. It has the same
geographical situation distribution as Apis florae but it is found up to
2000m of altitude. This species prefers also the free air and construct
one suspended wax on stone or tree. The wax can achieve 1.8m of height
and 20 cm of wide. This species is practically not domestic, but it may
supply much honey up to 20kg.
D) Apismellifera or Apismellifica: This species is found in many parts of
world (Europe Africa, Western Asia, America). This Apismellifera is more
known among the apis species of economic utilities.
