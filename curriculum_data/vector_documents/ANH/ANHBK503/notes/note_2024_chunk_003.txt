➢ Site dimension: Plan the site with larger area so that the scalability will
also be possible. Larger space help also facilitate in hive inspection and
maintenance.
➢ Distance from people habitation: Hives should be excluded from traffic,
constant noise, and disturbance from children and animals.
➢ Sources of nectar and melliferous plants: Nectar and pollen sources
must be within a short distance (roughly 1 mile) of the hives. Pollen is
essential for brood rearing, and nectar (honey) is the bees’ basic source of
energy.

➢ Away from agriculture chemical use (Absence of pesticides): Acquaint
yourself with the pesticides commonly used in the area, and place
colonies away from fields or other areas that are routinely treated with
pesticides.
➢ Basic Infrastructure:
❖ Market: When selecting an apiary site, you should consider that
market may not be too far to be supplied with bee products
(Honey, Propolis, etc..).
❖ Roads/Accessibility: There should be a good path leading to the
apiary so that you do not have to carry the hives and the basins or
containers of honey too far.
