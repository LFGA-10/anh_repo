4.3.4.4.Loss of queen
(a)Symptoms:
✓ Bees are restless in the hive.
✓ There is a wailing sound in the hive.
✓ There is either no brood or drone brood in worker cells.
✓ If there are eggs, there is more than one egg to a cell.
4.3.4.5. The beekeeper
It does happen that the beekeeper does not know his job and treats the bees in
a way contrary to their nature and needs.
The beekeeper must inform himself before setting up his hive. This manual, re-
read frequently and properly understood, will suffice.
