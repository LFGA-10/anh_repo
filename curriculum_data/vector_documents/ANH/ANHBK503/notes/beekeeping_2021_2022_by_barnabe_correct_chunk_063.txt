4.1.3. Principal characteristic of honey bee
4.1.3.1. Aggressive nest defense behavior
In certain period the bees tend to be aggressive, especially in dry season. The
cause of aggressive behavior may be the following:
✓ Shortage of food in area or in certain time
✓ Disturbance of colony by predators including human
✓ Introduction of foreign smell in hive or colony
✓ Preparation of for swarming or absconding
✓ Natural characters of a given colony
✓ Dark color and hair
4.1.3.2. Swarming
Swarming is natural colony division or reproduction. When a colony has
reached a large size and there are abundant resources available, the workers
will construct queen cells. These queen cells or swarm cells are usually located
around the edges of the comb.
