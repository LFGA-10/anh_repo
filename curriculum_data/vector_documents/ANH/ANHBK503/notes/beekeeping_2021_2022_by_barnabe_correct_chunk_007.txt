0. Introduction
Definition:
Beekeeping is the art of managing bees in order to obtain honey, beeswax and
other bee products for both food and income (and sometimes medicine).
Beekeeping (or apiculture, from Latin apis, bee) is the maintenance of honey
bee colonies, commonly in hives, by humans.
A beekeeper (or apiarist) keeps bees in order to collect honey and other
products of the hive (including beeswax, propolis, pollen, and royal jelly), to
pollinate crops, or to produce bees for sale to other beekeepers.
A location where bees are kept is called an Apiary or "Beeyard".
0.1. Importance of beekeeping and Beekeeping Constraints
(i) Importance of beekeeping
The main benefits from beekeeping are:
✓ The production of product such as honey,propolis and beewax which are
valuable source of income for small holder farmers.
✓ Bees help the pollination of flowers, plants and crops.
✓ Bees help to increase the quantity and quality of flowering crops (coffee,
banana, avocado, macadamia, mangoes, etc.).
✓ Bees fly 3 km from the hive so you benefit from other people’s flowers.
✓ Bees produce honey, beeswax and propolis (used in medicines) and other
products such as royal jelly, pollen, bee venom and bee brood.
✓ In Rwanda, honey is used in the manufacture of wine of banana (inkangaza)
✓ In Rwanda, honey is used in the manufacture of beer of sorghum (inturire).
✓ In traditional medicine, honey is used in several diseases treatment and
