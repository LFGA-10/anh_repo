4. Question: A lamb has been presented with a swollen, painful,
and foul-smelling foot. The veterinarian suspects a hoof
infection. As the veterinary assistant, you are asked to prepare
for examination and possible treatment. Describe the steps
you would take to properly examine the hoof and list three
potential infectious diseases the vet might be looking for.
Marking Guide (Total: 9 marks)
o Hoof Examination Procedure (6 marks):
▪ Restraint: Ensure the lamb is properly restrained,
ideally on a table with an assistant. (1 mark)
▪ Cleaning: Thoroughly clean the affected foot with
water and a brush to remove all debris and manure.
(2 marks)
▪ Inspection: Visually inspect the interdigital space,
heel bulbs, and hoof wall for lesions, swelling, or
foreign bodies. (1 mark)
▪ Palpation: Gently palpate the foot to check for pain,
heat, and swelling. (1 mark)
▪ Use of Tester: If available, use a hoof tester to
apply pressure and localize the source of pain. (1
mark)
