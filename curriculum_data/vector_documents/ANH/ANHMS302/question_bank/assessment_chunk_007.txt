3. Question: Compare and contrast the Hot Iron method and
the Wire Saw method for dehorning. In your answer, discuss
the age of the animal each method is suitable for, the level of
bleeding, and the risk of post-operative complications.
Marking Guide (Total: 9 marks)
o Age Suitability (2 marks):
▪ Hot Iron: Suitable for young calves (disbudding)
before the horn attaches to the skull. (1 mark)
▪ Wire Saw: Suitable for adult cattle with large, fully
developed horns. (1 mark)
o Level of Bleeding (2 marks):
▪ Hot Iron: Bloodless method; the heat cauterizes
blood vessels. (1 mark)
▪ Wire Saw: High risk of significant haemorrhage. (1
mark)
o Risk of Complications (5 marks):
▪ Hot Iron: Lower risk if done correctly. Main risks are
burns and potential for sinusitis if the calf is older.
(2 marks)
▪ Wire Saw: Higher risk of complications including
severe bleeding, sinusitis (as the frontal sinus is
opened), bacterial infection, and myiasis (fly strike).
