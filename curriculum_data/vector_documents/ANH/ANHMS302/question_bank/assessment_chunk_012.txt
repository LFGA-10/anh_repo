o b) Step-by-Step Procedure (10 marks):
▪ Step 1: Restraint: Safely restrain the animal,
ideally in a crush or cast it down on clean ground.
(1 mark)
▪ Step 2: Aseptic Preparation: Scrub the scrotal
area with surgical soap and apply antiseptic. The
surgeon must scrub hands and wear clean gloves.
(2 marks)
▪ Step 3: Anaesthesia: If the animal is mature,
infiltrate the spermatic cords and scrotal skin with
local anaesthetic. (1 mark)
▪ Step 4: Incision: Grasp the scrotum, pull down to
tense the skin, and make a vertical incision through
one side of the scrotum, exposing the testicle. (1
mark)
