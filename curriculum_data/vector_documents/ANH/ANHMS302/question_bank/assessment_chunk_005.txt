1. Question: A farmer has a 2-week-old calf that needs to be
castrated. He is worried about pain and bleeding. Based on
the module, which castration method would you recommend
and why? Describe the basic steps you would take to perform
this method.
Marking Guide (Total: 6 marks)
o Recommendation (1 mark): Recommend the Elastrator
(rubber ring) method as it is suitable for very young
animals.
o Justification (2 marks):
▪ It is a bloodless method, so there is no bleeding. (1
mark)
▪ It is quick and less painful for very young animals
as the area numbs quickly after application. (1
mark)
o Basic Steps (3 marks):
▪ Properly restrain the calf, with an assistant holding
it. (1 mark)
▪ Place the rubber ring on the elastrator tool and
open it. (1 mark)
▪ Ensure both testicles are in the scrotum, then slip
the ring over the scrotum, releasing it just above the
testicles to cut off blood supply. (1 mark)
