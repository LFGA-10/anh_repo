▪ Step 4: Incision: Grasp the scrotum, pull down to
tense the skin, and make a vertical incision through
one side of the scrotum, exposing the testicle. (1

▪ Step 5: Exteriorize Testicle: Push the testicle out
through the incision. (1 mark)
▪ Step 6: Ligate and Remove: Isolate the spermatic
cord. Place a ligature (tie) around it using
absorbable suture. (2 marks) Cut the cord distal to
the ligature (towards the testicle) using an
emasculator or scalpel. (1 mark)
▪ Step 7: Repeat & Post-op: Repeat for the second
testicle through the same incision or a new one.
Apply antiseptic and fly repellent to the wound. (1
mark)
o c) Complications and First-Aid Response (4 marks):
▪ Haemorrhage: If bleeding is severe, locate the
bleeding vessel and apply pressure with gauze. If
possible, clamp and tie it off with a ligature. (1
mark)
▪ Infection/Swelling: If signs of infection appear
post-op, isolate the animal, clean the wound with
antiseptic, and administer antibiotics. The wound
may need to be reopened to drain pus. (2 marks)
▪ Eventration (prolapse of intestine): This is an
emergency. Keep the exposed tissue moist with
sterile saline, cover with a clean cloth, and seek
immediate veterinary assistance. (1 mark)
