Marking Guide (Total: 15 marks)
o Evaluation of Potential Complications (6 marks):
▪ Infection: The swelling and fever are classic signs
of infection (e.g., septic funiculitis, peritonitis). (2
marks)

▪ Haemorrhage: While less likely with fever, internal
bleeding could contribute to swelling. (1 mark)
▪ Post-operative Swelling/Oedema: Normal to some
extent, but excessive swelling indicates a problem.
(1 mark)
▪ Clostridial Infection: A serious risk if aseptic
technique was poor. (2 marks)
o Immediate Post-Operative Care (5 marks):
▪ Confine the animal to a clean, well-bedded area for
a few hours to monitor for bleeding. (1 mark)
▪ Administer a long-acting antibiotic to prevent
infection. (1 mark)
▪ Apply a fly repellent to the wound. (1 mark)
▪ Keep the animal under close observation for the first
week for signs of swelling, stiffness, or depression.
(2 marks)
o Recommended Steps Now (4 marks):
▪ Isolate the animal immediately. (1 mark)
▪ Examine the wound: Bathe it with warm water and
an antiseptic. The wound may need to be opened
and drained to release pus. (2 marks)
