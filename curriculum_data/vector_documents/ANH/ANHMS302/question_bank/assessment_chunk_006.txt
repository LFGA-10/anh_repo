2. Question: You are preparing to suture a clean, deep laceration
on a dog's leg. List four different types of suture patterns
mentioned in the module and briefly describe a specific
situation where each pattern would be the most appropriate
choice.
Marking Guide (Total: 8 marks)
o 1 mark for each correctly named suture pattern, and 1
mark for a logical application. (Up to 4
patterns/applications, total 8 marks). Acceptable
answers include:
▪ Simple Interrupted: For a wound where security is
paramount or if infection is a concern, as each
suture is independent. (2 marks)
▪ Continuous Subcuticular: For closing the dermis
layer under the skin to provide a neat, cosmetic
closure with minimal suture marks on the surface.
(2 marks)
▪ Purse-string Suture: To close a lumen, such as
around the stump of the appendix or to hold a
cannulation tube in place. (2 marks)
▪ Interrupted Vertical Mattress: For wounds under
tension, as it provides strong support by taking a
deep bite of tissue. (2 marks)
