Topic 1 Selection of materials tools and

Dehorning or disbudding, is the process of removing or
arresting the growth of the horn by destroying the root of
the horn bud. Cattle, sheep, and goats are often dehorned
for economic and safety reasons. The procedure is
commonly performed early in an animal's life.
