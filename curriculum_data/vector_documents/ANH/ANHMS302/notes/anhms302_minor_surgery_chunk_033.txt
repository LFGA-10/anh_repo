2. Wound dressing
For a fast and safe healing, the wound needs to be dressed.
Wound dressing consists of applying some sterile dressing
materials with purposes of protecting wounds from further
injury and microbes, Absorb drainage, Promote comfort,
Cover unsightly wounds, Provide moist environment for
wound healing, Control bleeding for wounds that bleed
excessively.

✓
