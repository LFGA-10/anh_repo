2. The elastrator(loop, rubber ring) method
Elastic band castration cuts off blood supply to the
testicles.
A lack of blood supply kills the testicles. The equipment
for banding young animals less than three weeks of age
is called an elastrator.

An elastrator is the tool used to apply an elastic band to
