free of dust, moisture and manure. After use, syringes can
be disinfected by disassembling and washing with hot

reassembly. Soaps, alcohol and other disinfectants will
cause live or modified-live vaccines to become inactive.
Syringes used for live or modified-live vaccines should be
sterilized using either moist or dry heat. If it is necessary to
rinse out a syringe during use, then draw sterile saline or
sterile water into and out of the syringe several times.
1.3.2. Injection techniques
 Intramuscular Injections
Draw air into the syringe and inject the same volume of
air into the bottle as the volume you plan to take out of
the bottle to equalize the pressure. Failure to do so will
make it difficult to withdraw the contents of the vial or
bottle.
