Continuous subcuticular suture patterns
b)
The most commonly used running suture, the Simple
Continuous pattern begins with a simple suture at one end
of the wound. The tail is cut without the needle, and
suturing is continued. Sutures are snagged up as they are
done, except for the last one, which is left as a loop. The tail
is tied to the loop.
