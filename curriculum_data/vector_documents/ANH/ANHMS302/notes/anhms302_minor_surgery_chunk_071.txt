Continued ischemia induces severe cellular damage and

Necrotic tissue, such as the ischemic distal tail after
banding, is prone to infection with pathogens.
Clostridial organisms, ubiquitous in soil, may colonize the
wound and result in local or systemic infection. Tetanus
and gangrene have been reported after tail docking, and
vaccination against clostridia is recommended prior to
performing the procedure.
Rectal prolapse
✓
Ultra-short tail docking may also contribute to the
incidence of vaginal prolapses. Because when tails are
docked too short, the muscles attaching to the tail bone are
weakened.
A rectal prolapse is when a portion of the rectum protrudes
outside the anus. It is easy to recognize. The exposed tissue
is usually a bright, cherry red (at first). Eventually, the
exposed tissue becomes dry and cracked, causing more
irritation and straining.
