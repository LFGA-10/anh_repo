 Topic 2: wound suturing
Wound suturing is a key for a good healing. There are
different techniques and methods of wound suturing which
vary according to many factors such as the nature of the
wound, the availability of the suture materials, etc.
A. Suture materials
Suture is any strand of material used to approximate the
tissue edges and give artificial support while the tissue
heals naturally.
