granulomatous infections such as tuberculosis and

abscesses
Sterile abscesses are those which develop as a result of
non-living irritants such as drugs. If an injected drug like
penicillin is not absorbed, it stays where it was injected and
may cause enough irritation to generate a sterile abscess
with no infection involved.
 Topic 2: Treatment of abscess
Abscess maturation
1.
The treatment consists of hastening the maturation of the
abscess by application of counter irritant such as iodine
ointment 5%. Applying heat in the form of a warm
compress, such as a warm flannel, may help reduce any
swelling and speed up the abscess maturation as well as
healing. However, the flannel should be thoroughly washed
afterwards, to avoid spreading the infection.
