Shaving
b)
It is the removal of the hair around the wound using
scissors or blade. The best way to treat minor scratches,
tears and punctures is with the "clip and clean" method
of basic wound care. This involves clipping the hair from
around the wound and then washing it with an antiseptic
solution. c) Trimming
If part of the wound has dead or damaged skin then it may
need to be trimmed or removed. This is because infection is
more likely to develop in dead skin.
A wound that becomes infected after it has been stitched or
closed up, can cause serious complications. After the
wound is cleaned (and trimmed of dead or damaged tissue,
where necessary), a sterile dressing is normally applied.
Large, severe or deep bites may require an operation to
clean the wound and repair underlying structures that may
be damaged, such as tendons.
