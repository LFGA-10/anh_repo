Hoof trimming complications and prevention
Haemorrhage is the commonest complication when
✓
blood vessels (arteries and/or veins) are tampered with
during the operation.
Infections are also common when by mistake the
✓

which are therefore portal entry for some
microorganisms such as staphylococci and clostridia
