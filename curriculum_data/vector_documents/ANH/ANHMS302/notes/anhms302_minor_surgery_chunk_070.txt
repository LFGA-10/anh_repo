Main complications of caudectomy
2.
Haemorrhage, infections and Rectal prolapsed are the most
common complications, though they are rare when the
procedure is performed. Haemorrhage is usually minimal at
the early age. Instruments should always be thoroughly
cleaned and disinfected between animals. Medication for
pain control, antibiotics, and insect repellent may be
necessary after tail docking.
Haemorrhage
✓
Risk of serious haemorrhage (bleeding), particularly in older
lambs, which causes a major set-back or at worst is fatal;
leaves an open wound which can allow entry of bacteria,
not suitable in fly season; there is scientific evidence that it
is the most painful method; two people required if hygiene
to be maintained.
