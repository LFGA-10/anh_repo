Step 5. Position the needle holder
The needle holder releases the free
suture end. The right hand holding
the needle holder moves away from
the surgeon to be positioned
perpendicular to and above the
fixed suture end. A second throw
will be formed by the left hand as it
wraps the fixed suture end over and
around (arrow) the needle holder
jaws. If the surgeon were to place
the needle holder beneath the fixed
suture end, the ultimate knot
construction would be a granny
knot.
Step 6. Form the second suture
loop
The fixed suture end held by the left
hand is wrapped over and around
the needle holder to form the
second suture loop. With the suture
wrapped around the needle holder
jaws, the needle holder is moved to
