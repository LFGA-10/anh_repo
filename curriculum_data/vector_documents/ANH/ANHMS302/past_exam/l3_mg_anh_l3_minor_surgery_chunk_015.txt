Question 27. Explain the cutting and crushing method of tail docking in
lamb. (10marks)
Marking scheme of question 27:

- Cutting and crushing method of tail docking are docking with the use
of an Emasculator. (2)
- An emasculator has both a cutting and crushing mechanism. It can
be used for tail docking. (2)
- The crushing mechanism seals the blood vessels on the tail remaining
on the lamb, (2)
- while the cutting edge effectively removes the tail. (2)
- The emasculator should be left on the tail for approximately 30
seconds to help prevent bleeding. (2)
