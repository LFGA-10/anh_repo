The tool used when rumen trocarisation for gas escaping is
-trocar and cannula (2)
Its manipulation when uses is as follow:

- A small notch is made through the skin, exactly in the center of the
Para lumbar fossa to allow an easy insertion of the trocar; (2)
- Then, the trocar is powerfully inserted mounted with the cannula. After
that, the trocar is removed from the cannula to allow the escape of free
gas from the rumen; (2)
- Depending on the amount of gases and the type of trocar used, there
might be need for attachment of the cannula to the skin with aid of two
stitches on its either sides; (2)
- The cannula will be maintained for about 24 to 48hours for a complete
gas evacuation. (2)
