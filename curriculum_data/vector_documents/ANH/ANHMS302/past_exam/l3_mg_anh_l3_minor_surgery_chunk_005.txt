Marking scheme Question8:
The animal to be dehorned is restrained in the following manners:
- Young calves are run through a head gate (similar to a Cattle crush)
or haltered to ensure the safety of the calves and handlers.
- Calves more than a few months old are held in a head gate and their
head restrained with a dehorning table or chin bar.
- Smaller animals like sheep and goats may be restrained by hand or
with use of halter.
