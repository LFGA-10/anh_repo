Question 19. For fast and safe healing, a wound need to be sutured and
dressed but first of all cleaned. How the wound is cleaned?
(5marks)
Marking scheme question 19:
The wound cleaning as follow:
-first step of wound cleansing is:
- Wound cleaning and debridement (1) is required to help clear the
wound of debris, such as devitalized tissue or excessive exudates, and
make gentle irrigation of the wound with either warm sterile saline or
water via a syringe, rather than swabbing or bathing, should be
performed to minimize trauma to the wound and to maintain an
optimum healing environment. (1)
- Second step is Shaving (0.5) which is the removal of the hair
around the wound using scissors or blade involves clipping the hair
from around the wound and then washing it with an antiseptic
solution. (1)
- Third step is Trimming (0.5) that is removal of dead or damaged
skin, which may cause infection developed in dead skin. (1)
