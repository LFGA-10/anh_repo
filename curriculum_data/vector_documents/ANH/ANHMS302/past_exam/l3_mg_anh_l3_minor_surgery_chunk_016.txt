Marking scheme question29:
a) It should be performed carefully because it leaves an open wound that
can be the origin of serious complications. 3marks
b) The consequences which may follow saw dehorning are such as:
- Increased hemorrhage in adult animals; 1mark
- Risk of bacterial infection which results in sinusitis, production of pus
infection and possible brain abscessation; 1mark
- Parasitic invasion (myasis=fly maggot); 1mark
To prevent those consequences, it is better to:
- Dehorning that leave an open wound should not be used during fly
season. Paste 1and hot dehorning are acceptable any time of the year;
1mark
- Animal should be monitored for signs of infection following dehorning
such as redness, swelling and discharge; 1mark
- Avoid the use of elevated feeders immediately after the procedure to
prevent entry of debris into the wound minimize environmental
exposure as dust and rain may increase the risk of sinusitis; 1mark
- Never dehorn animal in wet weather because the healing rate is
decreased and the risk of infection increased. 1mark
