Question27: Discuss on storage conditions of cleaning equipment and
chemicals. (Any three conditions) 3marks
ANSWER
✓ Store cleaning supplies in an area that is clean and free of debris.
Make sure that there are not any temperature extremes in the area
where your cleaning supplies are stored. Another thing to make sure
of is that the area is dry.1mark
✓ Keep cleaning supplies in their original containers. If you mix your
own cleaners, make sure you use new clean bottles and label them to
avoid a mix up.1mark
✓ Be sure to keep your cleaning supplies stored in places where
children/or pets will not be able to get to them.1mark
✓ Store chemicals in their originally labeled containers and make sure
they are closed properly.1mark
✓ Never store chemicals near food, food storage areas or any tools or
equipment that will touch food. 1mark
✓ Always read instructions on the label before use, even if it’s product
you use regularly. You don’t want to accidentally use the product in
the wrong area or use it incorrectly. 1mark
