Question 1: Define the following veterinary terms
a) Pharmacology
b) Pharmacy
c) Pharmacodynamics 4marks
d) Pharmacokinetics
ANSWER
a) Pharmacology: The science and study of drugs, including the
chemistry and biological effects.1mark
b) Pharmacy: The art, practice or profession of preparing, preserving,
compounding and dispensing medical drugs.1mark
c) Pharmacodynamics: The science and study of how drugs produce
their effects.1mark
d) Pharmacokinetics: The study of the route medications take in the
body. How they are absorbed, transformed, distributed, metabolized
and eliminated.1mark
Reference:( LU1, store veterinary drugs according to their therapeutic
function, LO1, Identify and select forms of veterinary drugs,
Curriculum level 3, page 3 year 2020.

4marks
ANSWER
Antiseptic: is a chemical agent that reduces the microbial population on
skin and other living tissues.2marks
Disinfection: is the elimination of many or all pathogens organisms,
excluding spore forms, from inanimate object.2marks
