Question11: Differentiate live attenuated vaccines from inactivated
vaccines. 4marks
ANSWER
Live attenuated vaccine: use a weakened (or attenuated) form of the germ
that causes a disease. Because these vaccines are so similar to the nature
infection that they help prevent, they create a strong and long-lasting
immune response. 2marks
Inactivated vaccines: use killed version of the germ that causes a disease.
Inactivated vaccines usually don’t provide immunity (protection)that’s as
strong as live vaccines. So you may need several doses over time (booster
shots) in order to get ongoing immunity against diseases. 2marks
