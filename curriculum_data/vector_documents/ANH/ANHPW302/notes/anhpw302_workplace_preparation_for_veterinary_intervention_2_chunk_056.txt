2. Chemical methods of sterilization
Disinfection can be achieved by chemical methods. Chemicals used in disinfection are called disinfectants.
Different disinfectants have different target ranges, not all disinfectants can kill all microorganisms. Some
methods of disinfection such as filtration do not kill bacteria, they separate them out. Sterilization is an
absolute condition while disinfection is not.
