This can be term the best arrangement. Medical products come in different forms. Topic 2: Conservation material:

Technical inventory control of drugs and others supplies are usually taught in conjunction with business management concepts. However, besides the business aspects, medical and legal reason exists for proper storage and handling of drugs.

Among the conservation material we have the fridge, shelves, cool box, etc. Note that each material has its physical properties which permit it to conserve this kind of drugs and not for that drug due to its conservation requirements. For example drug that is not stored in the proper temperature and light can degenerate or become inactivated, providing little or no benefit.
