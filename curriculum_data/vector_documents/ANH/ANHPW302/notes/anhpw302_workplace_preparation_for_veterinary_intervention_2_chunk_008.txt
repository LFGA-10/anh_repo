A. Antiseptics and disinfectants:
An antiseptic is a chemical agent that reduces the microbial population on skin and other living tissues.
Note: An ideal antiseptic would have a broad spectrum of activity, low toxicity and high penetrability.
Disinfection is the elimination of many or all pathogenic organisms, excluding spore forms, from an
inanimate object.

ii. Chlorine compounds
Chlorine compound belong to large group of compound known as Halogens. Chlorine kills vegetative forms
of bacteria, algae, and fungi.
 Example: Chlorine is one of the few disinfectants effective against parvovirus.
iii. Iodine compounds and iodophors
Iodine compound and Iodophors are most commonly used as topical antiseptics before surgery procedure
or for disinfection of tissues. Iodine and high concentrations of Iodophors are bactericidal, virusidal,
protozoacidal and fungicidal.
iv. Chlorhexidine
Chlorhexidine, is a member of a class of antiseptic known as Biguanides, is one of the most commonly used
disinfectants and antiseptics compounds in veterinary medicine. It has virusidal, bactericidal (both gram-
positive and gram negative), and fungicidal properties.
