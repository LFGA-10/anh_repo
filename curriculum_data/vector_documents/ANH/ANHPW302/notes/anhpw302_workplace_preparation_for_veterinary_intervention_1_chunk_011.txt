Sulphonamides

Sulfonamide were the first antimicrobials used on widespread basis in human and veterinary medicine, because they have been in use for many years, many strains of bacteria have became resistant to them.

Microbial susceptibility: Sulfonamides are broad spectrum (including protozoa) and bacteriostatic.

Example: Sulfamethazine,Sulfadimethoxine,Sulfachlorpyridazine,Sulfamethoxazole,Sulfacetamideand Sulfasalazine.

Antifungals

This group of antimicrobial drugs was developed to be effective against many of the fungal organism that cause superficial mycoses.

Amphotericin B

Amphotericin B is effective against most of the deep mycosis; it is fungicidal against most organisms causing systemic mycoses
