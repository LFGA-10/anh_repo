LU1: Store veterinary drugs according to their therapeutic function
1.1 Identify and select forms of veterinary drugs
Topic 1: Definition of terminologies:
a. Pharmacology: The science and study of drugs, including the chemistry and biological effects
b. Pharmacy:
1. The art, practice, or profession of preparing, preserving, compounding, and dispensing medical drugs
2. A place where drugs are compounded or dispensed.
c. Pharmacodynamics: The science and study of how drugs produce their effects
d. Pharmacokinetic: The study of the route medications take in the body. How they are absorbed,
transformed, distributed, metabolized, and eliminated
e. Pharmacognosy: Deals with the nature and sources of natural drugs obtained from plants or
animals, either directly or indirectly
f. Drugs: A chemical used in the diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of disease.
g. Poisons: a substance that through its chemical action usually kills, injures, or impairs an organism
h. Antiseptic: A substance that inhibits the proliferation of infectious microorganisms.
i. Toxicology: the study of the conditions (including dose) under which exposure of living systems to
chemicals is hazardous.
j. Poisoning: Poisoning is a condition or a process in which an organism becomes chemically harmed
severely (poisoned) by a toxic substance or venom of an animal
Topic 2: Drug names
Drug nomenclature is the systematic naming of drugs, especially pharmaceutical drugs. In the majority
of circumstances, drugs have 3 types of names: chemical names, generic or nonproprietary names and
trade names, which are brand names
