The generation of wastes may present threats to the environment and public health, but this depends on
the type of waste management option and the way it is controlled. Waste is a complex mixture of different
substances, only some of which are intrinsically hazardous to health.
Topic 1: Classification of waste
In general, wastes are classified into biodegradable and non-biodegradable wastes.
1. Biodegradable
Biodegradable waste is a type of waste which can be broken down, in a reasonable amount of time, into its
base compounds by microorganisms and other living things regardless of what of what those compounds
may be. The main environmental threat of biodegradable wastes is the production of methane. wastes can
be used for composting or a resource of heat, electricity or fuel by means of incineration or anaerobic
digestion, production of energy in the form of biogas.
2. Non-biodegradable
This is the type of waste that cannot be broken down into its base compounds by microorganisms. They
constitute a problem by overwhelming landfills thus, recycling then be a good management.
