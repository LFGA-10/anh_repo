Tetracyclines

Tetracyclines are a group of bacteriostatic antimicrobials that have been used in veterinary medicine for many years, especially in animal’s food production.

Example: 	Oxytetracycline, 	Chlortetracycline, 	Demethylchlortetracycline,Tetracycline,Rolitetracycline, Methacycline,Minocycline,….).

Microbial susceptibility: Tetracyclines are broad-spectrum which comprises both aerobic and anaerobic gram –positive and gram-negative bacteria.

Quinolones

Quinolones, or fluoroquinolones, are a group of bactericidal antimicrobials that are continually expending in human and veterinary medicine.

Difloxacin:  is used for treatment of dermal, respiratory, and urinary tract infectionsin dogs.
