 Workplace cleaning and sanitization methods and techniques

Keeping your workplace clean means having a systematic and thorough approach to keep working and storage areas clean sanitise and free from garbage and pests. It also means following hygienic practices for handling equipment and disposing of waste or garbage. All of this is necessary to prevent or minimise the risk of cross-contamination of harmful bacteria, chemicals or even disease. This would pose significant safety hazards for customers and staff.

 Clean and sanitize instruments and equipment

In general, surgical instruments and medications that enter an already aseptic part of the body (such as the bloodstream, or penetrating the skin) must be sterilized to a high sterility assurance level. Preparation of injectable medications and intravenous solutions for fluid replacement therapy requires not only a high sterility assurance level, but also well-designed containers to prevent entry of adventitious agents after initial product sterilization.

 Sanitization, disinfection and sterilization

Sterilization is the process used to render an object free of all microorganisms while Sanitation is reduction of the number of bacterial contaminants to a safe level and Disinfection is the process that destroys most pathogenic microorganisms. Both sanitization and disinfection reduce the number of targeted pathogenic organisms to what are considered "acceptable" levels
