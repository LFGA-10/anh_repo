Topic 1: Important of cleaning of flat areas: ( Floors Walls, Ceiling, Shelves, Counters, Tables Benches)
Keeping your workplace clean means having a systematic and thorough approach to keep working and
storage areas clean, sanitized and free from garbage and pests. It also means following hygienic practices
for handling equipment and disposing of waste or garbage. All of this is necessary to prevent or minimize
the risk of cross-contamination of harmful bacteria, chemicals or even disease. This would pose significant
safety hazards for customers and staff
Important of cleaning of flat areas
 Floors: The complete floor, including all edges, corners, and main floor space, should have a uniform
finish or shine and be visibly clean, with no blood and body substances, dust, dirt, debris, or
spillages. Working floors need to be swept or vacuumed and washed at the end of every working
day, as dirty floors attract vermin and pests. Floors in the equipment and dry stores should be swept
daily, and washed at least once a week.
 Walls: All wall surfaces, including skirting, should be visibly clean, with no blood and body
substances, dust, dirt, debris, adhesive tape, or spillages
 Tables: All parts of the table (including wheels, castors, and underneath) should be visibly clean,
with no blood and body substances, dust, dirt, debris, adhesive tape, stains, or spillages
Topic 2: Procedure of cleaning:
 Cleaning is a process which will remove soil and prevent accumulation of residues which may
