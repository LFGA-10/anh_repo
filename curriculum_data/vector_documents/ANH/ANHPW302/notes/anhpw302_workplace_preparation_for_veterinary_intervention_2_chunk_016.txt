 Phenylbutazone. The safety and efficacy profile in addition to its affordability makes it is the most
commonly used NSAID in the horse and others species. Therapeutic uses: It is used to treat various
forms of lameness as well as osteoarthritis and other painful conditions of the limbs including soft
tissue or non-articular rheumatism.
 Ibuprofen and indomethacin: These two drugs are not routinely used in veterinary medicine due to
its low safety profile.
 Diclofenac: Diclofenac is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) taken or applied to reduce
inflammation and as an analgesic reducing pain in certain conditions. It is supplied as or contained in
medications under a variety of trade names. Diclofenac Sodium Injection for veterinary use is for the
treatment of various bacterial infections in sheep, swine, cattle, goats, and calves.
