Topic 4: Endocrine systems
Hormones drugs either natural or synthetic are used in food animals and horses to synchronize oestrus
cycle, terminate pregnancy, and induce ovulation.
A. Drugs affecting reproductive system (Hormones)
Those are:
1. Oestradiol (Oestradiol benzoate): Oestradiol is an oestrogen used for the treatment of misalliance and
hormonal urinary incontinence in bitches.
2. Megoestrol acetate (Ovarid): This is an oral progestogen, indicated for the prevention of oestrus in the
bitch.
3. Others hormones are: Gonadotropins, FSH, LH, Equine chorionic gonadotropine, Human chorionic
gonadotropin, progestin’s, progestogens, progestationalhormons, estrogens, prostaglandins, corticosteroid,
corticosteroid and oxytocin.
