LU1: Store veterinary drugs according to their therapeutic function

1.1 Identify and select forms of veterinary drugs

Topic 1: Definition of terminologies:

a. Pharmacology: The science and study of drugs, including the chemistry and biological effects b. Pharmacy:

The art, practice, or profession of preparing, preserving, compounding, and dispensing medical drugs

A place where drugs are compounded or dispensed.

Pharmacodynamics: The science and study of how drugs produce their effects

Pharmacokinetic: The study of the route medications take in the body. How they are absorbed, transformed, distributed, metabolized, and eliminated

Pharmacognosy: Deals with the nature and sources of natural drugs obtained from plants or animals, either directly or indirectly

Drugs: A chemical used in the diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of disease.

Poisons:  a substance that through its chemical action usually kills, injures, or impairs an organism

Antiseptic: A substance that inhibits the proliferation of infectious microorganisms.

Toxicology: the study of the conditions (including dose) under which exposure of living systems to chemicals is hazardous.

Poisoning: Poisoning is a condition or a process in which an organism becomes chemically harmed severely (poisoned) by a toxic substance or venom of an animal

Topic 2: Drug names

Drug nomenclature is the systematic naming of drugs, especially pharmaceutical drugs. In the majority of circumstances, drugs have 3 types of names: chemical names, generic or nonproprietary names and trade names, which are brand names.
