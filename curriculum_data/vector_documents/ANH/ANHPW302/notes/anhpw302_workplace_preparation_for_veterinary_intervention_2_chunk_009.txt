v. Quaternary ammonium compounds
Quaternary ammonium compounds or “Quats” are used in veterinary medicine to disinfect the surfaces of
inanimate objects. Quaternary ammonium compounds are effective against gram-positive bacteria,but they
are ineffective against bacterial spores and have poor efficacy against fungi and gram-negative bacteria.
 Example: Benzalkonium chloride.
vi. Peroxides and oxidants
These compounds generally exert a short-acting germicidal effect on most organisms through release of
nascent oxygen, which irreversibly alters microbial proteins.
B. Antibiotics:
Are the drugs that kill or inhibit the growth of micro-organism or microbes such as bacteria, protozoa and
fungi. The term antibiotic is often used interchangeably with the term antimicrobial.
