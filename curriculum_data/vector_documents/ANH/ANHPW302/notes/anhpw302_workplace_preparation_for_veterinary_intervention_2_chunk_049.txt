2.3 Store the materials tools and equipment
Topic 1: Types of storage materials tools instrument and equipment
a) Storing non sterile tools instrument and equipment
Instrument may corrode as result of adverse storage conditions. To prevent this, they should be stored in a
dry and dust-free condition. Major temperature fluctuation should be avoided in order to prevent
accumulation of moisture on instrument surface.
Chemicals may destroy metals when in direct contact with them or may emit corrosive vapors. Never store
your instruments near chemicals
b) Storing of sterile tools instruments and equipment
To guarantee instruments sterility up to the time of use, germ-tight packaging is absolutely essential.
Further requirements for protected storage of sterile supplies and prevention of corrosion damage includes
a dust-free and dry environment and the prevention of temperature fluctuation
c) Storing of materials tools instrument and equipment per function:
The instruments may be stored depending on their function such as:
 Instruments for identification of animals
 Diagnostic instruments
 Administration of medicine instruments
 Obstetrical instrument.

 Materials for meat inspection
