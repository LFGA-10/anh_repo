b. Trade Name
The trade name is also referred to as the brand name or proprietary name; it is the manufacturer’s name
for the medication. Notice that the brand name is very prominent on the label and is capitalized. It is
important to remember that different manufacturers may market a medication under different trade
names. The trade name is followed by an ®, which is the registration symbol. Some medications may have
the abbreviation™ after the trade name, which stands for trademark.
