 Cleaning is a process which will remove soil and prevent accumulation of residues which may
decompose or support the growth of disease causing organisms or the production of toxins.
The cleaning operation:
 Prewash (Clear the floor and flat areas) - the removal of gross particles before applying the cleaning
solution. This may be accomplished by flushing the equipment surface with cold or warm water under
moderate pressure. Very hot water or steam should not be used because it may make cleaning more
difficult.
 Washing (Sweep or vaccum) - the application of the cleaning compound. There are many methods of
subjecting the surface of equipment to cleaning compounds and solutions. Effectiveness and the
economy of the method
 Rinsing (Mop the floor and flat areas)- the removal of all traces of the cleaning solution with clean
potable water.
 Sanitization (Buff the floor and flat areas) generally dictate its use.
The process is performed either by using heat or a chemical concentration that will reduce the bacterial
count, including pathogens to a safe level on utensils and equipment after cleaning.
