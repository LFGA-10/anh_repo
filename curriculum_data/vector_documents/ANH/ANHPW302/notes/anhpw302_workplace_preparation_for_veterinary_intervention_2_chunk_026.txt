Venodilators relax systemic veins, increase venous capacitance, decrease cardiac filling pressures (preload),

1. Hydralazine

Hydralazine is a phthalazine derivative vasodilating agent. Hydralazine directly relaxes arteriolar smooth
muscle with little effect on the venous system.
Therapeutic uses: Hydralazine, in combination with furosemide, is especially useful for dogs with mitral
insufficiency and severe pulmonary edema by rapidly reducing arterial resistance (afterload). Hydralazine
has also been used in the treatment of myocardial failure and hypertension.
2. Amlodipine
Amlodipine besylate is dihydropyridine Ca2+channel blocker.
Therapeutic uses: Amlodipine is the drug of first choice for systemic arterial hypertension in cats. It can be a
useful adjunct therapeutic agent in hypertensive dogs.
3. Nitrates
Commonly used nitrates include sodium nitroprusside (for IV infusion), nitroglycerinetopical ointment (2%),
and oral isosorbidedinitrate.
C. Diuretics
Diuretic therapy is indicated to control edema and effusions that occur in chronic heart failure. Excessive
use of diuretics in heart failure exacerbates neurohormonal activation.
1. Furosemide: This loop-diuretic is used almost exclusively for animals with cardiogenic edema or effusion.
