iii. Aminoglycosides
Aminoglucosides are powerful group of antimicrobials used in veterinary medicine to combat a variety of
serious bacterial infections.
Page 7 of 61

Microbial susceptibility: Aminoglucosides are highly effective against many oxygen-dependant (aerobic)
bacteria but are ineffective against most anaerobic bacteria.
Example: Gentamicin,Amikacin,Neomycin,Streptomycin,Kanamycin,Tobramycin,andNetilmicin.
iv. Tetracyclines
Tetracyclines are a group of bacteriostatic antimicrobials that have been used in veterinary medicine for
many years, especially in animal’s food production.
Example: Oxytetracycline, Chlortetracycline, Demethylchlortetracycline,Tetracycline,Rolitetracycline,
Methacycline,Minocycline,….).
Microbial susceptibility: Tetracyclines are broad-spectrum which comprises both aerobic and anaerobic
gram –positive and gram-negative bacteria.
v. Quinolones
Quinolones, or fluoroquinolones, are a group of bactericidal antimicrobials that are continually expending
in human and veterinary medicine.
Microbial susceptibility:
 Enrofloxacin: is used in the treatment of dermal, respiratory, and urinary tract infections (including
prostatitis) in dogs, cats, and birds and in respiratory infections in cattle.
 Danofloxacin: is used for the treatment of bovine respiratory infections including Mannheimia
species.
 Difloxacin: is used for treatment of dermal, respiratory, and urinary tract infectionsin dogs.
 Orbifloxacin and Marbofloxacin: are used for the treatment of dermal, respiratory, and urinary tract
infections of dogs and cats.
 Orbifloxacin: is used for susceptible Gram (–) infections in horses.
