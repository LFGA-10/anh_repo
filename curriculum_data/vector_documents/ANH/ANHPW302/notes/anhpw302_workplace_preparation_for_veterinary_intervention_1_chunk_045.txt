2.3 Store the materials tools and equipment

Topic 1: Types of storage materials tools instrument and equipment

Storing non sterile tools instrument and equipment

Instrument may corrode as result of adverse storage conditions. To prevent this, they should be stored in a dry and dust-free condition. Major temperature fluctuation should be avoided in order to prevent accumulation of moisture on instrument surface.

Chemicals may destroy metals when in direct contact with them or may emit corrosive vapors. Never store your instruments near chemicals
