Topic 3: Composition of a drug
A form of a drug is traditionally composed by: active ingredient, excipient adjuvant and additives.
a. Active ingredient: Any substance or combination of substances used in a finished pharmaceutical
product (FPP), intended to furnish pharmacological activity or to otherwise have direct effect in the
diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment or prevention of disease, or to have direct effect in restoring,
correcting or modifying physiological functions in human beings."
b. Excipient: A more or less inert substance added in a prescription as a diluent or vehicle or to give
form or consistency when the remedy is given in pill form; e.g., simple syrup, vegetable gums,
aromatic powder, honey, and various elixirs.
c. Adjuvant: it is a substance that aids another, such as an auxiliary remedy
d. Additive: A substance added in small amounts to somethinglse to improve, strengthen, or
otherwisealterit.Additivesareusedforavarietyofreasons.Theyareaddedtofood,forexample,toenhance
tasteor color to prevent spoilage
