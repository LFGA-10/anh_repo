naming of drugs, especially pharmaceutical drugs. In the majority
of circumstances, drugs have 3 types of names: chemical names, generic or nonproprietary names and
trade names, which are brand names.
a. Chemical name: The chemical names are the scientific names, based on the molecular structure of
the drug. Chemical names are typically very long and too complex to be commonly used in
referring to a drug in speech or in prose documents.
Eg: 1-(isopropylamino)-3-(1-naphthyloxy) propan-2-ol" is a chemical name for propranolol.
b. Generic/nonproprietary: is more concise name given to the specific chemical compound. The
nonproprietary names are usually listed as active ingredient on drug labels and can be found on
the drug label for many different drug manufacturers.
 Example: Amoxicillin
c. Trade name: is a unique name a manufacturer gives its particularly brand of drug, because the
trade names are proper nouns they are capitalized. The names are followed by the marks ®or ™ to
signify that the trade name is registered trademark and cannot be legally used by other
manufacturers.
