1.2 Identify and select of veterinary drugs according to their therapeutic functions.
Topic 1: Drugs classification:
A drug may be classified by the chemical type of the active ingredient or by the way it is used to treat a
particular condition. Each drug can be classified into one or more drug classes. Drugs are classified into
different groups according to their chemical characteristics, structure and how they are used to treat
specific disease.
