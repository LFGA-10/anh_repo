4. Question: A new intern at the clinic is unsure about the
difference between cleaning, disinfection, and sterilization.
Using examples from the module, explain these three
processes and provide a practical example of when each would
be used in a veterinary clinic.
Marking Guide (Total: 9 marks)
o Cleaning (3 marks):
▪ Definition: Removal of visible soil, organic material,
and debris. It does not kill microorganisms. (1
mark)
▪ Example: Washing a stainless steel surgery table
with soap and water after a procedure to remove
blood and hair. (2 marks)
o Disinfection (3 marks):
▪ Definition: Process that destroys most pathogenic
microorganisms, but not necessarily bacterial
spores. (1 mark)
▪ Example: Wiping down an examination table with a
disinfectant solution like a quaternary ammonium
compound after a consultation. (2 marks)
