o b) Essential Instruments (8 marks):
▪ Dehorning: Disbudding iron, Barnes dehorner. (1
mark) Justification: For disbudding calves and
removing small horns in goats/sheep. (1 mark)
▪ Obstetrics: Calving ropes/chains, calf puller, eye
hooks. (1 mark) Justification: Essential for assisting
with difficult births in cattle. (1 mark)
▪ General Surgery/Castration: Burdizzo,
emasculators, scalpel handles/blades, surgical
scissors, artery forceps, needle holder, suture
needles. (2 marks) Justification: The Burdizzo is for
bloodless castration, emasculators for open
castration; the surgical pack is needed for any
wound suturing or minor surgeries. (1 mark)
▪ Restraint: Bull ring and lead, nose holder for cattle,
mouth gags. (1 mark) Justification: Crucial for safely
handling large animals like cattle and horses during
examination and treatment. (1 mark)
o c) Surgical Pack Preparation Protocol (6 marks):
▪ Step 1: Cleaning: Immediately after use,
instruments should be pre-rinsed with cold water to
remove blood and debris, then washed with warm
water and a neutral pH detergent. (2 marks)
▪ Step 2: Inspection & Drying: Instruments should
be inspected for damage, dried thoroughly to
prevent corrosion, and hinged instruments should
be opened. (2 marks)
