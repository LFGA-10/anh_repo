Level: Analyzing
3. Question: Compare and contrast the different classes of
antimicrobials (Antibiotics, Antiseptics/Disinfectants, and
Antifungals) based on their target microorganisms and general
usage in a veterinary practice.
Marking Guide (Total: 9 marks)
o Antibiotics (3 marks):
▪ Target: Kill or inhibit the growth of micro-
organisms, primarily bacteria. (1 mark)
▪ Usage: Used internally/externally to treat systemic
or localized bacterial infections. (1 mark)
▪ Example: Penicillin, Tetracycline. (1 mark)
o Antiseptics/Disinfectants (3 marks):
▪ Target: Broad spectrum including bacteria, fungi,
and viruses. (1 mark)
▪ Usage: Antiseptics are used on living tissue;
disinfectants are used on inanimate
objects/surfaces to reduce pathogens. (1 mark)
▪ Example: Chlorhexidine (antiseptic), Chlorine
compounds (disinfectant). (1 mark)
